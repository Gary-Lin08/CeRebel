# Running Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel running content summarizes **open biomechanics literature, sports-medicine consensus themes, and
reputable coaching education**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy
diagnosis, or individualized return-to-run protocols.

- Sharp pain, swelling, stress-fracture history, chest pain, or dizziness during running require evaluation
  by a qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to increase volume or intensity.
- Youth and masters athletes have different bone, tendon, and recovery tolerance; follow coach and medical
  guidance for growth-related pain (e.g., apophysitis) and cardiovascular screening when required.

## Not a training-plan or load-management system

Weekly mileage, intensity distribution, and race peaking are **not** enforced by body tracking alone.
This pack educates on visible gait tendencies; official training plans belong to coaches and licensed
professionals.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Foot strike position relative to center of mass when the camera is not true sagittal.
- Knee valgus in the frontal plane when filmed from the side only.
- Pelvis obliquity when shorts or occlusion hide hip landmarks.
- Cadence when foot contacts are missed at low frame rate.

Findings describe **visible tendencies**, not laboratory-grade kinematics or kinetics. Confidence should
be lower when joints are occluded or FPS is below ~24.

## Cadence and “ideal form”

Research shows relationships between step rate, stride length, and joint loading in controlled studies,
but **no single cadence or foot-strike pattern is optimal for every runner**. Changing cadence may help
some athletes and harm others if applied abruptly without progression. This pack avoids promising injury
prevention from a single cue.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Published studies report **group averages and correlations**, not one correct pose for every body type,
speed, or footwear. Terrain, fatigue, and shoe choice change appearance. Numeric checkpoints in this pack
are conservative coaching heuristics for product gating — not clinical thresholds.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, athletes, and parents who already have a training context. They do not
replace supervised learning for beginners or post-injury retraining.

## Emergency

For collapse, suspected cardiac event, or acute trauma on a run, follow venue emergency procedures and
seek immediate medical attention.

## Product behavior

- Running pack features may be gated as “coming soon” until detector validation completes — absence of a
  finding is not proof of safe mechanics or readiness to race.
- The system may refuse to analyze clips that appear to be other sports (golf swing, pitch, kick).

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. Guidelines change; retrieval
dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Change training load
with a qualified coach or clinician.”
