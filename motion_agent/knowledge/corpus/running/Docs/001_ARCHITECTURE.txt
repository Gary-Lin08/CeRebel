# Running Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first running gait coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains how the athlete moves
through repeatable stride phases. GPS, foot pods, and treadmill speed readouts are optional context — not
required for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Distance and fitness running** on level ground or treadmill, captured from side or rear oblique view.
- Continuous clips with at least two full stride cycles on each leg when possible.

Out of scope for automatic findings (glossary or future packs only):
- Race tactics, pacing strategy from watch data, track lane rules, relay exchanges.
- Hurdles, steeplechase, trail obstacle negotiation, starting blocks / sprint acceleration.
- Shoe prescription and orthotic medical advice.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, timing between
   contact proxies, pelvis and trunk orientation visible without wearables.
2. **Evidence layering**: systematic reviews and sports-medicine consensus (A/B+) inform load-related
   language; coaching cues and drills are advice (B), always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric targets are avoided as individual prescriptions; ranges are
   described qualitatively or attributed to published cohort summaries with youth/adult separation.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs
   so the motion pipeline can attach findings to stride windows.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned RunningPack)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Preferred joints: ankles, knees, hips/pelvis, shoulders, wrists (arm swing), head/neck when stable.
- Camera: sagittal or oblique sagittal improves trunk lean and overstride proxy; pure frontal improves
  crossover and hip-drop visibility.

### Run-likeness gate (conservative)

Before enabling full sport findings, a future detector may score:
- **Bilateral ankle periodicity**: repeated speed peaks consistent with running cadence.
- **Flight proxy**: intervals where both ankles rise together (running vs walking).
- **Trunk vertical oscillation** within running-typical band.

Score gates sport-mode selection only; it does not authorize medical advice.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated stride segmenters ship, align narrative phases to computable proxies per leg:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_IC | Stance-leg ankle downward velocity minimum | Contact proxy, not force-plate IC |
| E_MIDSTANCE | Max knee flexion between E_IC and E_TO | Single-leg support |
| E_TO | Ankle extension velocity peak before swing | Toe-off proxy |
| E_NEXT_IC | Following contact on same leg | Defines stride period |

Phases `contact` through `flight` are bounded by these events (see Phases/001).

### Coach brief generation

Future `build_coach_briefs(..., sport="running")` will consume:
- Stride windows and clip-level cadence estimates.
- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × phase alignment.
- One feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding (from Coaching/).

Chat agent (`prompt_mode=running` when shipped) will add sport analysis nudge and refuse mislabeled
golf or pitch captures.

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.
- Repeat stable finding IDs (`overstride`, `hip_drop`, etc.) in Findings and cross-link in Coaching.
- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time (manifest-driven).
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` as coaching truth — ops only.

## Youth vs adult

Cadence, stride length, and acceptable asymmetry differ by maturation. Chunks must tag
`audience=youth` or `audience=adult` where numeric bands appear. Never apply adult overstride
heuristics to U12 without widened tolerances.
