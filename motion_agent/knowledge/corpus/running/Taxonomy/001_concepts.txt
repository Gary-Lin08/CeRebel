# Running Gait — Concept Taxonomy (v1)

## Primary concepts

concept_id: distance_running
label_en: Distance running gait
definition: Cyclical locomotion with aerial phase; body-first analysis uses periodic ankle and trunk signals without GPS.
synonyms_en: jogging, fitness running, endurance running, easy run form
synonyms_zh: 跑步, 慢跑, 长跑姿态
parent: running
authority: B

concept_id: stride_cycle
label_en: Stride cycle
definition: Period from initial contact on one leg to next contact on the same leg; foundation for phase model.
synonyms_en: gait cycle, step cycle, running stride
related_phases: contact through flight
authority: B+

concept_id: cadence
label_en: Cadence (step rate)
definition: Steps per minute; computed from contact event timing on both feet.
synonyms_en: step rate, spm, steps per minute, turnover
related_series: series_cadence
authority: B+

concept_id: overstriding
label_en: Overstriding
definition: Foot landing noticeably ahead of body center at contact; associated with increased braking in literature.
synonyms_en: reaching with the foot, landing in front, heel strike far ahead
related_findings: overstride, low_cadence_overstride
authority: B+

concept_id: vertical_oscillation
label_en: Vertical oscillation
definition: Up-down displacement of pelvis or COM each stride; excessive bounce may waste energy.
synonyms_en: bounce, vertical displacement, bobbing
related_findings: excessive_bounce
authority: B+

concept_id: hip_drop
label_en: Hip drop (pelvis obliquity)
definition: Stance-side pelvis drops relative to swing side during single-leg support.
synonyms_en: Trendelenburg gait pattern, pelvic drop, hip sag
related_findings: hip_drop
authority: A/B+

concept_id: trunk_posture_running
label_en: Running trunk posture
definition: Sagittal alignment of thorax relative to vertical; forward lean from ankle vs hunch differ in coaching.
synonyms_en: posture, lean, torso angle
related_findings: trunk_lean_back, trunk_lean_excess_fwd
authority: B+

concept_id: arm_swing_running
label_en: Running arm swing
definition: Shoulder-driven counter-rotation to balance leg motion; asymmetry visible in ROM or timing.
synonyms_en: arm carriage, arm drive
related_findings: arm_swing_asymmetry
authority: B

concept_id: crossover_gait_running
label_en: Crossover gait
definition: Foot lands medial to body midline, sometimes crossing toward opposite side.
synonyms_en: cross-over, line cross, narrow base
related_findings: crossover_gait
authority: B+

concept_id: knee_valgus_running
label_en: Knee valgus on landing (visible proxy)
definition: Knee moves inward relative to hip–ankle line in frontal view during loading; product uses conservative proxy.
synonyms_en: knee collapse, dynamic valgus, knees in
related_findings: knee_valgus_load
authority: B+

## Phase concepts (align to running:phase_*)

concept_id: phase_contact
label_en: Touchdown
start_event: E_IC
body_focus: foot placement, ankle compliance, initial knee flexion

concept_id: phase_loading
label_en: Loading
body_focus: shock absorption, increasing knee flexion, hip stability

concept_id: phase_midstance
label_en: Mid-stance
body_focus: single-leg balance, pelvis level, trunk stack

concept_id: phase_propulsion
label_en: Propulsion
body_focus: hip/knee/ankle extension, forward drive

concept_id: phase_toe_off
label_en: Toe-off
body_focus: ankle plantarflexion, transition to swing

concept_id: phase_swing
label_en: Swing
body_focus: knee lift, foot clearance, arm counter-swing

concept_id: phase_flight
label_en: Flight
body_focus: aerial phase, late swing descent, minimal double-support in true running

## Capture concepts

concept_id: capture_sagittal_running
label_en: Sagittal-side running capture
definition: Camera perpendicular to direction of travel; best for overstride and trunk lean proxies.
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: capture_rear_running
label_en: Rear running capture
definition: Camera behind athlete; best for crossover and hip drop.
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: treadmill_running_capture
label_en: Treadmill capture
definition: Belt motion affects ground reference; interpret overstride relative to pelvis in image plane.
authority: ProductLogic
