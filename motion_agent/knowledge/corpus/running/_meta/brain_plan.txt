# CeRebel Running KB — Brain Plan (v0.1)
# Agent: BRAIN | Sport: running (distance / fitness gait)
# Aligns: SPORT_PACK_KB_SPEC.txt
# Product: body-first OpenSim coaching; NO GPS / foot pod required

================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for **running gait** from monocular or multi-view body
capture. Analysis emphasizes repeatable stride mechanics visible on the skeleton:
contact timing, trunk posture, hip/pelvis level, knee flexion in stance, arm swing,
and left–right asymmetry.

Out of scope v1:
- Medical diagnosis of stress fracture, plantar fasciitis, or IT band syndrome from video
- Prescriptive cadence targets without age/context disclaimer
- Sprint block starts and maximal-acceleration first 10 m (different phase model)

Build agent writes Findings/Coaching bodies from this map.

================================================================================
2. PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

- Measurable claims tie to reconstructed body: joint angles, segment speeds, timing.
- Phase scrub bar uses seven phase IDs below (stable contract for future RunningPack).
- Each finding maps to: phase window, series_id, feel cue, 30–90s drill.

  B for coaching cues; no C in automated thresholds.

================================================================================
3. PHASE MODEL (ONE STRIDE CYCLE)
================================================================================

Phase IDs (exact — for Phases/001_phase_model.txt):

| id            | label_en        | role |
|---------------|-----------------|------|
| contact       | Touchdown       | Lead foot accepts weight; deceleration begins |
| loading       | Loading         | Knee/hip flexion increases; shock absorption |
| midstance     | Mid-stance      | COM over base of support; single-leg balance |
| propulsion    | Propulsion      | Ankle/knee/hip extension; push toward toe-off |
| toe_off       | Toe-off         | Foot leaves ground; stance ends |
| swing         | Swing           | Thigh advances; knee flexes; foot clears ground |
| flight        | Flight / descent| Both feet airborne (running) or late swing prep |

Event definitions (body-only):

- ic_event: local minimum of lead-ankle vertical velocity OR max downward foot speed
  toward ground in stride window (contact proxy; not force-plate IC).
- midstance_event: frame of max knee flexion on stance leg between ic and toe_off.
- toe_off_event: local max of stance-ankle upward velocity or knee extension velocity peak
  before swing ankle overtakes stance side.
- next_ic_event: following ic on same leg (defines stride period for cadence).

Handedness / leg side: findings may tag `side=left|right|bilateral`. Default clip analysis
reports dominant asymmetry (higher severity side).

================================================================================
5. EVIDENCE-BANDED NORMS (NOT MEDICAL PRESCRIPTION)
================================================================================

ADULT recreational / distance (authority mostly B+ / A for injury themes):

- Cadence: cohort means often ~160–180 steps/min at easy pace; wide inter-individual
  spread. Heiderscheit et al. (2011) show +10% step rate can reduce peak hip/knee
  loading at same speed (B+). Product flags "low cadence" only below ~155 spm sustained
  WITH overstride proxy — not cadence alone.
- Overstride: foot landing markedly ahead of pelvis at contact associated in literature
  with higher braking impulse and injury risk themes (B+); express as qualitative +
  proxy metric, not cm prescription without scale calibration.
- Vertical oscillation: excessive bounce wastes energy; flag top quartile vs self-baseline
  when multi-stride clip available (B coaching).
- Trunk lean: slight forward lean from ankle common in distance running; excessive upright
  or leaning back at contact flagged (B+).
- Hip drop > ~5–8° pelvis obliquity in stance linked to glute med weakness themes (A/B+);
  conservative threshold for noisy 2D.

YOUTH (middle school / HS cross-country):

- Higher cadence and shorter stride length typical; do NOT apply adult overstride thresholds.
- Growth-related asymmetry may be transient; down-weight asymmetry severity U14 unless
  large magnitude or pain language (safety redirect).
