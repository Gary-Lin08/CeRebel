## Cadence and step-rate manipulation

- Heiderscheit et al. (2011), Med Sci Sports Exerc — manipulating step rate ±5% and ±10% at constant speed
  reported changes in hip and knee joint work and loading-related variables.
  URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21364480/
  Authority: B+
  Product use: low_cadence_overstride finding pairs low spm with overstride proxy; not cadence alone.

## Overstriding and braking (themes)

- Multiple kinematic studies report foot strike ahead of center of mass associated with greater braking impulse
  in sagittal treadmill analyses (individual papers vary by speed and population).
  Search hub: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=running+overstride+braking
  Authority: B+
  Product use: overstride finding; qualitative + overstride_proxy, avoid cm prescription without calibration.

## Vertical oscillation and economy

- Running economy literature links excessive vertical COM displacement with higher metabolic cost in some cohorts.
  Example search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=running+vertical+oscillation+economy
  Authority: B+
  Product use: excessive_bounce; prefer self-baseline within clip.

## Hip drop / frontal plane

- Pelvic drop during stance discussed in IT band syndrome and hip abductor endurance contexts (clinical observational).
  Example: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=running+hip+drop+pelvis
  Authority: A/B+ for association themes
  Product use: hip_drop at midstance; rear/front camera preference documented in Architecture.

## Knee valgus in running

- Dynamic knee valgus well studied in cutting/jumping; straight-line running frontal-plane collapse less uniform.
  Neuromuscular training reviews: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=knee+valgus+prevention
  Authority: B+
  Product use: knee_valgus_load only with frontal/oblique capture confidence.

## Arm swing asymmetry

- Arm swing reduces torsional momentum; asymmetry may affect economy (biomechanical modeling and experimental studies).
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=running+arm+swing+asymmetry
  Authority: B / B+
  Product use: arm_swing_asymmetry ROM comparison.

## Gait asymmetry and injury (non-diagnostic)

- Systematic reviews note associations between gait asymmetry metrics and injury history in runners — not predictive for individuals.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=running+gait+asymmetry+injury+systematic+review
  Authority: B+
  Product use: stride_asymmetry; pain → clinical redirect per Disclaimer.

## Youth running gait

- Developmental literature: children higher step frequency, shorter stride relative to stature.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=child+running+gait+cadence
  Authority: B+
  Product use: youth checkpoint bands widened in Quality/002.
