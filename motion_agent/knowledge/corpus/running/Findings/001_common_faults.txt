# Running Gait — Common Faults (finding cards) — v2

Authority: B / B+ summarized open literature; not diagnostic. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
finding_id stable keys: fault_<snake_case>. legacy_id kept for prior RAG slugs.

finding_id: fault_overstride
legacy_id: overstride
headline_en: Foot lands too far ahead of your body
phases: contact, loading
body_signal: At E_IC, stance ankle/foot landmark markedly ahead of pelvis center in sagittal view; overstride_proxy ratio above adult checkpoint band; knee may still be extending toward ground at contact.
why_it_matters: Reaching with the foot ahead of mass associates with greater braking and higher impact loading variables in lab studies — performance and tissue load may increase for some runners (B+). Not every rearfoot contact is harmful.
feel_cue: "Land with your foot under your hip, not reaching out front."
drill: 60 s ankle glides — jog in place then move forward slowly, foot under hip (Coaching/002_drills.txt drill_overstride_ankle_glide).
severity_default: medium
sources: Heiderscheit et al. 2011 step-rate/loading; running kinematics foot-placement reviews (B+)
youth_note: Youth often land closer under COM; raise overstride threshold before scoring.

finding_id: fault_low_cadence_overstride
legacy_id: low_cadence_overstride
headline_en: Slow turnover combined with long reach
phases: contact
body_signal: Cadence estimate below ~155 spm (adult heuristic, ≥4 IC events) AND overstride_proxy fails cp_contact_under_com_adult on same clip.
why_it_matters: Low step rate at constant speed implies longer strides; combined with overreach, braking may increase (B+). Cadence alone is not a fault — both signals required.
feel_cue: "Quicker, shorter steps — like running on hot coals."
drill: 90 s metronome +5% above self-counted cadence for easy jog (drill_low_cadence_metronome).
severity_default: medium
sources: Heiderscheit et al. 2011; step-rate retraining coaching literature (B+)
youth_note: Natural youth cadence often higher; do not force adult 155 spm floor.

finding_id: fault_excessive_bounce
legacy_id: excessive_bounce
headline_en: Too much up-and-down bounce
phases: flight, midstance
body_signal: vertical_oscillation peak-to-peak per stride exceeds clip self-baseline band or cohort heuristic (cp_vertical_oscillation).
why_it_matters: Excessive vertical COM oscillation may increase energy cost in some runners (B+). Often pairs with stiff push-off or overstriding.
feel_cue: "Run quiet and forward, not pogo up and down."
drill: 60 s quiet upper-body jog, hands on hips minimizing head bounce (drill_bounce_quiet_upper).
severity_default: low
sources: Running economy kinematics reviews (B+)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_trunk_lean_back
legacy_id: trunk_lean_back
headline_en: Torso too upright or leaning back at landing
phases: contact, midstance
body_signal: trunk_lean vs vertical shows insufficient forward tilt or apparent backward lean at midstance (cp_trunk_stack_neutral fail).
why_it_matters: Excessive upright/backward trunk at contact can coincide with overreach and braking (B+). Horizontal impulse may suffer.
feel_cue: "Slight forward lean from the ankles, eyes on the horizon."
drill: 45 s lean-from-ankle march then 30 s easy jog (drill_trunk_lean_ankle).
severity_default: medium
sources: Distance running form reviews; trunk posture in gait literature (B+)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_trunk_lean_excess_fwd
legacy_id: trunk_lean_excess_fwd
headline_en: Hunched forward from the waist
phases: midstance, propulsion
body_signal: Excessive thoracic flexion — shoulders ahead of hips without ankle-driven whole-body lean (cp_trunk_fwd_excess).
why_it_matters: Waist-hinge posture may restrict hip extension and breathing (B). Lumbar load may rise for some athletes — coach review, not diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Tall spine, lean the whole body like a tower tipping slightly forward."
drill: 60 s tall-posture low skips, ribs stacked over pelvis (drill_trunk_hunch_tall_skip).
severity_default: medium
sources: Coaching education; posture in endurance running (B)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_hip_drop
legacy_id: hip_drop
headline_en: Pelvis drops on the stance side
phases: midstance
body_signal: hip_drop / pelvis obliquity — swing-side hip lower than stance beyond ~5–8° proxy at midstance (cp_pelvis_level).
why_it_matters: Frontal-plane pelvic drop discussed with hip abductor endurance and ITB symptom associations (A/B+). Visible drop may indicate stability limit under single-leg load — not a diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Keep the belt line level — don't let the hip sag on the landing leg."
drill: 90 s single-leg balance each side, pelvis level (drill_hip_drop_balance).
severity_default: medium
sources: ITB/hip stabilizer biomechanics reviews; Trendelenburg assessment literature (A/B+)
youth_note: Mild drop common during growth; raise severity only if large and persistent.

finding_id: fault_crossover_gait
legacy_id: crossover_gait
headline_en: Foot crosses toward the midline
phases: contact, loading
body_signal: crossover_proxy — foot landmark medial to contralateral hip or crosses midline on rear view (cp_crossover).
why_it_matters: Narrow base and cross-over patterns appear in observational studies of some injured or fatigued runners (B+). Association not causation.
feel_cue: "Feet land hip-width, parallel tracks like skis."
drill: 60 s line runs without crossing midline (drill_crossover_line).
severity_default: medium
sources: Clinical observational running gait reports; hip stability coaching (B+)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_knee_valgus_load
legacy_id: knee_valgus_load
headline_en: Knee caves inward on landing (visible)
phases: loading, midstance
body_signal: Frontal/oblique camera shows knee medial to hip–ankle line during loading (cp_knee_valgus_visible). Side-only: downgrade confidence.
why_it_matters: Dynamic knee valgus is a load-distribution concern in jump/cut sports; in straight running, visible collapse may increase medial knee stress (B+). Not ACL diagnosis from video.
feel_cue: "Push the knee out over the middle toe as you land."
drill: 60 s mini-band lateral steps then 30 s jog knee-over-foot (drill_knee_valgus_band).
severity_default: medium
sources: Neuromuscular training and valgus literature; running frontal-plane studies (B+)
confidence_note: Requires frontal or oblique camera.
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_stiff_ankle_landing
legacy_id: stiff_ankle_landing
headline_en: Stiff, locked ankle at touchdown
phases: contact, loading
body_signal: Low ankle dorsiflexion increase after contact; minimal early knee flexion (cp_ankle_compliance). Needs clear sagittal high-FPS.
why_it_matters: Reduced compliance at contact may increase impact peaks in lab studies (B+). Often co-occurs with overstride.
feel_cue: "Soft ankle, soft knee — let the leg bend like a spring."
drill: 45 s ankle rolls + soft landing hops in place (drill_stiff_ankle_soft_land).
severity_default: medium
sources: Impact loading and knee/ankle flexion reviews (B)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_scissor_kick
legacy_id: scissor_kick
headline_en: Low knee lift — feet scissor near ground
phases: swing, flight
body_signal: Low swing-phase knee flexion; ankles pass close to ground or near contralateral leg (cp_swing_clearance). Fatigue increases appearance.
why_it_matters: Insufficient clearance may increase trip risk and alter stride efficiency (B). May indicate hip-flexor/hamstring fatigue — refer pain to clinician.
feel_cue: "Lift the heel toward the butt on the back swing; knees forward not feet back."
drill: 45 s butt kicks + 45 s easy jog carrying heel-lift cue (drill_scissor_butt_kick).
severity_default: low
sources: Sprint drill coaching adapted to distance (B); clearance in gait analysis (B+)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_stride_asymmetry
legacy_id: stride_asymmetry
headline_en: Left and right strides don't match
phases: contact, loading, midstance, propulsion, toe_off, swing, flight
body_signal: Stride time or contact time differs L/R > ~8–10% sustained (cp_stride_time_symmetry); optional ROM asymmetry on knee/hip.
why_it_matters: Persistent asymmetry warrants coach review; with pain may need clinical evaluation (A for pain guidance). Youth growth can produce temporary asymmetry (B+).
feel_cue: "Same rhythm both sides — listen for even footfalls."
drill: 90 s alternate single-leg hops then bilateral jog comparing sound (drill_asymmetry_hop_rhythm).
severity_default: medium
sources: Gait asymmetry and injury association reviews (non-diagnostic); developmental gait (B+)
youth_note: Temporary asymmetry during growth spurts — down-weight severity unless large.

finding_id: fault_rearfoot_reach_proxy
headline_en: Heel reaches far ahead before contact (rearfoot proxy)
phases: contact
body_signal: At E_IC, heel landmark contacts with ankle clearly ahead of knee; large foot–pelvis horizontal offset with extended knee (body-only foot-strike proxy — not force-plate classification).
why_it_matters: Rearfoot contact with overreach is discussed with higher braking GRF peaks in some lab cohorts (B+). Midfoot/forefoot can also overload — context matters.
feel_cue: "Let the foot kiss under you — don't reach with the heel first."
drill: 60 s soft midfoot taps in place then easy jog (drill_rearfoot_reach_soft_tap).
severity_default: medium
sources: Foot-strike and loading reviews (B+); coaching strike education (B)
confidence_note: Body-only proxy; shoe visibility and camera angle affect confidence.
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_forefoot_early_load
headline_en: Early forefoot-only load with stiff ankle
phases: contact, loading
body_signal: At E_IC, toe/forefoot landmark lowest with minimal heel descent through early loading; ankle plantarflexion remains high while knee stays stiff.
why_it_matters: Early forefoot loading without adequate compliance may concentrate load at metatarsals/Achilles in some runners (B). Not a prescription to change strike style.
feel_cue: "Allow a soft whole-foot acceptance — ankle yields a little."
drill: 45 s quiet landings from low hops focusing heel kiss after forefoot (drill_forefoot_soft_accept).
severity_default: low
sources: Forefoot strike loading summaries (B+); coaching (B)
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.

finding_id: fault_arm_crossing_midline
headline_en: Arms cross the body midline while swinging
phases: swing, flight
body_signal: Wrist path crosses trunk midline repeatedly; elbows swing across torso rather than mostly sagittal (cp_arm_cross_midline).

feel_cue: "Elbows drive back beside the ribs — hands don't cross the zipper."
drill: 60 s corridor arm swings jogging with elbows brushing pockets (drill_arm_corridor).
severity_default: low
sources: Arm swing mechanics coaching (B); segment coupling themes (B+)

finding_id: fault_fatigue_cadence_drop
headline_en: Cadence drops as the clip progresses (fatigue)
phases: contact
body_signal: Cadence in last third of clip ≥5–8% lower than first third at similar speed proxy; often with rising overstride_proxy (cp_fatigue_cadence).
why_it_matters: Fatigue-related cadence drop and stride lengthening are common end-of-run patterns and may raise braking load (B+). Coaching focus: maintain rhythm, not heroics.
feel_cue: "Keep the metronome in your legs when you get tired."
drill: 90 s late-run metronome hold at easy pace (drill_fatigue_cadence_hold).
severity_default: medium
sources: Fatigue kinematics in prolonged running (B+)
confidence_note: Needs clip long enough (≥20–30 s) and stable speed estimate.
youth_note: Cadence relative to height; avoid absolute overstride cm norms for growing athletes.
