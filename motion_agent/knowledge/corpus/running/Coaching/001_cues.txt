## Cue selection notes

- Prefer external focus cues for youth and beginners (B+ motor learning).
- Avoid pain-linked cues — use performance and balance language.
- Treadmill: emphasize pelvis-relative foot placement, not belt-relative reaching.
- One primary cue per brief; stack secondary in chat only.
