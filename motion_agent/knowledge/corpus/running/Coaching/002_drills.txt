# Running — Short Drills (30–90 s) — v2

Each drill lists targets: fault_*. Equipment-minimal. Youth: submax effort.
See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.

---

drill_id: drill_overstride_ankle_glide
targets: fault_overstride
duration: 60 s
description: Jog forward slowly focusing on foot touching down directly under hip. Start 10 s marching in place.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_low_cadence_metronome
targets: fault_low_cadence_overstride, fault_fatigue_cadence_drop
duration: 90 s
description: Count cadence 20 s easy jog. Set metronome 5% higher; jog 70 s matching beats. Progress +5% weekly max in supervised plans.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_bounce_quiet_upper
targets: fault_excessive_bounce
duration: 60 s
description: Hands on hips, easy jog minimizing head vertical movement.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_trunk_lean_ankle
targets: fault_trunk_lean_back
duration: 75 s
description: 15 s ankle-lean march → 30 s easy jog lean-from-ankle → 30 s hands-on-hips repeat.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_trunk_hunch_tall_skip
targets: fault_trunk_lean_excess_fwd, fault_forward_head_run
duration: 60 s
description: Low skipping emphasizing tall spine and ribs over pelvis. Not maximal plyometric height.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_hip_drop_balance
targets: fault_hip_drop, fault_fatigue_hip_drop_rise
duration: 90 s
description: Single-leg balance bare foot 30 s per leg × 3, pelvis level, slight knee bend. Optional mini-band above knees.
authority: A/B+

drill_id: drill_crossover_line
targets: fault_crossover_gait, fault_narrow_stance_base
duration: 60 s
description: Jog on straight line without feet crossing midline. Rear camera check.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_knee_valgus_band
targets: fault_knee_valgus_load
duration: 90 s
description: Mini-band above knees: 30 s lateral walk each direction → 30 s easy jog knee-over-middle-toe. Skip band if knee pain.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_stiff_ankle_soft_land
targets: fault_stiff_ankle_landing, fault_insufficient_knee_flex_stance
duration: 60 s
description: 15 s ankle circles each foot → 15 light hops soft landings → 30 s easy jog.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_arm_symmetry_cheek
targets: fault_arm_swing_asymmetry
duration: 60 s
description: Run elbows ~90°, brush hands cheek to cheek symmetrically. Remove handheld items.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_trunk_rotation_hands_hips
targets: fault_excessive_trunk_rotation, fault_pelvis_rotation_excess
duration: 60 s
description: Hands on hips easy jog limiting shoulder/pelvis twist; feel controlled trunk.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_scissor_butt_kick
targets: fault_scissor_kick
duration: 90 s
description: 30 s butt kicks in place → 60 s easy jog with heel-to-butt cue.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_asymmetry_hop_rhythm
targets: fault_stride_asymmetry, fault_contact_time_asymmetry, fault_push_off_asymmetry
duration: 90 s
description: 5 submax single-leg hops each leg → 60 s bilateral jog listening for even rhythm. Youth: replace hops with 20 s single-leg stand each side.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_rearfoot_reach_soft_tap
targets: fault_rearfoot_reach_proxy, fault_overstride
duration: 60 s
description: Soft midfoot taps in place 20 s then easy jog focusing foot under hip.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_forefoot_soft_accept
targets: fault_forefoot_early_load
duration: 45 s
description: Low hops quiet landings allowing heel kiss after forefoot; then 20 s jog.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_arm_corridor
targets: fault_arm_crossing_midline
duration: 60 s
description: Jog with elbows brushing pockets; imagine two vertical slots for hands.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_arm_drive_exaggerated
targets: fault_short_arm_drive, fault_arm_swing_asymmetry
duration: 60 s
description: Exaggerated cheek-to-cheek arm drives at easy jog for 60 s, then settle to natural.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_hip_ext_amarch
targets: fault_hip_extension_deficit
duration: 90 s
description: 60 s walking A-march with hip open cue → 30 s easy jog leaving thigh trailing at toe-off.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_early_heel_delay
targets: fault_early_heel_rise
duration: 45 s
description: Slow march delaying heel lift until midstance feel → short jog carrying cue.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_soft_knee_load
targets: fault_insufficient_knee_flex_stance, fault_stiff_ankle_landing
duration: 60 s
description: Soft-knee jogging in place 20 s then forward 40 s focusing bent-knee acceptance.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_pelvis_quiet_belt
targets: fault_pelvis_rotation_excess
duration: 60 s
description: Hands on hips, "quiet belt" jog minimizing pelvis yaw.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_shoulder_melt_jog
targets: fault_shoulder_hike_run, fault_forward_head_run
duration: 90 s
description: 45 s shoulder-roll reset → 45 s jog with melt-shoulders cue.
authority: B

drill_id: drill_stance_width_rails
targets: fault_narrow_stance_base, fault_crossover_gait
duration: 60 s
description: Dual parallel tapes hip-width; jog keeping one foot on each rail.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_contact_even_listen
targets: fault_contact_time_asymmetry, fault_stride_asymmetry
duration: 90 s
description: 5 hops each leg → 60 s even footfall listening jog.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_fatigue_cadence_hold
targets: fault_fatigue_cadence_drop, fault_low_cadence_overstride
duration: 90 s
description: Late in easy run (or after 5 min jog), hold metronome at early-run cadence 90 s.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_fatigue_hip_level
targets: fault_fatigue_hip_drop_rise, fault_hip_drop
duration: 60 s
description: Single-leg level holds 15 s each → short jog focusing level belt when "tired."
authority: A/B+

drill_id: drill_forward_head_jog
targets: fault_forward_head_run
duration: 90 s
description: 45 s wall chin glide → 45 s tall-head easy jog.
authority: B+

drill_id: drill_push_match
targets: fault_push_off_asymmetry
duration: 60 s
description: Alternate single-leg push marches 20 s → bilateral jog matching toe-off sound.
authority: B

---

## Progressions

1. Drill 30–90 s in warm-up or post-easy-run skill block.
2. Re-film same pace and camera after 2–3 weeks.
3. Do not stack more than two drill families same day for beginners.
