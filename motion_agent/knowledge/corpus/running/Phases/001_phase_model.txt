# Running Gait — Phase Model (body-first, OpenSim-friendly) — v2

## Scope

Applies to **steady-state distance running** (jog through marathon pace) on level surfaces. One **stride cycle**
is defined per stance leg from initial contact (contact proxy) on that leg until the next contact on the same leg.
Sprint-maximal mechanics and walk–run transitions may blur `flight` phase boundaries — widen UI windows when
cadence exceeds ~200 spm or speed is very low.

Convention: **stance leg** = leg accepting weight after E_IC; **swing leg** = contralateral limb.
Findings may tag `side=left|right`; phases below are described for a generic stance leg.

## Phase list (7 phases)

| phase_id   | label_en    | start_event | end_event   | body narrative |
|------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| contact    | Touchdown   | E_IC        | E_IC + n1   | Foot meets ground; ankle begins dorsiflexion; knee flexion initiates |
| loading    | Loading     | end(contact)| E_LOAD_END  | Shock absorption; peak knee flexion approaching |
| midstance  | Mid-stance  | E_LOAD_END  | E_PROP_START | COM over foot; single-leg balance; pelvis level ideally stable |
| propulsion | Propulsion  | E_PROP_START| E_TO        | Hip/knee/ankle extension; body accelerates upward/forward |
| toe_off    | Toe-off     | E_TO        | E_TO + n2   | Stance foot leaves ground; ankle plantarflexion peak |
| swing      | Swing       | end(toe_off)| E_SWING_MID | Thigh advances; knee flexes to clear ground |
| flight     | Flight      | E_SWING_MID | E_NEXT_IC   | Late swing / descent; both feet airborne in true running |

n1, n2 are small fixed frame buffers (e.g., 2–4 frames at 30 fps) for UI smoothing; RAG links use phase_id only.

## Phase blocks (detector_events + fault_links)

### phase_id: contact
start_end: E_IC → E_IC + n1
body_signals: Stance ankle height local min; foot vs pelvis sagittal offset; early knee flexion onset.
detector_events: E_IC
fault_links: fault_overstride, fault_low_cadence_overstride, fault_rearfoot_reach_proxy, fault_forefoot_early_load, fault_stiff_ankle_landing, fault_insufficient_knee_flex_stance, fault_crossover_gait, fault_narrow_stance_base, fault_trunk_lean_back, fault_contact_time_asymmetry, fault_fatigue_cadence_drop, fault_stride_asymmetry
youth_note: Higher natural cadence; shorter absolute contact duration — avoid adult timing templates on U12.

### phase_id: loading
start_end: end(contact) → E_LOAD_END
body_signals: Rising knee flexion; ankle dorsiflexion increase; frontal knee alignment if camera allows.
detector_events: E_IC, E_LOAD_END
fault_links: fault_overstride, fault_knee_valgus_load, fault_stiff_ankle_landing, fault_insufficient_knee_flex_stance, fault_early_heel_rise, fault_crossover_gait, fault_contact_time_asymmetry
youth_note: Mild frontal-plane wobble common; raise severity thresholds.

### phase_id: midstance
start_end: E_LOAD_END → E_PROP_START
body_signals: COM over stance foot; pelvis obliquity peak; trunk lean vs vertical; head stack.
detector_events: E_LOAD_END, E_PROP_START
fault_links: fault_hip_drop, fault_excessive_bounce, fault_trunk_lean_back, fault_trunk_lean_excess_fwd, fault_knee_valgus_load, fault_early_heel_rise, fault_narrow_stance_base, fault_shoulder_hike_run, fault_forward_head_run, fault_fatigue_hip_drop_rise
youth_note: Smaller vertical oscillation common — do not over-score bounce.

### phase_id: propulsion
start_end: E_PROP_START → E_TO
body_signals: Knee/hip extension dominant; trunk orientation; arm drive opposing swing.
detector_events: E_PROP_START, E_TO
fault_links: fault_hip_extension_deficit, fault_trunk_lean_excess_fwd, fault_excessive_trunk_rotation, fault_pelvis_rotation_excess, fault_short_arm_drive, fault_push_off_asymmetry, fault_shoulder_hike_run, fault_forward_head_run
youth_note: Hip extension ROM may be smaller; coach quality over adult angles.

### phase_id: toe_off
start_end: E_TO → E_TO + n2
body_signals: Peak ankle plantarflexion / upward ankle velocity; hip extension peak proxy.
detector_events: E_TO
fault_links: fault_hip_extension_deficit, fault_push_off_asymmetry, fault_early_heel_rise
youth_note: Earlier relative toe-off timing possible at high cadence.

### phase_id: swing
start_end: end(toe_off) → E_SWING_MID
body_signals: Swing knee flexion for clearance; arm swing ROM and path; trunk yaw.
detector_events: E_TO, E_SWING_MID
fault_links: fault_scissor_kick, fault_arm_swing_asymmetry, fault_arm_crossing_midline, fault_short_arm_drive, fault_excessive_trunk_rotation, fault_pelvis_rotation_excess
youth_note: Lower absolute knee lift acceptable if clearance safe.

### phase_id: flight
start_end: E_SWING_MID → E_NEXT_IC
body_signals: Both ankles elevated (true run); pelvis vertical range; late swing foot placement prep.
detector_events: E_SWING_MID, E_NEXT_IC
fault_links: fault_excessive_bounce, fault_scissor_kick, fault_arm_swing_asymmetry, fault_arm_crossing_midline, fault_stride_asymmetry
youth_note: Flight may be brief; walk-jog clips downgrade flight-dependent findings.

## Event definitions (computable proxies)

### E_IC (initial contact proxy)
- Stance-leg ankle vertical velocity minimal within stride window, OR local max of foot speed toward ground.
- Fallback: earliest frame where ankle height within ε of local minimum before rising.
- Not identical to force-plate IC without instrumented treadmill.

### E_LOAD_END
- Maximum knee flexion on stance between E_IC and E_TO.
- Fallback: E_IC + 15% stance duration if knee noisy.

### E_PROP_START
- First frame after E_LOAD_END where knee flexion velocity becomes extension-dominant.
- Fallback: E_LOAD_END + 5% stance duration.

### E_TO (toe-off proxy)
- Maximum stance-ankle upward velocity or peak knee extension angular velocity before foot leaves ground.
- Fallback: E_IC + 60% stance duration (adult jogging band).

### E_SWING_MID
- Maximum knee flexion on swing leg OR midpoint E_TO → E_NEXT_IC.
- Fallback: halfway between E_TO and E_NEXT_IC.

### E_NEXT_IC
- Next E_IC on the **same** leg — closes stride for cadence and asymmetry.

### Stance vs flight
- Running: both ankles elevated simultaneously → flight meaningful.
- Walking: no true flight → downgrade flight findings.

## Phase precedence

contact → loading → midstance → propulsion → toe_off → swing → flight → (next contact)

## Treadmill vs overground

Overstride proxy must use pelvis–foot relation in camera frame, not absolute ground distance.

## UI scrub bar labels

`contact`, `loading`, `midstance`, `propulsion`, `toe_off`, `swing`, `flight`
