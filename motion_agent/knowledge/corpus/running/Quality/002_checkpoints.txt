# Running Gait — Quality Checkpoints

Each checkpoint references phase_id(s), a measurable signal (series_id), a rule summary, failure finding_id,
and evidence authority level. Thresholds are product heuristics for noisy monocular capture — not clinical cutoffs.

## Notes on measurement

**overstride_proxy**: horizontal distance from pelvis center to ankle at E_IC, normalized by leg length estimate
(hip–ankle segment) when scale unknown — use ratio not absolute cm in product copy.

**cadence**: steps per minute from bilateral ic events; require ≥4 ic events for stable estimate.

**vertical_oscillation**: peak-to-peak pelvis vertical displacement per stride; self-baseline preferred when
athlete has ≥6 cycles in clip.

**hip_drop**: difference in hip height stance vs swing side at midstance; frontal camera preferred.

**crossover_proxy**: ankle x-position medial to contralateral hip landmark; rear-view camera preferred.

## Checkpoint pass semantics

v1 does not sum checkpoints into a 0–100 score. Coach UX may show “focus area” = highest-severity failed
checkpoint with matching finding card.

## Evidence citations (summary)

- Step rate and joint loading: Heiderscheit et al., *Med Sci Sports Exerc* 2011 (B+).
- Braking and foot strike position themes: systematic reviews on running kinematics (B+).
- Hip drop and pelvic stability: clinical and biomechanical literature on frontal plane control (A/B+).
- Arm swing asymmetry: coaching and biomechanics reviews on economy (B).
