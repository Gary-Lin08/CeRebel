# Baseball Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first baseball pitching coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the athlete’s body
is doing across recognizable pitching phases. Ball tracking, radar, and field context are optional enrichments,
not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- Overhand **pitching** from single-camera or multi-camera body capture.
- Right- and left-handed pitchers; mirror joint naming (trail arm = throwing arm).
- UI phase scrub bar: six stable ids — `set`, `lift`, `stride`, `landing`, `release`, `follow`
  (see `sport_analysis._baseball_window`).

Out of scope for automatic findings (may appear as glossary only):
- Ball flight (velocity, spin, break), strike zone, sign calling, pickoffs, defensive positioning.
- Hitting, catching, and base-running unless future packs extend ontology.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, timing between events,
   posture landmarks visible without a ball.
2. **Evidence layering**: clinical and federation sources (A/A+) inform safety and workload; biomechanics reviews
   (B+) inform relationships (e.g., trunk rotation before release); cues and drills are coaching advice (B),
   always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric angle “targets” are avoided as prescriptive medicine; ranges are
   described qualitatively or attributed to published summaries with youth/adult separation where relevant.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows.

## Motion pipeline integration

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Required joints for pitch-likeness scoring (see `pitch_score.py`): trail wrist, lead wrist, lead ankle.
- Handedness selects trail vs lead sides (default right-handed pitcher: trail = right).

### Pitch-likeness gate (conservative)

Before enabling full product findings, the pack computes a **soft** pitch-likeness score from:
- **Wrist speed burst**: peak trail-wrist speed vs median (expect high burst near release).
- **Stride signal**: lead-ankle speed peak before release vs speed at release frame (foot quieter at release).
- **Arm dominance**: trail-wrist peak vs lead-wrist peak near release.

Score alone does **not** authorize medical or workload advice; it only gates sport-mode selection and prevents
golf-swing false positives (explicit golf block in BaseballPack).

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to these computable proxies:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_RELEASE | argmax trail-wrist speed in clip | Primary timing anchor |
| E_STRIDE_PEAK | argmax lead-ankle speed before E_RELEASE | Landing / drive window |
| E_SETUP | low wrist+ankle speed at clip start | Optional if clip trimmed |
| E_FOLLOW_THROUGH | frames after E_RELEASE with elevated trail wrist then decay | Deceleration path |

Future detectors may add foot-strike classification, max external rotation frame, and trunk yaw rate peak;
ontology slots exist in Schema.

### Coach brief generation

`build_coach_briefs(..., sport="baseball")` consumes:
- Phase windows (when available).
- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × phase alignment.
- One feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding (from Coaching/).

Chat agent (`prompt_mode=baseball`) adds sport analysis nudge and refuses mislabeled golf captures.

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.
- Repeat stable finding IDs (`fault_*`) in Findings and cross-link in Coaching.
- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time (manifest-driven).
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` or raw manifest CSV as coaching truth — use as ops only.

| ProductLogic | Detector mapping, pack gates | Internal B |

## Youth vs adult

Workload and recovery content must tag `audience=youth` or `audience=adult` at chunk level.
Mechanics findings often overlap, but **volume and rest** rules differ by league — never merge tables without age band.

## Internationalization

v1 corpus is English for RAG. Localized coach copy may be layered later; phase IDs and finding slugs stay stable.
