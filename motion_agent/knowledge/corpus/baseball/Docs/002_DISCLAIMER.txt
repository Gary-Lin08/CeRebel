# Baseball Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel baseball content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and federation training
guidelines**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, or individualized
return-to-throw protocols.

- Pain, swelling, numbness, or sudden loss of velocity with shoulder or elbow symptoms require evaluation by a
  qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to “push through.”
- Youth athletes have different bone, growth plate, and workload tolerance than adults; follow league pitch-count
  and rest rules from your governing body (Little League, Baseball Canada, etc.).

## Not a pitch-count enforcement tool

Pitch Smart and NGB tables in this pack are **educational summaries**. Official game limits are enforced by
umpires, coaches, and league software — not by body tracking alone. Always verify the current rule set on the
publisher website listed in SOURCE_MANIFEST.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Shoulder rotation in the plane of the camera.
- Elbow flexion when the arm crosses the torso.
- Foot angle at landing when occluded by the mound or uniform.

Findings describe **visible tendencies**, not laboratory-grade kinematics. Confidence should be lower when
joints are occluded or frame rate is low.

## No guaranteed performance outcomes

Mechanics cues may support efficient movement patterns discussed in research, but **velocity, command, and health
outcomes depend on many factors** (training history, sleep, nutrition, cumulative workload, genetics, etc.).
Do not promise velocity gains from a single drill or cue.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Open literature reports **ranges and group averages**, not one correct pose for every pitcher. Tall vs short
pitchers, sidearm vs overhand, and mobility differences all change appearance. This pack avoids presenting
arbitrary degree targets as universal rules.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, parents, and athletes who already have a training plan. They do not replace
in-person supervision, especially for beginners learning basic throwing mechanics.

## Emergency

For acute injury on the field, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when needed.

## Product behavior

- The system may **refuse** to analyze a clip that appears to be another sport (e.g., golf swing labeled as pitch).
- Baseball pack features may be gated as “coming soon” until detector validation completes — absence of a finding
  is not proof of safe mechanics.

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. Federation rules and Pitch Smart tables
change; retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Follow your league’s pitch limits.”
