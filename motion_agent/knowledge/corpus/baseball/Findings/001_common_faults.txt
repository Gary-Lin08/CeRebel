# Baseball Pitching — Common Faults (Finding Cards)

Stable `fault_*` IDs align with `sport_analysis._baseball_report` where noted.
Legacy kebab slugs retained via `legacy_id` for RAG search. Body-first; not medical diagnosis.
See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt. Youth pitch-count messaging is coaching/load awareness only.

---

finding_id: fault_back_leg_soft
legacy_id: back-leg-soft
headline_en: Back leg stays bent through stride into landing
phases: stride, landing
body_signal: Series back-knee (knee_angle_trail): flex still high at landing (e.g. >25°) with extension Δ <8° from stride start to landing.
why_it_matters: Drive leg push may be incomplete before front foot lands; hip speed into trunk can be reduced in efficient sequencing models (B+).
feel_cue: "Push the rubber away — back leg straightens before the front foot lands."
drill: Rocker drill — landing position, rock back, drive and throw half effort ×10 (45–60s); see Coaching/002_drills.txt drill_rocker_drive_leg.
severity_default: high
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide (A); code back-leg rule (B+)
youth_note: Absolute knee angles differ by age; prefer extension delta vs adult lock targets.

finding_id: fault_separation_low
legacy_id: separation-low
headline_en: Hips and shoulders turn together at landing
phases: landing
body_signal: Series separation (lumbar_rotation proxy): |sep| < 25° at landing (conservative monocular threshold).
why_it_matters: Reduced hip–shoulder separation window; trunk may not store elastic stretch before arm acceleration in lab descriptions (A/B+).
feel_cue: "Belt buckle turns to the plate while the chest still faces the side."
drill: Hip-lead throws — narrow stance, hips first, shoulders closed one beat, half-effort throw ×10 (drill_flat_ground_separation).
severity_default: high
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide; fortenbaugh_pitching_biomech (A/B+)
confidence_note: Separation is lumbar_rotation proxy — not lab marker hip-shoulder separation.
youth_note: Emphasize stride balance and rest/count awareness; avoid absolute MER cutoffs from video.

finding_id: fault_front_knee_collapse
legacy_id: front-knee-collapse
headline_en: Front knee gives after landing
phases: landing, release
body_signal: Series front-knee: flex increases ≥8° from landing to release_t.
why_it_matters: Soft front-side block; energy may not transfer efficiently up the chain in coaching biomechanics summaries (B+).
feel_cue: "Land and stop — front thigh catches you, then push back through the ground."
drill: Stride-and-brace — land, hold knee still two counts, throw half effort ×10 (drill_braced_landing_holds).
severity_default: high
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide (lead knee at FC ~45° cohort mean — not individual prescription) (B+)
youth_note: Emphasize stride balance and rest/count awareness; avoid absolute MER cutoffs from video.

finding_id: fault_stride_short
legacy_id: stride-short
headline_en: Stride looks short relative to height band
phases: stride, landing
body_signal: Stride series / height ratio below band: adult <0.70, youth <0.55 (see Quality/002 checkpoints).
why_it_matters: Less forward momentum and separation time; may correlate with upright arm-dominant patterns (B coaching).
feel_cue: "Reach toward the catcher, not straight up."
drill: drill_stride_line — 45–60s
severity_default: medium
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide (~85% height cited in clinical guide summary); baseball_canada_pitching_mechanics (B+)
youth_note: Youth band lower (0.55); do not apply adult 0.70–0.95 when age unknown — use relative language.

severity_default: medium

youth_note: Lower MER expected; cue smoothness not adult lay-back angles.
