# Baseball Pitching — Quality Checkpoints

Required checkpoint catalog (brain_plan §6). Phases use UI ids: set, lift, stride, landing, release, follow.
Evidence levels: A = clinical/lab consensus; B+ = coaching heuristic / conservative product threshold.

| checkpoint_id          | phase(s)        | series/signal              | rule (summary)                         | fail→finding_id        | evidence |
|------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------|------------------------|----------|
| cp_stride_adult_band   | landing         | stride / height ratio      | 0.70–0.95 adult band                   | stride-short, stride-long | B+    |
| cp_stride_youth_band   | landing         | stride / height ratio      | 0.55–0.85 youth band                   | stride-short, stride-long | B+    |
| cp_back_leg_drive      | stride→landing  | back-knee Δ flex           | knee extends ≥8° by landing            | back-leg-soft          | B+       |
| cp_separation_landing  | landing         | separation                 | \|sep\| ≥ 25° (conservative)           | separation-low         | A norm, B+ threshold |
| cp_front_knee_brace    | landing→release | front-knee Δ flex          | Δ < 8° landing→release                 | front-knee-collapse    | B+       |
| cp_mer_band            | release         | shoulder ER (future)       | within age band                        | mer-insufficient       | A        |
| cp_trunk_sequence      | landing→release | pelvis vs trunk peak times | pelvis before trunk before hand peak   | trunk-late-rotate      | A        |
| cp_balance_set         | set, lift       | COM / ankle speed          | minimal drift pre-stride               | set-rush               | B        |
| cp_glove_side_stable   | stride→release  | lead wrist vs trunk        | lead arm not max speed before trunk    | glove-flyout           | B+       |
| cp_head_stack          | release         | head vs trunk (future)     | excessive lateral pull                 | head-pull              | B        |
| cp_pelvis_drift        | stride→landing  | pelvis AP vs set           | COM toward plate without hip rotation  | pelvis-drift           | B        |
| cp_arm_path_efficiency | release         | throwing-hand path         | excessive lateral loop (heuristic)     | arm-path-wide          | B        |

Thresholds marked “future” ship findings at lower confidence until coordinates validated.

---

## cp_stride_adult_band

phases: landing
series: stride (lead–trail ankle separation normalized by stature estimate)
pass: ratio between 0.70 and 0.95 of body height proxy.
fail: below 0.70 → stride-short; above 0.95 → stride-long.
reference: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide cites ~85% height at foot contact in clinical summary (not individual Rx).
cue: cue_stride_to_target
drill: drill_stride_line

---

## cp_stride_youth_band

phases: landing
pass: ratio 0.55–0.85 youth band (shorter relative stride normal).
fail: same finding_ids as adult band with youth ageBand metadata.
sources: pmc_youth_mechanics_sr; baseball_canada_pitching_mechanics
note: never apply adult 0.70 lower bound to U12 without age tag.

---

## cp_back_leg_drive

phases: stride, landing
series: back-knee (trail knee flexion °)
pass: knee flexion decreases by at least 8° from stride start to landing (extension).
fail: back-leg-soft — knee still heavily flexed at landing, Δ < 8°.
matches: sport_analysis._baseball_report back-leg rule.
cue: “Push the rubber away.”
drill: Rocker drill (see code default copy) or drill_rocker_step_rhythm.

---

## cp_separation_landing

phases: landing
series: separation (lumbar_rotation ° at landing)
pass: absolute separation ≥ 25° (product conservative vs lab ~40–60° cohort means).
fail: separation-low.
why_conservative: monocular OpenSim noise — brain_plan §5.
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide; fortenbaugh_pitching_biomech

---

## cp_front_knee_brace

phases: landing, release
series: front-knee
pass: front knee flex change from landing to release < 8°.
fail: front-knee-collapse.
cue: cue_brace_front_leg
drill: drill_braced_landing_holds, Stride-and-brace (in-app default)

---

## cp_mer_band

phases: release
series: shoulder ER trail (future)
pass: within age-appropriate band; adult lab MER often ~165–180° reported — not youth target.
fail: mer-insufficient when below youth p10 (future).
sources: pmc_clinician_pitching_guide; pmc_youth_mechanics_sr

---

## cp_trunk_sequence

phases: landing, release
signal: peak pelvis rotation before trunk before throwing-hand speed peak.
fail: trunk-late-rotate if hand peak precedes trunk peak.
sources: driveline_interpret_biomech_reports (sequencing concept); fortenbaugh_pitching_biomech (A)

---

## cp_balance_set

phases: set, lift
signal: COM drift, ankle speed near baseline before stride.
fail: set-rush
cue: cue_pause_at_top
drill: drill_rocker_step_rhythm

---

## cp_glove_side_stable

phases: stride, release
signal: lead wrist speed peak not before trunk rotation peak (planned).
fail: glove-flyout
cue: cue_glove_leads
drill: drill_glove_lead_target

---

## cp_head_stack

phases: release
signal: head lateral offset vs trunk (future)
fail: head-pull
cue: cue_smell_the_glove

## cp_pelvis_drift

phases: stride, landing
signal: pelvis translates toward plate without separation increase.
fail: pelvis-drift
cue: cue_stay_closed_longer
drill: drill_flat_ground_separation

---

## cp_arm_path_efficiency

phases: release
signal: trail wrist path lateral deviation heuristic.
fail: arm-path-wide
drill: drill_wall_drill_arm_path

---

## cp_capture_quality (gate)

phases: all
pass: full body visible; side/45° camera; includes landing through follow; ≥8 frames in window (code minimum).
fail: tier_insufficient_data — suppress high-confidence mechanical checkpoints.

cp_stride_*_band: 1.0

cp_arm_path_efficiency, cp_head_stack: 0.8
