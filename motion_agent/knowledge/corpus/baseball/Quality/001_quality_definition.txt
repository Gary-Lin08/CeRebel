# Baseball Pitching — Quality Definition (body-first)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel

Quality is **not** a single score like radar velocity or strike percentage. For this pack, a **quality pitch
delivery** (movement quality) means:

1. **Stable setup and rhythm** — controlled wind-up without rushing or postural sway that steals separation time.
2. **Timed lower-to-upper sequence** — pelvis/trunk rotation accelerates after lead leg begins accepting force;
   throwing arm peaks after the front side firms.
3. **Efficient arm path visible from body** — smooth circular path without obvious collapse of throwing-side
   posture at release; deceleration crosses the body rather than stopping short (“chicken wing”).
4. **Repeatable phase windows** — consecutive pitches show similar timing from foot plant proxy to release
   proxy (when multi-pitch sessions exist).
5. **Workload-aware training context** — for youth, quality includes adhering to pitch-count and rest guidance
   regardless of how “clean” one rep looks.

Quality findings are **coaching and load-management signals**, not medical grades.

Authority mix: B+ biomechanics relationships, B coaching consensus, A/A+ for youth volume policies.

## Dimensions of quality (orthogonal axes)

### axis_timing
Definition: Proximal-to-distal ordering — stride/plant → trunk → arm → wrist peak.
High quality: trunk peak rotation near plant/acceleration; release after front leg begins blocking.
Low quality: trunk rotates early; arm drags or pushes without lower-body anchor.

Measurable proxies:
- Time(E_RELEASE) - time(E_STRIDE_PEAK) within athlete’s typical band.
- Trunk yaw peak after lead ankle speed peak.

### axis_posture
Definition: Head/spine/pelvis organization through delivery.
High quality: head stays between feet through release; finish flexion is smooth, not collapsed sideways.
Low quality: head pulls glove-side early; excessive lean away from plate at finish.

Measurable proxies:
- Lateral head displacement relative to pelvis pre-release.
- Shoulder line tilt at release (2D proxy only).

### axis_stride_direction
Definition: Lead leg travels toward home plate area, not across body.
High quality: ankle path forward; plant allows hip rotation open.
Low quality: stride lands closed, blocking hip rotation; short/overlong stride for athlete size.

Measurable proxies:
- Lead ankle forward vs lateral displacement ratio.
- Stride length normalized by athlete height estimate (if available) or pelvis-ankle distance at plant.

### axis_arm_action
Definition: Throwing arm path and deceleration.
High quality: arm accelerates through release; follow-through finishes low across opposite thigh zone.
Low quality: low slot from collapsed shoulder; elbow stays bent beside body after release.

Measurable proxies:
- Elbow extension rate pre-release.
- Elbow flexion angle 200–400 ms post-release.

### axis_repeatability
Definition: Consistency across reps in session.
High quality: release timing SD low; phase durations stable.
Low quality: high variance — may indicate fatigue, pain avoidance, or inconsistent cues.

Requires multi-pitch session metadata.

### axis_workload_youth
Definition: Pitch volume and rest comply with league/NGB tables.
High quality: pitches within daily max; required rest observed.
Low quality: exceeded counts — flag regardless of mechanics.
Audience: youth only; source Pitch Smart / Baseball Canada / Little League.

## Quality tiers (product language)

tier_excellent:
- No medium/high findings in timing, posture, stride, or arm axes.
- Checkpoints cp_balance_leg_lift, cp_firm_front_side, cp_decel_across_body pass.
- Optional: pitch_likeness >= 0.8 when detector enabled.

tier_good:
- At most one medium finding; no high severity timing or decel faults.
- Checkpoints mostly pass with one soft fail.

tier_needs_work:
- Multiple medium findings OR one high severity fault (early trunk, chicken wing with high confidence).

tier_insufficient_data:
- Occluded joints, short clip, or non-pitch motion (golf guard, low pitch_likeness).
- Do not assign tier_excellent/good — prompt better capture.

---

## What quality is NOT

- Not command (strike zone accuracy) — no ball track.
- Not pitch-type effectiveness — no spin model v1.
- Not injury prediction — decel faults discuss stress patterns cautiously per disclaimer.

---

## Relationship to findings library

Each finding in Findings/001_common_faults.txt maps to one or more quality axes and lowers the relevant
axis score. Coach briefs prioritize faults that affect timing and decel axes for health-aware messaging.

---

## Youth vs adult weighting

Adults: emphasize timing, repeatability, decel path.
Youth: add axis_workload_youth with equal or higher priority than mechanical tier when counts are unknown
from video — prompt coach to log pitch count externally.

---

## Multi-pitch aggregation (future)

Session quality = weighted median of per-pitch axis scores with fatigue drift detection (late-session
timing degradation). v1 may only analyze single clips.
