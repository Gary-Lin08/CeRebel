# Baseball Team Practice — Pitching Development (body-first alignment)

Practice plans integrate CeRebel phase checkpoints, Pitch Smart workload rules, and short drills from
Coaching/002_drills.txt. Adjust for age, season phase, and games schedule.

Sources: MLB Pitch Smart (mlb_pitch_smart), Baseball Canada development (baseball_canada_pitch_counts),
Little League pitching rules (little_league_pitch_counts). Authority: A+ for counts when league specified; B for structure.

Disclaimer: Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt — coaches enforce official limits; app does not count pitches in game automatically v1.

---

## Practice design principles

1. **Warm-up before max intent** — general mobility, band work, progressive long toss per org policy.
2. **One mechanical theme per day** — e.g., separation OR decel, not five cues at once.
3. **Volume before velocity** — youth development prioritizes repeatable mechanics at submax effort until counts allow.
4. **Capture for feedback** — one side-view phone clip per bullpen for CeRebel review; avoid filming only release frame.
5. **Rest days sacred** — after threshold pitch counts, no live-arm bullpens; use drill_pitch_smart_rest_day.

## Sample 90-minute youth practice (12–14U, non-game day)

### Block A — Activation (15 min)
- Dynamic warm-up: legs, hips, T-spine rotation.
- Band external rotation and scap retractions — light, not fatiguing.
- 10 progressive toss pairs at 30–60 ft (org-specific cap).

### Block B — Skill theme: separation (20 min)
- Teach checkpoint cp_separation_window.
- drill_flat_ground_separation — 10 walk-throughs.
- drill_stride_line — 5 min no-throw, then 8 throws at 50% from flat ground.
- Optional: record one clip for CeRebel.

### Block C — Bullpen (25 min)
- Structured pitch count well below game max (example: 15–25 pitches at mixed intensity — coach sets exact number per Pitch Smart table for age).
- One cue only: cue_stay_closed_longer.
- 3 pitches filmed side view mid-block for feedback.

### Block D — Defense integration (20 min)
- Pitcher fielding practice (PFP): bunts, come-backers — 10 min.
- Maintains fault_posture_collapse_finish awareness via cue_fielding_ready.

### Block E — Cool down (10 min)
- Jog, static stretch brief, hydration.
- Log pitch count in team book / app external to CeRebel.

## Sample 60-minute high school practice (in-season, starter day -2)

### Block A (10 min) — Recovery mobility
- Lower body and posterior shoulder — no heavy lifting same day if game approaching.

### Block B (15 min) — One checkpoint review
- Review prior CeRebel clip: cp_decel_across_body or cp_firm_front_side based on last session findings.
- drill_follow_through_checkpoint OR drill_braced_landing_holds — 45–60 s blocks, 3 rounds.

### Block C (25 min) — Controlled bullpen
- 20–30 pitches, 70–80% intent, mix fastball only unless arm care plan allows off-speed.
- Avoid radar max chasing on short rest.

### Block D (10 min) — Charting / mental routine
- Target zones without ball track in app — external charting by catcher/coach.

## Pre-game bullpen (all ages)

Duration: 10–15 min, pitch count per coach (often 15–25 warm-up pitches).
- Progression: wrist flicks short distance → half distance → mound at increasing intent last 5 pitches.
- Film optional; do not delay game for analysis.
- Youth: strictly within same-day combined pitch limits including game pitches.

---

## Post-outing recovery (Pitch Smart aligned)

After outing, look up rest days required for pitches thrown (live table on Pitch Smart site).

General pattern (summarized — verify live table):
- More pitches thrown → more calendar days before next pitching appearance.
- **No** same-day max bullpen after max game outing for youth.

Activities during rest:
- Lower-body strength, mobility, hitting if not restricted by league.
- Zero full-effort mound work.

CeRebel: use drill_pitch_smart_rest_day as educational micro-block.

## Using CeRebel in team settings

### Filming protocol
- Side view, full body, 60 fps if available.
- Include windup through follow-through (minimum 16 frames for pitch_likeness).
- Consistent distance; avoid zoom changes mid-clip.

### Feedback loop
1. Upload clip → identify top 1–2 findings.
2. Assign one cue + one drill for next practice block B.
3. Re-film after 2–3 practices — compare repeatability axis in Quality/001_quality_definition.txt.

### Privacy
- Obtain parent/athlete consent for video analysis per org policy.

## Station rotation (large roster, 16 players)

Station 1 (4 pitchers): drill_stride_line no-throw
Station 2 (4 pitchers): flat ground throws with separation cue
Station 3 (4 pitchers): bullpen 8 pitches each rotating
Station 4 (4 players): fielding / rest

Rotate every 12 minutes; total live throws per athlete stays within daily development cap.

---

## Red flags — stop throwing segment

- Reported elbow/shoulder pain during practice.
- Visible fatigue: release timing SD spikes in multi-clip session (future feature).
- Athlete unable to complete deceleration without pain.

Action: remove from mound work; refer to medical professional; do not override with mechanics cues.

---

## Season periodization (high level)

Early season: mechanics themes, moderate volume.
Mid season: maintain checkpoints, monitor cumulative counts.
Late season / playoffs: reduce new mechanical experiments; prioritize rest and familiar cues.

Not individualized medical periodization — coaches adapt.
