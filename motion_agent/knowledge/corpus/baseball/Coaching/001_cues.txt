# Baseball Pitching — Coaching Cues (feel language)

Cues are internal-focus phrases linked to `fault_*` findings and phases. Avoid over-cueing youth with simultaneous
instructions; one cue per rep is preferred.

Disclaimer: See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt — not medical advice. Youth pitch-count cues are load-awareness education only.

---

## cue_stay_closed_longer

finding_ids: fault_separation_low, fault_pelvis_drift, fault_trunk_late_rotate, fault_early_trunk_rotation
phases: stride, landing
language_en: “Keep your chest pointed slightly away from the catcher until your front foot lands.”
language_alt: “Show your back pocket to the plate longer.”
avoid: “Twist harder” — often increases early rotation.
authority: CoachingAdvice B
source_ids: pmc_pitching_biomech_review

---

## cue_brace_front_leg

finding_ids: fault_front_knee_collapse, fault_separation_low, fault_posture_collapse_forward
phases: landing
language_en: “Land on a strong front leg — firm, not sinking.”
language_alt: “Front knee stays over shoelaces, not collapsing inside.”
avoid: “Lock the knee straight” — causes stiff landing.

## Cue selection rules for CeRebel briefs

1. Pick **one** cue matching highest-severity finding in the active phase window.
2. If capture_quality fails, cue = “Recapture side view with feet and arm visible.”
3. Never pair workload advice with a cue unless youth pitch count context is present in session metadata.
4. For fault_sport_mismatch_golf, suppress mechanics cues; instruct recapture only.

---

## Anti-patterns (do not use in product copy)

- “Throw as hard as you can every pitch” — conflicts with Pitch Smart youth guidance.
- “Snap the curve with your wrist” — pitch-type specific, out of scope v1.
- Exact elbow angle targets (“90 degrees at MER”) — not supported as universal norms.
- “This pattern causes Tommy John” — prohibited diagnostic language.
