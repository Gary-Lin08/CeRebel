# Baseball Pitching — Drills (30–90 s micro-blocks)

Drills link to `fault_*` findings and phases. Emphasize submaximal effort unless noted. Youth: prioritize form and
counts over radar chasing.

Disclaimer: Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt

---

## drill_stride_line

targets: fault_stride_short, fault_stride_long, fault_closed_stride_landing, fault_open_stride_landing, fault_lead_foot_closed_toe
phases: stride, landing
duration_s: 45–60
equipment: tape or chalk line toward home plate
space: mound or flat ground 10 m
steps:
1. Place line from push foot toward target.
2. Athlete performs 5–8 strides landing lead toe on/near line without throwing.
3. Add short throw from last stride only if warm-up complete.
progression: full delivery maintaining line for 5 pitches.
authority: CoachingAdvice B
source_ids: baseball_canada_pitch_counts (development context)

---

## drill_rocker_step_rhythm

targets: fault_set_rush, fault_stride_long, fault_tempo_rush_delivery
phases: set, lift, stride
duration_s: 60
equipment: optional metronome 50–70 bpm
steps:
1. Rocker step back, lift leg on beat 1, pause beat 2, stride on beat 3.
2. Repeat without ball 6–8 cycles.
3. Integrate throw on every third cycle at 50% effort.
youth_note: shorten cycle if balance fails.
authority: B

---

## drill_rocker_drive_leg

targets: fault_back_leg_soft, fault_soft_drive_leg_balance
phases: stride, landing
duration_s: 45–60
steps:
1. Start in landing posture; rock back onto drive leg.
2. Drive and land half effort ×10 focusing trail knee extension before plant.
3. Add throw on last 4 reps only if warm.
authority: B

---

## drill_braced_landing_holds

targets: fault_front_knee_collapse, fault_posture_collapse_forward
phases: landing
duration_s: 45
steps:
1. From short stride, land on front leg and freeze 2 seconds with stable knee flexion.
2. Coach checks knee over foot, not diving inward; chest stays tall.
3. 6 reps each side if switch pitcher; otherwise throwing side only.
authority: B
source_ids: asmi_youth_position

---

## drill_towel_drill_extension

targets: fault_mer_insufficient, fault_arm_path_wide, fault_arm_drag_late
phases: release
duration_s: 60
equipment: small towel
steps:
1. Place towel between throwing elbow and ribs loosely (not clamped tight for youth).
2. Throw easy long toss feeling extension through target without dropping towel early.
3. 8–10 throws at 60% effort.
avoid: max effort — form focus only.
authority: B

---

## drill_follow_through_checkpoint

targets: fault_arm_path_wide, fault_incomplete_deceleration, fault_finish_fall_off
phases: follow
duration_s: 60
steps:
1. After release, athlete taps opposite knee or shin with throwing hand before resetting.
2. 10 submaximal throws into net or partner hold.
3. Self-video one rep for decel path check.
clinical_note: emphasize complete deceleration pattern, not speed.
authority: B; clinical context A summarized pmc_pitch_count_clinical

---

## drill_glove_lead_target

targets: fault_head_pull, fault_glove_flyout, fault_separation_low, fault_glove_side_collapse, fault_early_trunk_rotation
phases: stride through release
duration_s: 45
steps:
1. Glove arm points at target from leg lift through foot plant.
2. Throw only when front foot lands; glove pulls to chest as trunk turns.
3. 8 throws at 70% effort.
authority: B

---

## drill_wall_drill_arm_path

targets: fault_mer_insufficient, fault_arm_path_wide, fault_arm_path_inverted_w, fault_arm_slot_inconsistent
phases: lift, release
duration_s: 45
equipment: wall or fence at shoulder distance
steps:
1. Stand sideways; simulate arm circle without ball, hand misses wall throughout.
2. Slow motion 10 circles, then 5 with baseball shadow throw.
authority: B

---

## drill_wrist_flick_partner_toss

targets: fault_trunk_late_rotate
phases: release
duration_s: 45
equipment: partner, tennis ball optional
steps:
1. Partner tosses ball waist high from 3 m.
2. Athlete focuses on late wrist snap into partner’s glove — minimal body turn.
3. 10 reps then integrate into one-step throw.
audience: advanced youth/adult; skip for raw beginners.
authority: B

---

## drill_one_knee_throws

targets: fault_trunk_late_rotate, fault_separation_low, fault_arm_drag_late
phases: landing, release
duration_s: 60
steps:
1. Throwing-side knee down; short distance 5–8 m.
2. Emphasize trunk rotation after arm starts back, hand fires last.
caution: reduces lower-body load — use as feel drill, not primary training volume for youth.
authority: B

---

## drill_balance_finish_hold

targets: fault_set_rush, fault_finish_fall_off
phases: follow
duration_s: 60
steps:
1. Throw into net; hold finish 2 seconds without stepping off rubber.
2. 8 reps; coach marks foot slip.
authority: B

---

## drill_lift_balance_holds

targets: fault_soft_drive_leg_balance, fault_set_rush
phases: set, lift
duration_s: 45
steps:
1. Lift lead leg to gather height; hold 2 s quiet balance.
2. Soft rock then stride without throw ×6; add throw ×4 at 50%.
youth_note: Hold 1 s if balance fails.
authority: B

---

## drill_stack_posture_holds

targets: fault_posture_lean_back, fault_posture_collapse_forward, fault_head_pull
phases: stride, landing, release
duration_s: 45
steps:
1. Walk-through stride focusing head over belt buckle.
2. 8 walk-throughs; 4 half-effort throws maintaining stack.
authority: B

---

## drill_mirror_side_view

targets: fault_head_pull, fault_arm_slot_inconsistent
phases: all
duration_s: 45
equipment: phone camera side view
steps:
1. Record 3 pitches side view.
2. Athlete compares head position and slot at release to setup frame.
3. Pick one cue from Coaching/001_cues.txt for next set.
authority: ProductLogic — supports insufficient_data recovery

## drill_flat_ground_separation

targets: fault_separation_low, fault_pelvis_drift, fault_early_trunk_rotation
phases: stride, landing
duration_s: 60
steps:
1. No mound; walk-through stride focusing closed chest while hips rotate under.
2. 10 walk-throughs, 5 throws at 50%.
youth: good pre-mound warm-up per many NGB progressions (summarized).
authority: B
source_ids: baseball_canada_pitch_counts

## drill_pitch_smart_rest_day

targets: fault_youth_pitch_count_awareness
phases: n/a
duration_s: 30 (education block)
steps:
1. Review pitch count from last outing vs Pitch Smart / league table.
2. If rest required, drill = mobility + lower-body strength, no full-effort throwing.
audience: youth
authority: A/B — mlb_pitch_smart, little_league_pitch_counts
note: Coaching/load education only — not a medical prescription from video.

## Drill safety

- Warm up with general throwing progression before arm-path drills.
- Stop if elbow or shoulder pain — refer to disclaimer.
- Do not exceed pitch counts during drill-heavy practices.
- No weighted-ball prescriptions without NGB guidance and coach supervision.
