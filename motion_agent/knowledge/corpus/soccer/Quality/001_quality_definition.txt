# Soccer Kick — Quality Definition (body terms)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel (v1)

Quality is **not** a single score on a 0–100 scale in this pack version. Instead, coaching UX presents
**checkpoints passed or failed** within the kick phase model, each tied to a visible body signal. A technically
“good” kick in match play may still fail a checkpoint if the camera angle hides the plant foot — confidence
metadata should accompany any automated message.

## Dimensions

### 1. Approach control
- Athlete arrives at plant with manageable COM speed and balanced arm action.
- Failure modes: rushed last step, excessive lateral drift, loss of trunk posture before plant.

### 2. Plant foot strategy
- Plant location and orientation support hip opening toward target (within visible camera limits).
- Support leg stiffness adequate through the strike window without excessive knee collapse.
- Failure modes: plant too close/far, plant pointing away from target (closed hip proxy), soft plant knee.

### 3. Kinetic chain timing
- Pelvis and trunk rotation precede or coincide with kicking-foot acceleration; not “all arm/leg” with frozen hips.
- Failure modes: upper body leaning back early, hips stay square through cocking.

### 4. Cocking and strike mechanics
- Kicking leg draws back with hip extension and knee flexion appropriate to intent (power vs pass).
- Forward trunk lean at strike proxy supports ball direction discussed in coaching literature (without ball track).
- Failure modes: minimal backswing, upright trunk at peak foot speed, kicking knee never extends through window.

### 5. Follow-through and recovery
- Kicking leg crosses body or opens per style; athlete does not “stop” at fake contact.
- Plant-side stability: hop or controlled release without collapse inward.
- Failure modes: abbreviated follow-through, immediate loss of balance on plant side.

### 6. Repeatability (session-level, future)
- Variance of E_KICK_PEAK timing relative to E_PLANT_SET across reps in one session.
- High variance suggests fatigue or inconsistent approach — flag for coach review, not automatic fault.

## Age bands

| band | typical ages | quality emphasis |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| youth | ~U8–U12 | Balance, plant consistency, fun/low load; avoid max-effort power cues |
| teen | ~U13–U17 | Progressive power; respect growth-related hamstring/groin load |
| adult | 18+ | Full checkpoint set with literature-backed bands where available |

Youth checkpoints use wider tolerance bands (see Quality/002_checkpoints.txt).

## Relationship to findings

Each checkpoint `fail_triggers` one or more finding IDs in `Findings/001_common_faults.txt`. Passing all
checkpoints does **not** imply injury-free mechanics — only that visible heuristics were satisfied.

## Evidence use

Checkpoint thresholds cite authority levels (A+, A, B+, B) in 002_checkpoints.txt. Thresholds marked B+ are
**conservative coaching heuristics** for monocular pose, not clinical diagnostic cutoffs.

## Out of scope for quality v1

- Ball launch angle, spin rate, shot speed.
- Tactical decision quality (pass choice, defensive pressure).
- Goalkeeper save probability.
