# Soccer Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first soccer kicking and striking coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the athlete’s body
is doing across recognizable kick phases. Ball tracking, goal context, and match tactics are optional enrichments,
not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Instep drive / power shot** and **side-foot pass** patterns from single-camera side or rear-quarter body capture.
- Right- and left-footed kickers; mirror joint naming (trail leg = kicking leg, lead leg = plant leg for a
  standard same-side approach).

Out of scope for automatic findings (may appear as glossary only):
- Ball spin, curve, launch angle, and post-contact flight.
- Defensive positioning, pressing triggers, set-piece choreography, goalkeeper mechanics.
- Dribbling feints, heading, and slide tackling unless future packs extend ontology.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, timing between plant and
   kicking-foot speed peak, trunk lean, hip orientation proxies visible without a ball.
2. **Evidence layering**: peer-reviewed kicking biomechanics and federation coaching science (A/B+) inform
   relationships (plant timing, trunk lean, support-leg stiffness); cues and drills are coaching advice (B),
   always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric angle targets are avoided as prescriptive medicine; ranges are
   described qualitatively or attributed to published summaries with youth/adult separation where relevant.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Minimum joints for kick-likeness scoring (future detector): kicking-side ankle, plant-side ankle, pelvis,
  both knees, trunk (shoulder or hip line).

### Kick-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings, a future SoccerPack may compute a **soft** kick-likeness score from:
- **Kicking-foot speed burst**: peak trail-ankle speed vs median (expect high burst near contact proxy).
- **Plant signal**: lead-ankle deceleration or low vertical velocity before kicking-foot peak.
- **Single-leg dominance**: trail-ankle peak vs lead-ankle peak in the strike window.

Score alone does **not** authorize medical or load advice; it only gates sport-mode selection and prevents
false positives from running or golf-like swings.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to these computable proxies:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_KICK_PEAK | argmax trail-ankle speed in clip | Primary timing anchor; not true ball contact without ball track |
| E_PLANT | lead-ankle speed local minimum before E_KICK_PEAK | Plant foot “set” window |
| E_APPROACH_END | last frame where both feet had elevated speed before E_PLANT | Run-up deceleration into plant |
| E_FOLLOW_END | frames after E_KICK_PEAK where trail knee flex increases and ankle speed decays | Follow-through and recovery |

Handedness convention for kicking: **trail = kicking foot**, **lead = plant foot** (right-footed kicker:
trail right, plant left).

### Coach brief generation (future)

When SoccerPack is live, `build_coach_briefs(..., sport="soccer")` will consume:

- Finding cards from `Findings/001_common_faults.txt` keyed by checkpoint failures.

## Capture guidance (for coaches and athletes)

- **Preferred camera**: side view (sagittal plane of kick) or mild rear-quarter; full body in frame including
  plant foot and kicking foot at follow-through.
- **Clip length**: include 2–4 approach steps through recovery (typically 1.5–3.0 s at 30–60 fps).
- **Surface**: flat ground or turf; note that soft sand or heavy mud changes plant-leg mechanics and may reduce
  finding confidence.
- **Youth**: use age-appropriate ball size and goal; do not cue maximum-effort shooting volume from this pack alone.
