# Soccer Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel soccer content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and federation training
science**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, or individualized
return-to-play protocols after groin, hamstring, knee, or ankle injury.

- Pain, swelling, locking, or giving-way in the hip, knee, or ankle during or after kicking require evaluation by
  a qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to continue high-volume shooting.
- Youth athletes differ in bone maturity, growth-related flexibility, and weekly load tolerance; follow your
  national governing body (NGB) and club medical staff for training volume.

## Not a substitute for qualified coaching

Automated briefs support athletes and coaches who already have technical supervision. They do not replace
in-person instruction for beginners learning approach angles, plant-foot placement, or heading safety.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Hip rotation and “open vs closed” plant foot when the camera is not perpendicular to the kick plane.
- Knee flexion when the kicking leg crosses the support leg or is occluded by the torso.
- Trunk lean when the athlete wears loose kit or the camera is elevated.

Findings describe **visible tendencies**, not laboratory-grade kinematics. Lower confidence when joints are
occluded, frame rate is below ~24 fps, or the clip is heavily cropped.

## No ball required — and no ball guarantees

This pack is designed for **body-only** analysis. Without ball tracking, “contact” is approximated by kicking-foot
speed peaks and timing relative to plant events. Cues may support movement patterns discussed in research, but
**accuracy, power, and injury outcomes depend on many factors** (fitness, sleep, cumulative load, prior injury,
playing surface, etc.). Do not promise goal-scoring or velocity gains from a single drill.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Published studies report **group averages and ranges**, not one correct pose for every player. Striker vs
midfielder styles, limb length, and mobility all change appearance. This pack avoids presenting arbitrary degree
targets as universal medical rules. Youth bands are separated from adult bands where literature differs.

## Heading and contact sports risk

This v1 pack focuses on **kicking**. Heading technique and aerial duel safety involve concussion protocols and
age-specific federation guidance — follow current IFAB/FIFA and your NGB; do not use kicking findings to justify
unsafe heading volume.

## Surface and equipment

Kicking on hard pavement, frozen ground, or with worn cleats changes load. Product text cannot see footwear or
surface; coaches must manage cumulative striking load especially in multi-sport youth schedules.

## Emergency

For acute injury on the field, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when needed.

## Product behavior

- Soccer pack features may be gated as **coming soon** until detector validation completes — absence of a finding
  is not proof of safe mechanics.
- The system may refuse to analyze clips that appear to be another sport when a live pack detector is confident
  elsewhere.

## Authority and updates

When `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` is present, it lists sources with authority levels. Federation curricula and
consensus statements change; retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Follow your coach and club
load guidelines.”
