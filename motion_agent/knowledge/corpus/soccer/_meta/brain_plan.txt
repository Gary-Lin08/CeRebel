4. PHASE MODEL (UI + DETECTOR ALIGNMENT)
================================================================================

Phase IDs (exact — target contract for future _soccer_window):

| id            | label_en        | label_zh       | role |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------|------|
| approach      | Approach run    | 助跑           | Steps in; rhythm & decel toward plant |
| plant_prep    | Plant prep      | 置脚准备       | Last 1–2 steps; CoM lowers; arms set |
| plant         | Plant           | 支撑脚着地     | Support foot contact; bracing window |
| backswing     | Backswing       | 后摆           | Kicking leg flexion / leg cocking |
| forward_swing | Forward swing   | 前摆           | Hip–knee extension; foot accelerates |
| contact_proxy | Contact window  | 触球窗口       | Peak kicking-foot speed ± brief span |
| follow        | Follow-through  | 随挥           | Deceleration; balance; recovery step |

Event definitions (body-only, OpenSim-friendly):

- contact_proxy_event: argmax ||v(kicking_ankle)|| in analysis window.
- backswing_end: last local max knee_flex_kicking before contact_proxy_event.
- backswing_start: knee_flex_kicking rises above run baseline after plant_prep.
- plant_event: support-side ankle speed minimum / vertical decel spike before
  contact_proxy (lead = support leg for right-foot kick: support left).
- plant_prep_start: CoM AP velocity toward target drops below 85% of approach peak
  OR last second step detected (fallback: contact_proxy − 35% window span).
- approach_start: clip start or first meaningful CoM velocity toward kick direction.
- follow_end: kicking_ankle speed < 40% of peak for ≥ 8% window span OR clip end.

Phase spans (defaults when events missing):
- approach: approach_start → plant_prep_start
- plant_prep: plant_prep_start → plant_event
- plant: plant_event → backswing_start (min 20 ms)
- backswing: backswing_start → backswing_end
- forward_swing: backswing_end → contact_proxy_event
- contact_proxy: contact_proxy_event ± 3% window span (min 25 ms)
- follow: end(contact_proxy) → follow_end

Footedness: kicking = striking foot; support = plant foot (RF kicker: kick right, plant left).
Capture: side view preferred (sagittal kick plane); oblique reduces hip rotation fidelity.

Phases/001_phase_model.txt (build): expand events, fallbacks, EN/ZH scrub copy, family notes.

5. APPROACH-RUN MECHANICS (COACHING + MEASUREMENT FOCUS)
================================================================================

Document in Biomechanics/001; checkpoints reference these body signals:

- **Step rhythm**: roughly even cadence in approach; last step often shorter (gather).
- **Deceleration**: CoM still moves toward target but AP speed drops before plant
  (not a full stop far from plant — coaching "run through the ball" = controlled decel).
- **Trunk angle**: slight forward lean into plant_prep (instep); more upright for side-foot.
- **Arm action**: contralateral arm forward at plant for balance; both arms quiet on side-foot.
- **Curved approach**: pelvis path arcs — tag only; same phase IDs (future: path curvature series).
- **Hip opening**: support hip externally rotates so plant toes point toward target line (proxy:
  foot segment yaw vs pelvis at plant_event).

Instep-specific approach cues: 4–6 steps typical at youth; longer at adult power shots (B+).
Side-foot-specific: 1–3 steps; minimal backswing; "pass through" not "swing over".

7. CLINICAL / COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED)
================================================================================

Use as RAG reference bands, NOT individual medical advice. Wording: "typical in published
cohorts" or "coaching consensus". Build agent adds URLs in SOURCE_MANIFEST.

ADULT instep drive (B+ / A where cited in systematic kicking reviews):
- Support knee flex at plant: often ~20–35° with bracing through contact window.
- Kicking knee flex peak in backswing: often ~80–110° before rapid extension (wide inter-athlete).
- Trunk lean forward at plant: commonly ~10–25° sagittal (style-dependent).
- Hip–trunk separation timing: pelvis rotation peak before or with kicking-foot speed peak (A).

ADULT side-foot pass (B+):
- Smaller backswing: kicking knee flex peak often lower than instep (~45–70°).
- More upright trunk at contact_proxy (~5–15° lean).
- Support foot closer to body midline; plant_offset smaller than instep.

YOUTH (B+ / federation coaching):
- Shorter approach; emphasis on plant stability and passing accuracy over max foot speed.
- Avoid cueing "snap hip like pros" under ~12 yrs; findings severity down-weight.
- No weighted-ball or overspeed kick prescriptions in product KB.

Exclude ball speed / launch angle from any checkpoint.
