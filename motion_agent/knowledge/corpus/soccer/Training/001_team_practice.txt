# Soccer Team Practice — Kicking and Striking (body-first training design)

Summarizes open NGB coaching structure and biomechanics-informed practice design. **Not** a replacement for your
club curriculum or licensed coach education courses. Cite live FIFA/UEFA/NGB resources for official progression tables.

---

## 1. Session goals aligned with motion analysis

When CeRebel soccer mode is available, team sessions can pair **one body checkpoint** with **one drill block**:

| Week theme | Checkpoint focus | Example drill (see Coaching/002) |
|------------|------------------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Plant consistency | Two-line plant |
| 2 | Hip open proxy | Cone plant window |
| 3 | Trunk through strike | Shoulder pole bow |
| 4 | Approach rhythm | Beep plant sync |
| 5 | Follow-through | Finish line tape |

Rotate themes rather than stacking five fixes in one session — matches “one main fix” product UX.

## 2. Typical practice structure (60–90 min field session)

**Warm-up (10–15 min)**

- General movement: jog, dynamic stretches, hip openers (no prolonged static stretch cold).
- Rondo or passing triangle at low intensity — not counted as striking load.

**Technical block (15–20 min)**

- Unopposed striking: side-foot pass accuracy first, then instep distance control.
- Use Coaching/002 drills in pairs; each athlete 6–10 quality reps, not maximal volume.

**Opposed / game-like (20–30 min)**

- Small-sided games with constraints (e.g., must use side-foot finish inside box) — tactical, not auto-scored by pack.

**Finisher (optional, 5–10 min)**

- Controlled repetition strikes on goal with coach count cap; record 1–2 side-view clips for motion upload.

**Cool-down**

- Low-intensity jog, brief mobility; monitor athletes reporting groin/hamstring tightness.

## 3. Progressions (no ball tracking required)

### Stage A — Static plant and swing

Athlete steps in, plants, swings without ball (shadow kicks). Emphasizes cp_plant_timing and cp_plant_knee_stiff.
Youth: 8 reps per foot; adults: 10–12.

### Stage B — Ball on ground, short distance

Pass to partner 5–10 m; focus finding from brief (e.g., plant-foot-closes-hip). Increase distance only when
checkpoint stable across two uploads.

### Stage C — Approach run-up

Add 2–4 step approach; film side view for approach-rushed and leaning-back findings.

### Stage D — Match speed

Introduce defender pressure or time limits; motion clips may be noisier — lower automated confidence expected.

---

## 4. Volume and load guidance (summarized, not reproduced tables)

Federation and sports-science sources emphasize:

- **Youth:** Limit consecutive maximal strikes; prioritize variety (passing, 1v1, small games) over repeated
  full-power shooting blocks. Follow your NGB’s recommended practice duration by age band (FIFA Grassroots
  principles — authority A+ for participation, B for technical detail).
- **Teen:** Monitor cumulative weekly header + strike load during growth spurts; club medical staff lead return
  from injury.
- **Adult amateur:** Hard surface and fatigue increase variability — schedule quality striking when legs are fresh.

CeRebel does **not** count balls struck in a session unless integrated with team data — treat load advice as
educational.

---

## 5. Partner and camera setup for team capture

- **Side view:** Camera on tripod at mid-torso height, 3–5 m from athlete, perpendicular to kick direction.
- **Full frame:** Include approach steps, plant foot, and follow-through foot.
- **Batch capture:** Line athletes; 30 s per player (one approach kick) during technical block.
- **Privacy:** Obtain consent for video upload in minors; follow school/club policy.

---

## 6. Using coach briefs in a team huddle

1. Show phase scrub (when live): “Here is plant vs strike.”
2. State one finding headline in plain language.
3. Assign one drill from Coaching/002 for next 5 minutes.
4. Re-record 2 athletes as demonstration of improvement — avoid public shaming on faults.

## 7. Equipment-minimal station rotation (4 stations, 8 players)

| Station | Activity | Finding target |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Wall rebounder side-foot | plant-too-close |
| 2 | Cone plant window | plant-foot-closes-hip |
| 3 | Corridor run-up | approach-wide |
| 4 | Freeze finish balance | plant-collapse |

2 minutes per station, rotate twice.

---

## 8. Integration with match day

- Avoid first-time technical overhaul on match day; use briefs for maintenance cues only.
- Post-match fatigued kicking clips are poor baseline for checkpoint thresholds.
