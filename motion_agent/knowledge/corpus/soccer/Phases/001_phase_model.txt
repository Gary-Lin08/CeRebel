# Soccer Kick — Phase Model (body-first, OpenSim-friendly) — v2

## Scope

Applies to **approach + instep-style strike** and **side-foot pass** with single plant foot and swinging
kicking leg. Volley body-shape findings reuse the same phase IDs with plant/cocking timing notes.
Penalty-style and curved run-ups map to the same events with wider approach windows.

Convention: **trail leg = kicking foot**, **lead leg = plant foot**.

## Phase list (7 phases)

| phase_id | label_en | start_event | end_event | body narrative |
|----------|----------|-------------|-----------|----------------|
| setup | Setup / pre-approach | clip_start | E_APPROACH_START | Oriented to target; minimal COM drift |
| approach | Run-up | E_APPROACH_START | E_APPROACH_END | Steps toward ball; COM velocity elevated |
| plant | Plant foot contact | E_APPROACH_END | E_PLANT_SET | Lead foot accepts weight; kicking leg countermovement |
| cocking | Leg cocking / backswing | E_PLANT_SET | E_KICK_PEAK | Kicking hip extends, knee flexes, ankle trails |
| strike | Strike window | E_KICK_PEAK ± 3 frames | Peak foot speed; trunk lean and hip rotation near max |
| follow | Follow-through | end(strike) | E_FOLLOW_END | Kicking leg swings across; plant may hop |
| recovery | Recovery | E_FOLLOW_END | clip_end | Deceleration; athletic stance |

## Phase blocks (detector_events + fault_links)

### phase_id: setup
start_end: clip_start → E_APPROACH_START
body_signals: Pelvis speed low; shoulder/hip orientation toward target; arm readiness.
detector_events: E_APPROACH_START
fault_links: fault_arms_collapsed
youth_note: Setup may be brief; widen window for U10.

### phase_id: approach
start_end: E_APPROACH_START → E_APPROACH_END
body_signals: COM speed elevated; step length pattern; lateral pelvis drift; arm counter-swing.
detector_events: E_APPROACH_START, E_APPROACH_END
fault_links: fault_approach_rushed, fault_approach_wide, fault_approach_short_steps, fault_arms_collapsed
youth_note: Single hop-step approaches normal — E_APPROACH may collapse.

### phase_id: plant
start_end: E_APPROACH_END → E_PLANT_SET
body_signals: Lead ankle speed drop; plant distance vs trail; plant knee flex; foot yaw; heel acceptance.
detector_events: E_APPROACH_END, E_PLANT_SET
fault_links: fault_plant_foot_closes_hip, fault_plant_too_close, fault_plant_too_far, fault_plant_knee_soft, fault_plant_toe_out_excess, fault_plant_heel_strike_hard, fault_approach_rushed, fault_volley_plant_late, fault_weak_foot_plant_mismatch, fault_non_kicking_arm_passive
youth_note: Shorter plant-set times; relax distance bands.

### phase_id: cocking
start_end: E_PLANT_SET → E_KICK_PEAK
body_signals: Trail hip extension / knee flex; pelvis yaw rate; trunk lean; opposite arm path.
detector_events: E_PLANT_SET, E_KICK_PEAK
fault_links: fault_backswing_short, fault_hips_stay_square, fault_hip_opens_late, fault_leaning_back, fault_trunk_rotation_absent, fault_side_foot_big_windup, fault_kicking_hip_adduction_early, fault_strike_no_acceleration, fault_volley_trunk_upright, fault_non_kicking_arm_passive
youth_note: Shorter cocking duration normal; do not force adult windup.

### phase_id: strike
start_end: E_KICK_PEAK − 3f → E_KICK_PEAK + 3f
body_signals: Peak ||v(trail_ankle)||; trunk lean; knee extension onset; ankle lock proxy; shoulder yaw.
detector_events: E_KICK_PEAK
fault_links: fault_leaning_back, fault_plant_foot_closes_hip, fault_plant_too_far, fault_plant_knee_soft, fault_knee_stiff_strike, fault_ankle_locked_strike, fault_shoulders_closed_target, fault_strike_no_acceleration, fault_volley_trunk_upright, fault_kicking_hip_adduction_early, fault_weak_foot_plant_mismatch
youth_note: Lower peak foot speed expected; use self-baseline.

### phase_id: follow
start_end: end(strike) → E_FOLLOW_END
body_signals: Trail ankle path length/cross-midline; plant hop; continued trunk rotation.
detector_events: E_KICK_PEAK, E_FOLLOW_END
fault_links: fault_follow_through_cut, fault_follow_across_body_short, fault_knee_stiff_strike, fault_plant_collapse, fault_shoulders_closed_target
youth_note: Abbreviated follow on passes is OK — check intent.

### phase_id: recovery
start_end: E_FOLLOW_END → clip_end
body_signals: COM settle; plant-side balance; return to athletic stance.
detector_events: E_FOLLOW_END
fault_links: fault_plant_collapse
youth_note: Quick reset common in game-like drills.

## Event definitions (computable proxies)

### E_KICK_PEAK
- Maximum ||v(trail_ankle)|| in analysis window (or max forward component if target calibrated).
- Contact proxy without ball tracking.
- Fallback: last peak above 70% global max before clip end.

### E_PLANT_SET
- First frame after E_APPROACH_END where lead-ankle speed drops below fraction of approach peak for ≥N frames.
- Fallback: E_KICK_PEAK − ~120–180 ms adult full swing.

### E_APPROACH_END
- Last frame before E_PLANT_SET with both ankles elevated speed.
- Fallback: E_PLANT_SET − one step (~300 ms).

### E_APPROACH_START
- First frame pelvis COM speed exceeds setup baseline OR first clear step after clip_start.
- Fallback: clip_start + 5% duration.

### E_FOLLOW_END
- Trail-ankle speed < 30% peak AND trail knee flexion increasing.
- Fallback: E_KICK_PEAK + 15% window span.

## Phase precedence

setup → approach → plant → cocking → strike → follow → recovery

## Side-foot / volley variants

Same phase IDs; coaching interpretation and thresholds differ:
- Side-foot: lower peak speed; shorter cocking; relaxed follow length.
- Volley: plant may be late relative to swing; emphasize fault_volley_* cards.
