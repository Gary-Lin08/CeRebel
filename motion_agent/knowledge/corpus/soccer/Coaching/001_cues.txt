## Global cueing principles

1. One main fix: highest-severity finding for brief UX.
2. Intent tag: pass vs shot — down-weight power cues for pass intent.
3. Youth: smooth and balanced; plant consistency over max power.
4. Camera: if hip-open cues fail due to oblique angle, suggest side-view recapture.
5. Non-dominant foot: mirror language; specify kicking foot in UI.
