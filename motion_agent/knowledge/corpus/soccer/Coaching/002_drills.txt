## Multi-finding warm-up block (team, ~5 min)

1. Dynamic leg swings (10 each leg) — no max height youth.
2. Stationary side-foot taps ×10.
3. Plant-and-freeze: approach, plant, hold 2 s, no kick.
4. One coached kick each athlete with single finding focus.

## Safety

- Stop on acute pain; refer per Disclaimer.
- Youth: cap full-effort instep counts per NGB guidance.
- Hard surfaces: prefer passing weight over maximal strike volume.
