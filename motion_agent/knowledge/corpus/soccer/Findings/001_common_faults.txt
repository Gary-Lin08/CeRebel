# Soccer Kick — Common Faults (finding cards) — v2

Authority: B / B+ summarized open literature and NGB coaching; not diagnostic. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
Stable keys use fault_* snake_case. legacy_id retains prior kebab-case slugs for RAG.

Body-first: plant and swing visible without ball tracking. E_KICK_PEAK = foot-speed contact proxy.

finding_id: fault_plant_foot_closes_hip
legacy_id: plant-foot-closes-hip
headline_en: Plant foot points away from target and closes the hip
phases: plant, cocking, strike
body_signal: At E_PLANT_SET and E_KICK_PEAK, plant-foot toe direction points substantially across intended kick line; pelvis yaw toward target limited (cp_hip_open_proxy).
why_it_matters: Support-foot orientation and pelvis rotation link to kicking-foot velocity and accuracy variability in biomechanics reviews. Closed hip can restrict trunk rotation and shift load — performance and load awareness, not diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Show the laces of your plant shoe to the target side of the field."
drill: 60 s cone plant window — plant so inside arch faces cone; 3 slow + 2 firm passes (drill_plant_cone_window).
severity_default: medium
sources: Lees & Nolan 1998 soccer biomechanics review (B+); UEFA/FIFA plant-foot coaching summaries (B)
youth_note: Plant stability and approach rhythm; avoid elite hip-speed absolutes.

finding_id: fault_plant_too_close
legacy_id: plant-too-close
headline_en: Plant foot lands too close to the ball line
phases: plant, cocking
body_signal: Lead ankle vs trail ankle at E_PLANT_SET inside lower band of normalized plant distance (cp_plant_distance_band); kicking knee may appear cramped at max cocking.
why_it_matters: Very short plant distance can limit hip extension and reduce swing arc; coaches associate with scooped or low strikes. Visible without ball.
feel_cue: "Leave a hand's width between plant and strike line."
drill: 45 s two-line plant — five instep strikes planting on outer line (drill_plant_two_line).
severity_default: medium
sources: Nunes et al. instep kicking kinematics reviews (B+); grassroots NGB manuals summarized (B)
youth_note: Plant stability and approach rhythm; avoid elite hip-speed absolutes.

finding_id: fault_plant_too_far
legacy_id: plant-too-far
headline_en: Plant foot lands too far from the ball line
phases: plant, strike
body_signal: Normalized plant distance above upper band at E_PLANT_SET; COM may lag behind kicking foot at E_KICK_PEAK.
why_it_matters: Over-reaching can reduce plant-leg stability and delay kicking-foot timing relative to COM (B+ open kicking studies).
feel_cue: "Step beside the ball, not past it."
drill: 45 s gate step — plant between ball-line and forward cone; 4 reps (drill_plant_gate).
severity_default: medium
sources: Kellis & Katis instep kick determinants (B+); coaching consensus (B)
youth_note: Plant stability and approach rhythm; avoid elite hip-speed absolutes.

feel_cue: "Chest over the ball; finish nose over laces."

severity_default: medium

finding_id: fault_side_foot_big_windup
headline_en: Side-foot pass uses an oversized backswing
phases: cocking, strike
body_signal: For side-foot intent (or low peak speed + medial foot orientation proxy), trail hip extension / knee flex at cocking exceeds pass-appropriate band (cp_sidefoot_windup).
why_it_matters: Large windup on push passes reduces accuracy and slows release in coaching models (B). Shot intent exempt.
feel_cue: "Short draw, soft push — pass doesn't need a golf backswing."

finding_id: fault_weak_foot_plant_mismatch
headline_en: Weak-foot kick shows plant and hip pattern mismatched to strong foot
phases: plant, cocking, strike
body_signal: When non-dominant kicking side tagged, plant distance and pelvis yaw differ from dominant-side self-baseline beyond band (cp_weak_foot_mismatch).
why_it_matters: Weak-foot inconsistency is expected; large plant/hip mismatch vs own strong foot guides bilateral practice (B). Not a pathology label.
feel_cue: "Copy your strong-foot plant movie on the other side."
drill: 90 s alternate-foot plant freezes then easy passes each foot (drill_weak_foot_mirror).
severity_default: low
sources: Bilateral kicking asymmetry research summaries (B+); NGB weak-foot progressions (B)
youth_note: Prioritize confidence and contact quality over matching adult power.
