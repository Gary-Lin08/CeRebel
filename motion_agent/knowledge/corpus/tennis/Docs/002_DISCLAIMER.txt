# Tennis Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel tennis content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and federation training
materials**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy, or individualized
return-to-play protocols after shoulder, elbow, wrist, or back injury.

- Pain, swelling, locking, numbness, or sudden loss of strength during serving or groundstrokes requires
  evaluation by a qualified clinician — do not use app feedback to play through symptoms.
- Junior athletes differ from adults in growth, coordination, and acceptable training volume; follow your
  national association’s age-appropriate progression (see Training/001 and SOURCE_MANIFEST youth sources).

## Not officiating or line calling

ITF rule summaries in this pack are educational. Foot faults, lets, and score disputes are decided by officials
and appropriate technology — not by body pose alone. Without calibrated court geometry, CeRebel does not claim
to detect foot faults or ball in/out.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Shoulder internal rotation and racket path when the arm is foreshortened toward the camera.
- Toss arm position when occluded by the head or hair.
- Knee flexion in deep knee bend when shorts or shadow obscure the joint.
- Hip rotation in the plane perpendicular to the camera.

Findings describe **visible tendencies** with confidence reduced when joints are occluded or frame rate is low.

## No guaranteed performance outcomes

Efficient sequencing described in research may support power and consistency for some athletes, but **serve speed,
accuracy, and match performance** depend on training history, fatigue, equipment, surface, and many non-mechanical
factors. Do not promise ranking or mph gains from a single cue or drill.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Elite players show wide variation in trophy pose, toss height, and groundstroke backswing length. Open literature
reports **group trends**, not one pose for every body type. This pack avoids arbitrary degree targets as universal
rules; youth athletes especially should not mimic adult professional models without qualified coaching.

## Load and overuse

High serve and overhead volume correlates with shoulder and elbow load in epidemiology literature. Automated briefs
may note visible mechanical patterns associated with load (e.g., all-arm swing, harsh deceleration) but **do not
diagnose** tendinopathy or predict injury from one clip.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, parents, and athletes who already have a training plan. They do not replace
on-court supervision, especially for beginners learning grip, footwork, and safe throwing-like serve motions.

## Emergency

For acute injury on court, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when needed.

## Product behavior

- Tennis pack features may be gated as **coming soon** until sport detection validates — absence of a finding is
  not proof of safe or legal technique.
- The system may analyze general human motion when tennis is not confirmed; sport-specific findings should not apply
  in that mode.

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. ITF rules and NGB training pages change;
retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from body motion — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Follow your coach and age-appropriate training load.”
