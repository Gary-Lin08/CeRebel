# Tennis Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first tennis coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the athlete’s body
is doing across recognizable **serve** and **groundstroke** phases. Ball tracking, line calling, racket sensors,
and Hawkeye-style trajectories are optional enrichments, not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Flat or kick serve** motion from side or rear oblique camera (body-visible).
- **Forehand and backhand** groundstrokes (open, semi-open, or neutral stance variants).
- Right- and left-handed players; default naming assumes right-handed (racket side = trail side on forehand).

Out of scope for automatic findings (glossary or future packs only):
- Ball spin rate, placement, net clearance, double-bounce rules.
- Doubles positioning, poaching, tactical patterns.
- Foot-fault adjudication without calibrated court geometry.
- String bed or racket model optimization.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles and heights where visible, segment speeds,
   timing between phase events, posture and weight-shift proxies without a ball.
2. **Evidence layering**: ITF rules (A+) for definitions; clinical and epidemiology reviews (A/B+) for load awareness;
   biomechanics reviews (B+) for kinetic-chain relationships; cues and drills are coaching advice (B), paired with
   disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric angle targets are not prescribed as universal medicine; qualitative
   patterns and published themes only, with youth/adult separation for training volume.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   future tennis detectors can attach findings to time windows.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS, duration.
- Minimum useful joints: both wrists, elbows, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, pelvis/spine proxy.
- Handedness flips racket-side vs non-racket-side labels.

### Tennis-likeness gate (future)

Conservative auto-select signals (not yet wired in `sports/registry.py`):
- **Serve-like**: asymmetric shoulder elevation window + single dominant wrist speed peak overhead height band.
- **Groundstroke-like**: lateral trunk rotation + horizontal wrist speed peak near mid-clip.
- **Reject**: golf-like vertical spine tilt with low asymmetric shoulder pattern; pure running gait.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to computable proxies:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Stroke |
|----------|--------------|--------|
| S_CONTACT_PROXY | argmax racket-side wrist speed | Serve |
| G_CONTACT_PROXY | argmax racket-side wrist speed | Groundstroke |
| S_TOSS_RISE | non-racket wrist vertical velocity peak early | Serve |
| S_TROPHY_LOAD | knee flexion local minimum before S_CONTACT_PROXY | Serve |
| G_UNIT_TURN | trunk yaw velocity peak before G_CONTACT_PROXY | Groundstroke |
| G_SPLIT | bilateral ankle speed micro-peak at clip onset | Groundstroke |

### Coach brief generation (future)

When `SportPack` for tennis ships, `build_coach_briefs` will consume phase windows, ranked finding cards,
one feel cue and one short drill per prioritized finding from Coaching/.

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; definitional sentences with repeated stable IDs (`fault_*`, `S_*`, `G_*`).
- Include authority level in prose where claims are evidence-backed (see SOURCE_MANIFEST).
- `_meta/*` excluded from indexing per product default.

## Evidence types (tennis pack)

## Youth vs adult

Training volume, court size, and ball compression differ by age band (`audience=youth` vs `adult` in Training/).
Mechanics cues overlap but **load and repetition** must not copy adult serve intensity to young juniors.
