# Tennis Sport Pack — Quality definitions (body-first)

“Quality” in CeRebel tennis mode means **observable body patterns** associated with coordinated, balanced strokes in
open coaching and biomechanics literature — not match outcomes, mph, or ranking. All dimensions are **non-diagnostic**.

## Dimensions

### dimension: leg_contribution
**Serve:** Visible knee flexion during S_TROPHY_LOAD and extension into S_DRIVE_UP before S_CONTACT_PROXY.
**Groundstroke:** Load leg knee flexion during G_LOAD with push into G_FORWARD_SWING.
**Poor sign:** Upright legs through load while racket-side wrist speed still high (all-arm pattern).
**Authority:** B+ kinetic chain reviews; B coaching consensus.

### dimension: trunk_rotation_timing
**Groundstroke:** Trunk rotation (pelvis/shoulder yaw proxy) peaks **before** G_CONTACT_PROXY, not simultaneously with maximum wrist speed only.
**Serve:** Trunk tilt and rotation build during S_TROPHY_LOAD and S_DRIVE_UP; not frozen upright through S_CONTACT_PROXY.
**Poor sign:** Shoulders and hips rotate together only at contact proxy with no prior coil.
**Authority:** B+.

### dimension: asymmetric_shoulder_load_serve
**Serve:** Racket-side shoulder elevated above lead shoulder during S_TROPHY_LOAD without excessive shrugging-only motion (visible elbow below or near shoulder height on racket side).
**Poor sign:** Both shoulders level and racket arm pushed solely forward with no elevation window.
**Authority:** B coaching; B+ variability in elite data — qualitative only.

### dimension: toss_arm_coordination
**Serve:** Non-racket arm reaches organized height during S_TOSS_RISE then clears downward or inward **before** S_CONTACT_PROXY without wild lateral drift at S_TROPHY_LOAD.
**Without ball:** Drift = non-racket wrist lateral offset from sternum midline on front/side view.
**Authority:** B coaching.

### dimension: deceleration_control
**Both strokes:** Racket-side wrist speed decreases progressively through S_DECEL or G_FOLLOW without sharp reversal (hitch) immediately after contact proxy.
**Poor sign:** Speed cliff then secondary spike (fighting the finish).
**Authority:** B+ load management themes; B coaching.

### dimension: dynamic_balance
**Serve:** Athlete lands inside court or recovers without excessive lateral fall after S_DECEL.
**Groundstroke:** Center of mass stays between feet through G_FOLLOW; no single-leg collapse away from shot direction.
**Authority:** B coaching.

### dimension: rhythm_tempo
**Serve:** Continuous motion S_READY → S_CONTACT_PROXY without long freeze at S_TROPHY_LOAD (> subjective “two-count pause” on video at 30fps ≈ 15+ frames static wrists).
**Poor sign:** Rush from ready to contact with skipped S_TROPHY_LOAD visibility.
**Authority:** B coaching.

### dimension: recovery_readiness
**Groundstroke:** G_RECOVERY shows racket centering and foot adjustment toward athletic stance.
**Authority:** B coaching.

## Youth vs adult quality interpretation

**Youth (audience=youth):** Smaller amplitude on leg_contribution and asymmetric_shoulder_load_serve; reward coordination and balance over deep adult-style knee bend. Follow red/orange/green court scaling in Training/001.

**Adult recreational:** Emphasize deceleration_control and leg_contribution to reduce all-arm habits; still avoid prescribing exact knee angles.

**Elite comparison mode (future):** Compare athlete to self over sessions, not to professional template skeletons.

## Scoring philosophy (product)

When automated scoring ships, each dimension maps 0–1 confidence from pose quality and phase alignment.
Low joint confidence caps scores. Missing G_SPLIT does not fail a groundstroke clip.
