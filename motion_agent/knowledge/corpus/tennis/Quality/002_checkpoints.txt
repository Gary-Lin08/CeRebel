# Tennis Sport Pack — Checkpoints catalog

Checkpoints are **pass/fail body observations** tied to phase IDs. Use for coach briefs and future automated rules.
Wording is qualitative; no universal degree targets.

---

## Serve checkpoints

### checkpoint_id: serve_knee_load_min
**Phase:** S_TROPHY_LOAD
**Dimension:** leg_contribution
**Pass:** Knees show visible flexion compared to S_READY (both knees or load-side deeper) while racket-side shoulder is elevated.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_knee_lock
**Measure proxy:** knee angle trend downward ≥ modest threshold vs ready, camera permitting.
**Youth:** Pass with shallower flexion if rhythm and balance pass.

### checkpoint_id: serve_shoulder_asymmetry
**Phase:** S_TROPHY_LOAD
**Dimension:** asymmetric_shoulder_load_serve
**Pass:** Racket-side shoulder higher than lead shoulder on side or rear oblique view.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_shoulder_tilt_flat
**Measure proxy:** shoulder height difference sign consistent for handedness.

### checkpoint_id: serve_toss_arm_stable
**Phase:** S_TOSS_RISE through S_TROPHY_LOAD
**Dimension:** toss_arm_coordination
**Pass:** Non-racket wrist not drifting far lateral to sternum midline at S_TROPHY_LOAD.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_toss_arm_drift
**Measure proxy:** horizontal wrist offset normalized by shoulder width.

### checkpoint_id: serve_continuous_tempo
**Phase:** S_READY → S_CONTACT_PROXY
**Dimension:** rhythm_tempo
**Pass:** No prolonged static hold at S_TROPHY_LOAD; motion flows to S_DRIVE_UP.
**Fail link:** fault_double_fault_rhythm_rush (rush variant) or coach note “frozen trophy” (future fault freeze)
**Measure proxy:** wrist speed near zero duration at trophy window.

### checkpoint_id: serve_drive_extension
**Phase:** S_DRIVE_UP
**Dimension:** leg_contribution
**Pass:** Knee extension and ankle rise visible before S_CONTACT_PROXY.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_knee_lock (if drive absent)

### checkpoint_id: serve_decel_smooth
**Phase:** S_DECEL
**Dimension:** deceleration_control
**Pass:** Racket-side wrist speed decreases smoothly; arm finishes across body or to opposite hip.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_poor_decel

### checkpoint_id: serve_landing_balance
**Phase:** S_DECEL
**Dimension:** dynamic_balance
**Pass:** Lead foot step into court without major lateral lean collapse.
**Fail link:** (info only in v1) — extreme collapse may reference fault_serve_excessive_arch if paired with hyperextension

### checkpoint_id: serve_trunk_lean_control
**Phase:** S_DRIVE_UP
**Dimension:** dynamic_balance
**Pass:** Trunk leans away from net on side view without extreme low-back arch dominating motion.
**Fail link:** fault_serve_excessive_arch

## Groundstroke checkpoints

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_early_turn
**Phase:** G_UNIT_TURN
**Dimension:** trunk_rotation_timing
**Pass:** Trunk yaw rotation largely completes before G_FORWARD_SWING peak wrist acceleration.
**Fail link:** fault_groundstroke_late_unit_turn

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_load_leg
**Phase:** G_LOAD
**Dimension:** leg_contribution
**Pass:** Load-leg knee flexion visible; pelvis stable into forward swing.
**Fail link:** fault_groundstroke_wide_stance_stuck (if wide but no flex)

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_body_then_arm
**Phase:** G_FORWARD_SWING
**Dimension:** trunk_rotation_timing
**Pass:** Trunk uncoiling precedes or coincides with early wrist acceleration, not arm-only punch with frozen pelvis.
**Fail link:** fault_groundstroke_all_arm

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_contact_proxy_peak
**Phase:** G_CONTACT_PROXY
**Dimension:** (timing anchor)
**Pass:** N/A — event marker for other checks.

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_finish_balance
**Phase:** G_FOLLOW
**Dimension:** dynamic_balance
**Pass:** Weight on balanced base; opposite arm counterbalances racket-side finish.
**Fail link:** fault_groundstroke_upright_recovery if athlete bolts upright without finish

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_recovery_center
**Phase:** G_RECOVERY
**Dimension:** recovery_readiness
**Pass:** Feet and racket return toward ready; no stuck wide open stance without reset.
**Fail link:** fault_groundstroke_wide_stance_stuck

### checkpoint_id: groundstroke_split_present
**Phase:** G_SPLIT
**Dimension:** rhythm_tempo
**Pass:** Optional — micro-hop or foot widen if clip includes preparation.
**Fail:** informational only if clip trimmed.
