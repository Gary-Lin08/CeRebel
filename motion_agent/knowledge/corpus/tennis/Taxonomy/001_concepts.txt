# Tennis Sport Pack — Concept taxonomy

## Stroke families

concept_id: stroke_serve
label: Tennis serve
synonyms: service motion, first serve, second serve, kick serve, flat serve, trophy pose, platform serve, pinpoint serve
stroke_family: serve
phase_chain: S_READY through S_DECEL
notes: Body-first — leg load, trunk tilt, shoulder elevation, wrist speed peak; no ball required.

concept_id: stroke_forehand
label: Forehand groundstroke
synonyms: forehand, FH, topspin forehand, open stance forehand, semi-open forehand, inside-out forehand
stroke_family: groundstroke
phase_chain: G_SPLIT through G_RECOVERY
dominant_side: racket-side arm drives G_FORWARD_SWING (RH default).

concept_id: stroke_backhand_one_hand
label: One-handed backhand
synonyms: 1H backhand, single-handed backhand, one hander
stroke_family: groundstroke
phase_chain: G_* with lead-arm emphasis in G_FORWARD_SWING

concept_id: stroke_backhand_two_hand
label: Two-handed backhand
synonyms: 2H backhand, double-handed backhand, two hander
stroke_family: groundstroke
phase_chain: G_* with dual-arm unit in G_LOAD and G_FORWARD_SWING

concept_id: stroke_backhand
label: Backhand groundstroke (generic)
synonyms: backhand, BH, slice backhand, topspin backhand
stroke_family: groundstroke
variants: stroke_backhand_one_hand, stroke_backhand_two_hand

concept_id: phase_s_ready

synonyms: ball toss preparation, guide arm up

concept_id: phase_trophy_pose

concept_id: phase_racket_drop

## Cross-cutting biomechanics

concept_id: phase_kinetic_chain
label: Kinetic chain
synonyms: segment sequencing, proximal to distal, leg drive, ground up, power transfer
stroke_family: both
authority: B+ biomechanics theme

concept_id: body_racket_side_wrist
label: Racket-side wrist
synonyms: hitting hand wrist, dominant hand wrist (context-dependent)
measure: speed peak, height at peak, path smoothness

concept_id: body_toss_arm
label: Non-racket arm (toss arm on serve)
synonyms: guide arm, left arm on right-handed serve
measure: elevation, lateral drift, timing before S_CONTACT_PROXY

concept_id: body_trunk_rotation
label: Trunk rotation
synonyms: hip rotation, shoulder rotation, separation, uncoiling
measure: yaw rate proxy, pelvis-to-shoulder timing before contact proxy

concept_id: body_knee_flexion
label: Knee flexion
synonyms: knee bend, loaded legs, athletic bend
measure: trend during S_TROPHY_LOAD and G_LOAD

concept_id: body_hip_shoulder_separation
label: Hip-shoulder separation
synonyms: coil, X-factor, trunk separation
measure: angle proxy during G_UNIT_TURN and G_LOAD

## Quality dimensions

concept_id: quality_rhythm
label: Serve rhythm and tempo
synonyms: timing, continuous motion, no pause at trophy, fluid serve
stroke_family: serve

concept_id: quality_balance
label: Dynamic balance
synonyms: stable landing, controlled deceleration, center of mass over base
stroke_family: both

concept_id: quality_leg_drive
label: Leg drive
synonyms: push off back foot, knee bend, explode up, use legs not just arm
stroke_family: serve
also: G_LOAD on groundstrokes
