# Tennis Sport Pack — Ontology (Schema)

## Entity: Player

Attributes:
- player_id (string, optional external)
- handedness: right | left
- audience_band: youth | adult | unknown
- sex_category: optional; normative training context only, not mechanics prescription

Relations:
- performs → StrokeInstance
- subject_of → FindingInstance

## Entity: StrokeType

Enum:
- serve
- forehand_groundstroke
- backhand_one_hand
- backhand_two_hand
- volley (future)
- overhead_smash (future)

Attributes:
- stroke_type_id (stable slug: stroke_serve, stroke_forehand, stroke_backhand_one_hand, stroke_backhand_two_hand)
- racket_side_wrist: trail | lead (depends on handedness and stroke)

## Entity: StrokeVariant

Optional tags on StrokeInstance (not separate phase chains in v1):
- serve: platform | pinpoint | flat | kick | slice (intent tag only)
- groundstroke: open_stance | neutral_stance | semi_open

## Entity: Phase

Attributes:
- phase_id (S_* serve, G_* groundstroke)
- stroke_family: serve | groundstroke
- applies_to_strokes: list of stroke_type_id (forehand and both backhands share G_*)
- ordinal (integer order within family)
- description_plain (English)

Relations:
- follows → Phase (previous in chain via precedes)
- contains_events → PhaseEvent

Serve chain: S_READY → S_TOSS_RISE → S_TROPHY_LOAD → S_RACKET_DROP → S_DRIVE_UP → S_CONTACT_PROXY → S_DECEL

Groundstroke chain: G_SPLIT → G_UNIT_TURN → G_LOAD → G_FORWARD_SWING → G_CONTACT_PROXY → G_FOLLOW → G_RECOVERY

## Entity: PhaseEvent

Attributes:
- event_id (often equals phase_id)
- start_rule (narrative, body-only)
- end_rule (narrative, body-only)
- detector_proxy (engineering string, optional)

## Entity: Finding

Attributes:
- finding_id (slug, prefix fault_)
- headline (plain language)
- primary_phase_id
- secondary_phase_ids (optional list)
- stroke_scope: serve | forehand | backhand_one_hand | backhand_two_hand | groundstroke_shared
- severity_hint: info | form | load
- body_signals (list of joint/segment observables)
- why_it_matters (performance/load awareness; non-diagnostic)
- audience_band: youth | adult | all
- authority_level: B+ | B typical
- evidence_type: ResearchFinding | CoachingAdvice
- requires_ball_track: false (v1 default)

Relations:
- anchored_in → Phase
- indicated_by → KinematicSignal (optional)
- has_cue → Cue
- has_drill → Drill
- alias_of → Finding (legacy shared groundstroke ids)

## Entity: KinematicSignal

Examples: peak_hand_speed, hip_shoulder_separation, trunk_yaw_rate, knee_flexion_trend, toss_arm_lateral_drift, center_of_mass_drift

Attributes:
- signal_id
- measured_on: joint or segment list
- measured_at_phase: phase_id

## Entity: Cue / Drill / Checkpoint

(Unchanged from v1 scaffold — see prior sections.)

## Entity: AudienceBand

Enum: youth | adult | all

Used on Finding, Player, LoadConcern, and optional EvidenceChunk filters.

## Entity: LoadConcern

Attributes:
- concern_id
- description (e.g., junior overhead volume)
- audience_band: youth | adult
- authority_level: A | B for published training policies

Not a Finding — referenced in Training/ and disclaimers.

## Entity: EvidenceChunk (RAG metadata)

Attributes:
- source_id (SOURCE_MANIFEST)
- authority_level (A+ … C per SPORT_PACK_KB_SPEC)
- evidence_type
- retrieved_at
- audience_band (optional)

## Naming conventions

- Phase IDs: prefix S_ (serve), G_ (groundstroke shared by FH/BH)
- Finding IDs: prefix fault_
- Legacy shared groundstroke faults keep fault_groundstroke_* ; forehand/backhand-specific add fault_forehand_* / fault_backhand*
- Cue IDs: cue_<finding_id>
- Drill IDs: drill_<finding_id>

## Handedness mapping (default right-handed)

Forehand: racket-side wrist = right wrist. Serve: racket-side = right; toss arm = left.
Backhand one-hand: forward swing lead arm = left for RH. Backhand two-hand: both hands on racket.
Left-handed: mirror lateral labels in product logic; corpus uses “racket-side” / “non-racket arm”.

## Product constraint (body-first)

All v1 Finding entities set requires_ball_track=false. Rule facts (let, foot fault) are glossary-only and not auto-detected without court calibration.
