# Tennis pack — brain plan (ontology + phase + finding map)
# Agent: BRAIN | Updated: 2026-10-21 | Contract: corpus/_templates/SPORT_PACK_KB_SPEC.txt

## Mission

Body-first coaching for **serve**, **forehand**, and **backhand** (one- and two-handed) from video pose / OpenSim-ready motion.
**Ball and racket object tracking are optional; v1 findings must not depend on them.** Contact is proxied by racket-side wrist speed peak (±2 frames).

## Stroke families (v1 product scope)

| family_id | Phases | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| serve | S_* chain (7) | Platform vs pinpoint stance = tag only; same phase IDs |
| forehand | G_* chain (7) | Open / semi-open / neutral stance = tag only |
| backhand_one_hand | G_* chain (7) | Lead-arm drive; shoulder external rotation emphasis in G_LOAD |
| backhand_two_hand | G_* chain (7) | Dual-arm unit; top-hand rotation cue in G_FORWARD_SWING |

Out of scope v1: volley, smash, return patterns, tactics, line calls, device racket speed.

## Phase models (detector events — no ball)

### Serve — `S_READY` … `S_DECEL` (7 phases)

| phase_id | start_event | end_event | Primary body signals |
|----------|-------------|-----------|----------------------|
| S_READY | static address | racket or toss arm begins upward path | foot width, knee soft, CoM over base |
| S_TOSS_RISE | non-racket arm lifts | toss arm near max height OR trophy begins | lead wrist height, lateral drift vs sternum |
| S_TROPHY_LOAD | coil visible | racket begins drop OR upward drive | knee flex min, racket-side shoulder elevation |
| S_RACKET_DROP | racket head falls below elbow | upward acceleration begins | elbow flex, scapular position |
| S_DRIVE_UP | leg push / trunk rise | S_CONTACT_PROXY | knee extension timing, trunk tilt |
| S_CONTACT_PROXY | peak racket-side wrist speed | speed < 85% of peak | shoulder abduction, elbow extension |
| S_DECEL | post-peak deceleration | stable land or recovery stance | follow-through path, landing knee/ankle |

### Forehand — `G_SPLIT` … `G_RECOVERY` (7 phases)

| phase_id | start_event | end_event | Primary body signals |
|----------|-------------|-----------|----------------------|
| G_SPLIT | clip onset or hop | G_UNIT_TURN begins | bilateral ankle micro-peak |
| G_UNIT_TURN | pelvis/trunk turn away | hand moves to max lay-back | hip–shoulder separation |
| G_LOAD | load leg accepts weight | G_FORWARD_SWING | knee flex, CoM over load foot |
| G_FORWARD_SWING | hand accelerates forward | G_CONTACT_PROXY | trunk uncoil vs arm |
| G_CONTACT_PROXY | peak racket-side wrist speed | 85% speed drop | elbow slot, wrist stability |
| G_FOLLOW | deceleration | finish across body or high wrap | balance, scapular upward rotation |
| G_RECOVERY | finish end | athletic ready | foot adjust, split-step prep |

### Backhand (1H and 2H) — same `G_*` IDs, family-specific emphasis

| phase_id | backhand_one_hand focus | backhand_two_hand focus |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| G_UNIT_TURN | lead shoulder retraction, racket tip back | compact dual-arm coil |
| G_LOAD | lead-side load, shoulder ext. rot. | both hands stay on racket, knee flex |
| G_FORWARD_SWING | lead arm drives; trunk stays sideways longer | top hand active; avoid bottom-hand-only pull |
| G_CONTACT_PROXY | reach vs rotation (elbow–trunk distance) | hands forward together at peak speed |
| G_FOLLOW | high finish over opposite shoulder | finish with hands on chest line |

## Youth vs adult (coaching copy — not medical norms)

**Youth (≈8–14, developmental)**
- Emphasize balance, rhythm, and repeatable toss-arm path over max wrist speed.
- Serve: avoid prescribing elite knee depth or hyperextension; flag `fault_serve_short_finish_youth` as informational when protecting volume.
- Groundstrokes: unit turn and recovery over power sequencing; do not fail clips against adult elite angle tables.
- Tag findings/cues with `audience_band: youth` where cues differ (see fault_backhand2_tight_shoulders_youth, fault_serve_short_finish_youth).

**Adult (recreational / amateur)**
- Full kinetic-chain cues (legs → trunk → arm) appropriate.
- Load-awareness language on serve volume OK; still no injury diagnosis from one clip.
- Open-stance forehand/backhand common; detectors must not assume closed stance.

**Shared (`all`)**
- Body-visible signals only; same phase IDs; handedness mirrored in product (racket-side wrist).
