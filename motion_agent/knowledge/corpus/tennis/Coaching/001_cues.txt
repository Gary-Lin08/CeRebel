# Tennis Sport Pack — Feel cues (body-first)

Cues pair to finding IDs in Findings/001_common_faults.txt. Language is athlete-facing; no ball required for practice.

---

## cue_fault_serve_knee_lock
**finding_id:** fault_serve_knee_lock
**phases:** S_TROPHY_LOAD, S_DRIVE_UP
**feel:** Ground feels heavy under both feet — push the floor away before the racket goes up.
**coach_note:** Look for knee angle change side view; youth use smaller squat amplitude.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_toss_arm_drift
**finding_id:** fault_serve_toss_arm_drift
**phases:** S_TOSS_RISE, S_TROPHY_LOAD
**feel:** Non-racket arm travels on a narrow vertical track in front of your chest.
**coach_note:** Drift is wrist lateral offset, not toss accuracy.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_shoulder_tilt_flat
**finding_id:** fault_serve_shoulder_tilt_flat
**phases:** S_TROPHY_LOAD
**feel:** Racket-side shoulder climbs like you’re reaching for a shelf above your head — lead shoulder stays lower.
**coach_note:** Avoid cueing extreme shrug; elevation with elbow bent is enough for most juniors.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_elbow_low
**finding_id:** fault_serve_elbow_low
**phases:** S_TROPHY_LOAD, S_RACKET_DROP
**feel:** Elbow up like a waiter’s tray before the racket drops behind the back.
**coach_note:** Side view best; frontal foreshortening can hide elbow height.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_poor_leg_sync
**finding_id:** fault_serve_poor_leg_sync
**phases:** S_DRIVE_UP
**feel:** Legs fire first — the racket rides the push up.
**coach_note:** Adult sequencing cue; youth emphasize visible bend before upward swing.
**authority:** B+

## cue_fault_serve_excessive_arch
**finding_id:** fault_serve_excessive_arch
**phases:** S_DRIVE_UP
**feel:** Ribs stay over hips while you reach up — grow tall instead of bending backward.
**coach_note:** If athlete reports back pain, stop and refer to clinician per disclaimer.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_poor_decel
**finding_id:** fault_serve_poor_decel
**phases:** S_DECEL
**feel:** After the hit, let the racket travel to the opposite hip pocket on a long exhale.
**coach_note:** Emphasize smooth wrist speed decay on video review.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_landing_imbalance
**finding_id:** fault_serve_landing_imbalance
**phases:** S_DECEL
**feel:** Soft front-foot stick into the court — then recover.
**coach_note:** Flag lateral fall or stuck rear foot; not a medical landing diagnosis.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_rhythm_rush
**finding_id:** fault_serve_rhythm_rush
**legacy_id:** fault_double_fault_rhythm_rush
**phases:** S_READY through S_CONTACT_PROXY
**feel:** Three quiet counts: bounce, set, fire — no sprint from ready to hit.
**coach_note:** Second-serve mental pressure often shows as rushed body; routine cue helps.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_short_finish_youth
**finding_id:** fault_serve_short_finish_youth
**phases:** S_DECEL
**feel:** Finish long and soft to the opposite pocket — no slam-stop.
**coach_note:** Youth volume-aware; stop on shoulder discomfort.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_serve_abbreviated_drop
**finding_id:** fault_serve_abbreviated_drop
**phases:** S_RACKET_DROP
**feel:** Light scratch behind the back before the throw up.
**coach_note:** Some abbreviated serves are stylistic — compare to athlete baseline.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_late_unit_turn
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_late_unit_turn
**phases:** G_UNIT_TURN
**feel:** Complete the shoulder turn until the non-racket hand sees the side fence, then swing.
**coach_note:** On fast incoming balls, shorten backswing instead of skipping turn.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_all_arm
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_all_arm
**phases:** G_UNIT_TURN, G_FORWARD_SWING
**feel:** Back points at the target longer — hand waits on the hip until the chest starts forward.
**coach_note:** Pair with open-stance load cues when feet are already open.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_flying_elbow
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_flying_elbow
**phases:** G_LOAD, G_FORWARD_SWING
**feel:** Elbow soft in the pocket — extend through the hit, don’t lift early.
**coach_note:** Towel-under-armpit drill for external feedback.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_open_no_load
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_open_no_load
**phases:** G_LOAD
**feel:** Wide base but soft knees — weight sinks into the outside leg before rotation.
**coach_note:** Fix load not width first on hard courts.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_over_rotate_finish
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_over_rotate_finish
**phases:** G_FOLLOW
**feel:** Belt buckle to the side fence — stop the spin, then recover.
**coach_note:** Distinguish from a healthy wrap with balanced feet.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_forehand_upright_recovery
**finding_id:** fault_forehand_upright_recovery
**phases:** G_FOLLOW, G_RECOVERY
**feel:** Finish with belt buckle still sideways; head stays level until the arm wraps.
**coach_note:** One-second finish hold in practice builds habit.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand1_dropped_shoulder
**finding_id:** fault_backhand1_dropped_shoulder
**phases:** G_LOAD, G_FORWARD_SWING
**feel:** Front shoulder stays high — chin near the front shoulder through contact.
**coach_note:** Side camera preferred for shoulder height.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand1_reaching
**finding_id:** fault_backhand1_reaching
**phases:** G_FORWARD_SWING, G_CONTACT_PROXY
**feel:** Rotate the chest past the path — don’t stab with a straight arm.
**coach_note:** Closed-stance constraint drill forces rotation.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand1_late_prep
**finding_id:** fault_backhand1_late_prep
**phases:** G_UNIT_TURN
**feel:** Racket tip to the back fence as you turn — early take-back.
**coach_note:** Shorten swing on pace; don’t skip prep entirely.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand2_bottom_hand_only
**finding_id:** fault_backhand2_bottom_hand_only
**phases:** G_FORWARD_SWING
**feel:** Top hand pushes the face through — both hands finish together.
**coach_note:** Top-hand-only shadows then recombine hands.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand2_tight_shoulders_youth
**finding_id:** fault_backhand2_tight_shoulders_youth
**phases:** G_LOAD, G_UNIT_TURN
**feel:** Turn so your back sees the side fence a little — stay soft in the shoulders.
**coach_note:** No forced end-range stretch for juniors.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_backhand_wide_stance_stuck
**finding_id:** fault_backhand_wide_stance_stuck
**phases:** G_LOAD
**feel:** Wide is fine — bend and push the ground to start the swing.
**coach_note:** Applies to 1H and 2H patterns.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_volley_arm_stab
**finding_id:** fault_volley_arm_stab
**phases:** V_FORWARD_BLOCK, V_CONTACT_PROXY

**finding_id:** fault_split_step_late

**finding_id:** fault_recovery_slow_recenter

**feel:** Finish, then beat your shadow back to the middle — small quick steps.

## Global cues (no single fault)

### cue_general_split_ready
**phases:** G_SPLIT, V_SPLIT
**feel:** Light hop lands on toes — feet live, knees soft, racket center.
**authority:** B

### cue_general_serve_routine
**phases:** S_READY
**feel:** Same bounce count and breath every time — body waits for your signal, not the scoreboard.
**authority:** B

### cue_general_decel_care
**phases:** S_DECEL, G_FOLLOW
**feel:** Long finish protects the shoulder — slow is strong on the way down.
**authority:** B+ awareness / B coaching

### cue_general_net_block
**phases:** V_READY, V_FORWARD_BLOCK
**feel:** Soft hands, firm body — meet the ball early in the court.
**authority:** B

## Youth scaling (audience=youth)

- Shorten verbal cues; use “sit, up, finish” for serve instead of three-beat adult routine.
- Trophy elevation cues should not reference professional toss height; emphasize smooth arm paths.
- Prefer fault_serve_short_finish_youth and fault_backhand2_tight_shoulders_youth over adult load depth cues.
- Sources: USTA red ball / 10U frameworks (B); Tennis Australia Hot Shots (B).
