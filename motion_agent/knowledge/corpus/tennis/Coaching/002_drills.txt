# Tennis Sport Pack — Short drills (30–90 seconds)

Drills link to finding IDs. Equipment-minimal; body-first shadows unless noted.

---

## drill_fault_serve_knee_lock
**targets:** fault_serve_knee_lock
**duration:** 60s
**setup:** Baseline or open space; racket optional.
**steps:**
1. Athlete holds athletic stance, racket face neutral.
2. 4 slow trophy poses with visible knee bend (2s hold each).
3. 4 continuous shadow serves emphasizing push from legs then upward drive.
**body focus:** Knee flexion at S_TROPHY_LOAD; extension before S_CONTACT_PROXY.
**sources:** USTA leg-drive serve progressions (B).

---

## drill_fault_serve_toss_arm_drift
**targets:** fault_serve_toss_arm_drift
**duration:** 75s
**setup:** No ball required.
**steps:**
1. 30s non-racket arm only: vertical lift to eye level, control descent.
2. 30s racket on hip; toss arm repeats while shoulders stay level.
3. 15s both arms — toss arm up as racket-side elbow lifts to trophy.
**body focus:** Non-racket wrist path stays near midline on front camera.
**sources:** ITF serve preparation drills summarized (B+).

## drill_fault_serve_rhythm_rush
**targets:** fault_serve_rhythm_rush, fault_double_fault_rhythm_rush
**duration:** 90s
**setup:** Phone metronome 50–60 BPM.
**steps:**
1. Beat 1: bounce/ready (S_READY).
2. Beat 2: trophy load (S_TROPHY_LOAD).
3. Beat 3: drive and contact proxy (S_DRIVE_UP → S_CONTACT_PROXY).
4. Repeat 10 cycles; last 30s remove metronome but keep same internal tempo.
**body focus:** Whole-chain duration and visible trophy window.
**sources:** ITF routine and rhythm coaching (B).

## drill_youth_serve_throw_progression
**targets:** fault_serve_knee_lock, fault_serve_short_finish_youth
**duration:** 60s
**audience:** youth
**setup:** Soft ball or no ball; throw motion emphasis legal for age group.
**steps:**
1. Throw imaginary ball overhand from trophy position — feet move naturally.
2. Link throw feeling to racket shadow without full adult knee depth.
**body focus:** Sequential leg → trunk → arm like throwing sports.
**sources:** USTA 10U serve progressions (B); Pitch Smart throwing parallels summarized (B, cross-sport).
