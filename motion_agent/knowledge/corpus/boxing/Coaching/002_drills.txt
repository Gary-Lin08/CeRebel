# Boxing Shadowboxing — Drills (30–90 s blocks, body-first)
# Each drill lists fault links, phase focus, equipment, youth note.
# No bag, mitts, or sparring — shadow form only.

================================================================================
STANCE & GUARD
================================================================================

drill_guard_march
  duration: 60 s
  phases: stance, guard, footwork
  faults: fault_hands_drop, fault_narrow_stance, fault_stiff_legs
  setup: Open floor space; optional mirror.
  steps: Athletic stance; march in place 20 s; add forward/back micro-steps 20 s; finish 20 s lateral shuffle without dropping hands.
  body_focus: Wrist height, base width, knee flex.
  youth: Shorter march; coach calls "freeze" in guard every 10 s.
  authority: B

drill_elbow_towel_guard
  duration: 45 s
  phases: guard
  faults: fault_elbows_flared
  setup: Small towel tucked under both elbows against ribs.
  steps: Hold guard while shifting weight lead-rear; towel must not fall. 3 rounds of 15 s.
  body_focus: Compact elbow position.
  youth: Use paper towel; less pressure.
  authority: B

drill_stance_reset_hop
  duration: 45 s
  phases: stance
  faults: fault_narrow_stance, fault_feet_crossed
  setup: Tape shoulder-width line on floor.
  steps: Light hop to land on line in orthodox/southpaw; guard up; 8 resets.
  body_focus: Ankle separation normalized to shoulder width.
  youth: Step reset instead of hop.
  authority: B

================================================================================
JAB & STRAIGHTS
================================================================================

drill_step_jab_shadow
  duration: 60 s
  phases: prep, extension, recoil
  faults: fault_reaching_jab, fault_slow_recoil, fault_rear_hand_drop_on_jab
  setup: Film oblique if possible.
  steps: Small lead step as jab peaks; recoil to guard before next rep. 10 jabs.
  body_focus: Step timing vs wrist peak; rear hand at cheek.
  youth: In-place jab only until step is stable.
  authority: B

drill_tunnel_jab_cross
  duration: 75 s
  phases: prep, extension, recoil, recovery
  faults: fault_elbow_flare_straight, fault_arm_only_punch, fault_combo_without_guard
  setup: Stand near wall for spatial reference (no contact required).
  steps: Slow jab-cross; pause 1 count in guard between pairs; 8 pairs at 50% speed.
  body_focus: Elbow path; hip turn on cross; guard reset.
  youth: Jab only for first week progression.
  authority: B

drill_pivot_cross_shadow
  duration: 75 s
  phases: prep, extension, footwork
  faults: fault_flat_rear_foot_cross, fault_arm_only_punch
  setup: Rear foot visible to camera if filming.
  steps: Emphasize rear heel lift on cross; 6 slow crosses each side (switch stance optional for southpaw practice).
  body_focus: Rear foot rotation proxy at E_ROTATION_PEAK.
  youth: Half-speed; fewer reps.
  authority: B+

================================================================================
HOOKS & UPPERCUTS
================================================================================

drill_hook_freeze_check
  duration: 60 s
  phases: extension, recoil
  faults: fault_hook_arm_extended, fault_wide_recoil
  setup: Mirror optional.
  steps: Lead hook to freeze at peak 1 s; verify bent elbow; recoil compact; 6 lead, 6 rear (advanced).
  body_focus: Elbow flexion at peak; recoil path.
  youth: Lead hook only; 4 reps.
  authority: B

drill_squat_uppercut_shadow
  duration: 45 s
  phases: prep, extension
  faults: fault_arm_only_uppercut
  setup: None.
  steps: Slight knee bend; extend legs as uppercut rises; slow tempo; 5 per hand.
  body_focus: Knee/hip extension timing with wrist rise.
  youth: Minimal depth; focus on sync not power.
  authority: B+

================================================================================
FOOTWORK & MOVEMENT
================================================================================

drill_short_step_ladder
  duration: 60 s
  phases: footwork, guard
  faults: fault_overstep, fault_feet_crossed
  setup: Two tape marks 30 cm apart.
  steps: Guard up; step lead foot mark to mark forward and back without crossing feet; 10 cycles.
  body_focus: Step length control.
  youth: Wider marks if balance needs help.
  authority: B

drill_pivot_circle
  duration: 90 s
  phases: footwork, stance
  faults: fault_lateral_leak_punch, fault_narrow_stance
  setup: Imagine circle diameter 1 m.
  steps: Lead foot pivots on circle; rear foot adjusts; guard maintained; 30 s each direction.
  body_focus: Base maintenance during pivot.
  youth: Larger circle; slower speed.
  authority: B

================================================================================
DEFENSE (SHADOW)
================================================================================

drill_slip_rope_imagined
  duration: 45 s
  phases: extension (slip)
  faults: fault_hands_drop_on_slip, fault_chin_up
  setup: None.
  steps: Slow slips L/R; hands stay at cheeks; 8 slips each side.
  body_focus: Head lateral displacement with wrist height stable.
  youth: Smaller slip amplitude.
  authority: B

drill_knee_soft_roll
  duration: 60 s
  phases: prep, extension (roll)
  faults: fault_stiff_roll
  setup: None.
  steps: Half-speed roll under imagined bar; knees visibly flex; 6 rolls each direction.
  body_focus: Knee flexion during trunk flexion.
  youth: Skip deep rolls; emphasize balance.
  authority: B

================================================================================
RHYTHM & COMBOS
================================================================================

drill_metronome_one_two
  duration: 90 s
  phases: full cycle
  faults: fault_combo_without_guard, fault_slow_recoil
  setup: Metronome 60–70 BPM.
  steps: Jab on beat 1, cross on beat 2, guard reset before next pair; 12 pairs.
  body_focus: E_GUARD_STABLE between peaks in beginner mode.
  youth: 50 BPM; single punches on alternate beats.
  authority: B

drill_breath_shadow_round
  duration: 90 s
  phases: guard, extension, recoil
  faults: fault_tense_shoulders, fault_hands_drop
  setup: Timer visible.
  steps: 90 s continuous shadow at 60% speed; inhale in guard, exhale on each punch; coach stops if hands drop.
  body_focus: Relaxation and guard discipline under light fatigue.
  youth: 45 s round; emphasize form stop early.
  authority: B

drill_belt_freeze_cross
  duration: 75 s
  phases: prep, extension
  faults: fault_incomplete_hip_rotation, fault_flat_rear_foot_cross, fault_arm_only_punch
  steps: Slow-mo cross; freeze at peak checking belt toward target and rear heel up; 8/side.
  authority: B+

drill_mirror_chin_nest
  duration: 60 s
  phases: guard
  faults: fault_guard_chin_gap, fault_chin_up, fault_hands_drop
  steps: Mirror guard holds with slow nod; chin nested, hands high; 5 holds.
  authority: B

drill_pocket_load_only
  duration: 45 s
  phases: prep, extension
  faults: fault_telegraph_windup, fault_shoulder_line_opens
  steps: Jab/cross leaving guard ≤15 cm then extend; partner flags big loops; 10 each.
  authority: B
