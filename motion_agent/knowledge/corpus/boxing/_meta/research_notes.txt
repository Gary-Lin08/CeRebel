1. NGB COACHING CONSENSUS — STANCE, GUARD, FOOTWORK (B)
================================================================================

Authority B | IBA Coach Manual 2019 (public PDF) + USA Boxing Grassroots Training Manual v01.1

Stance (orthodox and southpaw diagrams in IBA manual):
- Sideways-on ~45°; feet shoulder-width; weight balanced on both legs; knees slightly bent.
- Lead hand at eye level; chin down protected by lead shoulder; rear elbow near ribs; rear fist
  near chin; wrist aligned with forearm.
- Common faults flagged for coaching: feet too wide/narrow, flat heels, standing tall, chin up.

Footwork (both manuals):
- The foot closest to the direction of travel moves first; maintain shoulder-width and balance.
- Forward step: slight lead-foot lift → push from rear → lead toes land → rear foot slides up.
- Backward step: mirror with rear foot initiating retreat.

Teaching progression (USAB GTF / IBA aligned):
- Master punches in place before adding forward/back/side steps.
- Teach one punch with stance + movement before combinations.
- Emphasize body rotation on straight punches; rapid retraction along the same path to guard.

2. STRAIGHT PUNCH TECHNIQUE — COACHING FACTS (B, v1 PRODUCT FOCUS)
================================================================================

Lead straight (jab) and rear straight (cross) share NGB teaching themes:

Preparation / extension:
- Punching without trunk rotation is a listed common mistake (USAB GTF).
- Weight shift and hip/trunk rotation precede arm extension for rear-hand straight punches.
- Fist rotation (supination) and full extension with immediate retraction to guard.

Retraction faults (coaching-visible):
- Slow or low retraction; hand drifting sideways; head leaning; chin unprotected.

CeRebel phase mapping (internal B): B_GUARD → B_PREP → B_EXTEND (wrist speed peak proxy) →
B_RETRACT → B_RESET — no impact/contact required.

3. BIOMECHANICS — KINETIC CHAIN & PUNCH TYPES (B+)
================================================================================

3A. Proximal-to-distal sequencing (open kinetic chain)

Authority B+ | Liu et al. 2022, Frontiers in Physiology (PMC9798280, CC BY 4.0)
- Lead straight punch: torso → arm segments accelerate and brake sequentially; distal hand
  velocity and momentum depend on timing of segment deceleration.
- Elite vs junior: higher peak/contact punch velocity; elite show greater lead-leg peak force,
  impulse, and rate of force development (RFD) — rear-leg indices less discriminating in this cohort.
- Training implication (research wording, not medical prescription): emphasize sequential
  acceleration–braking and lead-leg explosive strength for lead straight performance.

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9798280/

3B. Cross / hook / uppercut — skill level differences (future pack extension)

Authority B+ | Dinu & Louis 2020 (PMC7739747, CC BY 4.0)
- Elite boxers produce higher force and velocity for cross, hook, uppercut vs juniors.
- Juniors show higher relative shoulder contribution; elites use more integrated trunk/pelvis
  timing (especially on cross).
- Cross: front vs rear foot GRF split ~55/45 (both groups) — weight transfer pattern for rear-hand
  straight useful for cross coaching heuristics in orthodox athletes.

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7739747/

3C. Lower limbs, GRF, fatigue

Authority B+ | Stewart et al. 2025 (PMC12729554 — verify license on PMC record)
- Amateur boxers: cross among highest punch force, GRF, and RFD pre-fatigue.
- Lower-body fatigue circuit reduced punch force (~4% mean; larger drops in weaker punch types).
- Supports product copy linking stable base + leg drive to sustainable shadowboxing form (not
  conditioning prescriptions without coach oversight).

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729554/

3D. Effective mass and technique vs body size

Authority B+ | Kacprzak et al. 2025, Applied Sciences (CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.3390/app15074008)
- Effective mass (peak force / fist acceleration at impact) higher for straight punches (jab, cross)
  than hooks despite hooks sometimes showing higher peak force.
- Body composition and years training did not correlate with effective mass — technique and
  impulse–acceleration timing dominate.
- Product: avoid “heavier athlete = better punch” simplifications; cue rotation and linear mechanics.

URL: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/7/4008

3E. Amateur 3D punch descriptors (lab bag work)

Authority B+ | Stanley et al. 2018 (DOI 10.1080/24748668.2018.1525651; NC-ND on Chester REP)
- Six punch types on bag: straight vs hook vs uppercut differ in fist velocity, delivery time,
  shoulder/elbow angular velocities.
- Jab: shortest delivery time (~405 ms in reported cohort); lead hook highest peak fist velocity
  (~11.95 m/s) in that study.
- Jab-only significant lead vs rear GRF asymmetry among punches tested.
- Use as population descriptors for punch-family taxonomy; do NOT publish as universal targets.

URLs: https://doi.org/10.1080/24748668.2018.1525651
       https://chesterrep.openrepository.com/handle/10034/621889

3F. Measurement methods

Authority B+ | Dunn et al. 2019 Translational Sports Medicine (Wiley — summarize only)
- Punch integrator protocol for peak force, impulse, RFD reliability — informs future “quality”
  checkpoints if external sensors added; not required for body-only v1.

URL: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/tsm2.71

4. SAFETY, RULES, AND HEAD PROTECTION (A+ / A) — NOT HARM CONTENT
================================================================================

Guard mechanics (B) directly support safety: chin down, hands high, elbows in — consistent across
IBA/USAB coaching manuals.

Federation rules (A+):
- USA Boxing National Rule Book (2026 effective via hub): medical clearance, fouls, headgear/glove
  rules, stop scenarios — glossary + disclaimer support only.
- IBA Technical & Competition Rules Apr 2026: medical and safety requirements cannot be weakened
  at national level.

URL (USAB): https://www.usaboxing.org/usa-boxing-rulebook
URL (IBA): https://www.iba.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260415-IBA-Technical-Competition-Rules.pdf

Head impact biomechanics (A — clinical awareness ONLY):

Authority A | Walilko et al. 2005 BJSM (PMC1725037)
- Laboratory gloved straight punches to instrumented head form: reports translational/rotational
  head acceleration scales with effective mass and impact velocity.
- CeRebel use: reinforce why NGBs teach compact guard and neck stability; NEVER frame as
  “how to maximize head injury,” knockout strategy, or illegal striking.

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1725037/

Concussion / return-to-sport: out of pack scope — redirect to licensed medical providers and
  federation medical rules (brain_plan exclusion).
