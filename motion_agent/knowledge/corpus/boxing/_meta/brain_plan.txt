================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for **technique coaching** in boxing fundamentals:
orthodox/southpaw **stance**, **guard**, **jab**, **cross**, **footwork**, and
**shadowboxing form** (solo movement quality). Emphasis on balance, segment
sequencing, retraction, and safe deceleration — not combat outcomes.

In scope v1:
- Straight punches (lead jab, rear cross) as body patterns
- Stance width, weight distribution, guard hand position (body-visible)
- Basic footwork: step-in, step-out, pivot on lead foot for cross (coaching heuristics)
- Shadowboxing rhythm: guard → prep → extension proxy → retract → reset

Out of scope (do NOT author in this pack):
- Fight tactics, targeting vulnerable areas, hurting opponents, sparring strategy
- Clinch, illegal blows, referee rules except brief glossary for RAG disambiguation
- Medical diagnosis, concussion return-to-sport (redirect to licensed providers)
- Heavy bag / pad timing that requires equipment contact detection
- Advanced combinations beyond jab–cross form chains (future extension only as glossary stubs)

Build agent writes full Findings/Coaching bodies from this map; brain agent does not.

2. PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

- All measurable claims tie to reconstructed body: joint angles, segment speeds,
  COM over base, hand height relative to head/shoulders.
- No requirement for gloves, mitts, opponent silhouette, or impact events.
- **Extension proxy** = argmax ||v(striking_hand_wrist)|| in punch window (not contact).
- Youth vs adult: separate audience bands for volume/load language; no adult power
  cues for youth shadowboxing.
- Authority: B+ / B for NGB coaching consensus; A/A+ only for clinical load/volume
  when cited in Biomechanics; no C in findings thresholds.
- Coaching tone: fitness and skill development; never instruct how to inflict harm.

3. MOTION FAMILIES (TOP-LEVEL)
================================================================================

1. STANCE_GUARD — static or micro-movement ready position
2. JAB — lead-hand straight extension from guard (orthodox: left jab default copy)
3. CROSS — rear-hand straight with hip/trunk rotation (orthodox: right cross default)
4. FOOTWORK — stepping and pivot patterns between punches (shadowboxing travel)
5. SHADOW_ROUND — repeating guard–punch–reset cycles without equipment (future combo tags)

Handedness: **lead** = jab side; **rear** = cross side. Default corpus labels
orthodox (RH lead = left). Southpaw: mirror lateral language in product logic.

4. PHASE MODEL (5–8 PHASES, BODY-ONLY EVENTS)
================================================================================

Shared punch chain (jab and cross reuse IDs; family tag distinguishes copy):

| phase_id   | label_en        | label_zh (build) | role |
|------------|-----------------|------------------|------|
| B_GUARD    | Guard           | 抱架/预备        | Hands high; stable base; minimal COM drift |
| B_PREP     | Preparation     | 蓄力/转髋准备    | Weight shift, hip/trunk coil before extension |
| B_EXTEND   | Extension       | 出拳/伸展        | Striking wrist speed peak (extension proxy) |

Footwork overlay (optional sub-chain between resets):

| phase_id   | label_en        | role |
|------------|-----------------|------|
| F_PLANT    | Base plant      | Both feet under hips; no crossed stance |
| F_STEP     | Step            | Lead or trail ankle displacement toward/away |
| F_PIVOT    | Pivot           | Trail foot rotates on ball for cross power (heuristic) |

Shadowboxing clip (typical 3–8 s): B_GUARD → (F_STEP optional) → B_PREP → B_EXTEND →
B_RETRACT → B_RESET → B_GUARD …

Event definitions (OpenSim-friendly, no opponent):

- extend_event: argmax ||v(striking_wrist)|| in analysis window.
- prep_start: local min striking-wrist speed after guard + rising hip rotation rate (future).
- retract_end: striking wrist returns within guard volume proxy (height between chin and brow band).
- guard_stable: both wrists above elbow height proxy and COM within base (heuristic).

================================================================================
6. COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED — NOT MEDICAL ADVICE)
================================================================================

Wording for build: "typical in coaching literature" / "common teaching emphasis".
Do not prescribe degrees as injury prevention guarantees.

ADULT (fitness / amateur boxing fundamentals; B+ coaching consensus unless noted):

- Guard: both hands between chin and forehead band; elbows in (not flared wide).
- Jab: extension from shoulder; full retract before next punch; rear hand stays home.
- Cross: hip and trunk rotate before or with rear shoulder; weight transfers through
  mid-foot; retract rear hand to guard.
- Stance: feet shoulder-width or slightly wider; knees soft (~15–30° flex heuristic);
  weight balanced or slightly forward on balls of feet — not rigid upright.
- Footwork: avoid crossing feet; step length proportional to leg length (normalize).

YOUTH (non-contact shadowboxing emphasis; B / B+):

- Shorter steps; emphasis on form and control over speed.
- No cueing "hit harder" or power-max language; use "sharp extension" / "snap back".
- Volume caps: reference general youth PA guidelines in Training/ — not fight prep.
