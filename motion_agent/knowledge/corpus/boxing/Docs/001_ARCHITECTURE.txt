# Boxing Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first boxing technique coaching** focused on
**shadowboxing form** in CeRebel. The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and
explains what the athlete’s body is doing across recognizable strike and movement phases. Punch impact on pads,
bags, or opponents; scoring; and fight strategy are optional enrichments, not prerequisites for coaching briefs
or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Shadowboxing**: stance, guard, footwork, and fundamental punches thrown to empty space (jab, cross, hooks,
  uppercuts) plus basic head-and-torso defensive shapes (slip, roll, pull) performed without contact.
- **Orthodox and southpaw** stances; lead vs rear hand naming follows stance tag.
- Single-camera or multi-camera body capture; no gloves or target markers required for core findings.

Out of scope for automatic findings (may appear as glossary only):
- Heavy bag, mitts, double-end bag, speed bag technique and load management.
- Sparring, clinch, inside fighting, feints against a reacting partner.
- Knockout power metrics, punch force, glove weight rules, amateur/pro competition rulesets.
- Medical concussion protocols beyond general safety pointers in disclaimer docs.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, timing between events,
   weight shift proxies, trunk rotation visible without a target or opponent.
2. **Evidence layering**: peer-reviewed striking biomechanics and boxing kinematics summaries (B+) inform
   relationships (hip–shoulder sequencing, ground reaction concepts in shadow); national boxing and coach-education
   progressions (B) inform practice structure; cues and drills are coaching advice (B), always paired with
   disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric angle “targets” are avoided as prescriptive medicine; ranges are
   described qualitatively or attributed to published summaries with youth/adult separation where relevant.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows per strike or per defensive cycle.
5. **Stance fork**: orthodox uses `stance=orthodox`; southpaw uses `stance=southpaw` — mirror lead/rear joint
   naming in all signals.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Required joints for boxing-likeness scoring (future `boxing_score.py`): both wrists, both elbows, both shoulders,
  pelvis/COM proxy, both ankles, head/neck proxy.
- Stance metadata selects lead vs rear side (default orthodox facing camera at ~45°: lead = left for jab hand).

### Boxing-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings, a **soft** boxing-likeness score may use:
- **Guard height**: both hands above waist proxy for ≥30% of clip when not in extension.
- **Hand speed bursts**: alternating or sequential wrist speed peaks consistent with shadow combinations.
- **Stance width**: ankles not collapsed to walking stride; moderate knee flex baseline.

Score alone does **not** authorize medical advice; it only gates sport-mode selection and reduces false positives
from dance or generic arm-waving clips.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to these computable proxies per **strike cycle**:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_GUARD_STABLE | low wrist speed; hands near guard zone | Between strikes |
| E_PREP_START | lead or rear wrist begins moving away from guard with same-side hip rotation onset | Chamber / step-in |
| E_EXTENSION_PEAK | argmax \|\|v(striking_wrist)\|\| in cycle window | Primary timing anchor |
| E_RECOIL_START | wrist speed drops after peak; elbow flex increases | Return path begins |
| E_GUARD_RETURN | wrists re-enter guard height band | End of cycle |
| E_SLIP_PEAK | lateral head displacement local max with low hand speed | Defense-only clips |
| E_STEP_LAND | lead ankle vertical velocity zero-crossing after foot advance | Footwork segments |

Strike type tagging (metadata, not separate phase lists): `jab | cross | hook | uppercut | slip | roll | step`.

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.

- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time (manifest-driven).
- Tag chunks with `stance=orthodox|southpaw` and `strike_type` where mechanics differ.
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` or raw manifest CSV as coaching truth — use as ops only.

## Youth vs adult

Growing athletes should emphasize **technique volume at moderate intensity** in shadow work before heavy bag
loading. Head trauma risk applies to sparring, not shadowboxing; still teach neutral neck alignment and avoid
max-effort wild swings without supervision.

Absolute reach, guard width, and hip rotation amplitude differ with limb length and mobility; avoid a single
“model fighter” degree template in product copy.
