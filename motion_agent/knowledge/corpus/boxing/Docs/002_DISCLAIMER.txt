# Boxing Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel boxing content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and coach-education
progressions**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, neurologic
evaluation, or individualized return-to-sport protocols after concussion, hand, wrist, elbow, shoulder, or neck
injury.

- Pain, dizziness, headache, vision changes, numbness, or joint instability during shadowboxing or any training
  require evaluation by a qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to “train through.”
- Youth athletes need age-appropriate supervision, shorter rounds, and emphasis on form over speed; follow your
  national boxing federation, school, or club policies on contact and sparring (this pack addresses **non-contact
  shadow form only**).

## Not a combat or scoring tool

This pack does **not** judge fight outcomes, judge round scoring, certify amateur/pro eligibility, or replace
certified coaching credentials. Shadowboxing analysis cannot measure effective punch power against an opponent.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Wrist and elbow angles when the punching arm is foreshortened toward or away from the camera.
- Trunk rotation in the transverse plane when the athlete faces the camera squarely or at steep angles.
- Head slip distance when hair, hood, or motion blur occlude the head proxy.
- Foot pivot and hip turn when legs overlap or shorts occlude knees.

Findings describe **visible tendencies** in shadow form, not laboratory-grade punch kinematics with force plates.
Confidence should be lower when joints are occluded, frame rate is below ~30 fps for fast combinations, or the
clip mixes unrelated exercise (burpees, dance) with brief arm swings.

## No guaranteed performance outcomes

Mechanics cues may support movement patterns discussed in research and coaching literature, but **ring
performance, power on pads, and injury risk depend on many factors** (strength, fatigue, prior injury, sleep,
contact load, etc.). Do not promise knockouts or ranking improvements from a single shadow drill.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Open literature reports **ranges and group averages** across skill levels and punch types. Stature, stance width
preference, and mobility all change appearance. This pack avoids presenting arbitrary degree targets as universal
rules, especially for youth.

## Head and neck

Shadowboxing includes optional defensive head motion. Athletes with cervical spine conditions or recent concussion
should follow clinician guidance before aggressive slip/roll volume. Automated findings on head position are
**coaching hints**, not clinical diagnosis.

## Hand and wrist load

This v1 pack excludes heavy bag and mitt impact. Shadow work is low external load, but repeated maximal-speed
flailing can still irritate shoulders or elbows — stop if pain occurs.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, parents, and athletes who already have a training plan. They do not replace
in-person supervision, especially for beginners learning stance and guard for the first time.

## Emergency

For acute injury during training, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when
needed. Suspected concussion requires removal from contact training and medical evaluation — shadow-only practice
does not eliminate concussion history considerations for return to sparring elsewhere.

## Product behavior

- Boxing pack features may be gated as “coming_soon” until detector validation completes — absence of a finding
  is not proof of safe or optimal mechanics.
- Mislabeled stance (orthodox vs southpaw) may invert lead/rear findings; users should confirm stance tag.

## Authority and updates

Sources are summarized in coaching and biomechanics docs with authority levels (B/B+). Federation syllabi change;
retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official coach-education source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Shadowboxing tips from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. This is not sparring or fight coaching.”
