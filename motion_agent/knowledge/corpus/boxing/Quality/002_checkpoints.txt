# Boxing Shadowboxing — Checkpoint Catalog
# Format: checkpoint_id | phase | stance | body signal | pass heuristic | fail → finding | authority

================================================================================
STANCE / BASE
================================================================================

checkpoint_stance_width
  phase: stance
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: medial-lateral ankle separation normalized by shoulder width
  pass: athletic base — roughly shoulder-width to slightly wider; rear heel light but not fully crossed over lead
  fail_low: ankles closer than ~0.65× shoulder width with unstable sway → fault_narrow_stance
  fail_cross: lead foot crosses past rear midline during prep → fault_feet_crossed
  authority: B (coaching consensus)
  youth: slightly wider base may pass if knees soft and guard stable

checkpoint_knee_flex_ready
  phase: stance, guard
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: average knee flexion angle
  pass: visible soft flex — not locked straight, not deep squat
  fail: knees locked straight with upright rigid posture → fault_stiff_legs
  authority: B

checkpoint_weight_centered
  phase: stance
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: COM horizontal position between ankle markers
  pass: COM within central third of base; slight lead bias OK for orthodox jab setups
  fail: COM over lead toe with rear heel high before any step → fault_over_lead_weight
  authority: B

================================================================================
GUARD
================================================================================

checkpoint_hands_high
  phase: guard, recovery
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: wrist height vs chin and sternum proxies
  pass: both wrists at or above sternum; lead hand near cheek line in front view when visible
  fail: either wrist below navel proxy between cycles → fault_hands_drop
  authority: B

checkpoint_elbows_in
  phase: guard
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: elbow abduction angle from trunk in frontal/oblique view
  pass: elbows within body silhouette — not flared wide like a T
  fail: elbows markedly abducted with open ribs → fault_elbows_flared
  authority: B

checkpoint_chin_down
  phase: guard, extension
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: head pitch proxy (chin toward sternum)
  pass: slight tuck — not staring at ceiling during extension
  fail: head tilted up exposing neck line during strike peak → fault_chin_up
  authority: B+ (safety coaching consensus)

================================================================================
PREP / WEIGHT TRANSFER
================================================================================

checkpoint_hip_before_hand
  phase: prep
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: timing of pelvis yaw rate vs striking wrist speed onset (rear hand and hooks)
  pass: hip rotation or weight shift begins within ~80 ms (frame-adjusted) of hand leave guard on power punches
  fail: E_EXTENSION_PEAK with no pelvis yaw and no weight shift on cross/hook → fault_arm_only_punch
  authority: B+

checkpoint_jab_step_optional
  phase: prep, footwork
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: lead foot advance before lead wrist peak on jab
  pass: either in-place jab with small weight shift OR step lands before or at extension peak (style-dependent)
  fail: large lean without foot adjustment losing base → fault_reaching_jab
  authority: B

checkpoint_shoulder_relax
  phase: prep
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: lead shoulder elevation vs guard (hunching)
  pass: shoulders not pinned to ears before strike
  fail: exaggerated shrug before extension → fault_tense_shoulders
  authority: B

================================================================================
EXTENSION / STRIKE
================================================================================

checkpoint_elbow_track_straight
  phase: extension
  strike_type: jab, cross
  signal: elbow path relative to wrist — frontal/oblique
  pass: elbow travels roughly with wrist; no large lateral chicken-wing at full extension
  fail: elbow flares outward past trunk line at peak → fault_elbow_flare_straight
  authority: B

checkpoint_hook_elbow_angle
  phase: extension
  strike_type: hook
  signal: elbow flexion at E_EXTENSION_PEAK
  pass: bent elbow hook shape — not fully extended slap
  fail: hook thrown with near-straight arm → fault_hook_arm_extended
  authority: B

checkpoint_uppercut_path
  phase: extension
  strike_type: uppercut
  signal: wrist vertical rise from loaded hips
  pass: hand rises from hip/knee line with bent elbow; shoulder not fully internally rotated into neck
  fail: arm-only upward swing without knee/hip extension hint → fault_arm_only_uppercut
  authority: B+

checkpoint_non_striking_hand_home
  phase: extension
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: non-striking wrist remains in guard zone during single-shot focus reps
  pass: rear hand stays at cheek when jab extends (beginner model); advanced may slip rear hand slightly — flag low severity only if guard opens toward target
  fail: rear hand drops to waist during jab peak → fault_rear_hand_drop_on_jab
  authority: B

================================================================================
RECOIL / RECOVERY
================================================================================

checkpoint_recoil_speed
  phase: recoil
  stance: orthodox, southpaw
  signal: time from E_EXTENSION_PEAK to wrist back inside guard band
  pass: retraction within ~300 ms at 60 fps for beginner shadow (scale with fps)
  fail: hand lingers extended >500 ms before return → fault_slow_recoil
  authority: B

checkpoint_pull_not_push_return
  phase: recoil
  signal: elbow flexion increases as wrist speed toward guard
  pass: hand draws back along compact path
  fail: wide looping return path past ear → fault_wide_recoil
  authority: B

checkpoint_guard_reset_between
  phase: recovery
  signal: E_GUARD_STABLE achieved before next E_PREP_START in combo drills
  pass: at least 2 frames stable guard between distinct peaks in beginner combos
  fail: next punch starts from waist level without reset → fault_combo_without_guard
  authority: B

================================================================================
FOOTWORK
================================================================================

checkpoint_step_size_control
  phase: footwork
  signal: lead ankle displacement per step
  pass: short adjusting steps — not lunging walk
  fail: step length > ~1.2× shoulder width with rear foot drag → fault_overstep
  authority: B

checkpoint_pivot_rear_heel
  phase: footwork, prep (cross/hook)
  signal: rear foot rotation on cross (orthodox)
  pass: rear heel lifts or pivots allowing hip turn on power straight
  fail: planted flat rear foot with forced trunk twist only → fault_flat_rear_foot_cross
  authority: B+

================================================================================
DEFENSE (SHADOW)
================================================================================

checkpoint_slip_without_hands_drop
  phase: extension (slip variant)
  signal: E_SLIP_PEAK with wrist heights
  pass: hands remain at guard while head displaces laterally
  fail: both hands drop during slip → fault_hands_drop_on_slip
  authority: B

checkpoint_roll_spine_flex
  phase: prep, extension (roll variant)
  signal: trunk flexion with lateral head offset
  pass: knees flex; roll is curved not stiff waist bend only
  fail: legs straight with excessive neck flexion only → fault_stiff_roll
  authority: B

================================================================================
CAPTURE / METADATA
================================================================================

checkpoint_stance_tag_match
  phase: all
  signal: lead hand consistency with stance metadata
  pass: orthodox → left wrist leads jab cycles when identifiable
  fail: metadata mismatch → suppress handed findings; flag product warning only
  authority: ProductLogic

All checkpoints: prefer **unclear** over fail when camera angle invalidates signal (see Docs/001_ARCHITECTURE).
