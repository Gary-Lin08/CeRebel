# Boxing Shadowboxing — Quality Definition (body-first)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel

For this pack, **shadow form quality** is not fight effectiveness or pad power. It is the degree to which visible
body mechanics align with patterns associated in open literature and reputable coaching with:

1. **Stable athletic base** — balanced stance width, knees softly flexed, weight ready to shift without crossing feet.
2. **Guard discipline** — hands return to a protective zone between strikes; head stays relatively neutral when not
   slipping by design.
3. **Kinetic sequencing** — lower-body and trunk contribution visible before or with hand acceleration on rear-hand
   and power punches; jab may be lighter but still connected to weight shift.
4. **Controlled extension and recoil** — full but not hyperextended elbows on straights; hands retract along tight
   paths after E_EXTENSION_PEAK.
5. **Rhythm and repeatability** — similar event ordering (prep → peak → guard return) across reps in a clip when
   comparing cycles.

Quality scores in product code (future) should combine checkpoint pass rate, phase confidence, stance tag correctness,
and capture quality (occlusion flags) — not a single “pro template” silhouette.

## Dimensions

| dimension_id       | plain label                 | primary phases        |
|--------------------|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| base_stability     | Stance and balance          | stance, footwork       |
| guard_discipline   | Hands home between strikes  | guard, recovery        |
| weight_transfer    | Shift with the punch        | prep, extension        |
| trunk_rotation     | Hip-shoulder line on power  | prep, extension, drive |
| extension_control  | Reach without winging       | extension, drive       |
| recoil_habit       | Retract after peak          | recoil, recovery       |
| head_neutral       | Chin not floating up        | guard, extension       |
| footwork_order     | Step then punch or clear reset | footwork, prep      |

Each dimension maps to checkpoints in `002_checkpoints.txt`. A cycle can be “high guard_discipline, moderate
trunk_rotation” — product copy should avoid single-number grades without dimension breakdown.

## Evidence basis

- Biomechanics of punching and martial-arts striking kinematics (B+): role of lower limb and trunk in hand speed.
- Boxing coach-education syllabi and amateur boxing skill frameworks (B): guard, foot placement, shadow rounds.
- Clinical consensus on neck loading (A): avoid extreme end-range head postures during high-volume shadow — referenced
  for head_neutral severity, not individual injury prediction.

## Youth vs adult quality framing

**Youth (approx. ≤14U):** prioritize guard return, short combinations (2–3 punches), and slow tempo over maximum hand
speed or deep slips. Stance may be slightly wider for balance; that is not automatically a fault.

**Adult / post-puberty:** same dimensions; allow greater rotation amplitude and faster combos without labeling
“excessive” unless combined with loss of guard or base (e.g., feet crossed, hands down between strikes).

Never tell youth to mimic elite professional combination speed or deep head movement volume without coach supervision.

## Capture quality gates

Downgrade or suppress findings when:

- Punching arm parallel to image plane (foreshortening).
- Frame rate < 24 fps for fast double combinations (misses E_EXTENSION_PEAK ordering).
- Feet or hips occluded during rotation.
- Clip labeled `stance=southpaw` but lead hand inconsistent (tag mismatch).

## Relationship to findings

Checkpoints **pass/fail/unclear** at phase windows. Findings fire when checkpoints fail with sufficient confidence
or when fault-specific rules in Findings/001 trigger. Quality definition does not duplicate fault headlines — it
states the positive model.
