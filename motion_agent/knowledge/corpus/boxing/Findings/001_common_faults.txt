# Boxing Shadowboxing — Common Faults (finding cards)
# Canonical v2. Scope: NON-CONTACT shadow form only — no sparring or partner contact.
# Guard/chin language is posture and habit only — not concussion protection. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.

---

finding_id: fault_hands_drop
headline_en: Hands fall below guard between or during strikes
phases: guard, recovery, extension
body_signal: Either wrist below navel height proxy for ≥4 frames between E_EXTENSION_PEAK events or during slip when hands should stay high.
why_it_matters: Open guard habits in shadow often carry into mitt work; coaching syllabi emphasize consistent guard (performance habit — not injury prediction).
feel_cue: "Cheek to cheek — both phones ringing, keep them at your face."
drill: 60s guard march: small steps, hands at cheeks; coach calls random numbers; athlete taps own cheek without dropping hands.
severity_default: medium
sources: Amateur boxing coach education guard modules (B)
youth_note: Guard and chin habits; reset between punches — no power-volume prescriptions.

finding_id: fault_arm_only_punch
headline_en: Cross or hook fires from the shoulder without hip or weight shift
phases: prep, extension
body_signal: E_EXTENSION_PEAK on rear hand with pelvis yaw rate near zero and COM lateral shift below threshold vs prep window.
why_it_matters: Biomechanics literature links lower-limb and trunk drive to hand speed; arm-only shadow patterns limit sequencing transfer (performance).
feel_cue: "Push the floor with the ball of your back foot — let the hip start the punch."
drill: 75s shadow cross slow-mo: 50% speed, freeze at hip turn then hand; 8 reps each side filmed oblique.
severity_default: medium
sources: Punching kinematics reviews (B+); coach-education sequencing (B)
youth_note: Guard and chin habits; reset between punches — no power-volume prescriptions.

feel_cue: "Elbow rides the lane — punch through a narrow tunnel in front of your chest."

severity_default: medium
sources: Boxing fundamentals manuals summarized (B); straight punch kinematics (B+)
youth_note: Guard and chin habits; reset between punches — no power-volume prescriptions.

feel_cue: "Bend the phone — hook around the corner at ninety, not a windmill."
