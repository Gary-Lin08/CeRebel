# Boxing Sport Pack — Ontology (Schema)

## Entity: Boxer

Attributes:
- boxer_id (string, optional external)
- stance: orthodox | southpaw
- audience_band: youth | adult | unknown

Relations:
- performs → PunchInstance | ShadowSession
- subject_of → FindingInstance

## Entity: PunchType

Enum:
- jab (lead straight)
- cross (rear straight)
- footwork_step (non-contact travel)
- shadow_round (repeated form cycle)

Attributes:
- punch_type_id (stable slug)
- striking_hand: lead | rear (for jab/cross)

## Entity: BoxingPhase

Attributes:
- phase_id (e.g., B_GUARD, B_EXTEND, F_STEP)
- phase_family: punch | footwork | guard
- ordinal (integer order within punch chain)
- description_plain (English)

Relations:
- follows → BoxingPhase
- contains_events → PhaseEvent

## Entity: PhaseEvent

Attributes:
- event_id
- start_rule (narrative, body-only)
- end_rule (narrative)
- detector_proxy (optional engineering string)

Examples:
- extend_peak_wrist — argmax striking-hand wrist speed
- guard_stable — wrists in guard band, COM over base
- retract_complete — striking wrist returns toward guard volume

## Entity: Finding

Attributes:
- finding_id (slug, prefix fault_)
- headline (plain language)
- phase_id (primary)
- punch_type optional (jab | cross | any)
- severity_hint: info | form | balance | load
- body_signals (list of measurable descriptions)
- why_it_matters (balance, efficiency, joint load — non-diagnostic, non-combat)
- authority_level: B+ | B typical
- evidence_type: CoachingConsensus | BiomechRelationship

Relations:
- has_cue → Cue
- has_drill → Drill

## Entity: Cue

Attributes:
- cue_id
- finding_id (foreign)
- feel_text (athlete-facing, body-first)
- coach_text (optional longer)

## Entity: Drill

Attributes:
- drill_id
- finding_id
- duration_sec (30–90 typical)
- setup (mirror, clear floor space; no partner striking)
- instructions (ordered steps)
- youth_variant (optional shorter/slower copy)

## Entity: Checkpoint

Attributes:
- checkpoint_id
- phase_id
- series_id (from brain_plan measurement surface)
- quality_dimension (from Quality/001)
- pass_description (body observable)
- fail_links → Finding

## Entity: LoadConcern

Attributes:
- concern_id
- description (volume, repetition speed — not fighting)
- audience_band
- authority_level A | A+ when citing PA or sports medicine summaries

Not a Finding — used in Training/ disclaimers.

## Entity: EvidenceChunk (RAG metadata pattern)

Attributes:
- source_id (from SOURCE_MANIFEST)
- authority_level
- evidence_type
- retrieved_at
- audience_band optional

## Naming conventions

- Punch phases: prefix B_
- Footwork phases: prefix F_
- Finding IDs: prefix fault_
- Cue IDs: cue_<finding_id>
- Drill IDs: drill_<finding_id>

## Handedness mapping (default orthodox)

Jab: lead hand = left wrist for orthodox; cross: rear hand = right wrist.
Southpaw: mirror lead/rear in product logic; corpus may say "lead hand" / "rear hand".

## Out-of-ontology (glossary only, not coached as tactics)

- Opponent, ring generalship, knockdown, illegal blow — disambiguation for RAG only;
  no Finding or Drill entities tied to harming others.
