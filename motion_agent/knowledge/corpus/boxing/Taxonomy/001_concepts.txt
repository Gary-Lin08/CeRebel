# Boxing Sport Pack — Concept taxonomy
# Scope: stance, guard, jab, cross, footwork, shadowboxing form — technique only

## Core concepts (with synonyms for RAG)

concept_id: punch_jab
label: Jab
synonyms: lead hand straight, front hand punch, 1, stiff jab, probing jab (form only)
punch_type: jab
striking_hand: lead
notes: Body-first uses lead wrist speed peak and retraction — not target or impact.

concept_id: punch_cross
label: Cross (straight rear hand)
synonyms: straight right (orthodox), rear hand straight, 2, back hand punch
punch_type: cross
striking_hand: rear
notes: Hip/trunk rotation before/at extension proxy; not power targeting language.

concept_id: stance_orthodox
label: Orthodox stance
synonyms: right-handed stance, left foot forward, left lead
stance: orthodox

concept_id: stance_southpaw
label: Southpaw stance
synonyms: left-handed stance, right foot forward, right lead
stance: southpaw

concept_id: guard_boxing
label: Boxing guard
synonyms: hands up, high guard, on-guard, defensive posture, protect chin (posture cue)
maps_to: B_GUARD
notes: Coaching posture only — not sparring tactics or counter-punching.

concept_id: shadowboxing
label: Shadowboxing
synonyms: shadow boxing, air work, form rounds, solo boxing, mirror work
motion_family: SHADOW_ROUND
notes: Solo repetition of stance, footwork, jab, cross, reset — no opponent.

concept_id: footwork_step_in
label: Step in
synonyms: advance, forward step, closing distance (form), push step
maps_to: F_STEP
notes: Balance and base width — not closing on an opponent.

concept_id: footwork_step_out
label: Step out
synonyms: retreat step, pull back, create space (form)
maps_to: F_STEP

concept_id: footwork_pivot
label: Pivot
synonyms: rear foot pivot, ball of foot turn, hip turn setup
maps_to: F_PIVOT
links: fault_footwork_heel_lift

concept_id: phase_extension
label: Punch extension
synonyms: reach, snap, full extension, straight punch out
maps_to: B_EXTEND

concept_id: phase_retraction
label: Retraction / bring hand back
synonyms: recoil, snap back, return to guard, hand home
maps_to: B_RETRACT
links: fault_jab_no_retract, fault_cross_no_retract

concept_id: quality_hip_drive
label: Hip drive on cross
synonyms: rotate hips, sit down on punch, trunk rotation, torque from core
maps_to: B_PREP, B_EXTEND
authority: B+ coaching theme

concept_id: quality_guard_discipline
label: Guard discipline
synonyms: keep rear hand up, don't drop hands, protect on jab
maps_to: B_GUARD, B_EXTEND
links: fault_jab_rear_hand_drop, fault_guard_low_hands

concept_id: quality_base_balance
label: Base and balance
synonyms: stay balanced, don't fall in, stable platform, athletic base
maps_to: B_GUARD, F_PLANT, F_STEP
links: fault_balance_sway, fault_stance_narrow

concept_id: fault_dropped_rear_hand
label: Rear hand drops
synonyms: lazy rear hand, right hand down on jab (orthodox), open guard
links: fault_jab_rear_hand_drop

concept_id: fault_all_arm_punch
label: Arm-only punch
synonyms: no hip, punching with arm only, stiff cross, no rotation
links: fault_cross_all_arm, fault_cross_no_hip

concept_id: body_lead_wrist
label: Lead wrist
synonyms: jab hand, front hand wrist, left wrist (orthodox)
measure: speed peak at B_EXTEND, path straightness proxy

concept_id: body_rear_wrist
label: Rear wrist
synonyms: cross hand, back hand wrist, right wrist (orthodox)
measure: speed peak at B_EXTEND, height during opponent-less jab phase

concept_id: body_hip_rotation
label: Hip / pelvis rotation
synonyms: hip turn, core rotation, trunk twist
measure: yaw rate proxy B_PREP → B_EXTEND

concept_id: body_stance_width
label: Stance width
synonyms: foot spacing, base width, shoulder-width stance
measure: ankle separation normalized to stature

concept_id: youth_shadowboxing
label: Youth non-contact boxing fitness
synonyms: kids boxing fitness, youth form, non-contact striking fitness
audience: youth
authority: B
notes: Form and movement quality; no competition fight prep in product KB.
