# Human (General Motion) Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **sport-agnostic body coaching** when CeRebel does not
confidently match a specialized sport pack (running, baseball, golf, etc.). The product reconstructs human
motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints or OpenSim-derived kinematics) and explains how the person organizes
head, ribs, pelvis, limbs, and center of mass through simple movement phases.

Corpus pack id: **human**. Product registry id: **general** (`HumanMotionPack`).

Primary motion scope (v1):
- Standing, stepping, reaching, squatting, lunging, jumping prep, throwing prep, and mixed daily-athletic
  clips where activity type is unknown or mixed.
- Clips of roughly 2–30 seconds with visible full body preferred.
- Coaching emphasizes **posture stack**, **balance**, **limb–torso timing**, and **phase-ordered** feedback.

Out of scope for automated findings:
- Naming a specific sport technique as “correct” without sport context.
- Diagnosing scoliosis, disc injury, concussion, or neurological deficit from pose alone.
- Implement trajectory, ball release point, or court/field rules.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: segment angles, relative landmark positions,
   timing of weight shift, left–right symmetry of ROM, head–pelvis alignment proxies.
2. **Evidence layering**: postural control and clinical movement literature (A/B+) inform load and balance
   language; coaching cues and drills are advice (B), always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric targets are cohort heuristics or self-baseline comparisons —
   not prescriptions for individuals.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs
   so briefs can attach findings to prep, action, or recover windows.
5. **Sibling tool corpus**: measurement policy and OpenSim coordinates are defined in
   `motion_agent/knowledge/corpus/opensim_motion_tools.txt` (listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt`).

## Motion pipeline integration (HumanMotionPack)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Preferred joints: pelvis, lumbar/thorax proxies (shoulder–hip midlines), head, knees, ankles, wrists.
- Camera: frontal or oblique frontal for stack and knee tracking; sagittal for hip hinge and forward head.

### General-motion gate (conservative)

Human pack is the **fallback** when sport detectors return `matched=False`. A future enhancement may score:
- **Posture stack stability**: variance of head–shoulder–hip alignment in setup and recover.
- **Bilateral symmetry**: shoulder height, arm ROM, step width consistency.
- **Phase detectability**: at least one clear prep and one action velocity peak on a primary segment.

Low scores still allow generic coaching; they reduce confidence on phase-scoped findings.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated activity classifiers ship, align narrative phases to computable proxies:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_SETUP | Clip start or local minimum of pelvis speed | Ready posture |
| E_PREP_END | Max eccentric joint flexion or max backward COM displacement | Wind-up / load |
| E_ACTION_PEAK | Max resultant hand/foot speed or max knee/hip extension velocity | Main effort |
| E_RECOVER_END | Speed returns below threshold; stack re-forms | End of rep |

Phases `setup` through `reset` are bounded by these events (see Phases/001).

### OpenSim / tool alignment

When motion tools are available (see opensim_motion_tools.txt):
- Prefer **measured** joint angles and COM from `get_motion_state` / `get_joint_trajectory` over RGB guesswork.
- State that inverse dynamics and GRF are **estimated**, not force-plate truth.
- Use SMPL-X names (`left_hip`, `pelvis`, `right_knee`) in series_id documentation.

### Coach brief generation

`build_briefs(..., sport="general")` consumes:
- Clip-level stack and balance proxies.
- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × phase alignment.
- Instant briefs may use `human_posture` id when no finding fires (see human.py).

### RAG ingest metadata

Suggested chunk tags:
- `sport=human`, `product_pack=general`
- `authority_level` from SOURCE_MANIFEST per section
- `phase_id`, `finding_id` when present in source file headers

### Relationship to sport packs

When a sport pack later matches with higher confidence, sport-specific findings supersede generic ones for
the same clip. Human pack content remains useful for warm-up, cooldown, and cross-training posture review.
