# Human (General Motion) Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel human/general motion content summarizes **open biomechanics literature, physical therapy education
themes, sports coaching fundamentals, and public health movement guidance**. It is **not** a substitute for
licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, chiropractic or osteopathic treatment plans, or
return-to-play clearance.

- Sharp pain, numbness, tingling, joint locking, chest pain, severe headache, or dizziness during movement
  require evaluation by a qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to increase load or ignore symptoms.
- Visible posture in video does not reveal bone structure, disc pathology, or neurological conditions.
  Do not label someone with a clinical syndrome from pose estimation alone.
- Youth, pregnant athletes, and post-surgical clients need individualized supervision; generic drills may be
  inappropriate without professional screening.

## Not a sport-technique authority

This pack is intentionally **sport-agnostic**. When CeRebel recognizes a specific activity (running gait,
pitching, golf swing, etc.), users should prefer that sport pack for technique detail. General cues like
“stack your ribs over pelvis” support many activities but do not replace sport-specific coaching progressions.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation and monocular OpenSim pipelines can mis-estimate:

- Pelvis tilt and lumbar curve when clothing is baggy or the camera is oblique.
- Knee alignment in the frontal plane when filmed from the side only.
- Head position when hair or hats occlude ear/eye landmarks.
- Center of mass path when scale (height in meters) is uncertain.

Findings describe **visible tendencies**, not laboratory-grade kinematics or kinetics. Confidence should
be lower when joints are occluded, FPS is below ~24, or the clip shows heavy motion blur.

## “Ideal posture” and norms

Research and coaching literature report **group averages and training conventions**, not one correct pose for
every body type, limb length, or activity history. Slight asymmetry can be normal. Numeric checkpoints in
this pack are conservative coaching heuristics for product gating — not clinical cutoffs for pathology.

## Balance and fall risk

Balance drills suggested here are for **coached practice on stable ground** with spotting when needed.
CeRebel does not assess environmental hazards (wet floors, equipment, vision impairment). Older adults and
neurologically impaired individuals need professional fall-risk screening beyond video analysis.

## Load and training volume

Posture and coordination cues do not replace **progressive overload planning**, rest, or nutrition. Increasing
training intensity based solely on “better looking” motion in one clip can increase injury risk if tissues
are under-recovered.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, teachers, athletes, and parents who already have a movement context. They
do not replace supervised instruction for beginners learning complex lifts or plyometrics.

## Emergency

For collapse, suspected cardiac event, head trauma, or acute musculoskeletal injury during activity, follow
venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention.

## Product behavior

- HumanMotionPack (`general`) is **live** as fallback when no sport pack matches — absence of a finding is not
  proof of safe mechanics or readiness for maximal effort.
- The system may refuse analysis when clips are too short, too occluded, or appear to violate safety policies.

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. Guidelines change; retrieval dates
matter. OpenSim pipeline notes in sibling file `opensim_motion_tools.txt` describe tool behavior, not medical
evidence.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“General body coaching from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Use a qualified
coach or clinician for sport technique and rehab.”

## Data privacy (group / school contexts)

When filming teams or classes, organizations should follow local policies for storing video of minors.
CeRebel motion analysis is not a substitute for mandated pre-participation physical exams or concussion
protocols. Coaches remain responsible for environment safety (surface, spacing, equipment).

## General pack vs sport pack disclaimer

If both general and sport findings appear during product transitions, sport-specific disclaimers from the
matched sport pack may add context (e.g., running cadence). Users should read the sport pack Docs/002 when
available; this human pack disclaimer remains the baseline for fallback coaching.
