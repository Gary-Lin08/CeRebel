# General Human Motion — Team Practice and Group Film Sessions

Body-first capture for PE classes, strength rooms, cross-training groups, and pre-season movement screens
when sport-specific packs may not apply. Summarizes reputable coaching practice patterns (authority B);
verify school district policies and medical staff requirements locally.

Corpus pack id: human. Product pack id: general (HumanMotionPack fallback).

---

## 1. Session goals for motion analysis

- **Consistency**: film fundamental patterns (squat, lunge, step, reach, single-leg balance) before fatiguing sport work.
- **Repeatability**: full body in frame, 2–4 reps minimum, stable camera.
- **Context tag**: barefoot vs shoes, warm-up vs post-workout, known injury history (coach note only — app does not store PHI by default).

CeRebel briefs prioritize **one or two** general findings per athlete per session — avoid cue overload in PE settings.

---

## 2. Warm-up structure (before filming)

Typical 8–12 minute general movement prep (B coaching consensus):

1. Locomotor wake-up: march, skip, or light jog 2–3 minutes (optional, not required for static screen).
2. Dynamic mobility: arm circles, hip circles, leg swings (front/back), ankle rocks.
3. Activation: 10 bodyweight squats + 10 hip hinges slow tempo.
4. Balance primer: 10 s single-leg stand each side.

Youth: gamify balance (stick challenge); shorten hinge volume for U10.

---

## 3. Camera setup for groups

**Frontal (preferred for stack, knees, shoulders)**:
- Camera chest height, 3–4 m from athlete, full body vertical FOV.
- Avoid wide-angle distortion at frame edges.

**Sagittal (forward head, hip hinge)**:
- Perpendicular to athlete profile; capture head to feet.
- One athlete at a time in lane setup.

**Oblique 45° (compromise view)**:
- Useful when single camera only; lower confidence on knee valgus — document in session log.

Multiple athletes: queue individually; keep background static for pose stability.

---

## 4. Standard film menu (sport-agnostic)

Use consistent tasks so week-to-week comparison works:

| Task id | Duration | Phases exercised | Notes |
|---------|----------|------------------|-------|
| task_stand_setup | 5 s hold | setup | Baseline stack |
| task_bodyweight_squat | 3 reps slow | prep, load, action, recover | Primary lower-body screen |
| task_reverse_lunge | 2 each leg | load, balance | Single-leg sway |
| task_hip_hinge | 5 reps | prep, load | Dowel optional |
| task_arm_raise | 3 each arm | action | ROM asymmetry |
| task_single_leg_balance | 10 s each | load | Balance wobble |

Coaches may subset for time; document which tasks were filmed.

---

## 5. Using phase scrub in a group setting

- Show athletes **setup vs load** for stack breaks — visual learning lands faster than jargon.
- Compare **action peak** frame for knee tracking (frontal) without stopping entire class on one metric.
- If HumanMotionPack is active (no sport match), instant brief may ask about stack — tie to task_stand_setup.

When sport pack later matches same clip library, re-label sessions in archive (engineering workflow).

---

## 6. Weekly micro-cycle pattern (example)

**Week A — screen week**:
- Day 1: film menu full, assign one drill from Coaching/002 per athlete from top finding.
- Day 2: skill sport practice (sport pack if detected on different clips).
- Day 3: repeat film menu subset (squat + balance only) after drill week.

**Week B — drill emphasis**:
- Daily 5-minute station: stack breath squat OR hip hinge stick from Coaching/002.
- Film only on Day 5 for progress check.

Masters: extend recover pauses; reduce hop drills (`drill_stick_landing`) if joint sensitive.

---

## 7. Integration with OpenSim / tool-assisted review (optional)

When session pipeline produces OpenSim states (see opensim_motion_tools.txt):
- Coach review station: pull pelvis tilt and knee angle at bottom of squat **from tools**, not eyeball alone.
- Teach athletes difference between **observation** (video) and **measurement** (tool output).

Tools unavailable: rely on 2D pose with documented lower confidence in briefs.

---

## 8. Privacy and youth safeguarding

- Film only with organizational consent; avoid posting identifiable minors publicly.
- Focus feedback on **movement**, not body appearance or weight.
- Refer pain reports to athletic trainer or school nurse — app findings are not triage.

---

## 9. Common session mistakes (coaching)

- Filming only the **fast** rep — hides rushed_prep and incomplete_recover.
- Side camera only, then asking why knee valgus finding missing — explain camera gating.
- Giving five cues at once — violates one-cue rule in Coaching/001.

---

## 10. Handoff to sport-specific coaching

When athletes later film running, pitching, or golf with dedicated packs:
- General stack findings remain relevant in warm-up.
- Sport technique cues supersede generic hinge cues during sport skill reps.
