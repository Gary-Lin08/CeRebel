# General Human Motion — Concept Taxonomy (v1)

Corpus pack id: human. Product pack id: general.

## Primary concepts

concept_id: general_human_motion
label_en: General human motion coaching
definition: Sport-agnostic body-first analysis of posture, balance, and limb–torso coordination from reconstructed motion.
synonyms_en: human motion, body mechanics, movement quality, general coaching
synonyms_zh: 人体动作, 通用姿态, 身体协调
parent: human
authority: B

concept_id: posture_stack
label_en: Posture stack (head–rib–pelvis)
definition: Vertical alignment relationship among head, thorax, and pelvis in standing and dynamic tasks; coached as stacked segments not rigid military posture.
synonyms_en: neutral spine stack, rib cage over pelvis, head over shoulders, axial alignment
related_findings: posture_stack_break, forward_head, ribcage_flare_anterior, pelvis_anterior_tilt_excess
related_series: series_stack_alignment, series_head_forward, series_pelvis_tilt
authority: B+

concept_id: head_forward_posture
label_en: Forward head posture (proxy)
definition: Head landmark anterior to shoulder–pelvis line in sagittal view; common desk and phone habit; product uses proxy not cervical diagnosis.
synonyms_en: text neck (colloquial), head poke, cranial protraction
related_findings: forward_head
authority: B+

concept_id: ribcage_position
label_en: Rib cage position
definition: Thorax orientation relative to pelvis; excessive anterior flare or collapse affects stack and breathing access in movement.
synonyms_en: flared ribs, open ribs, sunken chest
related_findings: ribcage_flare_anterior, posture_stack_break
authority: B

concept_id: pelvis_orientation
label_en: Pelvis orientation
definition: Anterior/posterior tilt and levelness of pelvis relative to femurs and trunk; visible in sagittal and frontal views when landmarks stable.
synonyms_en: APT, posterior tilt, pelvic level, hip hike
related_findings: pelvis_anterior_tilt_excess, lateral_pelvis_drop, posture_stack_break
authority: B+

concept_id: balance_and_weight_shift
label_en: Balance and weight shift
definition: Control of center of mass over base of support during prep, single-leg tasks, and recovery.
synonyms_en: weight transfer, COM control, stability
related_findings: single_leg_balance_wobble, insufficient_weight_shift, excessive_lateral_lean
related_series: series_com_sway
authority: B+

concept_id: limb_torso_coordination
label_en: Limb–torso coordination
definition: Timing and path of arms and legs relative to trunk rotation and stack; arms should not fight pelvis during many athletic prep patterns.
synonyms_en: dissociation, segment coupling, kinetic chain (coaching term)
related_findings: arms_fight_torso, stiff_spine_no_hip_hinge, rushed_prep
authority: B+

concept_id: hip_hinge_pattern
label_en: Hip hinge pattern
definition: Primary flexion at hip with relative lumbar stability when bending, squatting, or preparing to jump; distinguished from spine-only rounding.
synonyms_en: butt back, hip dominant bend, deadlift pattern (generic)
related_findings: stiff_spine_no_hip_hinge
authority: B+

concept_id: knee_alignment_frontal
label_en: Frontal knee alignment (proxy)
definition: Knee position relative to hip and ankle in frontal/oblique view; inward collapse flagged conservatively without pathology label.
synonyms_en: knee valgus, knees cave in, dynamic valgus
related_findings: knee_valgus_visible
related_series: series_knee_valgus
authority: B+

concept_id: movement_phases_generic
label_en: Prep–action–recover phases
definition: Coarse phase model from setup through reset applicable across activities before sport-specific segmentation.
synonyms_en: eccentric-concentric, wind-up, follow-through, recovery
related_phases: setup, prep, load, action, follow_through, recover, reset
authority: B

concept_id: bilateral_symmetry
label_en: Bilateral symmetry
definition: Left–right comparison of shoulder height, limb ROM, and step width; asymmetry may be normal within bands.
synonyms_en: L-R balance, side-to-side evenness
related_findings: shoulder_height_asymmetry, limb_rom_asymmetry
related_series: series_shoulder_height, series_limb_rom_asym
authority: B

concept_id: capture_quality_general
label_en: General motion capture quality
definition: Confidence in pose estimation for stack and balance claims; depends on FPS, occlusion, and camera plane.
synonyms_en: film quality, landmark confidence
parent: general_human_motion
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: opensim_measurement_policy
label_en: OpenSim measurement policy
definition: When motion tools available, prefer tool-reported angles and COM; distinguish observation, measurement, inference per sibling corpus file.
synonyms_en: GEM-X tools, get_motion_state, joint trajectory
external_ref: motion_agent/knowledge/corpus/opensim_motion_tools.txt
authority: B

## Synonym expansion for RAG

When users ask about “posture,” expand to: posture_stack, head_forward_posture, ribcage_position, pelvis_orientation.
When users ask about “balance,” expand to: balance_and_weight_shift, single_leg_balance_wobble, series_com_sway.
When users ask about “coordination,” expand to: limb_torso_coordination, movement_phases_generic, rushed_prep, incomplete_recover.

## Authority note

No concept at authority C is used for automated checkpoint failure in this pack.
