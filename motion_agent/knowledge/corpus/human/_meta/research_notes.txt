## Postural control and balance

- Systematic and narrative reviews on quiet standing and single-leg sway describe increased sway path with age
  and after fatigue; mediolateral sway particularly sensitive on narrow base.
  Search hub: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=postural+control+sway+balance
  Authority: B+
  Product use: single_leg_balance_wobble; com_sway checkpoint; not clinical BESS replacement.

## Trunk–pelvis coordination

- Open-access articles in Gait & Posture corpus on pelvis–thorax coupling during locomotion and rotational sports.
  Search hub: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=trunk+pelvis+coordination+kinematics
  Authority: B+
  Product use: arms_fight_torso, posture_stack_break narrative; phase model prep/action windows.

## Forward head posture (proxy themes)

- Neck extensor activity and upper quarter kinematics with forward head positions in laboratory studies;
  effect sizes vary; sedentary population prevalence high.
  Search hub: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=forward+head+posture+neck+kinematics
  Authority: B+
  Product use: forward_head finding; sagittal camera gating; no cervical diagnosis.

## Lumbopelvic rhythm and hip hinge
