# General Human Motion — Common Faults (finding cards) — v2

Authority: B / B+ summarized open literature; not diagnostic. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
Corpus pack id: human. IDs use fault_<snake_case>; legacy_id keeps prior bare slugs.

Sport-agnostic: posture, hinge, squat-ish, single-leg, reach, gait basics, shoulder asymmetry.

---

finding_id: fault_forward_head
legacy_id: forward_head
headline_en: Head sits too far in front of your stack
phases: setup, recover
body_signal: Head landmark (ear/nose proxy) anterior to shoulder–pelvis line in sagittal view at setup or after action.
why_it_matters: Sustained forward head posture is common with desk/phone habits and may increase neck and upper-back muscle effort in some populations (B+). Tendency only — not cervical diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Pull the crown of your head up and slightly back — eyes level."
drill: 45 s wall chin glide (drill_forward_head_wall).
severity_default: low
sources: Postural alignment education; PMC neck posture reviews summarized (B+)
youth_note: Pattern quality (hinge vs squat, quiet single-leg) over perfect posture angles.

finding_id: fault_posture_stack_break
legacy_id: posture_stack_break
headline_en: Head, ribs, and pelvis lose their stack under movement
phases: load, action
body_signal: Stack alignment proxy fails cp_stack_neutral during load/action while setup was acceptable, OR continuous failure across setup and action.
why_it_matters: Loss of axial organization can reduce power transfer and increase compensatory motion elsewhere (B+). Not a “weak core” diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Ribs down, pelvis neutral — move as one cylinder before you add speed."
drill: 60 s stacked breath squat (drill_stack_breath_squat).
severity_default: medium
sources: Trunk–pelvis coordination literature; fundamental strength coaching (B/B+)
youth_note: Pattern quality (hinge vs squat, quiet single-leg) over perfect posture angles.
