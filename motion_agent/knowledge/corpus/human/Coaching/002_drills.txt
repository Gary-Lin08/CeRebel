# General Human Motion — Drills (30–90 s) — v2

Sport-agnostic, bodyweight-first. Stop if pain. Each drill maps targets: fault_*.

---

drill_id: drill_forward_head_wall
targets: fault_forward_head
duration: 45 s
description: Heels/hips/upper back lightly on wall; slide head back without lifting chin; hold 5 s / release 5 s.

drill_id: drill_stack_breath_squat
targets: fault_posture_stack_break
duration: 60 s
description: Hand on sternum and pelvis; slow squat 3 s down/up 4–6 reps keeping rib–pelvis unit.

drill_id: drill_exhale_reach
targets: fault_ribcage_flare_anterior, fault_overhead_arch_lumbar
duration: 45 s
description: Exhale fully, reach arms forward without ribs flaring; 6–8 reps.

drill_id: drill_pelvis_clock
targets: fault_pelvis_anterior_tilt_excess
duration: 60 s
description: Soft knees; rock pelvis A/P; find neutral; hold 10 s × 3.

drill_id: drill_single_leg_level
targets: fault_lateral_pelvis_drop, fault_single_leg_balance_wobble
duration: 45 s
description: Single-leg stand 10 s each side; pelvis level; wall finger touch OK.

drill_id: drill_mini_squat_knee_track
targets: fault_knee_valgus_visible
duration: 60 s
description: Light miniband above knees optional; ½ squat 8 reps pushing knees out over toes.

drill_id: drill_hip_hinge_stick
targets: fault_stiff_spine_no_hip_hinge, fault_knee_dominant_hinge
duration: 90 s
description: Dowel along spine; hinge hips back keeping contacts; 6 slow reps.

drill_id: drill_flamingo_focus
targets: fault_single_leg_balance_wobble, fault_base_of_support_narrow
duration: 60 s
description: Barefoot 15 s each leg, eyes forward; quiet sway.

drill_id: drill_step_pause_load
targets: fault_insufficient_weight_shift
duration: 45 s
description: Step forward, pause 2 s with ~70% on front foot; alternate 4 each.

drill_id: drill_corridor_march
targets: fault_excessive_lateral_lean, fault_gait_trunk_lean
duration: 45 s
description: March between two markers without leaning into them; 30 s continuous.

drill_id: drill_shoulder_roll_reset
targets: fault_shoulder_height_asymmetry, fault_rounded_shoulders
duration: 45 s
description: Slow shoulder rolls backward 8 reps; pause at lowest even hang.

drill_id: drill_mirror_reaches
targets: fault_limb_rom_asymmetry, fault_reach_shoulder_hike
duration: 60 s
description: Alternate arm raises to same height 6 per side facing mirror.

drill_id: drill_counted_prep_squat
targets: fault_rushed_prep, fault_squat_depth_shallow
duration: 60 s
description: 3-count eccentric, pause, 1-count up; 5 bodyweight reps.

drill_id: drill_stick_landing
targets: fault_incomplete_recover
duration: 45 s
description: Small hop, land soft, hold stack 3 s; 4 reps.

drill_id: drill_cross_crawl_slow
targets: fault_arms_fight_torso
duration: 60 s
description: Slow opposite elbow-to-knee march; 20 s each pattern side.

drill_id: drill_wall_angels
targets: fault_rounded_shoulders, fault_thoracic_extension_excess
duration: 45 s
description: Back to wall; slide arms in angel pattern keeping ribs soft; 6 reps.

drill_id: drill_seated_wall_reach
targets: fault_thoracic_extension_excess, fault_reach_shoulder_hike
duration: 45 s
description: Seated against wall; reach arms up without cranking upper back; 8 reps.

drill_id: drill_box_squat_target
targets: fault_squat_depth_shallow, fault_trunk_collapse_squat
duration: 60 s
description: Sit to box/target depth then stand; 6 controlled reps.

drill_id: drill_squat_tripod
targets: fault_heels_rise_squat
duration: 60 s
description: Wall-assisted squat holds emphasizing foot tripod; 3 × 10 s.

drill_id: drill_goblet_shape
targets: fault_trunk_collapse_squat, fault_hip_shift_lateral_squat
duration: 60 s
description: Hands at chest as counterweight; 6 slow squats keeping chest proud and hips centered.

drill_id: drill_reach_melt
targets: fault_reach_shoulder_hike
duration: 45 s
description: Wall slides melting shoulder away from ear; 8 reaches.

drill_id: drill_deadbug_reach
targets: fault_overhead_arch_lumbar, fault_ribcage_flare_anterior
duration: 60 s
description: Dead-bug with opposite arm reach keeping ribs down; 6/side.

drill_id: drill_gait_corridor_walk
targets: fault_gait_trunk_lean, fault_excessive_lateral_lean
duration: 60 s
description: Walk corridor path with tall stack; 40–60 s continuous.

drill_id: drill_gait_longer_step
targets: fault_gait_short_step
duration: 60 s
description: Line walks with slightly longer heel-to-toe steps; quiet feet.

drill_id: drill_squat_hip_center
targets: fault_hip_shift_lateral_squat
duration: 60 s
description: Mirror squat focusing hips centered between feet; 6 reps.

drill_id: drill_hinge_not_squat
targets: fault_knee_dominant_hinge, fault_stiff_spine_no_hip_hinge
duration: 90 s
description: Soft-knee dowel hinge emphasizing hips back before knee bend; 6–8 reps.

drill_id: drill_scap_pushup_plus
targets: fault_scapular_wing_proxy, fault_rounded_shoulders
duration: 45 s
description: Incline or wall push-up plus holds 5 s × 6; glue scapula.

drill_id: drill_bos_widen
targets: fault_base_of_support_narrow, fault_single_leg_balance_wobble
duration: 45 s
description: Stance-width reset marches at hip width; 30–45 s.

drill_id: drill_combo_stack_review
targets: fault_posture_stack_break, fault_forward_head
duration: 90 s
description: 30 s wall head glide + 30 s stacked breath squat + 30 s single-leg level each side.

---

## Team / PE notes

- Film before/after one drill only when testing change.
- Youth: shorten balance durations; skip band if uncomfortable.
