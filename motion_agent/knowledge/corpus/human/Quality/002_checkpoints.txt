# General Human Motion — Quality Checkpoints

Each checkpoint references phase_id(s), a measurable signal (series_id), a rule summary, failure finding_id,
and evidence authority level. Thresholds are product heuristics for noisy monocular capture — not clinical cutoffs.

|---------------|----------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|----------|----------|

| cp_stack_neutral_setup_youth | setup | stack_alignment | widened band; flag only gross loss of stack | posture_stack_break | B+ | youth |
| cp_forward_head_setup | setup, recover | head_forward | head landmark markedly anterior to shoulder–pelvis line (sagittal) | forward_head | B+ | adult |

## Notes on measurement

**stack_alignment**: composite of sagittal head offset and frontal shoulder–hip line deviation; prefer OpenSim
pelvis and neck angles when tools available (see opensim_motion_tools.txt).

**head_forward**: ear or nose proxy anterior to acromion–hip midline; requires approximate sagittal camera.

**knee_valgus**: frontal/oblique camera only; side view must not trigger this checkpoint (product should gate).

**com_sway**: pelvis or model COM lateral displacement normalized by foot width estimate.

**prep_action_ratio**: time from E_PREP_START to E_PREP_END divided by time to E_ACTION_PEAK.

**limb_rom**: peak wrist or ankle path range during action phase for symmetric tasks (jump prep, arm raise).

## Checkpoint pass semantics

v1 does not sum checkpoints into a 0–100 score. Coach UX may show “focus area” = highest-severity failed
checkpoint with matching finding card. HumanMotionPack may show instant posture brief when no checkpoint fails.

## Evidence citations (summary)

- Postural control and COM sway: PMC reviews on balance (B+).
- Trunk–pelvis coordination: Gait & Posture open-access themes (B+).
- Knee valgus in squatting: neuromuscular training literature (B+); non-diagnostic in general pack.
- Coaching stack cues: NSCA/ACE education level (B).

Full URLs in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` and `_meta/research_notes.txt`.

## Youth checkpoint overrides

When `audience=youth` on session metadata:
- Use cp_stack_neutral_setup_youth instead of cp_stack_neutral_setup.
- Use cp_shoulder_height_asym_youth instead of cp_shoulder_height_asym.
- Do not apply adult forward-head shaming copy; still fire forward_head when gross proxy fails.

## OpenSim tool precedence

When session has valid OpenSim states (see opensim_motion_tools.txt):
- pelvis_tilt, knee_valgus, and COM series may be read from tool trajectories at E_PREP_END and E_ACTION_PEAK.
- If tool series conflicts with noisy 2D pose by > agreed tolerance, prefer tool for brief text with uncertainty note.

## Confidence down-ranking rules (product logic)

Down-rank or suppress checkpoint failure when:
- Camera labeled sagittal-only and checkpoint requires frontal (knee valgus, shoulder asymmetry, lateral lean).
- Clip duration < 1.5 s or fewer than one detectable prep peak.
- Recover phase truncated at clip end — suppress cp_recover_incomplete unless collapse visible in action.

## Severity ordering suggestion (non-normative)

For multi-fail clips, suggested coach priority when severities tie:
1. knee_valgus_visible (if frontal confidence high)
2. posture_stack_break under load
3. single_leg_balance_wobble
4. rushed_prep
5. forward_head at setup only

KB documents priority for UX design; medical risk ranking is explicitly out of scope.
