# Volleyball Approach — Coaching Cues (v2)
# Grouped by finding_id; mix internal and external focus
# Long drill prose lives in Coaching/002_drills.txt

## fault_jump_behind_contact
legacy_id: jump-behind-contact
cue_external: "Reach over the net at your highest point."
cue_internal: "Feel tall ribs — hand finishes the swing at peak height."
cue_external: "Hang in the air one beat before you whip."

## fault_jump_early_reach
legacy_id: jump-early-reach
cue_external: "Floor fires first, hand fires second."
cue_internal: "Push ankles and knees before the shoulder spins."
cue_external: "Jump on time — don't swing on the way up from the plant."

## fault_arm_only_swing
legacy_id: arm-only-swing
cue_external: "Jump like you're blocking — then swing."
cue_internal: "Power from glutes, not only deltoid."
cue_external: "Big bounce, small arm at the start."

## fault_soft_plant
legacy_id: soft-plant
cue_external: "Sit in the last step."
cue_internal: "Load the quad on the brake step."
cue_external: "Heels heavy, then spring."

## fault_short_approach
legacy_id: short-approach
cue_external: "Start behind the back line in practice — build distance."
cue_internal: "Count four steps with the last two explosive."

## fault_penultimate_flat
legacy_id: penultimate-flat
cue_external: "Long-deep second-to-last."
cue_internal: "Drop the hips on penultimate, rise on plant."

## fault_asymmetric_takeoff
legacy_id: asymmetric-takeoff
cue_external: "Two feet leave together."
cue_internal: "Even pressure under big toe both sides."

## fault_late_trunk_open
legacy_id: late-trunk-open
cue_external: "Chest to net, then arm."
cue_internal: "Rotate belly button first, hand last."

## fault_landing_stiff
legacy_id: landing-stiff
cue_external: "Quiet feet — bend knees."
cue_internal: "Absorb in the legs, not the lower back."
coach_note: Load awareness only — not ACL diagnosis. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.

## fault_slow_approach
legacy_id: slow-approach
cue_external: "Accelerate like a runway."
cue_internal: "Fast feet on the last two steps only."

## fault_narrow_plant
legacy_id: narrow-plant
cue_external: "Skateboard width base."
cue_internal: "Stable hips over a strong platform."

## fault_wide_plant
legacy_id: wide-plant
cue_external: "Power under your center."
cue_internal: "Pull last step in — don't straddle."

## fault_spike_no_penult_load
cue_external: "Long-deep second-to-last — drop the hips, then spring."
cue_internal: Same family as fault_penultimate_flat.

## fault_spike_short_approach
cue_external: "Start farther back — build the runway."

## fault_spike_early_arm
cue_external: "Chest to net first — then snap the elbow."
cue_internal: "Arm waits while the trunk opens."

## fault_spike_late_jump
cue_external: "Floor first — jump on time, swing at the top."

## fault_spike_stiff_landing
cue_external: "Quiet feet — bend like a spring."
coach_note: Same family as fault_landing_stiff; disclaimer applies.

## fault_approach_crossover_chaotic
cue_external: "Run a straight line to the plant — steps under your hips."
cue_internal: "Quiet hips side-to-side; drive forward."

## fault_approach_last_step_overreach
cue_external: "Plant under you — last step powerful, not a lunge."
cue_internal: "Feel the plant under the belt buckle."

## fault_arm_swing_elbow_lead
cue_external: "Elbow high like a bow — then throw the hand past the elbow."
cue_internal: "High elbow slot, then whip."

## fault_swing_no_draw
cue_external: "Pull the hitting elbow back like drawing a bow before you jump."
cue_internal: "Feel the stretch behind the shoulder, then release."

## fault_hitting_shoulder_open_early
cue_external: "Keep your hitting shoulder closed until you’re rising — then open and swing."
cue_internal: "Stay coiled through takeoff."

## fault_landing_single_leg_dominant
cue_external: "Two feet together — share the landing."
cue_internal: "Even pressure left and right."

## fault_landing_forward_fall
cue_external: "Sit back into the landing — hips and knees share the bend."
cue_internal: "Hinge hips, don't fold at the waist only."

## fault_block_ready_upright
cue_external: "Athletic ready — soft knees, weight forward, hands ready at shoulders."
cue_internal: "Spring-loaded base, not a stick."

## fault_block_arms_low
cue_external: "Hands at shoulder shelves — ready to press up."
cue_internal: "Pre-load the elbows before the jump."

## fault_jump_timing_rushed
cue_external: "Approach… plant… then jump — give the plant one beat."
cue_internal: "One quiet count on the plant before you leave."

## fault_com_wrist_desync
cue_external: "Meet the swing at the top of your jump — rise and whip together."
cue_internal: "Feel hand speed peak while you are still tall."

## General ready-phase cues (no finding required)

cue_external: "Athletic ready — weight forward."
cue_external: "See the setter, loose shoulders."

## Jump serve variant (variant=serve)

cue_external: "Same jump-then-strike order as attack."
cue_internal: "Toss is timing — body still uses legs-first on strike."
