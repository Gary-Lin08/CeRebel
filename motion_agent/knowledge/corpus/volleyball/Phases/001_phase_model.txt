# Volleyball Attack Approach — Phase Model (v2)
# Scope: attack approach (3-step / 4-step); jump serve shares takeoff→landing spine; block-ready posture body-only
# Body-only OpenSim / pose keypoints; no ball required
# Stable phase IDs — UI labels + SP_* detector ids

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Seven phases describe one complete approach-jump-strike-land cycle using events
approximated from multi-joint tracking without a ball. Overlap at boundaries is
normal (trunk rotation begins during plant; arm swing continues into landing).

Handedness: trail = hitting side (right-side hitter: trail = right wrist).

UI scrub bar: ready | approach | plant | takeoff | swing | contact | landing
Detector aliases: SP_READY | SP_APPROACH | SP_PENULT | SP_PLANT | SP_TAKEOFF | SP_SWING | SP_CONTACT | SP_LANDING

================================================================================
PHASE TABLE
================================================================================

| phase_id | SP_* alias   | label_en       | start event        | end event          |
|----------|--------------|----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| ready    | SP_READY     | Ready / base   | clip_start         | E_APPROACH_START   |
| approach | SP_APPROACH  | Approach steps | E_APPROACH_START   | E_PLANT            |
| (penult) | SP_PENULT    | Penultimate    | E_PENULTIMATE      | E_PLANT            |
| plant    | SP_PLANT     | Plant / load   | E_PLANT            | E_COM_RISE         |
| takeoff  | SP_TAKEOFF   | Takeoff        | E_COM_RISE         | E_COM_PEAK         |
| swing    | SP_SWING     | Airborne swing | E_COM_PEAK         | E_CONTACT          |
| contact  | SP_CONTACT   | Contact window | E_CONTACT - 2 fr   | E_CONTACT + 3 fr   |
| landing  | SP_LANDING   | Landing        | end(contact)       | E_LAND_STABLE      |

Jump serve variant:
- May collapse `approach` if athlete uses minimal steps; annotate variant=serve.
- `ready` includes pre-toss stance; toss not detected without ball.

================================================================================
PHASE DETAILS (detector_events / fault_links)
================================================================================

### ready / SP_READY
**Start/end:** See table.
**Body signals:** Athletic base; knees soft; hands ready.
**detector_events:** E_READY_STABLE
**fault_links:** fault_block_ready_upright, fault_block_arms_low

### approach / SP_APPROACH
**Body signals:** Horizontal ankle/pelvis speed rising; step cadence.
**detector_events:** E_APPROACH_START, approach_speed peak
**fault_links:** fault_short_approach, fault_spike_short_approach, fault_slow_approach, fault_approach_crossover_chaotic, fault_jump_timing_rushed

### SP_PENULT (internal split of late approach)
**Body signals:** Penultimate knee flex; long/deep step.
**detector_events:** E_PENULTIMATE
**fault_links:** fault_penultimate_flat, fault_spike_no_penult_load

### plant / SP_PLANT
**Body signals:** Last foot contact; knee flex load; step width.
**detector_events:** E_PLANT
**fault_links:** fault_soft_plant, fault_narrow_plant, fault_wide_plant, fault_approach_last_step_overreach, fault_swing_no_draw, fault_spike_early_arm, fault_jump_timing_rushed

### takeoff / SP_TAKEOFF
**Body signals:** Pelvis vertical velocity positive; bilateral leave; coil retained.
**detector_events:** E_COM_RISE, E_COM_PEAK (end)
**fault_links:** fault_arm_only_swing, fault_asymmetric_takeoff, fault_jump_early_reach, fault_spike_late_jump, fault_hitting_shoulder_open_early, fault_com_wrist_desync

### swing / SP_SWING
**Body signals:** Trail wrist acceleration; trunk open; elbow path.
**detector_events:** wrist accel onset; trunk_rotation rate peak
**fault_links:** fault_late_trunk_open, fault_spike_early_arm, fault_arm_swing_elbow_lead, fault_swing_no_draw, fault_jump_behind_contact, fault_arm_only_swing

### contact / SP_CONTACT
**Body signals:** argmax ||v(trail_wrist)|| — NOT verified ball contact.
**detector_events:** E_CONTACT (= contact_peak_wrist)
**fault_links:** fault_jump_behind_contact, fault_jump_early_reach, fault_com_wrist_desync, fault_late_trunk_open

### landing / SP_LANDING
**Body signals:** Knee/hip flex after E_LAND; bilateral accept; quiet stick.
**detector_events:** E_LAND, E_LAND_STABLE
**fault_links:** fault_landing_stiff, fault_spike_stiff_landing, fault_landing_single_leg_dominant, fault_landing_forward_fall

================================================================================
EVENT DEFINITIONS (body-only)
================================================================================

E_READY_STABLE
  Rolling std of pelvis position below threshold for ≥6 frames at clip start.
  Fallback: first frame.

E_APPROACH_START
  Max ankle horizontal speed exceeds baseline × 1.8 for 3 consecutive frames after ready.
  Fallback: ready_end + 8% of window span.

E_PENULTIMATE (internal, splits approach)
  Second foot strike before E_PLANT: ankle vertical velocity zero-cross with deceleration spike.
  Used for penultimate_knee series and finding fault_penultimate_flat.

E_PLANT
  Last foot contact before takeoff: final ankle height local minimum / speed deceleration before E_COM_RISE.
  Fallback: E_COM_RISE minus ~100–140 ms at 60 fps (low confidence).

E_COM_RISE
  Pelvis vertical velocity v_y > 0.04 m/s (scale-normalized in product code when available).

E_COM_PEAK
  argmax pelvis height in [E_COM_RISE, E_CONTACT + 5 frames].

E_CONTACT
  argmax ||v(trail_wrist)|| in analysis window.
  Primary timing anchor. NOT verified ball contact.

E_LAND
  First frame after E_CONTACT where either ankle vertical velocity ≈ 0 and height near post-airborne minimum.

E_LAND_STABLE
  E_LAND + frames until both knee flexion rates below threshold (settled stance).

================================================================================
TIMING RELATIONSHIPS (qualitative, B+)
================================================================================

Skilled indoor hitters often show:
- Approach speed peak in late approach/plant before vertical impulse.
- COM rise begins at or just after plant; arm remains partially back during early takeoff.
- Wrist speed peak (E_CONTACT) at or after COM peak for many high sets; quick-tempo attacks may show
  near-simultaneous peaks — product uses banded checkpoint, not single ideal delay.

Youth: slower approach, lower COM rise, smaller frame delay between COM peak and E_CONTACT.

================================================================================
CLIP TRIMMING GUIDANCE
================================================================================

Ideal: full ready stance through stable landing; side or 45° camera showing feet and hitting arm.
Minimum: 0.4 s before E_APPROACH_START through E_LAND_STABLE.
Late trim loses ready/approach findings; early trim loses landing absorption findings.

================================================================================
3-step vs 4-step approach
================================================================================

Same phase IDs. Four-step patterns show longer `approach` duration and may have clearer E_PENULTIMATE.
Metadata step_pattern=3|4 set by coach or future cadence detector.
