# Volleyball — Common Faults (finding cards)
# Authority: B / B+ open biomechanics + NGB coaching. Not diagnosis.
# See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt. Body-first; E_CONTACT = hitting-hand speed peak (no ball).
# ACL / knee language is load-awareness only — never diagnostic.

---

finding_id: fault_jump_behind_contact
legacy_id: jump-behind-contact
headline_en: You may be peaking your jump before your swing finishes
phases: swing, contact, SP_SWING, SP_CONTACT
body_signal: E_COM_PEAK occurs substantially earlier than E_CONTACT (e.g. >10 frames at 60 fps adult band). COM height may fall while trail_wrist speed is still rising toward peak.
why_it_matters: Hitting while descending can reduce reachable window and timing margin on higher sets (performance). Athletes often report "jumping behind the ball" when this pattern appears — body proxy without ball track.
feel_cue: "Stay tall through the swing — punch the hand up at the top of your jump, not on the way down."
drill: 60s approach-jump reach-and-hold: at peak jump, freeze hitting hand high for 1 s before landing (no ball); 4 reps.
severity_default: medium
sources: USA Volleyball approach timing coaching summaries (B); PMC spike sequencing reviews (B+)
youth_note: Soft landing and approach rhythm over jump-height comparisons.

finding_id: fault_jump_early_reach
legacy_id: jump-early-reach
headline_en: Hand speed peaks before you fully rise
phases: takeoff, swing, contact, SP_TAKEOFF, SP_SWING
body_signal: E_CONTACT precedes E_COM_PEAK by more than youth/adult band in Quality/002. Wrist speed peak during early takeoff; limited COM rise amplitude.
why_it_matters: Early arm fire often cuts leg drive contribution — common on rushed sets (performance).
feel_cue: "Legs first, hand second — let the floor push you up, then whip the arm."
drill: 45s pause takeoff: plant → jump without arm swing → add arm only after pelvis rises; 5 slow reps.
severity_default: medium
sources: Open kinematic chain reviews volleyball/baseball overhead (B+); coaching progressions (B)
youth_note: Soft landing and approach rhythm over jump-height comparisons.

finding_id: fault_arm_only_swing
legacy_id: arm-only-swing
headline_en: Swing looks mostly arm-driven with little jump
phases: takeoff, swing, contact, SP_TAKEOFF, SP_SWING
body_signal: Low COM rise with high wrist speed ratio OR weak E_COM_RISE near-simultaneous with wrist acceleration only; minimal pelvis height change plant→peak.
why_it_matters: Reduces power from legs and trunk; increases relative shoulder demand in high-volume training (load awareness, not diagnosis).
feel_cue: "Jump like the floor is hot — push the ground away before you open the shoulder."

severity_default: high

youth_note: Soft landing and approach rhythm over jump-height comparisons.

finding_id: fault_landing_stiff
legacy_id: landing-stiff
headline_en: Landing looks stiff in the knees
phases: landing, SP_LANDING
body_signal: Knee flex increase within 8 frames of E_LAND below band (cp_landing_knee_flex fail). Upright torso with minimal hip/knee give.
why_it_matters: Stiff landings increase impact rates in population landing literature (load awareness); recovery for next play may suffer. Not an ACL diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Land quiet — bend like a spring, not a stick."
drill: 45s soft landing sticks: approach jump land on two feet hold 2 s with knees visibly flexed; 5 reps.
severity_default: high
sources: ACL/landing literature general athletics (A/B+ awareness); volleyball coaching soft-landing drills (B)
youth_note: Prefer lower jump volume and emphasize quiet landings; follow club jump-count policies.
