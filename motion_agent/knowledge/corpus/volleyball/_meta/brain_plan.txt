================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

RAG-ready knowledge for three coach-facing skill families (v1):

1. SPIKE_APPROACH — multi-step approach, penultimate plant, vertical jump, hitting-arm swing
2. SERVE — standing / jump serve body chain (toss-arm and strike-arm proxies only)
3. PASS_PLATFORM — ready posture, movement to ball, forearm platform, extension and recovery

Shared assumptions:
- Contact timing uses **contact proxy** (argmax hitting-side or strike-side wrist speed), not ball touch.
- Setters, blocks, digs (non-platform), and beach tactics: glossary-only in v1 taxonomy.
- Medical diagnosis and return-to-play: out of scope; redirect in Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.

Build agent writes full Findings/Coaching bodies from this map; brain agent does not.

================================================================================
2. PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

- All measurable claims tie to reconstructed body: joint angles, segment speeds, COM height,
  ankle step rhythm, forearm platform orientation proxy (elbow–wrist segment vs trunk).
- Never require ball position, spin, net height, line calls, or court calibration for findings.
- Each user-facing finding maps to: phase window, body signal, feel cue, 30–90s drill, sources.
- Youth vs adult numeric norms: separate bands; never present youth numbers as adult targets.
- Authority: A/A+ for federation safety & volume; B+ for biomech reviews; B for NGB coaching;
  no C-tier thresholds in product findings.

3. SKILL FAMILIES & PHASE MODELS (5–8 PHASES EACH)
================================================================================

--- 3A. SPIKE APPROACH (prefix SP_) ---

| id            | label_en        | label_zh   | body role |
|---------------|-----------------|------------|-----------|
| SP_READY      | Ready / prep    | 预备       | Athletic stance, minimal COM drift |
| SP_APPROACH   | Approach steps  | 助跑       | Step rhythm; ankles accelerate forward |
| SP_PENULT     | Penultimate     | 倒数第二步 | Low COM, knee flex load before plant |
| SP_PLANT      | Plant           | 制动步     | Lead foot decel; hips open into jump |
| SP_TAKEOFF    | Takeoff / jump  | 起跳       | COM rise; bilateral or asymmetric push |
| SP_SWING      | Arm swing       | 挥臂       | Trunk rotation; hitting-side wrist speed ↑ |
| SP_CONTACT    | Contact proxy   | 击球瞬间   | Peak hitting-side wrist speed in clip |
| SP_LANDING    | Landing         | 落地       | Decel, knee flexion, balance |

Event definitions (body-only):
- contact_proxy_sp: argmax ||v(hitting_wrist)|| in analysis window.
- plant_event: local max deceleration of lead_ankle speed before takeoff_event.
- takeoff_event: max positive vertical velocity of COM (or pelvis) after plant_event.
- penult_window: ~1 step before plant_event via ankle velocity cadence.

Handedness: hitting_side = dominant spike hand (RH attacker: right wrist = hitting_wrist).

--- 3B. SERVE (prefix SV_) ---

| id            | label_en        | label_zh   | body role |
|---------------|-----------------|------------|-----------|
| SV_READY      | Ready           | 准备       | Platform or pin-point foot stance; quiet COM |
| SV_LOAD       | Load / coil      | 蓄力       | Knee flex, trunk slight counter-rotation |
| SV_TOSS_ARM   | Toss-arm rise   | 抛球手     | Non-strike arm elevation (no ball track) |
| SV_DRAW       | Draw / backswing| 引拍       | Strike-side elbow/wrist drops behind torso |
| SV_STRIKE     | Strike proxy    | 击球       | Peak strike-side wrist speed |

Variant tags (same chain v1): standing_float, jump_float, jump_topspin.
Toss accuracy and net clearance: OUT OF SCOPE for automated findings.

--- 3C. PASS PLATFORM (prefix PS_) ---

| id            | label_en        | label_zh   | body role |
|---------------|-----------------|------------|-----------|
| PS_READY      | Ready position  | 准备姿势   | Neutral athletic base, platform arms ready |

| PS_PLATFORM   | Platform form   | 平台       | Forearms joined; elbow angle; shoulders forward |
| PS_CONTACT    | Contact proxy   | 触球瞬间   | Platform segment stable; minimal wrist flick |

6. CLINICAL / COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED — NOT MEDICAL ADVICE)
================================================================================

ADULT (college / open; B+ / A kinematic reviews):
- Spike: penultimate COM lowering; arm swing peak often near COM height peak (sequencing, B+).
- Hip–shoulder separation before arm swing (B+/A theme); flag early arm if wrist peak before trunk peak.
- Landing knee flexion for load absorption (A clinical theme); stiff landing heuristic B+.
- Serve: knee flex in load; jump serves show COM rise before strike proxy (B+).
- Pass: platform in front of torso; move-through with leg drive (B+ coaching).

YOUTH (U14; B+ / federation safety):
- Lower jump and arm speed peaks — no adult speed thresholds.
- Serve: body pattern over power; float progressions.
- Pass: wider base; down-weight form severity unless pain language.
- Volume: federation load guidance (A+ summaries); no max plyo prescriptions in KB.
