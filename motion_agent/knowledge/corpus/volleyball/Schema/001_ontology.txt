# Volleyball Sport Pack — Ontology (Schema v0.1)
# Aligns: _meta/brain_plan.txt, SPORT_PACK_KB_SPEC.txt
# Body-first; contact = wrist/platform proxies — not ball track.

## Entity: Player

Attributes:
- player_id (string, optional external)
- handedness: right | left
- hitting_side: right | left (dominant spike/serve hand)
- audience_band: youth | adult | unknown
- surface_context: indoor | beach | unknown (tags only v1)

Relations:
- performs → SkillInstance
- subject_of → FindingInstance

## Entity: SkillFamily

Enum (v1):
- spike_approach
- serve
- pass_platform

Future (glossary only): block, set, dig

Attributes:
- skill_family_id (stable slug)
- phase_chain_prefix: SP_ | SV_ | PS_

## Entity: SkillInstance

Attributes:
- instance_id
- skill_family_id
- contact_proxy_side: hitting | strike | platform

Relations:
- has_phase_sequence → VolleyballPhase (ordered)
- yields → BiomechObservation

## Entity: VolleyballPhase

Attributes:
- phase_id (SP_PENULT, SV_LOAD, PS_PLATFORM, …)
- skill_family: spike_approach | serve | pass_platform
- ordinal (integer within family)
- label_en, label_zh optional
- description_plain

Relations:
- follows → VolleyballPhase
- contains_events → VolleyballEvent

Spike phases: SP_READY, SP_APPROACH, SP_PENULT, SP_PLANT, SP_TAKEOFF, SP_SWING, SP_CONTACT, SP_LANDING
Serve phases: SV_READY, SV_LOAD, SV_TOSS_ARM, SV_DRAW, SV_STRIKE, SV_FOLLOW
Pass phases: PS_READY, PS_MOVE, PS_PLATFORM, PS_CONTACT, PS_EXTEND, PS_RECOVER

## Entity: VolleyballEvent

Attributes:
- event_id (contact_proxy_sp, plant_event, takeoff_event, toss_arm_peak, platform_set)
- start_rule, end_rule (narrative, body-only)
- detector_proxy optional

## Entity: MeasurementDefinition

Subclasses: JointAngle, SegmentSpeed, SegmentOrientation, COMDerived

Attributes:
- series_id (brain_plan section 5)
- unit
- primary_phases

Examples:
- hitting_hand_speed → SegmentSpeed (spike)
- strike_hand_speed → SegmentSpeed (serve)
- platform_angle → SegmentOrientation (pass)
- jump_height_proxy → COMDerived (spike)

## Entity: Finding

Attributes:
- finding_id (prefix fault_)
- headline
- skill_family_id
- phase_id (primary)
- severity_hint: info | form | load
- body_signals (list)
- why_it_matters (non-diagnostic)
- authority_level: B+ | B | A (landing themes)
- evidence_type: BiomechRelationship | CoachingAdvice

Relations:
- has_cue → Cue
- has_drill → Drill
- occurs_in → VolleyballPhase

v1 finding_id set: fault_spike_no_penult_load, fault_spike_short_approach, fault_spike_early_arm,
fault_spike_late_jump, fault_spike_stiff_landing, fault_serve_knee_lock, fault_serve_toss_arm_drift,
fault_serve_poor_decel, fault_pass_broken_platform, fault_pass_absorb_only, fault_pass_narrow_base

## Entity: Cue

Attributes:
- cue_id (cue_<finding_id>)
- finding_id
- feel_text (body-first)
- coach_text optional

## Entity: Drill

Attributes:
- drill_id (drill_<finding_id>)
- finding_id
- duration_sec (30–90)
- setup, instructions
- youth_variant optional

## Entity: Checkpoint

Attributes:
- checkpoint_id (cp_*)
- phase_id(s)
- series_id
- pass_description
- fail_triggers → Finding

## Entity: LoadConcern

Attributes:
- concern_id, description, audience_band, authority_level
Used in Training/ — not a Finding.

## Entity: EvidenceChunk (RAG metadata)

Attributes:
- source_id, authority_level (A+|A|B+|B|C), evidence_type, retrieved_at, audience_band optional

## Naming conventions

- Spike: SP_* | Serve: SV_* | Pass: PS_*
- Findings: fault_* | Events: *_proxy when speed-peak based

## Handedness (default RH attacker)

hitting_wrist = right on spike; toss arm = left on serve; pass platform symmetric.

## Properties

- volleyball:precedes, volleyball:occursIn, volleyball:measuredOn, volleyball:failTriggers
- volleyball:skillFamily, volleyball:authorityLevel, volleyball:ageBand
