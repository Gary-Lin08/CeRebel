# Volleyball coaching knowledge — disclaimer (CeRebel)

## Not medical advice

Content in this sport pack is for **education and movement awareness** only. It is not medical advice, physical
therapy, or a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician, athletic trainer, or physician.

Stop activity and seek professional care if you have pain, swelling, instability, numbness, or symptoms that worsen
with jumping or overhead hitting.

## Monocular and pose-reconstruction limits

CeRebel infers body motion from video and biomechanical models. Joint angles and speeds are **estimates** affected by
camera angle, clothing, occlusion, frame rate, and model error. Do not use app output to diagnose shoulder impingement,
ACL injury, or other conditions.

## Contact and ball position

Without ball tracking, "contact" in this pack means a **hitting-hand speed peak proxy**, not verified ball touch.
Coaching about "jumping under the ball" is expressed as timing between center-of-mass motion and arm swing — a
heuristic that may differ from true ball-relative contact.

## Youth athletes

Growth plates and fatigue sensitivity matter in volleyball jump volume. Youth-specific bands in this pack are
coaching references, not league rules. Follow your national federation and school/club policies for practice load,
and consult qualified coaches for progression.

## Evidence and thresholds

Numeric checkpoints are **coaching heuristics** derived from open literature and federation coaching summaries (see
SOURCE_MANIFEST). They are not universal standards for every body type, skill level, or playing surface (indoor vs beach).

## Emergency

This product does not handle on-court emergencies. Follow venue safety protocols for head injury, cardiac events, or
severe acute injury.
