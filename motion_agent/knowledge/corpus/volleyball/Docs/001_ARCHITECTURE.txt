# Volleyball Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first volleyball attack and jump-serve coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video and explains what the athlete’s body is doing across recognizable
approach-and-jump phases. Ball tracking, set height, and net context are optional enrichments, not prerequisites for
coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Attack approach** with airborne arm swing (spike/tip motion family).
- **Jump serve** where footwork and overhead strike share the same phase spine (variant=serve).

Out of scope for automatic findings (may appear as glossary):
- Ball flight, set accuracy, block timing vs hitter, dig/pass platform angles, rotation violations.
- Beach-specific tactical positioning unless session metadata tags surface=beach.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, COM motion, step timing,
   left-right symmetry visible without a ball.
2. **Evidence layering**: federation rules (A+) for glossary; clinical reviews (A) for load awareness; biomechanics
   summaries (B+) for sequencing; cues and drills (B) always paired with Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric targets are coaching heuristics with youth/adult separation, not prescriptions.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to stable phase IDs in Phases/001_phase_model.txt.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned / v1 narrative)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Minimum joints for attack-likeness heuristics: trail wrist, lead wrist, both ankles, pelvis or mid-hip.
- Handedness: trail = hitting side (default right-side hitter: trail = right).

### Attack-likeness gate (conservative)

Soft score from body-only features:
- **Vertical burst**: pelvis height rise from plant to swing vs clip baseline.
- **Wrist speed burst**: peak trail-wrist speed vs median (expect high peak near contact window).
- **Approach signal**: ankle horizontal speed peak in approach/plant before takeoff.

Score gates sport-mode selection only; it does not authorize medical advice.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_CONTACT | argmax trail-wrist speed in clip | Primary anchor; not ball contact |
| E_COM_PEAK | argmax pelvis height before E_CONTACT | Jump apex proxy |
| E_COM_RISE | pelvis vertical velocity threshold | Takeoff onset |
| E_PLANT | last foot strike before E_COM_RISE | Ankle decel / height minimum |
| E_APPROACH_START | ankle speed rise after ready | First step |

Future detectors: dual-foot takeoff classification, block jump separate model.

### Coach brief generation

Consumes phase windows, top finding cards, one feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding from Coaching/.
Example user question mapping: "jumping under vs behind" → findings jump-behind-contact, jump-early-reach with
COM–wrist timing explanation (heuristic).

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary; stable finding IDs repeated in Findings and Coaching cross-links.
- Tag `authority_level` and `ageBand` at ingest from manifest.
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` as coaching truth.

## Youth vs adult

Workload and jump volume content tags `audience=youth` or `audience=adult`. Mechanics checkpoints share structure
but different numeric bands in Quality/002 and Biomechanics/001.
