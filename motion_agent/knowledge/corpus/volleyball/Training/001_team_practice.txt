# Volleyball Team Practice — Body-First Coaching Patterns (v1)

Purpose: How coaches use CeRebel phase scrub + one-main-fix UX in team settings without ball tracking.
Audience tags: youth vs adult load pointers; not league rule replacements.

---

## 1. Capture setup for useful briefs

**Camera**
- Side or 45° to approach lane; full body from ready through landing in frame.
- 60 fps preferred for COM–wrist timing checkpoints.
- Avoid shooting only from behind baseline (hides knee flexion).

**Session tagging**
- step_pattern: 3 | 4
- variant: attack | serve
- ageBand: youth | adult
- surface: indoor | beach (softens COM thresholds)

**Partner positioning**
- During hitting lines, diggers off camera lane reduce occlusion.
- Jump serve: capture from side; toss may occlude arm — still usable for takeoff/landing.

---

## 2. Warm-up (10–15 min) — movement quality before volume

- Dynamic ankle/knee/hip (leg swings, lunges, inchworms).
- **Shadow approaches** × 6 each athlete: no ball, focus rhythm penultimate→plant.
- **Soft landing sticks** × 5: links to landing-stiff finding prevention (not injury guarantee).

Youth: shorter warm-up jumps; no max-effort plyo before age-appropriate progression (Volleyball Canada LTAD themes, B).

---

## 3. Skill block — approach footwork (15 min)

Structure:
1. Coach demo 3-step and 4-step on same phase scrub labels (ready→landing).
2. Athletes walk-through last two steps only (penultimate-flat, soft-plant drills).
3. Add arm swing; record 1 clip each; review one finding in app.

**One-main-fix rule**: Pick highest-severity finding aligned to team goal (e.g. COM timing day → jump-behind-contact cues only).

Adults: add approach speed build with cone depth.
Youth: emphasize rhythm and plant bend over absolute speed.

---

## 4. Hitting line integration (20–30 min)

Traditional hitting line with setter; CeRebel adds parallel **shadow lane**:
- Every third rotation: shadow approach + jump without ball for clean kinematics.
- When using live sets, expect noisier COM–wrist timing — coach notes set tempo in metadata.

Volume guidance (summarized — verify live NGB/LTAD):
- Youth: limit consecutive max jumps; rotate shadow vs live hits; more water breaks.
- Adult club: periodize jump-heavy days vs serve-receive focused days.

Do not reproduce copyrighted pitch-count-style tables; point coaches to federation LTAD pages in SOURCE_MANIFEST.

---

## 5. Jump serve segment (10 min, optional)

- Progression: standing serve → approach serve without jump → full jump serve.
- Body-only feedback on leg-then-strike sequencing (jump-early-reach, arm-only-swing).
- Toss quality coached traditionally; app does not score toss.

---

## 6. Block and pass (glossary practice — no v1 automated findings)

- Block jumps: film for future pack; use general landing-soft cues.
- Pass platform: shoulder angle and hip hinge coached manually; taxonomy concept forearm_pass only.

---

## 7. Cool-down (5–8 min)

- Walk court edges; static calf/hip flexor brief holds.
- Team review: one phase label quiz (what happens in `plant` vs `takeoff`?) reinforces RAG vocabulary.

---

## 8. Weekly structure example (high school club)

| Day | Emphasis | CeRebel focus |
|-----|----------|---------------|
| Mon | Serve + pass | serve variant timing |
| Tue | Approach footwork | penultimate, plant checkpoints |
| Wed | Off or lift | — |
| Thu | Hit lines | COM–wrist timing |
| Fri | Scrimmage | landing absorption spot checks |

Adjust for tournament weeks — reduce max-effort jump rep targets.

---

## 9. Using phase scrub in film review

1. Scrub to `plant` — check knee flex together as team.
2. Scrub to `contact` — note wrist peak frame vs `takeoff` COM rise in timeline overlay (when UI available).
3. Assign drill from Coaching/002 matching single finding; re-record 3 reps.

---

## 10. Safety and inclusion

- Pain → remove from jump lines; clinician evaluation.
- Athletes with prior ACL reconstruction: follow medical clearance; app does not clear athletes.
- Mixed skill groups: pair by approach pattern not jump height to reduce comparison anxiety.
