# Volleyball Approach — Quality Definition (v1)

## Purpose

Define what "good" attack approach quality means in **body-measurable terms** for CeRebel coaching UX.
v1 does not emit a single numeric quality score; coaches see **checkpoint pass/fail** with linked findings.

## Quality dimensions

### 1. Approach rhythm and length (approach, plant)

Forward speed builds on the last steps; penultimate step loads the jump; total approach distance fits athlete
stature and set tempo. Failures: slow-approach, short-approach, penultimate-flat.

Evidence: coaching consensus (B); approach cadence in open kinematics (B+).

### 2. Plant and vertical impulse (plant, takeoff)

Plant leg accepts force with adequate knee flexion then extends into takeoff; COM rises after plant, not during
only arm motion. Failures: soft-plant, arm-only-swing.

Evidence: B+ jump-spike reviews; B NGB footwork progressions.

### 3. Trunk–arm sequencing (takeoff, swing, contact)

Trunk rotation and opening contribute before or with rapid hand acceleration; not a late afterthought.
Failures: late-trunk-open, arm-only-swing.

Evidence: B+ proximal-to-distal hitting literature.

### 4. Jump–contact timing proxy (swing, contact)

Relationship between COM peak and wrist speed peak within age-banded heuristics — supports answering
"jumping under vs behind" without ball track. Failures: jump-behind-contact, jump-early-reach.

Evidence: B product heuristic; B+ variability in elite cohorts (no single ms target).

### 5. Takeoff symmetry (takeoff, swing)

Left and right ankles leave ground within reasonable window; excessive stagger may reduce power transfer.
Failures: asymmetric-takeoff, narrow-plant, wide-plant.

Evidence: B coaching; camera angle sensitivity noted.

### 6. Landing absorption (landing)

Knees and hips flex on landing; trunk stays relatively quiet. Failures: landing-stiff.

Evidence: A/B+ landing load literature (general athletics); not injury prediction.

## Age bands

- **youth**: middle school through early HS; lower speed and COM rise expectations.
- **adult**: club college and open; standard bands in Quality/002.
- Session metadata `ageBand` overrides default adult when set.

## Confidence modifiers

Reduce confidence when:
- Camera primarily head-on (poor sagittal knee angles).
- Clip trimmed missing plant or landing.
- Single-foot takeoff style tagged by coach (some hitters use left-right stagger by design).
