## 1. Kinetic chain (approach → hit → land)

Published spike and jump-serve analyses commonly describe:

1. **Horizontal approach** — Athlete gains speed on the last steps; ground reaction forces shift toward the plant foot.
2. **Eccentric loading** — Penultimate and plant steps show knee flexion; hip extensors and quadriceps load elastically (B+).
3. **Vertical impulse** — Ankle, knee, and hip extension drive COM upward during takeoff.
4. **Trunk rotation and tilt** — Pelvis and trunk rotate toward the net; increases hitting hand path length (B+).
5. **Arm swing** — Hitting arm draws back then accelerates; elbow extends; wrist speed peaks near contact proxy (B+).
6. **Landing** — Bilateral or staggered foot contact; knee flexion dissipates momentum (B+ general athletics).

CeRebel does not compute joint torques or ground reaction forces in v1 — only kinematic proxies from pose/OpenSim.

Sources: PMC-indexed volleyball spike reviews (search "volleyball spike kinematics"); summarized in SOURCE_MANIFEST.

## 2. Measurement definitions on body model

### COM vertical (series: com_vertical)

Proxy: pelvis center or mid-hip height; vertical velocity from finite differences.
Use: takeoff onset (E_COM_RISE), jump apex (E_COM_PEAK), arm-only vs leg-driven jump discrimination.

### Approach speed (series: approach_speed)

Max of left/right ankle speed magnitude in horizontal plane during approach/plant phases.
Use: slow-approach, short-approach context.

### Plant knee / penultimate knee (series: plant_knee, penultimate_knee)

Sagittal knee flexion angle at event frames. **Camera sensitivity**: 45° side view preferred; front view underestimates flexion.

### Arm swing (series: arm_swing)

||v(trail_wrist)||; E_CONTACT = argmax in window.
Disclaimer: not ball speed; not contact point height.

### Trunk rotation (series: trunk_rotation)

lumbar_rotation or shoulder-line yaw relative to facing at ready; use rate peak timing vs wrist acceleration.

### Step width (series: step_width)

Mediolateral distance between ankles at E_PLANT.

### COM–contact timing

Δt = frame(E_CONTACT) - frame(E_COM_PEAK). Negative = contact before apex; positive = contact after apex.
Coaching heuristic for "behind the ball" language when COM peaks well before hand speed peak (B, product).

## 3. Norm bands (typical published ranges — not prescriptions)

### Adult indoor hitters (club/college; high variance)

| Signal | Typical band (literature/coaching summaries) | Authority |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------|
| Approach last-step speed | moderate–high vs self baseline | B+ |
| Plant knee flex at takeoff | ~30–50° sagittal (high variance) | B+ |
| COM rise (normalized) | visible distinct peak before landing | B+ |
| Wrist speed peak timing vs COM | often near apex ± few frames | B+ / B product |
| Trunk rotation | substantial before hand peak | B+ |

### Youth (middle school / early HS)

| Signal | Notes | Authority |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Approach speed | lower absolute speeds; emphasize rhythm over max | B |
| Jump height | lower COM rise; do not compare to elite cm targets | B |
| Arm speed ratio | lower peak/median ratio expected | B |
| Volume | limit maximal jump-spike reps per practice (LTAD) | B+ |

Never present youth numbers as adult targets in UI copy.

## 4. Jump serve (kinematic overlap)

Jump serves use similar leg drive and trunk rotation; horizontal approach may be minimal. Toss phase is **not** body-scored without ball.
Strike phase maps to `contact`; landing checkpoints identical.

---

## 5. Load and injury context (careful wording)

Reviews associate high volleyball jump volume with patellar tendinopathy and shoulder overload in some cohorts (A epidemiology).
**Single-clip kinematics cannot predict injury.** Findings like arm-only-swing and landing-stiff are framed as efficiency and load
awareness cues, not diagnoses.

## 7. Limitations (v1)

- No ball → no true "contact point" or set-relative jump height.
- 2D/monocular error on shoulder rotation and knee flexion.
- Block, set, and pass motions not detected by approach phase model.
- Beach sand changes jump height; use metadata surface=beach for softer COM thresholds.
