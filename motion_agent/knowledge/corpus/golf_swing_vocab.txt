Golf swing vocabulary (seed knowledge pack)

Phases (full swing)
- Address: setup posture before the club moves; ball position, stance width, posture.
- Takeaway: early club motion away from the ball; often one-piece with arms/shoulders.
- Backswing: club rises to the top; trail hip/shoulder turn, wrist hinge (cocking).
- Transition: change of direction from backswing into downswing; lower body often leads.
- Downswing: club accelerates toward impact; sequencing (hips → torso → arms → club).
- Impact: clubface meets ball; face angle and path largely set ball start/curvature.
- Follow-through / finish: post-impact continuation to a balanced finish pose.

Path / face language
- Club path: direction the clubhead is moving through impact (in-to-out / out-to-in).
- Face angle: clubface pointing at impact relative to target line (open / closed / square).
- Angle of attack (AoA): upward or downward clubhead motion through impact.
- Fade / draw: intentional curvature; slice / hook typically larger unintentional curves.

Sides
- Lead side: left for a right-handed player; right for a left-handed player.
- Trail side: opposite of lead. Confirm handedness from RGB before labeling.

Short game (brief)
- Chip: low running shot near the green; putting stroke: pendulum-like with minimal wrist.
- Pitch: higher, softer landing; bunker: sand splash technique, ball often not struck first.

When analyzing a swing in this pack
1. Identify handedness and club type from RGB if relevant.
2. Segment phases with timestamps using motion + video tools.
3. Prefer joint/pelvis/thorax trajectories over single-frame posture for sequencing claims.
