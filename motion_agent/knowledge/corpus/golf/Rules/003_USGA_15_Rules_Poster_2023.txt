/one.LPStroke-and-Distance (red or yellow)
Play from the relief area based on where your 
previous stroke was made.
/three.LPLateral Relief (red only)
Drop a ball within a two club-length relief area measured 
from where your ball last crossed the edge of the penalty 
area, and no closer to the hole than that point.
/two.LPBack-On-the-Line (red or yellow)
Drop a ball on a line that goes from the hole through the 
point where your ball last crossed the edge of the penalty 
area and it must come to rest within one club-length of 
where it ﬁrst hits the line.

movement, including wind or gravity.
