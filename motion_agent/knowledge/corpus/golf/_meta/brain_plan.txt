1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for **full-swing body mechanics** detectable from monocular /
OpenSim reconstruction:

- Posture and spinal angle through address → impact window
- Pelvis and thorax rotation, separation (X-factor coaching language)
- Weight shift / CoM drift (qualitative + estimated GRF patterns)
- Kinematic sequencing (proximal-to-distal timing proxies)
- Balance through finish

v1 swing family: **driver / full iron full swing** (shared phase chain). Chip, pitch, putt:
glossary only in Taxonomy; separate phase models are stretch goals.

Out of scope v1:
- Ball flight, spin, launch angle, carry, curve prediction
- Clubface angle, club path, AoA as **required** product signals (may cite as vendor vocabulary only)
- Rules adjudication as coaching findings (use Rules/ corpus separately)
- Medical diagnosis, return-to-play after back/hip/elbow injury
- Verbatim NGB / TPI proprietary curriculum

Build agent writes full Phases/Quality/Findings/Coaching/Training bodies from this map;
brain agent does not.

4. PHASE MODEL (UI + DETECTOR ALIGNMENT)
================================================================================

Phase IDs (exact — target contract for future _golf_window):

| id            | label_en        | label_zh     | maps_from_existing_taxonomy |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| address       | Address         | 站位         | golf:address                |
| takeaway      | Takeaway        | 起杆         | (new; early backswing)      |
| backswing     | Backswing       | 上杆         | golf:backswing (top segment)|
| transition    | Transition      | 转换         | (new)                       |
| downswing     | Downswing       | 下杆         | golf:downswing              |
| impact_proxy  | Impact window   | 触球窗口     | golf:impact (proxy event)   |
| finish        | Finish          | 收杆         | golf:follow_through         |

Event definitions (body-only, OpenSim-friendly):

- impact_proxy_event: argmax ||v(lead_wrist)|| or hand cluster speed toward target line
  in analysis window (fallback: peak thorax rotational velocity toward target if wrist noisy).
- top_of_backswing: local min lead_arm elevation velocity OR max trail_shoulder external
  rotation before reversal (fallback: impact_proxy − 45% window span).
- transition_start: first sustained reversal of pelvis yaw velocity after top_of_backswing.
- takeaway_start: first frame club-side hand speed exceeds address baseline (or clip start + 5% if static).
- address_end / takeaway_start: same event.
- downswing_start: transition_start (lower body leads cueing references this window).
- finish_event: lead_foot stable + thorax rotation velocity < 25% of downswing peak for
  ≥ 10% window span OR clip end.

Phase spans (defaults when events missing):
- address: clip start → takeaway_start
- takeaway: takeaway_start → 25% of backswing duration to top
- backswing: end takeaway → top_of_backswing
- transition: top_of_backswing → downswing_start (min 30 ms)
- downswing: downswing_start → impact_proxy_event
- impact_proxy: impact_proxy_event ± 4% window span (min 30 ms)
- finish: end(impact_proxy) → finish_event

Capture: down-the-line (DTl) preferred for rotation and sway; face-on (FO) supplements
vertical posture and hip height. Oblique reduces separation fidelity — Quality gates apply.

Phases/001_phase_model.txt (build): expand events, fallbacks, EN/ZH scrub copy, handedness notes.

Seed vocabulary alignment: golf_swing_vocab.txt phases (Address … Follow-through) → table above;
"Transition" and "Takeaway" explicit in product IDs.

5. FULL-SWING MECHANICS (COACHING + MEASUREMENT FOCUS)
================================================================================

Document in Biomechanics/001_overview.txt; cite Bourgain et al. 2022 + Watson et al. 2026
(summaries only — full PDF text stays in Biomechanics/00*_*.txt).

Themes to cover (body-first):
- **Setup**: hip hinge, knee flex, spine angle; avoid prescribing absolute ° as medical norm.
- **Turn**: pelvis then thorax in backswing; separation peak timing (X-factor language).
- **Transition**: pelvis leads reversal before peak hand speed (kinematic sequence proxy).
- **Ground interaction**: trail-side load in backswing → lead-side bracing into impact_proxy;
  qualitative CoP shift (Watson review — timing matters, not magnitude alone).
- **Posture maintenance**: hip/spine flexion loss (early extension proxy) from address to impact_proxy.
- **Finish**: balanced rotation onto lead side, belt buckle toward target (coaching language).

Limitations block (mandatory): monocular depth error, estimated GRF ≠ force plate, no CHS
in checkpoints unless externally measured and tagged.

7. CLINICAL / COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED)
================================================================================

Wording: "typical in published cohorts" / "coaching consensus" — NOT personal medical advice.

ADULT full swing (B+; Bourgain 2022, Watson 2026, open kinematics literature):
- Separation peak often occurs before impact_proxy; collapse before impact associated with
  power loss in reviews (do not state causal injury claims).
- Skilled cohorts: higher GRF magnitude AND better timing vs less skilled (Watson).
- Methodological variance for X-factor definitions — product uses consistent internal series only.

YOUTH (B / B+ coaching; no adult CHS tables in findings):
- Shorter backswing amplitude; prioritize balance and rotation quality over speed cues.
- Avoid adult "ground force" max-out language; down-weight GRF magnitude checkpoints.
- Separate audience tag: youth | adult | all in ontology.

Exclude from checkpoints: club_speed, ball_speed, smash factor, launch angle, spin rate
(Taxonomy measurement concepts remain for glossary / future authorized sensor ingest).
