4. SAFETY
================================================================================

### 4.1 What was reviewed

Indexed content includes extensive Rules/medical-exception language (equipment for medical reasons),
handicap illness/injury administrative rules, and **peer-reviewed injury epidemiology** inside
Biomechanics PDF text (low-back pain, crunch factor, ACL loading mentions, elbow overuse).

No consumer-facing `Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt` in pack. Engineering guardrails exist only in
`golf_biomechanics_conventions.txt` (estimated GRF not force-plate; no invented CHS).

### 4.2 Risk register (live GolfPack + RAG)

  [S-1] **No diagnosis / injury prediction**
        Do not label "golfer's elbow," disc injury, ACL tear, or LBP causation from video.
        Biomech reviews discuss associations — RAG must not surface as user-specific diagnosis.

  [S-2] **Lumbar / X-factor / crunch factor firewall**
        Literature is mixed on X-factor, crunch factor, and LBP causality. Automated "excessive
        X-factor" findings without B+ sourced cards and disclaimer are **P0 blockers**.

  [S-3] **GRF / CoP overclaim**
        Watson 2026 links GRF/CoP to CHS on force platforms. Monocular OpenSim estimates are
        not equivalent — copy must match `golf_biomechanics_conventions.txt` and disclaimer.

  [S-4] **No prescription**
        No stop-playing, brace, PT protocol, or return-to-play timelines from app output.

  [S-5] **Youth**
        No junior norms in corpus; do not apply adult elite kinematic peaks to juniors when age unknown.

  [S-6] **Rules vs coaching confusion**
        Rules answers are A+ but not medical/legal advice; handicap injury provisions are administrative,
        not clinical guidance for swing coaching.

  [S-7] **Pain and play-through**
        Coaching cues in product strings ("biggest thing to fix") must not encourage playing through
        sharp back pain, radicular symptoms, or acute joint locking.

  [S-8] **Ball/club metrics**
        Taxonomy impact-linked measurements (face_angle, dynamic_loft) require launch monitor or
        club track — forbid implying these from body-only pose.

### 4.3 Safety gates before treating KB as coaching authority

  - [ ] `Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt` present; referenced in Coaching/ and LLM golf preamble
  - [ ] Findings cite authority ≥ B+ for load/injury-adjacent wording
  - [ ] Biomech overview separates population research from individual user claims
  - [ ] Quality/ documents low-confidence capture (oblique angle, missing feet, heavy occlusion)
  - [ ] Optional: RAG filter to deprioritize injury paragraphs in technique mode

Safety verdict: **GAP (high)** for live coaching mode until disclaimer + Findings/Quality exist;
**moderate** for rules/handicap/equipment Q&A with version/disclaimer in UI.
