# Golf Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel golf content summarizes **open-access biomechanics reviews, public coaching articles, and governing-body rules
where indexed separately**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy, or individualized
return-to-play protocols after back, hip, knee, elbow, or wrist injury related to golf.

- Acute pain, numbness, locking, swelling, or pain that worsens with rotation or weight shift require evaluation by a
  qualified clinician — do not use an app finding to justify continuing high-volume full swings.
- Long-form biomechanics extracts in this corpus discuss injury epidemiology and spinal loading in research populations;
  those statistics **do not** diagnose your condition or prescribe load limits.
- Youth and junior golfers differ in bone maturity, coordination, and weekly tolerance; follow your national governing
  body (NGB), school athletic staff, and club professional for training volume and equipment fitting.

## Not a substitute for qualified coaching

Automated briefs support athletes and coaches who already have technical supervision. They do not replace in-person
instruction for grip, setup, course management, or rules decisions. Rules and handicap material in this pack is reference
only — verify current R&A / USGA publications before competition.

## Body-first product scope (no launch monitor required)

This Sport Pack is designed for **body motion** analysis from video (pose / OpenSim-style reconstruction):

- **Impact window** in product copy means an **impact_proxy** aligned to peak lead-hand speed toward the target (or a
  documented fallback), not verified club–ball contact.
- Findings and checkpoints do **not** require ball flight, clubhead speed, face angle, attack angle, or path tracks.
- When those metrics appear in glossary or research summaries, they are **context only**, not user-facing pass/fail targets
  unless externally measured and explicitly tagged.

## Monocular and estimated kinetics limitations

Single-camera 2D pose and depth-from-video pipelines can mis-estimate:

- Pelvis and thorax rotation when the camera is oblique to the target line (down-the-line preferred for turn fidelity).
- Spine flexion (“posture”) when clothing is loose or the athlete wears a cap that occludes the neck.
- Lateral sway and weight shift when scale or floor plane is uncertain.
- **Estimated ground reaction force (GRF)** and centre-of-pressure (CoP) trends derived from motion — these are **not**
  force-plate measurements. Literature magnitudes (Newtons, multiples of body weight) must not be shown as personal targets
  without validated sensors.

Findings describe **visible tendencies** with confidence downgrades when joints are occluded, frame rate is low, or the
clip is heavily cropped.

## Biomechanical “norms” and population bands

Published studies report **group averages and ranges** with heterogeneous methods (especially X-factor and kinematic
sequence definitions). Stature, mobility, age, sex, and swing style all change appearance. This pack avoids presenting
arbitrary degree targets as universal medical rules.

- **Adult** cohort statistics from systematic reviews must not be applied to **youth** coaching copy.
- Youth sections emphasize balance, rotation quality, and NGB-appropriate volume — not maximizing ground force or clubhead
  speed language copied from elite male driver studies.

## Ground interaction and power cues

Coaching text may describe qualitative weight shift, hip bump, and “use the ground” timing. Effective force use in research
is often about **timing and coordination**, not raw magnitude alone. Do not promise distance gains, handicap improvement,
 or injury prevention from a single drill or one analyzed swing.

## Outdoor play, footwear, and surface

Range mats, wet grass, slope, and spike type change traction and perceived ground interaction. The product cannot see
footwear or lie quality. Coaches should manage cumulative full-swing volume on hard mats especially for juniors and
players with prior lower-back or hip discomfort.

## Heat, hydration, and fatigue

Dehydration and end-of-session fatigue alter balance and rotation timing. One analyzed clip may not represent typical form
for that athlete on that day.

## Rules, equipment, and licensing

Verbatim rules, handicap, and equipment list extracts are official publications. Commercial redistribution may require
separate rights review — see _meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt. Live rules hubs supersede frozen PDF text for adjudication.

## Emergency

For acute injury on the range or course, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when needed.

## Product behavior

- Golf pack features may be gated until detector validation completes — absence of a flagged finding is not proof of safe
  mechanics.
- The system may refuse to analyze clips that appear to be another sport when another pack detector is confident elsewhere.

## Authority and updates

When `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` is present, it lists sources with authority levels (A+ rules, B+ peer-reviewed summaries,
B public coaching). Coaching curricula change; retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from body motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Follow your coach and junior load
guidelines.”
