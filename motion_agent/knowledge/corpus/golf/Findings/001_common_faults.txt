# Golf Sport Pack — Common faults (finding cards)

Authority: B / B+ summarized open literature and NGB coaching; not diagnostic. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
Body-measurable only; club/ball track not required. Trail = dominant hand (right-handed default).

---

finding_id: fault_early_extension
headline_en: Early extension — standing up out of posture before the hit
phases: downswing, impact
body_signal: Pelvis moves toward ball on DTL; spine angle from horizontal increases markedly before impact proxy; head rises vertically relative to address; hips may thrust while shoulders lag.
why_it_matters: Coaching and biomechanics literature link loss of spine angle to inconsistent low-point control and compensations through the arms (B+ themes); cannot infer ball flight from body alone.
feel_cue: "Keep your belt buckle back while your hips turn — chest stays over the ball longer."
drill: 60s chair or wall hip-depth shadows: maintain light hip contact with imaginary wall through downswing (see Coaching/002_drills.txt drill_wall_spine_tilt).
severity_default: high
sources: Golf swing biomechanics systematic review (B+, PMC); PGA teaching summit posture summaries (B)
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_early_extension_thrust
headline_en: Early extension — pelvic thrust toward the ball without matching turn
phases: downswing, impact
body_signal: Pelvis AP translation toward ball line dominates over pelvic yaw; belt-buckle moves forward while shoulder open angle remains small at impact proxy.
why_it_matters: Thrust-dominant early extension often pairs with blocked rotation and hanging-back compensations in coaching chains (B); distinguish from intentional stack-and-tilt variants — qualitative only.
feel_cue: "Turn the belt buckle to the target — don't shove the hips at the ball."
drill: 45s wall-gate turn: hips lightly on wall, rotate open without pushing wall forward (shadow).
severity_default: high
sources: Open swing posture reviews (B+); PGA instruction articles (B)
youth_note: Juniors often "stand up" from lack of strength — cue turn first, not deeper squat.

finding_id: fault_over_the_top_transition
headline_en: Upper body starts the downswing — shoulders spin toward target before hips
phases: transition, downswing
body_signal: Shoulder yaw toward target peaks early while pelvis rotation still minimal; trail elbow may lift away from rib cage on DTL; wrist path steepening visible on face-on.
why_it_matters: Common pattern associated with out-to-in path in coaching models (B); body-only mode flags sequence, not club path.
feel_cue: "Bump the ground with your lead hip, then turn — pants pocket to target before chest."
drill: 45s step-in swings: small step toward target with lead foot, then swing (shadow, no ball) — drill_step_through_shadow.
severity_default: high
sources: Open kinematic sequence reviews (B+); LPGA instructor education materials (B)
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_cast_from_top
headline_en: Casting — trail arm straightens and releases angle too early from the top
phases: transition, early downswing
body_signal: Trail elbow extension rate high immediately after top; wrist speed jumps before pelvis accelerates; loss of lag visible as early hand height drop on DTL.
why_it_matters: Reduces stored angular momentum in coaching kinetics models (B+ awareness); often pairs with weak transition_sequence checkpoint.
feel_cue: "Keep the trail elbow soft longer — pull the butt of the club toward the ball, not throw the hands."
drill: 60s pump drill body-only: from top, stop at hip height twice, then third rep through finish (drill_pump_from_top).
severity_default: medium
sources: Biomechanics reviews on wrist/club lag proxies (B+); teaching pro drill libraries (B)
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_hanging_back
headline_en: Hanging back — weight stays on trail side through impact proxy
phases: downswing, impact
body_signal: Pelvis remains behind address position at impact proxy; lead knee flexed or collapsed without bracing; trail head/shoulder stack behind ball line on DTL.
why_it_matters: GRF/CoP literature associates pressure shift to lead side with skilled players and speed (B+); hanging back often coincides with inconsistent strike in coaching observation (B).
feel_cue: "Push the ground with your trail foot until your lead side catches you."
drill: 45s lead-leg-only finish holds: swing to finish, lift trail foot 2 s — drill_lead_foot_finish_hold.
severity_default: high
sources: Watson et al. GRF/CoP systematic review 2026 (B+); PGA ground-force coaching themes (B)
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_weight_shift_late

feel_cue: "Feel the lead foot press as you start down — not after the hit."

severity_default: medium
sources: Watson et al. 2026 (B+); PGA pressure-shift cues (B)

finding_id: fault_sway_not_turn
headline_en: Sway instead of turn — lateral slide off the ball without hip rotation
phases: backswing through transition
body_signal: Pelvis translates away from target on face-on with limited shoulder turn; head moves off center; trail knee straightens without hip internal rotation cues.
why_it_matters: Reduces rotational power and stable low point in coaching frameworks (B); distinguish from intentional stack-and-tilt variants — qualitative only.
feel_cue: "Turn your back to the target — don't slide your hips sideways."
drill: 60s feet-together shadow swings: narrow base exposes sway; focus rotation (drill_feet_together_balance).
severity_default: medium
sources: Open swing reviews (B+); TPI-style movement screening summaries (B, non-diagnostic)
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_short_arm_swing
headline_en: Short backswing turn — arms lift without shoulder rotation
phases: backswing
body_signal: Shoulder yaw small address→top; wrists high but spine vertical; elbows disconnected from trunk on DTL.
why_it_matters: Limits coil and tempo options for many recreational players (B coaching); juniors may be intentional — compare to athlete baseline.
feel_cue: "Turn your lead shoulder under your chin — let the club ride the turn."
drill: 60s cross-arm rotation: hands on opposite shoulders, practice turn without club (drill_cross_shoulder_turn).
severity_default: medium
sources: LPGA junior curriculum (B); kinematics reviews (B+)
youth_note: Shorter absolute turn is normal for juniors; use self-comparison, not adult elite peaks.

finding_id: fault_incomplete_shoulder_turn
headline_en: Incomplete shoulder turn at the top relative to athlete baseline
phases: backswing, transition
body_signal: Peak shoulder yaw below athlete's own warm-up baseline or coaching band; lead shoulder does not pass under chin proxy; often paired with early transition rush.
why_it_matters: Incomplete coil reduces available rotation window before downswing in coaching models (B); not a universal degree target.
feel_cue: "Give yourself time to finish the turn — feel the trail knee load before you start down."
drill: 60s turn-and-pause: reach top, hold 1 s checking lead shoulder under chin, then slow through (drill_top_hold_check).
severity_default: low
sources: LPGA/PGA turn fundamentals (B); B+ kinematics summaries
youth_note: Soft trail knee and tempo matter more than elite shoulder-turn depth; compare to prior clips.

finding_id: fault_tempo_hitch_at_top

finding_id: fault_tempo_rush_downswing

feel_cue: "Count three going back, one coming through — don't fire from the top."

severity_default: medium
