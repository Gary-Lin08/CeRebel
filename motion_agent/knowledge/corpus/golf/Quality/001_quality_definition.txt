# Golf Sport Pack — Quality definitions (body-first)

“Quality” in CeRebel golf mode means **observable body patterns** associated with coordinated, balanced full
swings in open biomechanics and coaching literature — not score, handicap, or carry distance. Dimensions are
**non-diagnostic**; pain or injury requires qualified clinical evaluation (see Docs/ when DISCLAIMER is added).

Impact and club metrics (attack angle, path, dynamic loft) are **out of scope** for pose-only scoring.

---

## Dimensions

### dimension: setup_balance
**Phases:** address
**Good sign:** Feet shoulder-width or athletic width; pelvis midline between ankles on face-on; minimal lateral
head sway while addressing; knee flex visible (not locked upright).
**Poor sign:** Weight visibly stacked on toes or heels only; large pre-swing lateral pelvis shift; excessive
spine rounding or extreme reverse tilt held statically.
**Authority:** B coaching setup consensus; B+ postural themes in swing reviews.

### dimension: takeaway_connection
**Phases:** takeaway through early backswing
**Good sign:** Arms and trunk move together; trail wrist path smooth without early independent lift (“pickup”);
pelvis rotation lags shoulders slightly on DTL.
**Poor sign:** Wrist-only takeaway with minimal torso turn; abrupt first move.
**Authority:** B+ kinematic chain literature; B teaching pro vocabulary.

### dimension: backswing_turn_and_width
**Phases:** backswing (through top)
**Good sign:** Shoulder turn away from target; lead arm reasonably extended (not collapsed across chest);
stable head vertical band on DTL; weight loads trail side without excessive sway off ball line.
**Poor sign:** Short turn with arms-only lift; large lateral slide instead of rotation; loss of posture (early extension of spine before top).
**Authority:** B+; variability high among skilled players — compare athlete to self across sessions.

### dimension: transition_sequence
**Phases:** transition, downswing
**Good sign:** Lower body initiates toward target while shoulders remain partially closed; wrist speed valley
at top followed by coordinated acceleration — pelvis rotation visible before maximum shoulder rotation rate toward target.
**Poor sign:** Shoulders and arms fire down while pelvis stalls (over-the-top body pattern proxy); immediate cast from top without lower-body shift.
**Authority:** B+ kinematic sequence reviews; B coaching.

### dimension: ground_force_use
**Phases:** transition through impact
**Good sign:** Visible push from trail leg into lead side; lead hip clears; ankles show purposeful pressure shift
(CoP themes — qualitative without force plates).
**Poor sign:** Upright swing with no leg drive; hanging back on trail side through impact proxy; early lead heel lift.
**Authority:** B+ GRF/CoP systematic review (2026); not force-plate scores in product v1.

### dimension: spine_angle_maintenance
**Phases:** backswing through impact
**Good sign:** Forward bend established at address largely preserved on DTL until after impact proxy; head stays
in stable corridor relative to pelvis.
**Poor sign:** Early extension — pelvis thrusts toward ball, spine angle increases before impact proxy (“standing up”).
**Authority:** B+ coaching and biomechanics; common fault card fault_early_extension.

### dimension: impact_posture_proxy
**Phases:** impact window
**Good sign:** Lead side firm; pelvis open; trail heel may rise on full driver swings without losing balance;
head behind or near setup position on DTL (coaching heuristic, not universal norm).
**Poor sign:** Heavy trail-side lean at peak wrist speed; lead knee collapsed inward severely.
**Authority:** B coaching; B+ variability in elite data.

### dimension: follow_through_balance
**Phases:** follow_through
**Good sign:** Weight finishes on lead foot; athlete holds finish 1+ seconds without stepping back or stumbling;
smooth wrist speed decay after peak.
**Poor sign:** Fall-back off lead foot; immediate recoil into address; harsh arm stop (“chicken wing” freeze).
**Authority:** B coaching; B+ deceleration load themes (awareness only).

### dimension: rhythm_tempo
**Phases:** whole chain address → follow_through
**Good sign:** Continuous motion without long freeze at top (> ~15 frames static wrists at 30 fps ≈ 0.5 s coach flag);
backswing and downswing durations in proportion for that athlete’s baseline.
**Poor sign:** Rush from top; or excessive pause then violent downswing (two-count hitch).
**Authority:** B coaching tempo; product compares self over time, not tour averages.

## Youth vs adult quality interpretation

**Youth (audience=youth):** Reward athletic setup, balanced finish, and smooth tempo over adult-amplitude shoulder
turn or aggressive leg drive. Shorter backswings and softer transition are normal. Avoid cueing maximum X-factor or
“snap” that increases spinal load. Follow Training/001 youth volume guidance.

**Adult recreational:** Emphasize transition_sequence, spine_angle_maintenance, and follow_through_balance to reduce
compensations that correlate with inconsistency in coaching observation (not injury prediction).

**Senior / limited mobility:** Narrower turn and shorter follow-through may still pass setup_balance and
follow_through_balance if symmetric and pain-free per athlete report.

---

## Scoring philosophy (product)

Each dimension maps to 0–1 confidence from pose quality, phase alignment, and camera view tags.
Low joint confidence caps scores. Missing address frames (trimmed clip) does not fail a swing if takeaway→finish visible.
