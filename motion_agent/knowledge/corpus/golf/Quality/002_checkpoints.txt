# Golf Sport Pack — Checkpoints catalog

Checkpoints are **pass/fail body observations** tied to phase IDs from Phases/001_phase_model.txt and detector ids in
`golf_phases.py`. Wording is qualitative; no universal degree targets. Fail links reference Findings/001_common_faults.txt.

---

## Setup & takeaway

### checkpoint_id: golf_setup_athletic_flex
**Phase:** address
**Dimension:** setup_balance
**Pass:** Knees show flexion vs straight lock; feet stable; pelvis between ankles on face-on when visible.
**Fail link:** fault_setup_locked_legs
**Measure proxy:** Knee angle trend vs extended reference; ankle speed near zero.
**Youth:** Pass with higher posture and narrower stance if balanced.

### checkpoint_id: golf_takeaway_one_piece
**Phase:** takeaway
**Dimension:** takeaway_connection
**Pass:** Trail wrist motion begins with visible torso rotation or stable spine — not isolated wrist lift only.
**Fail link:** fault_wrist_only_takeaway
**Measure proxy:** Torso yaw rate non-zero in same window as wrist speed rise.

### checkpoint_id: golf_backswing_turn
**Phase:** backswing
**Dimension:** backswing_turn_and_width
**Pass:** Shoulder line rotates away from target on DTL; backswing duration non-trivial vs takeaway.
**Fail link:** fault_short_arm_swing
**Measure proxy:** Shoulder yaw change address→top key_time.

## Top & transition

### checkpoint_id: golf_top_stable_posture
**Phase:** backswing (top key_time)
**Dimension:** spine_angle_maintenance
**Pass:** Forward spine tilt from address largely preserved at top on DTL; no major early pelvis thrust toward ball.
**Fail link:** fault_early_extension (if thrust begins before downswing)
**Measure proxy:** Pelvis–head line angle change vs address.

### checkpoint_id: golf_transition_lower_first
**Phase:** transition
**Dimension:** transition_sequence
**Pass:** Pelvis rotation toward target begins while shoulders still partially closed vs target line.
**Fail link:** fault_over_the_top_transition, fault_cast_from_top
**Measure proxy:** Pelvis yaw velocity sign flip before peak shoulder yaw toward target.

### checkpoint_id: golf_no_top_freeze_rush
**Phase:** transition → downswing
**Dimension:** rhythm_tempo
**Pass:** Motion continues from top without prolonged static wrists then single-frame explosion.
**Fail link:** fault_tempo_hitch_at_top
**Measure proxy:** Wrist speed near-zero duration at top window.

## Downswing & impact proxy

### checkpoint_id: golf_downswing_ground_shift
**Phase:** downswing
**Dimension:** ground_force_use
**Pass:** Weight shift toward lead side visible; lead hip clears before impact proxy; trail foot not static only.
**Fail link:** fault_hanging_back, fault_sway_not_turn
**Measure proxy:** Ankle/pelvis horizontal shift toward target; lead knee extension trend.

### checkpoint_id: golf_maintain_spine_tilt
**Phase:** downswing through impact
**Dimension:** spine_angle_maintenance
**Pass:** Spine angle from DTL does not dramatically increase (stand up) before impact proxy frame.
**Fail link:** fault_early_extension
**Measure proxy:** Trunk flexion angle trend downswing→impact.

### checkpoint_id: golf_impact_lead_side_firm
**Phase:** impact
**Dimension:** impact_posture_proxy
**Pass:** Lead leg appears braced; pelvis open; excessive trail-side lean absent on face-on.
**Fail link:** fault_hanging_back, fault_slide_excessive
**Measure proxy:** Lead knee flexion stable; pelvis position vs address.

## Finish

### checkpoint_id: golf_finish_weight_lead
**Phase:** follow_through
**Dimension:** follow_through_balance
**Pass:** Lead foot bears weight; trail foot light or on toe; athlete holds finish briefly.
**Fail link:** fault_fall_back_finish, fault_chicken_wing_stop
**Measure proxy:** Center of mass over lead ankle proxy; wrist speed smooth decay.

### checkpoint_id: golf_decel_smooth
**Phase:** follow_through
**Dimension:** follow_through_balance
**Pass:** Trail wrist speed decreases progressively after impact proxy without sharp hitch.
**Fail link:** fault_abrupt_deceleration
**Measure proxy:** Jerk on wrist speed post-peak.

## Short game note (future extension)

Pitch/chip clips may skip full backswing top valley; checkpoints above apply to **full swing** windows only.
Partial swings: use setup_balance and follow_through_balance only.

## Registry hooks

Each checkpoint_id is stable for coach briefs and future rule engine. Phase ids must match: address, takeaway,
backswing, transition, downswing, impact, follow_through.
