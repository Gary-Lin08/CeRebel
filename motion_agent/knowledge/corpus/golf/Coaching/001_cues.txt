# Golf Sport Pack — Feel cues (body-first)

Cues pair to finding IDs in Findings/001_common_faults.txt. Athlete-facing language; practice without ball or club is valid when noted.
**Disclaimer:** Coaching cues are not medical advice. Athletes with pain should stop and consult a qualified clinician.

---

## cue_fault_early_extension
**finding_id:** fault_early_extension
**phases:** downswing, impact
**feel:** Belt buckle stays away from the ball — turn your hips without standing up to peek.
**coach_note:** DTL camera essential; compare spine angle address vs impact proxy.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_early_extension_thrust
**finding_id:** fault_early_extension_thrust
**phases:** downswing, impact
**feel:** Turn the belt buckle to the flag — don't shove the hips at the ball.
**coach_note:** Face-on + DTL; separate thrust from intentional stack patterns.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_over_the_top_transition
**finding_id:** fault_over_the_top_transition
**phases:** transition, downswing
**feel:** Lead hip bumps toward the target before your chest faces the flag.
**coach_note:** Distinguish from slide — rotation must accompany bump.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_cast_from_top
**finding_id:** fault_cast_from_top
**phases:** transition, downswing
**feel:** Trail elbow stays bent longer — hands fall with gravity, not thrown at the ball.
**coach_note:** Slow-motion review at top→hip height window.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_hanging_back
**finding_id:** fault_hanging_back
**phases:** downswing, impact
**feel:** Push the ground with your trail foot until your lead side wins the tug-of-war.
**coach_note:** Pair with finish hold on lead foot drill.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_weight_shift_late
**finding_id:** fault_weight_shift_late
**phases:** impact, follow_through
**feel:** Lead foot presses as you start down — not after the hit.
**coach_note:** Compare lead ankle load timing vs impact proxy.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_sway_not_turn
**finding_id:** fault_sway_not_turn
**phases:** backswing, transition
**feel:** Turn your back pocket away from the target — hips rotate, not slide off the tee.
**coach_note:** Face-on view; head should stay in narrow vertical band.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_slide_excessive
**finding_id:** fault_slide_excessive
**phases:** downswing, impact
**feel:** Rotate into a firm lead leg — don’t surf sideways toward the target.
**coach_note:** Often co-occurs with fault_hanging_back on opposite reps — track trend.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_head_lateral_sway
**finding_id:** fault_head_lateral_sway
**phases:** takeaway, backswing, downswing
**feel:** Keep your head in a quiet box — turn under a still chin.
**coach_note:** Face-on; do not freeze neck rigidly.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_short_arm_swing
**finding_id:** fault_short_arm_swing
**phases:** backswing
**feel:** Lead shoulder travels under your chin — width comes from turn, not lifting elbows.
**coach_note:** Youth may have shorter turn by design; use self-comparison.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_incomplete_shoulder_turn
**finding_id:** fault_incomplete_shoulder_turn
**phases:** backswing, transition
**feel:** Finish the turn — trail knee loads before you start down.
**coach_note:** Baseline vs athlete warm-up, not elite degree targets.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_wrist_only_takeaway
**finding_id:** fault_wrist_only_takeaway
**phases:** takeaway
**feel:** Chest and club handle move together — no flipping the wrists first.
**coach_note:** First 12 inches of motion is the coaching window.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_takeaway_steep_lift
**finding_id:** fault_takeaway_steep_lift
**phases:** takeaway
**feel:** Push the handle low and wide first — then let it rise with the turn.
**coach_note:** Stop reps at knee height before full swings.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_takeaway_inside_roll
**finding_id:** fault_takeaway_inside_roll
**phases:** takeaway
**feel:** Glove logo faces the ball longer — chest turns, hands stay in front.
**coach_note:** DTL check lead arm ahead of trail hip at hip-high.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_tempo_hitch_at_top
**finding_id:** fault_tempo_hitch_at_top
**phases:** transition
**feel:** One smooth breath from top to hit — no pause at the sky.
**coach_note:** Metronome 3:1 helps; avoid forcing longer backswing.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_tempo_rush_downswing
**finding_id:** fault_tempo_rush_downswing
**phases:** transition, downswing
**feel:** Count three going back, one coming through — don't fire from the top.
**coach_note:** Pair with pump drill if cast co-occurs.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_fall_back_finish
**finding_id:** fault_fall_back_finish
**phases:** follow_through
**feel:** Finish on your lead leg like a statue facing the target.
**coach_note:** Trail foot toe touch only; heel up is fine on driver.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_chicken_wing_stop
**finding_id:** fault_chicken_wing_stop
**phases:** follow_through
**feel:** Arms reach toward the target together, then wrap — don’t pull the lead elbow back early.
**coach_note:** Check for hanging back on prior phase.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_flying_trail_elbow
**finding_id:** fault_flying_trail_elbow
**phases:** transition, downswing
**feel:** Trail elbow soft near your shirt seam as you start down.
**coach_note:** Towel-under-arm optional for feel only.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_lead_arm_collapse_top
**finding_id:** fault_lead_arm_collapse_top
**phases:** backswing, transition
**feel:** Soft-straight lead arm — width from turn, not from bending the elbow.
**coach_note:** Never force hyperextension; mobility limits OK.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_setup_locked_legs
**finding_id:** fault_setup_locked_legs
**phases:** address
**feel:** Athletic knees — soft enough to bounce, stable enough to push.
**coach_note:** Seniors: flex within comfort; no deep squat cue.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_setup_spine_too_upright
**finding_id:** fault_setup_spine_too_upright
**phases:** address
**feel:** Hinge from the hips — butt back, chest over the ball space.
**coach_note:** DTL spine inclination vs vertical.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_setup_narrow_stance
**finding_id:** fault_setup_narrow_stance
**phases:** address
**feel:** Feet under the outsides of your shoulders — athletic, not feet together.
**coach_note:** Chips may use narrower stance intentionally.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_setup_weight_heels
**finding_id:** fault_setup_weight_heels
**phases:** address
**feel:** Balls of the feet and heels share the load — ready athletic stance.
**coach_note:** Mid-foot bounce before first swing.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_reverse_pivot
**finding_id:** fault_reverse_pivot
**phases:** backswing, transition
**feel:** Load the trail leg like a coiled spring — don't lean into the target going back.
**coach_note:** Face-on pressure / ankle load proxy.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_impact_lead_knee_lock
**finding_id:** fault_impact_lead_knee_lock
**phases:** downswing, impact
**feel:** Brace the lead leg soft-firm — catch yourself, don't lock like a stick.
**coach_note:** Contrast with front-knee collapse patterns from other sports language — golf wants athletic brace.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_abrupt_deceleration
**finding_id:** fault_abrupt_deceleration
**phases:** follow_through
**feel:** Let the swing paint a full rainbow around your body — no emergency brake at the ball.
**coach_note:** Smooth wrist speed decay on graph review.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_incomplete_hip_clearance
**finding_id:** fault_incomplete_hip_clearance
**phases:** impact, follow_through
**feel:** Clear the lead hip behind you — make room for the arms to pass.
**coach_note:** Pair with slide vs rotate gate drill.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_chip_stand_up
**finding_id:** fault_chip_stand_up
**phases:** address, downswing, impact
**feel:** Stay in your chip posture — chest over the ball space until after the hit.
**coach_note:** Short swing length; posture window only.
**authority:** B

## cue_fault_chip_trail_weight
**finding_id:** fault_chip_trail_weight
**phases:** address, impact, follow_through
**feel:** Lean slightly into the lead side before you chip — stay there through the finish.
**coach_note:** ~60% lead pressure feel; not force-plate truth.
**authority:** B

## Positive cues (no fault id)

### cue_balanced_setup
**phases:** address
**feel:** Weight in the middle of your feet — toes and heels both feel the ground.
**authority:** B

### cue_full_finish_hold
**phases:** follow_through
**feel:** Hold your finish until the club stops moving — count “one-one-thousand.”
**authority:** B

### cue_tempo_continuous
**phases:** address through follow_through
**feel:** Backswing and downswing share one heartbeat — no second wind-up at the top.
**authority:** B

---

## Youth scaling

Use one cue per rep. Prefer balance, tempo, and finish holds over “snap” or maximum turn cues.
Reference Training/001 for session length and multi-sport recovery.

Sources: PGA/LPGA instructor education (B); Findings/001 linkage (product); biomechanics themes summarized (B+).
