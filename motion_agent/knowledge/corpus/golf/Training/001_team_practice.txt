# Golf Sport Pack — Team & practice organization (body-first)

Summarizes open national-governing-body and sport-science guidance for **structuring practice** when CeRebel
captures body motion (range, team academy, school golf). Not a substitute for professional coaching certification.
**Youth load:** summarize principles with pointers to official resources — do not reproduce proprietary load tables.

---

## Practice goals aligned with product

1. **Repeatable capture:** Stable camera, one primary swing per clip, consistent DTL or face-on angle.
2. **Phase-rich reps:** Full swings from address through balanced finish (matches Phases/001).
3. **Quality over volume:** Fewer focused reps beat fatigued blocks for pose-based feedback.
4. **Body-first stations:** Stations below need no ball tracking; launch monitor optional for separate analytics lane.

---

## Sample team stations (60–90 min academy block)

### Station A — Setup & tempo (15 min)
- drill_athletic_address_bounce + cue_balanced_setup
- drill_metronome_3_to_1 (Coaching/002)
- Capture: 3 shadow swings filmed for automated phase cards

### Station B — Turn & takeaway (15 min)
- drill_cross_shoulder_turn + drill_takeaway_pause_check
- Coach checkpoint: golf_takeaway_one_piece, golf_backswing_turn

### Station C — Transition & ground shift (20 min)
- drill_step_through_shadow + drill_pump_from_top
- Capture: 2 filmed swings DTL emphasizing transition_sequence dimension

### Station D — Finish & balance (15 min)
- drill_lead_foot_finish_hold + drill_full_rainbow_finish
- Team challenge: hold finish 3 s without trail foot replant

### Station E — Optional skills (15 min, ball allowed)
- On-course or range hitting **separate** from CeRebel scoring pipeline unless product integrates launch data later.
- Keep body-capture station ball-free for consistent pose signal.

Rotate groups of 3–4 athletes per station; one coach operator manages camera tripod.

## Individual practice (recreational)

**Weekly template (3 sessions × 30–40 min body-focused):**
- Session 1: Setup + takeaway drills; film 5 drivers or longest club comfortable.
- Session 2: Transition drills + wall spine tilt; film 5 mid-irons or hybrid shadows.
- Session 3: Finish holds + tempo metronome; film 5 wedges full swing.

Compare phase timing to prior week in product — not to tour benchmarks.

---

## Filming & environment

- **Tripod height:** Roughly hand height, centered on athlete sternum; DTL perpendicular to target line proxy.
- **Lighting:** Avoid backlit sunset; silhouette breaks joint confidence.
- **Clothing:** Contrast against background; avoid identical colors to range mat.
- **Safety:** Ensure clearance for full follow-through; station spacing ≥ 2 club lengths between athletes in shadow work.

## Youth development (audience=youth)

**Principles (summarized from public PGA Jr. League and LPGA* Girls Golf themes — authority B):**
- Emphasize fun, athletic movement, and balance over maximizing clubhead speed.
- Limit repetitive maximal-effort driver swings when athletes report fatigue or growth-related discomfort.
- Multi-sport participation supported by general pediatric sport science — avoid year-round single-sport overload for pre-teen athletes (consult latest ACSM / NGB position summaries via SOURCE_MANIFEST URLs).
- **Session length:** 45–60 min total for 8–12 age groups with water breaks; skill stations over continuous hitting.
- **Cues:** One cue per swing; positive finish holds; no aggressive “snap” coaching for developing athletes.
- **Capture:** Parent/coach films from safe distance; avoid distracting athlete mid-swing.

**Red flags:** Reported pain, dizziness, or joint swelling — stop activity; medical evaluation required (not diagnosable from video).

## High school / collegiate team notes

- Pre-practice: dynamic warm-up (general movement prep — leg swings, thoracic rotation openers) 8–10 min; not a replacement for team AT protocols.
- Pair athletes for peer filming; coach reviews CeRebel phase cards in classroom mode post-practice.
- Periodization: integrate body-capture review days on technical weeks; reduce filming volume during tournament weeks to limit cognitive load.

---

## Adaptive & senior golf

- Allow shorter backswings and narrower stance while still scoring setup_balance and follow_through_balance.
- Chair/seated modifications for balance-impaired athletes — phases may truncate; document `trim_start_event` in metadata when address absent.
