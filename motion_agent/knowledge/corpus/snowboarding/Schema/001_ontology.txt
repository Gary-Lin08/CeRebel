# Snowboarding Sport Pack — Ontology (Schema)

## Entity: Rider

Attributes:
- rider_id (string, optional external)
- stance_orientation: regular | goofy | unknown
- audience_band: youth | adult | unknown
- lead_foot: left | right (derived from stance when known)

Relations:
- performs → RideInstance
- subject_of → FindingInstance

## Entity: RideInstance

Attributes:
- clip_id (session reference)
- terrain_context: flat | gentle_pitch | unknown
- turn_family: heel_side | toe_side | linked | traverse_only | unknown

Relations:
- has_phase_sequence → SnowboardPhase (ordered)
- observed_on → BiomechObservation

## Entity: TurnFamily

Enum (coaching tags, not auto-classified in v1):
- heel_side
- toe_side
- linked
- traverse_only

Attributes:
- turn_family_id (stable slug)
- fis_glossary_terms optional (frontside, backside — narrative only)

## Entity: SnowboardPhase

Attributes:
- phase_id (e.g., S_READY, T_APEX, T_TRANSITION)
- phase_family: stance | turn | transition
- ordinal (integer order within turn chain)
- description_plain (English)

Relations:
- follows → SnowboardPhase
- contains_events → PhaseEvent

## Entity: PhaseEvent

Attributes:
- event_id
- start_rule (narrative, body-only)
- end_rule (narrative)
- detector_proxy (optional engineering string)

Examples:
- apex_body_proxy — max lateral trunk inclination or knee angulation peak
- initiate_knee_flex — lead knee flexion rise after traverse
- transition_flatten_proxy — local min trunk inclination between apex peaks

## Entity: Finding

Attributes:
- finding_id (slug, prefix fault_ or concern_ for safety-adjacent)
- headline (plain language)
- phase_id (primary)
- turn_family optional
- severity_hint: info | form | balance | load | safety
- body_signals (list of measurable descriptions)
- why_it_matters (performance, balance, control — non-diagnostic)
- authority_level: B+ | B typical; A+ only when tied to FIS equipment rules via SafetyGuideline link
- evidence_type: CoachingConsensus | BiomechRelationship | SafetyGuidelineRef

Relations:
- has_cue → Cue
- has_drill → Drill
- anchored_in → SnowboardPhase

## Entity: Cue

Attributes:
- cue_id
- finding_id (foreign)
- feel_text (athlete-facing, body-first)
- coach_text (optional longer)

## Entity: Drill

Attributes:
- drill_id
- finding_id
- duration_sec (30–90 typical)
- setup (minimal equipment; prefer on-snow lesson progressions)
- instructions (ordered steps)
- off_snow_variant optional (carpet, balance board — B coaching)

## Entity: Checkpoint

Attributes:
- checkpoint_id
- phase_id
- quality_dimension (from Quality/001)
- series_id (measurement surface)
- pass_description (body observable)
- fail_links → Finding (optional)

## Entity: SafetyGuideline

Attributes:
- guideline_id (e.g., safety_helmet_on_snow)
- headline
- authority_level: A+ for FIS compulsory equipment rules; B+ for general on-snow safety
- audience_band: youth | adult | all
- product_action: remind | redirect | stop_and_seek_care (for clinical redirects in Disclaimer only)

Relations:
- cites → EvidenceChunk (fis/ paths)
- related_finding → Finding (optional, e.g., concern_fall_stiff_brace)

Not a performance Finding — surfaced in Coaching and Docs/002.

## Entity: LoadConcern

Attributes:
- concern_id (e.g., load_concussion_redirect)
- description
- audience_band
- authority_level A | A+ for clinical consensus summaries (Disclaimer)

Used in Training/ and Docs — not scored as form faults.

## Entity: EquipmentRule

Attributes:
- rule_id (e.g., fis_crash_helmet_compulsory)
- equipment_type: helmet | back_protector (SBX — reference only)
- standard_refs (CE, ASTM F2040, EN 1077 — narrative, cite manifest)
- authority_level: A+

Relations:
- applies_to → SafetyGuideline

## Entity: EvidenceChunk (RAG metadata pattern)

Attributes:
- source_id (from SOURCE_MANIFEST)
- authority_level
- evidence_type
- retrieved_at
- corpus_path optional (e.g., fis/Rules/008_precisions-sbfsfk-southern-hemisphere-2627.txt)

## Naming conventions

- Turn phase IDs: prefix T_
- Static stance phase: prefix S_
- Finding IDs: prefix fault_ (form); concern_ (safety-adjacent observable)
- Safety guideline IDs: prefix safety_
- Cue IDs: cue_<finding_id>
- Drill IDs: drill_<finding_id>

## Handedness / lead-trail mapping

Default **regular** (left foot forward):
- lead leg = left; trail leg = right
- Goofy: mirror lead/trail in product logic; corpus may say "lead knee"

## Measurement definitions (series_id)

JointAngle: knee_angle_lead, knee_angle_trail, hip_flex proxy
SegmentOrientation: trunk_inclination_lateral, pelvis_yaw, thorax_yaw
Derived: knee_angulation, shoulder_pelvis_separation, com_lateral_offset

All observations are proxies — no snowboard edge angle entity in v1.
