================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for **beginner–intermediate alpine riding** on groomed
terrain: **stance**, **linked turns**, **edge control**, and **body mechanics**
(angulation, flex/extension, upper–lower body separation). Safety content covers
**falls**, **falling technique awareness**, and **helmet / protective equipment**
(FIS-aligned where competition equipment is cited).

In scope v1:
- Regular / goofy stance identification (body-facing copy; binding angles not required)
- Heel-side and toe-side turn chains as body patterns (no edge sensor)
- Traverse, initiation, apex, release, transition between turns
- Athletic stance: knee flex, hip position, quiet upper body, head/eyes level
- Static or slow-gliding stance checks in short clips

Out of scope v1 (glossary / future only):
- Halfpipe, slopestyle, big air trick catalog (see fis/ + snowboard_aerial_vocab.txt)
- Board tuning, wax, sidecut radius, binding DIN (equipment not visible reliably)
- Avalanche, backcountry route planning, tree-well rescue
- Medical diagnosis, concussion return-to-sport (redirect in Docs/002_DISCLAIMER)
- Judging scores, race gates, SBX start mechanics

Build agent writes full Findings/Coaching bodies from this map; brain agent does not.

2. PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

- Measurable claims tie to reconstructed body: knee/hip flexion, trunk lean, pelvis
  rotation, ankle/knee asymmetry proxies, COM over feet, head stability.
- **No snowboard edge angle** from video alone — use body proxies (knee angulation,
  hip lead, lateral trunk inclination vs fall line proxy from global motion).
- **Turn apex proxy** = frame of max lateral trunk inclination or max knee flexion
  asymmetry mid-clip (detector TBD; document in Phases/001).
- Youth vs adult: separate audience bands for speed/terrain language; no adult
  "charge hard" cues for youth learners.
- Authority: B+ / B for NGB and open coaching consensus; **A+ for FIS helmet rules**
  when citing competition equipment; no C in finding thresholds.
- Never invent clinical angle norms as injury prevention guarantees.

4. PHASE MODEL (7 PHASES, BODY-ONLY EVENTS)
================================================================================

Linked-turn chain (reuse for heel-side and toe-side; tag edge_side in copy):

| phase_id      | label_en        | label_zh (build) | role |
|---------------|-----------------|------------------|------|
| T_TRAVERSE    | Traverse / setup| 横滑/预备        | Cross-hill or pre-turn balance; knees soft |
| T_INITIATE    | Initiation      | 入弯/换刃准备    | Begin edge engagement; pressure into lead leg |
| T_BUILD       | Edge build      | 加压/立刃        | Increasing angulation; board curve proxy via body |
| T_APEX        | Apex            | 弯心             | Max edge / max turn force (body proxy peak) |
| T_RELEASE     | Release         | 释压/出弯        | Reduce edge pressure; extend through legs |
| T_EXIT        | Exit            | 出弯完成         | Turn complete; prepare next direction |
| T_TRANSITION  | Transition      | 换刃过渡         | Flatten or edge change between turns |

Static stance chain (short clips, no full turn):

| phase_id   | label_en     | role |
|------------|--------------|------|
| S_READY    | Ready stance | Athletic flex; hands quiet; gaze forward |

Typical clip (3–10 s turn): T_TRAVERSE → T_INITIATE → T_BUILD → T_APEX → T_RELEASE
→ T_EXIT → (T_TRANSITION if linked) → …

Event definitions (OpenSim-friendly, no board track):

- apex_event: argmax |lateral_trunk_inclination| OR max (|knee_flex_lead − knee_flex_trail|)
  in turn window (build picks one primary in Phases/001).
- initiate_event: first sustained increase in lead-knee flexion after traverse stability.
- transition_event: local minimum in lateral trunk inclination between apex peaks (linked turns).

Phases/001_phase_model.txt (build): expand events, fallbacks, EN/ZH labels, scrub UX,
camera caveats (foreshortening when camera is uphill/downhill).

6. COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED — NOT MEDICAL ADVICE)
================================================================================

Wording: "common teaching emphasis" / "typical in open coaching literature" (B+).
Do not cite exact edge angles in degrees on the snowboard — not observable in v1.

ADULT (groomed green–blue terrain; B+ unless noted):

- Stance width: roughly shoulder-width between feet (binding stance proxy via ankle
  separation when visible); avoid extreme narrow or wide without purpose.
- Knees: soft athletic flex (~15–40° band heuristic on model knees); avoid locked
  legs in turns and traverses.
- Hips: centered over board (COM between ankles); avoid persistent "back seat" with
  hips far behind ankles and stiff uphill leg.
- Upper body: quiet chest; shoulders roughly parallel to board direction proxy;
  head up, eyes toward intended path — not buried in snow or sky-gazing.
- Turn initiation: lower body leads (knees/hips); avoid shoulders rotating into turn
  before pelvis (upper-body rotation fault).
- Edge control feel: pressure through whole foot; flex to absorb; extend to release
  (coaching language — not measured edge pressure).

YOUTH (lessons / family terrain; B / B+):

- Smaller turns, slower speed emphasis; shorter traverse segments.
- No "send it" / cliff / park progression in product findings.
- Helmet always for on-snow activity (see safety section); wrist guards optional
  community guidance — mark B, not product threshold.
