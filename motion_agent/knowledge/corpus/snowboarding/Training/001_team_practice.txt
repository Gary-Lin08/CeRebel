# Snowboarding — Training and Practice Structure
# Authority: B (instructor / NGB progression patterns); not a substitute for on-snow certification
# Audience tags: youth | adult | team_clinic | recreational

================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This file describes how to organize practice sessions that align with CeRebel phase models and finding cards.
It supports coaches, school teams, and recreational riders building repeatable reps for video analysis.
It does not prescribe competition periodization for FIS calendars — see fis/ for event rules.

================================================================================
SESSION TEMPLATE — RECREATIONAL HALF DAY (2–3 hours on snow)
================================================================================

Block 1 — Warm-up and mobility (15 min, flat or gentle green)
- Boot walk, ankle circles, squat holds (drill_wall_squat_boots).
- One slow traverse each direction focusing on checkpoint_athletic_flex_traverse.
- Safety: spacing on cat tracks; never block lift lines.

Block 2 — Skill theme: alpine linked turns (45–60 min)
- Theme example: "quiet upper body" (fault_shoulder_leads_turn).
- Progression: garland speed bleed → j-turn lead press → 6 linked turns on green.
- Video: one follow-cam clip per rider for CeRebel review (optional).
- Rest: 5 min hydration each 30 min cold exposure.

Block 3 — Skill theme: air basics (30–45 min, park or small jump per ability)
- Prerequisites: helmet, terrain park etiquette, coach sign-off for jump features.
- Ladder: compress_extend_flat → absorb_roller → straight_air_shape (Coaching/002_drills).
- Stop ladder on stiff landing finding or rider fatigue.

Block 4 — Free ride / integration (30 min)
- Apply morning theme on easy blue; no new skills introduced.
- Debrief: one cue per rider from automated or coach observation.

Youth modification:
- Shorter park block; emphasize speed control and landing absorption over air time.
- Adult-to-youth ratio per resort policy (often 1:6 beginner, tighter for park).

================================================================================
SESSION TEMPLATE — TEAM CLINIC (8–12 athletes, 3 hours)
================================================================================

Station rotation (15 min × 3 stations × 2 rounds = 90 min core)

Station A — Alpine initiation (coach at fall line view)
- drill_j_turn_lead_press
- drill_hip_lead_cone
- Metrics: fault_no_lead_load, fault_shoulder_leads_turn

Station B — Turn shape and completion (coach at side view)
- drill_railroad_tracks
- drill_straight_glide_reset
- Metrics: fault_upright_skid, fault_stuck_in_turn

Station C — Jump fundamentals (jump coach + park flagger)
- drill_compress_extend_flat
- drill_absorb_roller
- drill_two_foot_stick (flat) before any kicker
- Metrics: fault_no_preload, fault_stiff_leg_landing

Full-group debrief (20 min):
- Show one anonymized clip; map visible phase scrub bar to athlete experience.
- Assign one homework cue + one drill from Coaching/ for next session.

Equipment check (10 min at start):
- Helmets for park; bindings visually inspected; riders confirm leash policy where required.

================================================================================
PROGRESSION LADDER — ALPINE (skill levels, not ages)
================================================================================

Level 1 — Wedge / sideslip control
- Phases emphasized: traverse, completion
- Findings often: fault_locked_legs_traverse, fault_rushed_entry (low severity)
- Avoid: aggressive apex lean cues

Level 2 — Basic linked skidded turns
- Phases: full alpine chain at low speed
- Findings: fault_upright_skid (learning), fault_shoulder_leads_turn

Level 3 — Dynamic skidded turns on blue terrain
- Phases: transition quality gains weight
- Findings: fault_hoppy_transition, fault_straight_leg_apex

Level 4 — Carving introduction (optional track)
- Requires appropriate terrain; still body-proxy only in product
- Findings: fault_no_lead_load, fault_head_misaligned

Level 5 — Small park / rollers (parallel track with alpine)
- Introduce family=air_jump on rollers before kickers

================================================================================
PROGRESSION LADDER — AIR / PARK
================================================================================

Step 0 — Flat ground mechanics (off-feature)
- preload, extension, landing absorption dry patterns

Step 1 — Rollers and berms without leave-ground intent

Step 2 — Ollie on flat and small pop

Step 3 — Straight air small kicker (Coaching drill_straight_air_shape)

Step 4 — Grab introduction with RGB confirmation (not automated v1)

Step 5 — Spin entry only with dedicated spin coach and feature progression

Do not advance steps while fault_stiff_leg_landing or fault_one_foot_landing persists at current step.

================================================================================
DRYLAND / PRE-SEASON (optional, 30–45 min gym)
================================================================================

- Squat and lunge patterns for eccentric strength (general S&C, B — not sport-specific load prescription).
- Balance board or bosu: quiet stance proxy for fault_arm_flail_traverse.
- Video review classroom: phase IDs without on-snow risk.

Youth: emphasize play-based balance; avoid maximal plyometric volume.

================================================================================
INTEGRATION WITH CeRebel PRODUCT
================================================================================

Recommended clip capture for analysis:
- Alpine: side view, full turn, 60 fps when possible, 3–5 linked turns per clip.
- Air: follow-cam showing approach, takeoff, landing; include 0.5 s recover post-land.
- Metadata: family tag, stance if known, terrain difficulty, switch=true when applicable.

Coach workflow:
1. Rank findings by severity and phase alignment.
2. Assign one cue (Coaching/001) and one drill (Coaching/002) per priority fault.
3. Re-shoot after 20–40 min deliberate practice block.

For FIS rule questions (course inspection, helmet standards, SBX testing protocol), use agent RAG over fis/ —
not this Training file.

================================================================================
SAFETY AND SUPERVISION MINIMUMS
================================================================================

- Match terrain to ability; follow resort signage and park feature size labels.
- Never practice inverted aerials without qualified progression environment.
- Head injury protocol: stop riding after significant head impact; seek medical evaluation.
- Group management: stop above rider, never below on narrow trails.

See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt for full safe-use language.

================================================================================
SAMPLE WEEK — IN-SEASON REC TEAM (adult, 3 on-snow days)
================================================================================

Day 1: Alpine theme — transitions (drill_silent_transition, drill_straight_glide_reset)
Day 2: Air theme — preload and absorption ladder only
Day 3: Integration free ride + one video review evening (indoor)

Adjust volume for cold, altitude, and fatigue. Skip park day if stiff landing faults cluster in prior session.
