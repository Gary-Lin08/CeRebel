# Snowboarding — Phase Model (v1)
# Scope: alpine linked turn (family=alpine_turn) and basic jump (family=air_jump)
# Aligns: body-only OpenSim / pose keypoints; board not required
# Stable phase IDs — do not rename without migration note in _meta/pack_readme.txt

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Two motion families share ontology but use different phase chains. Most resort coaching clips map to
**alpine_turn** (carving or skidded linked turns). **air_jump** covers ollies, small kickers, and park jumps
where takeoff and landing are visible.

Stance convention:
- **Regular**: left foot lead (forward), right foot trail.
- **Goofy**: right foot lead, left foot trail.
- **Unknown stance**: use “lead leg” / “trail leg” in copy; mirror joint names when stance is set in metadata.

Handedness is irrelevant; lead/trail and frontside/backside rotation language follow snowboard convention
(see snowboard_aerial_vocab.txt for aerial terms).

================================================================================
EVENT DEFINITIONS — ALPINE (body-only)
================================================================================

E_TRAVERSE_STABLE
  Definition: rolling std of pelvis speed and knee flexion rate below threshold for ≥10 frames.
  Fallback: first frame of clip.
  Use: trim dead time before turn; completion phase end when stable re-achieved.

E_TURN_INIT
  Definition: horizontal COM path curvature increases AND lead-knee flexion begins eccentric phase before apex.
  Proxy: sign change in lateral COM acceleration + d(knee_flex_lead)/dt < -threshold for 3 frames.
  Fallback: 15% of clip duration from start if no clear traverse.

E_EDGE_ENGAGE
  Definition: trunk lean proxy (shoulder midpoint vs pelvis lateral offset) exceeds turn-specific baseline.
  Proxy: |offset_x(shoulders - pelvis)| crosses upper quartile of pre-turn window.
  Note: not true edge angle — camera-dependent; flag low confidence on head-on camera.

E_STEER_MID
  Definition: midpoint between E_EDGE_ENGAGE and E_TURN_APEX by COM path arc length (planar proxy).
  Fallback: temporal midpoint if path noisy.

E_TURN_APEX
  Definition: local maximum of lateral lean proxy OR local minimum turn radius proxy on COM path.
  Proxy: argmax |shoulder_pelvis_lateral_offset| in [E_EDGE_ENGAGE, E_UNWEIGHT_START].

E_UNWEIGHT_START
  Definition: knee extension rate increases on both legs with decreasing lean magnitude (unweighting for edge change).
  Proxy: d(avg_knee_angle)/dt > +threshold while |lean| decreases for 3 frames.

E_NEW_EDGE
  Definition: lean proxy sign crosses zero OR lead/trail knee flexion pattern inverts relative to apex window.
  Marks start of completion phase toward next traverse.

================================================================================
EVENT DEFINITIONS — AIR (body-only)
================================================================================

E_PRELOAD_START
  Definition: approach segment with relatively steady COM speed followed by increasing knee/hip flexion.
  Proxy: avg knee flexion increases monotonically for ≥5 frames before takeoff.

E_TAKEOFF
  Definition: argmax vertical velocity of pelvis/COM in analysis window.
  Primary timing anchor for air family.
  Note: without board contact model, this proxies pop off snow or kicker lip.

E_AIR_PEAK
  Definition: local maximum COM height after E_TAKEOFF.
  Proxy: argmax pelvis_y in [E_TAKEOFF, E_LAND_CONTACT].

E_LAND_CONTACT
  Definition: first frame after E_AIR_PEAK where vertical COM velocity becomes strongly negative and knee flexion
  rate spikes (impact absorption onset).
  Proxy: min COM_y in landing window OR max d(knee_flex)/dt after peak.
  Fallback: E_AIR_PEAK + 40% of remaining clip.

E_LAND_STABLE
  Definition: E_LAND_CONTACT + frames until COM speed and knee flexion rate settle below threshold.
  Used for landing balance findings.

================================================================================
TIMING RELATIONSHIPS (qualitative, B+ / B literature)
================================================================================

Alpine:
- Skilled riders often show progressive lead-leg loading into initiation, then symmetric or counter-rotated
  upper body through steering (style-dependent).
- Transition phases are brief; excessive upper-body rotation early can precede washed-out turns (coaching B).

Air:
- Preload depth and timing correlate with takeoff height in training populations (B+); product flags timing and
  symmetry, not absolute air time without calibration.
- Landing absorption: peak knee flexion typically occurs within ~100–300 ms of contact proxy (frame-rate dependent).

Youth: smaller amplitude in preload and landing; shorter flight — use youth-tagged checkpoints in Quality/002.

================================================================================
CLIP TRIMMING GUIDANCE
================================================================================

- Alpine: include full turn from stable traverse through re-center; avoid clipping only apex unless comparing apex shape.
- Air: include approach ≥0.5 s before preload when possible; include recover ≥0.3 s after landing.
- Switch riding: same events; metadata tag `switch=true` for cue copy only in v1.

================================================================================
CAPTURE ANGLES
================================================================================

| angle              | alpine confidence | air confidence |
|--------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Side / fall line   | High              | High           |
| Front / back       | Low (lean hidden) | Moderate       |
| Follow-cam         | Moderate          | High for takeoff/land |
