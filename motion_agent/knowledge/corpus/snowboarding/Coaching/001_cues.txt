fault_locked_legs_traverse
================================================================================
finding_id: fault_locked_legs_traverse
phases: traverse
primary_cues:
  - "Athletic ready — soft knees, ankles loose like shock absorbers."
  - "Shin on tongue — feel the front of the boot, not locked calves."
avoid_saying:
  - "Bend knees to 90 degrees" (no universal degree target without measurement context).
feel_bridge: Connects to checkpoint_athletic_flex_traverse.

fault_no_lead_load
================================================================================
finding_id: fault_no_lead_load

  - "Start the turn from the front binding — press the lead foot."
  - "Nose follows your press; tail follows naturally."

fault_shoulder_leads_turn
================================================================================
finding_id: fault_shoulder_leads_turn
phases: initiation, steering
primary_cues:
  - "Belt buckle points down the hill — hips lead, chest quiet."
  - "Separate upper and lower: shoulders stay over the board longer."
advanced_note:
  Counter-rotation in dynamic turns is intentional at high skill — do not over-cue elites from pose alone.

================================================================================
fault_upright_skid
================================================================================
finding_id: fault_upright_skid
phases: steering, apex
primary_cues:
  - "Tilt with the whole body — knees into the hill, not just ankles."
  - "Feel pressure on the uphill edge of your boots through the turn."
youth_note:
  On green terrain, partial skids are OK while learning — cue patience before aggressive angulation.
