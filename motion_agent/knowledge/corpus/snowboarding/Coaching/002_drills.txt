# Snowboarding — Drills
# Linked to finding_id; duration 30–90 s unless noted; authority B coaching unless noted

================================================================================
DRILL FORMAT
================================================================================

Each drill lists: drill_id, finding_id, family, terrain, steps, safety, progression.

================================================================================
drill_soft_ankle_traverse
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_soft_ankle_traverse
finding_id: fault_locked_legs_traverse
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Green / gentle blue, wide open
duration: 60–90 s active riding per repetition; 3 reps
steps:
  1. Traverse fall line at moderate speed with visibly flexed knees.
  2. Every 15 m, pulse ankles/knees through 3 small flex-extension cycles without stopping.
  3. Resume traverse; note quieter ride over micro-terrain.
safety: Clear uphill visibility; buddy stop above on crowded days.
progression: Remove pulse; maintain flex through linked turns.

================================================================================
drill_quiet_hands_pockets
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_quiet_hands_pockets
finding_id: fault_arm_flail_traverse
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Green
duration: 45 s (3–4 turns)
steps:
  1. Light fingertips on front hip pockets for two complete turns.
  2. Release arms to neutral for two turns; compare shoulder rotation in video.
safety: Do not tuck hands inside pockets if balance is uncertain — use light touch only.

================================================================================
drill_garland_speed_bleed
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_garland_speed_bleed
finding_id: fault_rushed_entry
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Gentle blue
duration: 90 s
steps:
  1. Traverse across hill; initiate turn uphill until speed drops noticeably.
  2. Complete turn back to fall line without straight-lining.
  3. Repeat 5 garlands before attempting full linked turns down slope.
progression: Shorten uphill segment as control improves.

================================================================================
drill_j_turn_lead_press
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_j_turn_lead_press
finding_id: fault_no_lead_load
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Flat to green pitch
duration: 60 s
steps:
  1. From slow traverse, press lead foot to start J-turn until board nearly points uphill.
  2. Stop; switch sides. 4 each direction.
stance: Call out lead foot explicitly for regular/goofy groups.

drill_hip_lead_cone
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_hip_lead_cone
finding_id: fault_shoulder_leads_turn
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Green with visual marker (cone, tree)
duration: 60 s
steps:
  1. Place marker at turn exit; rider looks at marker only after hips initiate turn.
  2. Coach verbal "hips" at initiation for 6 turns.
progression: Remove verbal cue; review video for shoulder lead timing.

drill_wall_squat_boots
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_wall_squat_boots
finding_id: fault_collapsed_torso
family: both
terrain: Flat staging area (not on lift ramp)
duration: 45 s (8 slow reps)
steps:
  1. Boots on, board unstrapped or one foot strapped per resort policy.
  2. Squat with back/skis against lodge wall or imaginary wall — knees forward, chest tall.
safety: Flat ground only; not on cat tracks or slopes.

drill_railroad_tracks
================================================================================
drill_id: drill_railroad_tracks
finding_id: fault_upright_skid
family: alpine_turn
terrain: Very gentle green
duration: 90 s
steps:
  1. Attempt matched edge tracks in short-radius turns — focus on tilt sensation.
  2. If skidding persists, reduce turn size and speed.
youth: Primary learning drill; not a "fail" environment.

================================================================================

finding_id: fault_one_foot_landing
family: air_jump
terrain: Flat snow or gym mat off-snow
duration: 45 s
steps:
