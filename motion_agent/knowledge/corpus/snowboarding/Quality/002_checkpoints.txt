# Snowboarding — Checkpoint Catalog
# Format: checkpoint_id | phase | family | body signal | pass heuristic | fail → finding | authority

================================================================================
TRAVERSE / SETUP (alpine_turn)
================================================================================

checkpoint_athletic_flex_traverse
  phase: traverse
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: avg knee flexion, hip flexion, ankle dorsiflexion proxy
  pass: visible flex at knees and hips; not locked straight legs while moving
  fail: extended knees with upright trunk “locking” through traverse → fault_locked_legs_traverse
  authority: B (instructor consensus)
  youth: same; smaller absolute flex OK

checkpoint_quiet_arms_traverse
  phase: traverse
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: wrist speed relative to COM speed
  pass: arms low-moderate motion vs gross flailing
  fail: sustained high wrist speed / wide arm swing unrelated to turn prep → fault_arm_flail_traverse
  authority: B

checkpoint_com_stable_speed
  phase: traverse
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: COM speed coefficient of variation over traverse window
  pass: controlled speed before init; no chaotic acceleration spike unless tagged race_start
  fail: erratic speed into initiation → fault_rushed_entry
  authority: B

================================================================================
ANTICIPATION / INITIATION (alpine_turn)
================================================================================

checkpoint_progressive_lead_load
  phase: anticipation, initiation
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: lead-knee flexion increase before E_TURN_APEX vs trail knee
  pass: lead leg shows measurable load progression (style allows counter-flex variants — prefer unclear if ambiguous)
  fail: no lead-side loading before apex with upright skid posture → fault_no_lead_load
  authority: B+

checkpoint_upper_body_quiet_init
  phase: initiation
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: shoulder rotation rate vs pelvis yaw proxy
  pass: shoulders do not lead turn by large independent twist before steering phase
  fail: early counter-rotation spike → fault_shoulder_leads_turn
  authority: B

checkpoint_flex_not_collapse
  phase: anticipation
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: trunk height vs knee flexion (hip hinge vs collapse)
  pass: hips back modestly; head stable relative to pelvis
  fail: chest dropped toward knees with loss of spine length → fault_collapsed_torso
  authority: B

================================================================================
STEERING / APEX (alpine_turn)
================================================================================

checkpoint_lateral_lean_present
  phase: steering, apex
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: shoulder–pelvis lateral offset (2D proxy for angulation)
  pass: measurable lean into turn when camera angle supports
  fail: upright “vertical stack” through apex at moderate speed → fault_upright_skid
  authority: B+ (carved vs skidded distinction approximate without board track)
  note: low confidence on head-on camera → unclear

checkpoint_knee_flex_maintained_apex
  phase: apex
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: knee flexion at E_TURN_APEX vs traverse
  pass: knees remain flexed; not fully extended at max lean
  fail: leg extension at max lean → fault_straight_leg_apex
  authority: B

checkpoint_head_exit_direction
  phase: steering, apex
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: head orientation proxy toward turn exit (when visible)
  pass: head generally oriented down fall line or exit (coaching “look where you go”)
  fail: head rotated strongly into snow or up hill → fault_head_misaligned
  authority: B

================================================================================
TRANSITION / COMPLETION (alpine_turn)
================================================================================

checkpoint_smooth_unweight
  phase: transition
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: rate of change of lean magnitude + knee extension timing
  pass: brief unweight; not prolonged hop or pivot-only skid
  fail: abrupt extension hop with COM pop → fault_hoppy_transition
  authority: B

checkpoint_completion_recenter
  phase: completion
  family: alpine_turn
  signal: COM path returns toward stable traverse metrics
  pass: shoulders and pelvis align toward new traverse within window
  fail: sustained counter-rotated stuck posture → fault_stuck_in_turn
  authority: B

================================================================================
APPROACH / PRELOAD (air_jump)
================================================================================

checkpoint_approach_balanced
  phase: approach
  family: air_jump
  signal: lateral COM drift, shoulder level
  pass: COM over base; shoulders level within tolerance
  fail: large lateral lean or open shoulders → fault_off_balance_approach
  authority: B

checkpoint_preload_depth_controlled
  phase: preload
  family: air_jump
  signal: knee/hip flexion delta from approach to E_TAKEOFF
  pass: visible preload without maximal squat depth (qualitative)
  fail: no preload before takeoff → fault_no_preload
  fail: excessive collapse with trunk dive → fault_overcollapsed_preload
  authority: B+

checkpoint_arms_stable_preload
  phase: preload
  family: air_jump
  signal: wrist position relative to shoulders
  pass: arms contribute to balance, not wild windmill
  fail: large asymmetric arm swing → fault_asymmetric_arm_preload
  authority: B

================================================================================
TAKEOFF / FLIGHT (air_jump)
================================================================================

checkpoint_extension_through_takeoff
  phase: takeoff
  family: air_jump
  signal: knee extension timing vs E_TAKEOFF
  pass: legs extend through takeoff window after preload
  fail: takeoff with flexed knees only (no extension phase) → fault_weak_extension_takeoff
  authority: B+

checkpoint_trunk_quiet_flight
  phase: flight
  family: air_jump
  signal: trunk flexion change rate mid-air
  pass: controlled posture for skill level; large tweaks flagged only at advanced tag
  fail: uncontrolled pike or wild arch before landing prep → fault_loose_trunk_air
  authority: B

================================================================================
LANDING / RECOVER (air_jump)
================================================================================

checkpoint_landing_flexion_absorb
  phase: landing
  family: air_jump
  signal: peak knee flexion within 300 ms of E_LAND_CONTACT (frame-adjusted)
  pass: visible flexion absorption; hips travel down after contact
  fail: minimal knee flex change → fault_stiff_leg_landing
  authority: B+

checkpoint_symmetric_landing_base
  phase: landing
  family: air_jump
  signal: ankle/knee flexion L vs R at peak absorption
  pass: both legs share load within tolerance
  fail: single-leg catch with large lateral COM shift → fault_one_foot_landing
  authority: B

checkpoint_recover_stable
  phase: recover
  family: air_jump
  signal: COM speed and knee flexion rate after E_LAND_STABLE
  pass: rider re-centers to athletic stance
  fail: continued backward or heel-heavy collapse → fault_landing_backseat
  authority: B

================================================================================
YOUTH OVERRIDES (both families)
================================================================================

For audience_band=youth:
- Downgrade severity for upright_skid if speed proxy is low (learning wedge turns).
- Upgrade severity for stiff_leg_landing (growth-plate and skill-appropriate landings emphasis).
- Do not fail preload_depth for small amplitude if timing checkpoint passes.

================================================================================
AUTHORITY LEGEND
================================================================================

A+ = FIS safety/equipment pointers (via separate fis/ RAG)
B+ = peer-reviewed / systematic reviews on landing and turn mechanics
B  = instructor progression consensus, open coaching texts
