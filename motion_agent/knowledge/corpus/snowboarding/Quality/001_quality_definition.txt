# Snowboarding — Quality Definition (body-first)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel

For this pack, **riding quality** is not competition score, race time, or style points. It is the degree to which
visible body mechanics align with patterns associated in open literature and reputable instructor progressions with:

1. **Stable athletic posture** — flexed ankles/knees/hips with controlled trunk alignment during traverse and turn
   completion, without chronic collapse or rigid lockout visible in pose data.
2. **Clear turn or air sequencing** — recognizable phase order (alpine: init → steer → apex → transition; air:
   preload → takeoff → absorb) with timing that supports control rather than rushed edge changes or late extension.
3. **Balanced loading** — lead/trail leg contributions appropriate to the motion family; gross asymmetry flagged when
   confidence is sufficient, not as moral judgment of “bad style.”
4. **Controlled landings (air family)** — visible flexion absorption and re-centering without prolonged stiff-legged
   impact posture in the landing window.

Future product scores may combine checkpoint pass rate, phase detector confidence, capture quality flags, and
stance metadata — not a single “pro rider” template match.

## Dimensions

| dimension_id        | plain label                 | primary phases (alpine)     | primary phases (air)   |
|---------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|-------------------------|
| athletic_stance     | Athletic ready stance       | traverse, completion        | approach, recover       |
| turn_rhythm         | Turn rhythm & initiation    | anticipation, initiation    | —                       |
| edge_control_proxy  | Steering & edge engagement  | steering, apex              | —                       |
| transition_clean    | Smooth edge transition      | transition, completion      | —                       |
| upper_quiet         | Upper body discipline       | initiation, steering        | approach, flight        |
| preload_timing      | Preload-to-pop timing       | —                           | preload, takeoff        |
| takeoff_symmetry    | Symmetric takeoff drive     | —                           | takeoff                 |
| landing_absorption  | Landing flexion & balance   | —                           | landing, recover        |
| speed_control       | Speed management proxy      | traverse, apex              | approach                |

Each dimension maps to checkpoints in `002_checkpoints.txt`. A clip can be “high landing_absorption, moderate
transition_clean” — product copy should present dimension breakdowns, not one opaque grade.

## Evidence basis

- Biomechanics and sports-science summaries of snowboarding and related board-sport landing mechanics (B+):
  flexion absorption, COM path in turns, estimated load sensitivity to stiff landings.
- Instructor progression consensus (B): stacked alignment, progressive flexion, eyes toward exit, quiet arms in turns.
- Federation safety culture (A+ pointers): helmet and venue rules live in FIS docs — referenced, not duplicated as norms.

## Youth vs adult quality framing

**Youth (approx. ≤13–14):** prioritize speed control, balanced stance, and soft landings over aggressive lean angles,
large preload, or spin entry. Terrain park features should match skill per resort/NGO policy — not encoded as degrees.

**Adult / post-puberty:** same dimensions; allow greater lean and air time without labeling “excessive” unless paired
with landing faults or loss of completion stability.

Never instruct youth to copy elite competition cork rotations or maximum-flex preload without qualified supervision.

## Capture quality gates

Downgrade or suppress findings when:

- Camera is head-on to rider during alpine turn (lean proxy unreliable).
- Frame rate < 30 fps for jump landing (misses E_LAND_CONTACT timing).
- Lower body occluded by snow, feature, or other athletes.
- Clip tagged `family=air_jump` but no airborne COM rise detected (variant mismatch).
- Stance unknown and asymmetry finding depends on lead/trail labeling.

## Relationship to findings

Checkpoints **pass / fail / unclear** at phase windows. Findings fire when checkpoints fail with sufficient confidence
or when fault-specific rules in Findings/001 trigger. This document states the positive model; faults live in Findings.
