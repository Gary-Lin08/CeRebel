# Snowboarding Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first snowboarding coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints, SMPL-X / OpenSim pipelines) and
explains what the rider’s body is doing across recognizable phases on snow. Board edge angle, snow contact,
and feature geometry are optional enrichments — not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1 corpus):

1. **Alpine linked turns** — traverse, initiation, edge engagement, steering, transition, and re-centering
   (resort carving and basic turn mechanics visible from side or oblique camera).
2. **Basic air mechanics** — approach, preload, takeoff, flight, landing absorption, and recovery (ollie-style
   jumps, small kickers, park jump landings where the full body is visible).
3. **Competition vocabulary cross-links** — FIS discipline terms (halfpipe, slopestyle, snowboard cross, parallel
   giant slalom) appear in taxonomy and disclaimers; automated phase detectors for judged runs are future work.

Out of scope for automated body-only findings (may appear as glossary or rules RAG via `fis/`):

- Exact board edge angle without board tracking or known board model.
- Grab name classification without RGB confirmation (see corpus `snowboard_aerial_vocab.txt`).
- Gate-by-gate race line in snowboard cross when the rider is occluded by features or other athletes.
- Official judging scores, trick DD tables, and quota math (retrieve from FIS packs, do not infer from pose).

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, COM height and velocity,
   trunk lean, knee/hip flexion timing, shoulder line vs pelvis, ankle/knee symmetry — never require board sensors.
2. **Stance-aware naming**: regular vs goofy sets lead/trail legs and “frontside/backside” rotation language;
   product copy mirrors `snowboard_flip_conventions.txt` for aerial rotation (intrinsic somersault vs world Euler).
3. **Evidence layering**: open biomechanics and sports-science reviews (B+) inform load and sequencing relationships;
   national governing body and instructor progressions (B) inform training structure; cues and drills are coaching
   advice (B), always paired with disclaimer docs.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to stable `phase_id` values in
   `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` so the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows.
5. **Motion-family fork**: `family=alpine_turn` vs `family=air_jump` share ontology slots but use different phase
   chains (documented in one phase file with variant tags, same pattern as basketball jump vs free throw).

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Required joints for snowboard-likeness scoring (future): pelvis/COM, both knees, both hips, both ankles,
  shoulders, head, both wrists (for grab hints only with RGB).
- Stance metadata: `regular` | `goofy` | `unknown` — when unknown, findings use symmetric language and lower
  confidence on lead/trail-specific cues.

### Snowboard-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings, a **soft** snowboard-likeness score may use:

- **Lateral COM path** with periodic turning (alpine): horizontal COM velocity direction changes over 2–6 s windows.
- **Vertical COM burst** (air): rapid rise then fall with knee flexion spike at landing.
- **Trunk flexion range** consistent with riding (moderate hip/knee flex, not gait-only walking).

Score alone does not authorize medical advice; it gates sport-mode selection and reduces false positives from
skateboarding-on-flat or generic jumping jacks.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to computable proxies (details in Phases/001):

| Event ID (alpine) | Proxy signal | Notes |
|-------------------|--------------|-------|
| E_TRAVERSE_STABLE | low knee flexion rate; COM speed steady | Setup window |
| E_TURN_INIT | lead knee flexion increase + COM path curvature increase | Initiation |
| E_EDGE_APEX | local max lateral lean proxy (shoulder–pelvis offset) | Apex |
| E_TRANSITION | lead/trail knee flexion crossover; lean sign change | Edge change |

| Event ID (air) | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|-------|
| E_PRELOAD_MIN | local max knee/hip flex before takeoff | Load |
| E_TAKEOFF | argmax vertical velocity of COM | Takeoff anchor |
| E_AIR_PEAK | local max COM height | Apex |
| E_LAND | local min COM height after peak + knee flex spike | Landing |

Aerial rotation classification must use intrinsic somersault tools per product prompts — not pelvis_tilt alone.

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.
- Repeat stable finding IDs (`fault_*`) in Findings and cross-link in Coaching.
- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time (A+ for FIS term pointers, B+ for biomech).
- Tag chunks with `family=alpine_turn|air_jump` where mechanics differ.
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` as coaching truth — ops only.
- For rulebook questions, prefer retrieving `fis/` sources via `search_knowledge_base` filters.

## Youth vs adult

Young riders use shorter limbs, lower mass, and often softer snow — absolute edge angles and jump sizes differ.
**Progression** emphasizes balance, speed control, and landing absorption before spin load or large kickers.
Avoid prescribing elite-competition air time or rotation counts for children; separate youth checkpoints in Quality/002.

## Terrain and equipment caveats

Stance width, boot flex, board length, and binding angles change visible posture. Automated findings describe
**patterns relative to the rider’s own baseline across reps**, not a single “tournament stance” template.

## Internationalization
