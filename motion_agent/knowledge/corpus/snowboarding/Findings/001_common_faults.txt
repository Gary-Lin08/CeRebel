# Snowboarding — Common Faults (finding cards)
# Canonical v2. Alpine turn + park jump body cues. Helmet/wrist-guard framing in Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.
# Body-first — board edge pressure inferred from pose proxies only.

---

finding_id: fault_locked_legs_traverse
headline_en: Locked legs while traversing — limited shock absorption and turn readiness
phases: traverse
body_signal: Knee flexion near extension sustained ≥12 frames while COM speed > threshold; minimal ankle/knee flexion variance.
why_it_matters: Instructor/coaching consensus (B/B+): form and control framing — not individual injury prediction. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER (helmet/wrist).
feel_cue: "Athletic ready — soft knees, ankles loose like shock absorbers."
drill: 60–90s soft-ankle traverse: pulse ankles/knees every 15 m on green (drill_soft_ankle_traverse).
severity_default: medium
sources: PSIA-AASI-style instructor literature summarized (B); turn/park mechanics reviews (B+)
youth_note: Soft ankles; keep helmet/wrist safety framing; no park pressure cues.
