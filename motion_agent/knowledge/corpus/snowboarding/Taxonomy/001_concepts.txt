# Snowboarding Sport Pack — Concept taxonomy

## Core concepts (with synonyms for RAG)

concept_id: skill_stance
label: Snowboard stance
synonyms: athletic stance, ready position, neutral stance, base position, centered stance, balanced on board
phase_family: stance
maps_to: S_READY
notes: Body-first — ankle width, knee flex, hip over feet; binding angles optional metadata.

concept_id: skill_regular_goofy
label: Regular and goofy stance
synonyms: regular foot, goofy foot, left foot forward, right foot forward, which foot leads
authority: B+ coaching / FIS glossary
notes: Product mirrors lead/trail legs; does not require binding hardware visibility.

concept_id: skill_linked_turns
label: Linked turns
synonyms: S-turns, C-turns, continuous turns, flowing turns, carve to carve, turn linking
phase_family: turn
maps_to: T_INITIATE through T_TRANSITION

concept_id: skill_heel_side_turn
label: Heel-side turn
synonyms: heelside, heel edge turn, backside turn (context-dependent), falling leaf heelside
phase_family: turn
turn_family: heel_side

concept_id: skill_toe_side_turn
label: Toe-side turn
synonyms: toeside, toe edge turn, frontside turn (context-dependent), falling leaf toeside
phase_family: turn
turn_family: toe_side

concept_id: skill_traverse
label: Traverse
synonyms: cross-hill, across the fall line, side slip traverse, riding across slope
phase_family: turn
maps_to: T_TRAVERSE

concept_id: skill_edge_control
label: Edge control
synonyms: edging, on edge, hold an edge, pressure control, grip, carve vs skid
authority: B+ coaching theme
notes: Product uses body angulation proxies — not measured metal edge angle.

concept_id: skill_angulation
label: Angulation
synonyms: knee angulation, hip angulation, bank the board, tilt, lateral flex, create edge angle with body
authority: B+ biomechanics / coaching
links: fault_edge_flat_skid, fault_edge_uphill_leg_straight

concept_id: skill_flex_extension
label: Flex and extension
synonyms: absorb, extend, pump legs, up unweighting, down unweighting, compression, decompression
phase_family: turn
maps_to: T_BUILD, T_RELEASE

concept_id: skill_upper_lower_separation
label: Upper / lower body separation
synonyms: quiet upper body, stable shoulders, hips lead, counter-rotation, don't rotate shoulders first
links: fault_turn_shoulders_first, fault_turn_upper_body_rotate

concept_id: skill_pressure_lead_foot
label: Pressure and lead leg
synonyms: pressure on front foot, initiate with lead leg, steer from front foot, weight forward in turn
maps_to: T_INITIATE
authority: B coaching heuristic

concept_id: phase_apex
label: Turn apex
synonyms: middle of turn, max edge, belly of turn, sharpest part of turn
maps_to: T_APEX

concept_id: phase_transition
label: Edge transition
synonyms: edge change, switch edges, from heel to toe, flatten board, between turns
maps_to: T_TRANSITION

concept_id: quality_balance
label: Balance on snow
synonyms: centered, stacked, over the board, not in the back seat, stable base
links: fault_stance_back_seat, fault_traverse_off_balance

concept_id: quality_head_eyes
label: Head and vision
synonyms: look where you go, head up, eyes down the hill, don't look at board tip
links: fault_head_not_level

concept_id: fault_back_seat
label: Back-seat riding
synonyms: sitting back, hips back, weight on back foot, scared lean back
links: fault_stance_back_seat

concept_id: fault_skid
label: Skidded turn
synonyms: sliding turn, sideways slip, no carve, chattering, washing out
links: fault_edge_flat_skid

## Safety concepts

concept_id: safety_helmet_on_snow
label: Snowboard helmet
synonyms: crash helmet, ski helmet, certified helmet, wear a helmet, FIS helmet rule, ASTM F2040, EN 1077
authority: A+ FIS competition equipment; B+ general recreation
type: SafetyGuideline
notes: Compulsory for FIS snowboard events; strongly recommended all on-snow riding in product copy.

concept_id: safety_fall_awareness
label: Falling safely (awareness)
synonyms: how to fall, tuck and roll, don't stick arms out, avoid wrist jam, slide to stop
authority: B coaching awareness — not medical protocol
type: SafetyGuideline

concept_id: safety_terrain_ability
label: Terrain and speed control
synonyms: match terrain to ability, green run, slow zone, crowded slope, visibility, tree well awareness (glossary)
authority: B+

concept_id: concern_stiff_fall
label: Stiff bracing before a fall
synonyms: locked out, arms straight, stiff landing, caught edge fall prep
links: concern_fall_stiff_brace
severity: safety

concept_id: load_concussion
label: Head injury / concussion awareness
synonyms: hit head, dazed, concussion, see a doctor
authority: A clinical consensus (Disclaimer redirect only)
type: LoadConcern
notes: Not a form finding; no diagnostic language in coaching drills.
