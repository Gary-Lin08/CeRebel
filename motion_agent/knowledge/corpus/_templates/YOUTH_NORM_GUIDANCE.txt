# Youth vs adult coaching norms (cross-pack guidance)

Use with `youth_note:` on finding cards and Quality checkpoints.
Do NOT invent clinical cutoffs; prefer relative / prior-clip comparisons for youth.

## Principles

1. Prefer athlete-vs-self baselines over pro templates for ages under ~16.
2. Down-weight absolute ROM depth faults (deep knee bend, full hip turn, extreme
   shoulder turn) unless the athlete’s own prior clips show they can do it safely.
3. Up-weight balance, rhythm, recovery, and load-sharing faults (stiff landing,
   rushed tempo, arm-only patterns).
4. Never frame findings as injury diagnosis. Youth soft-tissue and growth-plate
   language belongs only in Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt referral copy.
5. Pitch / throw sports: pitch-count and rest messaging is coaching policy (NGB
   themes), not a medical prescription from video.

## Per-sport emphasis (product v2)

| Sport | Youth emphasis | Down-weight |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| golf | tempo, posture hold, soft trail knee | elite shoulder-turn degrees |
| baseball | stride balance, glove side, rest/count awareness | MER angle absolutes |
| tennis | serve rhythm, split-step, recovery | trophy depth vs pros |
| basketball | landing education, free-throw routine | dip depth vs NBA |
| running | cadence relative to height, hip level | absolute overstride cm |
| soccer | plant stability, approach rhythm | elite hip-speed norms |
| swimming | continuous catch, breath timing | race-pace kick volume |
| weightlifting | brace and path under light load | depth under fatigue |
| yoga | balance foot placement, long spine | forcing heel-to-floor |
| boxing (shadow) | guard + chin, reset between punches | power-shot volume |
| volleyball | landing stiffness, approach rhythm | jump-height vs elite |
| snowboarding | soft ankles, helmet/wrist framing | park progression pressure |
| human | hinge vs squat pattern, single-leg quiet | “perfect” posture angles |
