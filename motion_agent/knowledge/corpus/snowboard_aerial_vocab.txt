Snowboarding aerial vocabulary (demo knowledge pack)

- Backside / frontside: rotation direction relative to the rider's lead shoulder and
  the wall or feature — not the same as frontflip vs backflip.
- Cork / off-axis: flips that are not purely about a left-right somersault axis;
  may combine tilt and twist.
- Switch: riding with the non-dominant foot forward; facing for intrinsic somersault
  should still follow travel / COM velocity when possible.
- Grab names (indy, mute, stalefish, etc.) describe hand-to-board contact and are
  primarily visual; use RGB frame/clip tools to confirm grabs.

When classifying a flip in this demo
1. Check RGB around initiation and apex.
2. Check intrinsic_somersault_net_change_deg / flip_direction_hint over the aerial.
3. Use world-frame cumulative rotation only for how much rotation occurred.
