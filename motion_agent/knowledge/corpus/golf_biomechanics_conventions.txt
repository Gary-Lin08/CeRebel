Golf biomechanics conventions (CeRebel / motion agent)

Handedness
- Lead / trail are relative to the player's handedness, not absolute left/right.
- Confirm from video (grip, stance, ball side) before mapping SMPL-X left_* / right_*
  joints to lead/trail language.

Swing plane & rotation
- Pelvis and thorax axial rotation (and their separation, often called X-factor in
  coaching language) are useful sequencing cues; report OpenSim DOF names when citing
  numbers from tools.
- Do not invent clubhead speed, smash factor, or launch numbers unless tools or the
  knowledge base provide them.

Ground forces
- OpenSim estimated GRF is not force-plate truth. Prefer qualitative loading patterns
  (trail → lead weight shift) and cite estimated dynamics uncertainty when discussing
  push-off or bracing.

Temporal windows
- Prefer phase windows (e.g. transition→impact) over a single timestamp for sequencing,
  weight transfer, and hip/shoulder turn questions.

Knowledge base
- Prefer corpus paths under golf/ and golf_*.txt for technique and Rules of Golf content.
- Shared opensim_motion_tools.txt covers pipeline notes, not golf rules.
