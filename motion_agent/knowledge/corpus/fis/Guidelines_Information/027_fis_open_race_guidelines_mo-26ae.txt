Guidelines for Organisers of FIS Open Moguls and Aerials Events 
 
 
• The event must have a full judges panel, 1 Head Judge, 7 Judges for MO and 5 
Judges for AE 
 
• Minimum 2 nations must be represented in the Judges Panel 
 
• Minimum 3 countries must be entered per gender in the event 
 
• Only one OPN Event per season and nation can be scheduled in Moguls, Dual Moguls 
and Aerials 
 
• Must have live scoring and prompt results transmission to FIS 
 
• The FIS Freestyle Scoring Software must be used 
 
• The Course must meet Continental Cup or higher specifications, AE site must have 
triple jump. 
 
• The Course and Event is pre-approved by FIS Race Director 
 
• An invitation must be sent out 90 days prior to the arrival day and has to be posted and 
distributed to all Freestyle Nations.
