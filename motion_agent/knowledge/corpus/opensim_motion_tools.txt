OpenSim / GEM-X motion pipeline notes for the CeRebel demo

Coordinate system
- OpenSim ground: Y-up, X-forward, Z-right. Positions in metres; angles in radians
  unless a tool returns degrees.

What the tools measure
- get_motion_state: joint angles, COM, contact, root orientation at a timestamp.
- get_joint_trajectory: time series + statistics over an interval; for pelvis/flips
  inspect continuous root Euler and intrinsic somersault series.
- Dynamics tools (moments, GRF, power) are *estimated* from monocular OpenSim
  inverse dynamics — not force-plate ground truth. State uncertainty when citing them.

SMPL-X naming
- Prefer SMPL-X style names (right_knee, left_hip, pelvis). Aliases like knee_r are
  accepted by the resolver.

Evidence policy
- Do not invent numerical measurements from RGB alone when a motion tool can provide them.
- Distinguish observation (video), measurement (tools), and inference.
