Snowboard flip orientation conventions (CeRebel / motion agent)

Frontflip vs backflip
- In a backflip, the rider rotates backward: the head goes rearward relative to the
  direction of travel / facing, and the board comes up and over toward the back.
- In a frontflip, the rider rotates forward: the head dives toward the snow / downhill
  first, and the board follows over the front.

Tool signals (do not confuse frames)
- root_euler_* and root_cumulative_rotation_* are OpenSim WORLD / ground frame (Y-up).
  Use them for flip *extent* (how many degrees the body rotated), NOT for deciding
  front vs back from the Euler X sign alone.
- intrinsic_somersault_* is rider-intrinsic rotation about the horizontal rightward
  axis (facing × world_up). Sign convention: positive = backflip direction;
  negative = frontflip direction.
- OpenSim pelvis_tilt can stall or reverse near ±180° even when the body keeps spinning.
  Prefer continuous root orientation / intrinsic somersault for aerial flips.

Facing estimate
- Rider facing is taken from horizontal COM velocity when speed is sufficient;
  otherwise the pelvis body +X projected onto the ground plane.
