# Basketball Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel basketball content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and federation skill
progressions**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, or
individualized return-to-play protocols after knee, ankle, shoulder, or wrist injury.

- Pain, swelling, instability, or sharp joint symptoms during jumping or shooting require evaluation by a
  qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to “play through.”
- Youth athletes have different bone maturity, growth-plate sensitivity, and training tolerance than adults;
  follow your league’s age-appropriate practice guidelines (USA Basketball, FIBA Mini Basketball, school athletic
  policies).

## Not a game-stat or scouting tool

Make percentage, shot charts, and play-by-play data are **not** inferred from body pose alone. This pack does
not certify eligibility, rank players, or replace certified coaching credentials.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Shooting elbow flexion when the arm is foreshortened toward or away from the camera.
- Wrist and finger orientation (release angle) without ball markers.
- Knee valgus and ankle roll on landing when legs overlap or shorts occlude joints.
- Trunk lean in the sagittal plane when the capture angle is oblique.

Findings describe **visible tendencies**, not laboratory-grade kinematics. Confidence should be lower when
joints are occluded, frame rate is below ~60 fps for jump shots, or the athlete is heavily contested (body shape
changes).

## No guaranteed performance outcomes

Mechanics cues may support movement patterns discussed in research and coaching literature, but **shooting
accuracy, consistency under fatigue, and injury risk depend on many factors** (practice quality, sleep,
strength, cumulative jump load, psychology, etc.). Do not promise percentage improvements from a single drill.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Open literature reports **ranges and group averages** across skill levels and shot types (free throw vs jump shot).
Tall vs short shooters, one-motion vs two-motion styles, and mobility differences all change appearance. This
pack avoids presenting arbitrary degree targets as universal rules, especially for youth.

## Jump load and landing

Repeated hard landings with poor alignment can increase stress on knees and ankles (clinical consensus, A).
Automated landing findings are **coaching hints**, not clinical diagnosis of ACL or patellofemoral conditions.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches, parents, and athletes who already have a training plan. They do not replace
in-person supervision, especially for beginners learning basic shooting footwork and rim approach.

## Emergency

For acute injury on court, follow venue emergency procedures and seek immediate medical attention when needed.

## Product behavior

- Basketball pack features may be gated as “coming_soon” until detector validation completes — absence of a
  finding is not proof of safe or optimal mechanics.
- Free throw and jump shot variants use different lower-body checkpoints; mislabeled clip type may reduce accuracy.

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. Federation youth guidelines change;
retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.
