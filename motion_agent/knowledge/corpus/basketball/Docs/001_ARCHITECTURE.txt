# Basketball Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first basketball shooting coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the athlete’s body
is doing across recognizable shooting phases. Ball tracking, make/miss labels, and defender context are optional
enrichments, not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Jump shot** (catch-and-shoot or off-dribble rise) from single-camera or multi-camera body capture.
- **Free throw** (static line, no jump) as a parallel phase variant with shared upper-body events.
- Right- and left-handed shooters; mirror joint naming (shooting arm = trail side for R-hand shooter facing camera).

Out of scope for automatic findings (may appear as glossary only):
- Ball arc, release angle, spin rate, shot distance, contest level, screen timing.
- Layups, dunks, post moves, passing, defense, rebounding unless future packs extend ontology.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment speeds, timing between events,
   posture landmarks visible without a ball.
2. **Evidence layering**: peer-reviewed shooting kinematics (B+) inform relationships (e.g., leg drive before
   arm extension); federation and NGB skill progressions (B) inform youth practice structure; cues and drills are
   coaching advice (B), always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: numeric angle “targets” are avoided as prescriptive medicine; ranges are
   described qualitatively or attributed to published summaries with youth/adult separation where relevant.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows.
5. **Shot-type fork**: jump shot uses `variant=jump`; free throw uses `variant=ft` — same upper-body release
   logic, different lower-body phase labels (no airborne landing checkpoint on FT).

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Required joints for shot-likeness scoring (future `shot_score.py`): shooting wrist, off-hand wrist, both ankles,
  shooting-side elbow, pelvis/COM proxy.
- Handedness selects trail vs lead sides (default right-handed shooter facing camera: trail = right shooting arm).

### Shot-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings, a **soft** shot-likeness score may use:
- **Wrist speed burst**: peak shooting-wrist speed vs median (expect high burst near release).
- **Vertical COM rise** (jump shot): COM or hip height increase before release vs quiet COM (free throw).
- **Elbow extension timing**: shooting elbow flexion decreases into release window.

Score alone does **not** authorize medical advice; it only gates sport-mode selection and prevents golf/pitch
false positives when dedicated pack ships.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated event detectors ship, align narrative phases to these computable proxies:

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_SETUP | low wrist+ankle speed at clip start | Stance / routine |
| E_DIP_MIN | local minimum knee flex (both or shooting-side leg) before drive | Jump shot load |
| E_COM_RISE | positive vertical velocity of pelvis/COM | Jump shot only; absent or minimal on FT |
| E_SET_POINT | shooting wrist near local max height before release peak speed | “Set” window |
| E_RELEASE | argmax \|\|v(shooting_wrist)\|\| in analysis window | Primary timing anchor |
| E_LAND | shooting-side ankle vertical velocity zero-crossing after release | Jump shot landing |
| E_FT_HOLD | low wrist speed for ≥200 ms after release | Free throw finish hold |

Future detectors may add off-hand separation frame, max knee extension timing, and trunk lean angle;
ontology slots exist in Schema.

### Coach brief generation (planned)

`build_coach_briefs(..., sport="basketball")` will consume:

- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × phase alignment.
- One feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding (from Coaching/).

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.
- Repeat stable finding IDs (`fault_*`) in Findings and cross-link in Coaching.
- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time (manifest-driven).
- Tag chunks with `shot_variant=jump|ft|both` where mechanics differ.
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` or raw manifest CSV as coaching truth — use as ops only.

## Youth vs adult

Jump volume, rim height, and ball size differ by age band. **Free throw and form shooting volume** can be high
with low impact; **repeated max-effort jump shooting** in growing athletes should follow NGB rest and monitoring
guidance — never merge youth and adult workload tables without an age tag.

Mechanics findings often overlap, but absolute joint angles and release height differ with stature and limb length;
avoid single “model shooter” degree targets in product copy.
