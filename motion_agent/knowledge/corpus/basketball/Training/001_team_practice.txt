# Basketball Team Practice — Shooting Development (jump shot + free throw)
# Authority: B (NGB/coaching structure) + B+ (motor learning). Summaries, not verbatim manuals.

================================================================================
PRACTICE DESIGN GOALS
================================================================================

1. **High-quality repetitions** with clear body focus (one checkpoint per block).
2. **Separate jump shot and FT blocks** — different rhythm, different fatigue.
3. **Film-friendly stations** for CeRebel capture (side + front angles when possible).
4. **Youth-appropriate volume** — form and FT volume can exceed game-speed jump volume.

================================================================================
SAMPLE 90-MINUTE PRACTICE (HIGH SCHOOL VARSITY — ADULT BAND)
================================================================================

| block | time | focus | body checkpoint |
|-------|------|-------|-----------------|
| dynamic warm-up | 12 min | ankle/hip mobility, landing prep | soft two-foot hops |
| form shooting | 15 min | set → release → hold | elbow under wrist plane |
| FT routine | 10 min | same movie every rep | setup stillness + hold |
| catch-and-shoot | 15 min | dip-rise-set timing | COM rise before release |
| off-movement screens | 12 min | land balanced | symmetric landing flex |
| competitive FT | 8 min | pressure routine | no rush in setup |
| cool-down | 8 min | stretch, review film | — |

Adjust total jump-shot attempts based on weekly game load; no single universal cap in this doc — follow school S&C policy.

================================================================================
YOUTH PRACTICE (12U EXAMPLE — SHORTENED)
================================================================================

| block | time | focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| warm-up | 10 min | movement games, no max hops |
| form close to basket | 15 min | one-hand finish, guide hand open |
| FT intro | 8 min | 4-step routine, hold finish |
| spot shooting (no contest) | 12 min | feet paint, soft land |
| small-sided games | 20 min | shooting optional, not tracked here |

Emphasis: balance, routine, fun — avoid competitive "make X or run" during growth years without coach oversight.

================================================================================
STATION ROTATIONS (4 STATIONS × 4 MIN)
================================================================================

Station A — Form line (no jump): one-hand form finish drill, 8 reps each side.
Station B — Dip-rise no shot: metronome dips, body-only.
Station C — Free throw routine: breath + hold, 5 reps.
Station D — Partner elbow check: set holds, verbal feedback.

Film one rotation per athlete per week for CeRebel review (optional product workflow).

================================================================================
JUMP SHOT PROGRESSION (SKILL LADDER)
================================================================================

1. Set point and finish hold (no jump, no rim).
2. Dip-rise with arm path (no ball).
3. Form shot close to basket, 1-hand emphasis.
4. Spot jump shot, open stance, no defense.
5. Catch-and-shoot with passer; add light closeout.
6. Game-speed reps with landing stick cue.

Regression: if landing faults persist, return to Station B + landing mat work (Coaching/002 drill_landing_stick).

================================================================================
FREE THROW TEAM PROTOCOL
================================================================================

- Athletes document **personal routine step count** (e.g., bounce ×3, breath, set).
- Team rule: no shot until setup stillness ≥1 s (coach whistle optional).
- Track **body routine compliance** in film review, not just makes.
- Under pressure: simulate timeout FTs with same routine — avoid shortening setup (fault_rushed_free_throw).

Research note: routine consistency associates with lower variability in some FT kinematic studies (B+); not a guarantee of percentage.

================================================================================
PRE-GAME SHOOTING (20 MIN CAP TEMPLATE)
================================================================================

1. 5 min form close
2. 5 min game spots (3–5 makes per spot, quality > quantity)
3. 5 min FT with full routine
4. 5 min one game-speed movement shot

Avoid introducing new mechanics on game day; CeRebel clips should compare to prior practice baselines.

================================================================================
LOAD MANAGEMENT REMINDERS
================================================================================

- Jump shooting volume adds to daily jump load with rebounding and defense drills.
- Communicate with S&C when athletes report knee/ ankle soreness — reduce contested jump reps.
- This pack does not prescribe medical rest; refer to athletic trainer.

================================================================================
CAPTURE TIPS FOR COACHES USING CEREBEL
================================================================================

- Jump shot: film from **front** for elbow/guide hand; **side** for trunk and dip depth.
- Free throw: front angle at line; include full routine in clip.
- Clip length: routine start through landing or FT hold (see Phases/001).
- Label session `variant=jump` or `variant=ft` for correct checkpoints.
