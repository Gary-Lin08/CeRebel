# Basketball Shooting — Feel Cues
# Linked to fault_* IDs and phases. Authority mostly B (coaching); pair with Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.

## Global cues (jump shot + free throw)

cue_balance_first
  phases: setup, follow
  text_en: "Land where you leave — finish balanced before you run back on defense."
  maps_findings: fault_narrow_base, fault_asymmetric_landing, fault_stiff_landing
  authority: B

cue_rim_eyes
  phases: set, release
  text_en: "See the rim over the ball — eyes finish the target, not the floor."
  maps_findings: fault_head_jerk_release, fault_low_set_point
  authority: B

cue_relaxed_wrist
  phases: release, follow
  text_en: "Snap is soft — wrist loose, fingers down like dipping in warm water."
  maps_findings: fault_wrist_only_release, fault_chicken_wing_finish
  authority: B

  text_en: "Floor pushes you up; the ball rides the elevator, not the fishing pole."
  maps_findings: fault_arm_only_shot, fault_no_load, fault_lateral_leak

  phases: dip, drive
  text_en: "Small sit, fast go — load the legs like a pogo, not a deep squat."

cue_vertical_channel
  phases: drive, transfer
  text_en: "Jump in a phone booth — hips and shoulders rise in one column."

  authority: B

## Youth-safe cue variants (shorter, no max-effort language)

cue_youth_feet_paint
  phases: setup
  text_en: "Step back to your footprints — same feet every rep."
  audience: youth
  maps_findings: fault_narrow_base, fault_feet_misaligned
  authority: B

cue_youth_soft_land
  phases: follow
  text_en: "Quiet feet on landing — bend knees like sitting on a low chair."
  audience: youth
  maps_findings: fault_stiff_landing
  authority: B

## Cues to avoid in automated product copy (manual coach only)

- "Snap your elbow" (can encourage hyperextension pain)
- "Throw at the rim" (conflicts with arc language)
- "Jump as high as you can every rep" (youth load concern)
