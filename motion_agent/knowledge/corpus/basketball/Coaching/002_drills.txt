# Basketball Shooting — Drills (30–90 s blocks, body-first)
# Each drill lists fault links, phase focus, equipment, youth note.

================================================================================
FORM & UPPER BODY (NO RIM REQUIRED)
================================================================================

drill_one_hand_form_finish
  duration: 60–75 s
  phases: set, release, follow
  faults: fault_short_arm_release, fault_flyaway_elbow, fault_chicken_wing_finish
  setup: Athlete at shooting line or anywhere with ceiling clearance; optional light ball or sock ball.
  steps: Set with shooting hand only; guide hand behind back. Extend through gooseneck, hold 2 s. 8–10 reps.
  body_focus: Elbow path, wrist above elbow at set, hold after release.
  youth: Use smaller ball; reduce reps to 6; emphasize hold not height.
  authority: B

drill_guide_hand_open
  duration: 45–60 s
  phases: set, release
  faults: fault_guide_hand_push
  setup: Two-hand grip at set; guide hand on side of ball.
  steps: Rise to set; at extension guide hand opens and drops without touching ball path. 10 reps slow.
  body_focus: Off-hand wrist speed near zero at release window.
  youth: Same; coach watches off-hand from front.
  authority: B

drill_wall_set_touch
  duration: 60 s
  phases: transfer, set
  faults: fault_low_set_point, fault_arm_only_shot
  setup: Stand arm’s length from wall, side angle optional.
  steps: Dip lightly, rise, bring shooting hand to wall at forehead height without pushing wall. 8 reps.
  body_focus: Set height and upward path before extension.
  youth: No jump required — rise on toes optional.
  authority: B

================================================================================
LOWER BODY / JUMP SHOT
================================================================================

drill_dip_rise_no_shot
  duration: 60–90 s
  phases: dip, drive
  faults: fault_no_load, fault_excessive_dip, fault_asymmetric_dip
  setup: Filmed from front; tape line between feet.
  steps: Athletic stance; controlled dip (knees flex), immediate rise to full extension on balls of feet; no arm shoot. 10 reps on metronome 80 BPM.
  body_focus: Symmetric knee flex, COM rises before any arm motion.
  youth: Shallower dip; 6 reps.
  authority: B+

drill_channel_vertical_hop
  duration: 75 s
  phases: drive, transfer
  faults: fault_lateral_leak, fault_arm_only_shot
  setup: Two parallel tape lines 15 cm apart.
  steps: Dip-drive hop staying inside channel; arms can mimic shot at top. 8 hops.
  body_focus: Lateral COM control.
  youth: Channel wider; hop height moderate.
  authority: B

drill_landing_stick
  duration: 90 s
  phases: follow
  faults: fault_stiff_landing, fault_asymmetric_landing
  setup: Mat recommended; film from front.
  steps: After form jump shot or hop-shoot, land and freeze 1 s with matched knee bend. 6 shots or hops.
  body_focus: Knee flexion symmetry at E_LAND_STABLE proxy.
  youth: Lower hop; prioritize soft knees.
  authority: A/B+ landing education summarized

================================================================================
FREE THROW ROUTINE
================================================================================

drill_ft_breath_routine
  duration: 90 s
  phases: setup, follow
  faults: fault_rushed_free_throw, fault_chicken_wing_finish
  setup: Line at FT distance optional; no make required.
  steps: Personal routine (e.g., 3 bounces, inhale, set, shoot, hold finish 2 s). Repeat 4 complete routines without rushing first step.
  body_focus: Low wrist speed in setup window; hold after release.
  youth: Shorten routine; same order every time.
  authority: B+

drill_ft_quiet_legs
  duration: 60 s
  phases: setup, release
  faults: fault_arm_only_shot (ft variant — arm-only FT push)
  setup: Seated-to-stand optional contrast; standing at line.
  steps: Knees soft but no heel lift; shoot 5 FTs focusing on legs quiet, power from smooth extension.
  body_focus: Minimal COM rise (< jump threshold).
  youth: Step-in closer if needed for success.
  authority: B

================================================================================
TEAM STATIONS (SEE Training/001 FOR CONTEXT)
================================================================================

drill_partner_elbow_check
  duration: 45 s
  phases: set
  faults: fault_flyaway_elbow
  setup: Partners face each other at 2 m.
  steps: Shooter holds set; partner calls "in" or "out" based on elbow relative to wrist. 6 sets.
  body_focus: Frontal plane elbow position.
  youth: Use verbal only, no touching.
  authority: B

drill_metronome_catch_shoot_tempo
  duration: 90 s
  phases: dip, drive, release
  faults: fault_excessive_dip, fault_arm_only_shot
  setup: Passer optional; metronome 100 BPM for advanced.
  steps: On beat: catch-dip-rise-release pattern; adjust dip depth to stay on beat. 8 reps.
  body_focus: Tempo linking dip to release.
  youth: Slower BPM; pass from close distance.
  authority: B

================================================================================
V2 CATCH-AND-SHOOT / FT RHYTHM
================================================================================

drill_ft_metronome_rhythm
  duration: 90 s
  phases: setup, release, follow
  faults: fault_ft_rhythm_break, fault_rushed_free_throw, fault_ft_early_arm
  setup: FT line optional; metronome or counted beats.
  steps: Personal routine on 4 even beats; hold finish 2 s; 5 reps.
  body_focus: Consistent setup window; legs before arm.
  youth: Fewer dribbles; same order.
  authority: B+

drill_catch_plant_freeze
  duration: 75 s
  phases: setup, dip
  faults: fault_catch_shoot_late_pivot, fault_inside_pivot_collapse, fault_catch_shoot_hop_back
  setup: Partner toss or self-toss.
  steps: Catch, freeze feet square 1 count, then dip-rise form shot; 8 reps.
  body_focus: Plant stability; no hop-back.
  youth: Closer toss; freeze longer.
  authority: B

drill_forehead_set_hold
  duration: 60 s
  phases: set, release
  faults: fault_set_behind_head, fault_low_set_point
  setup: Side camera preferred.
  steps: Rise to forehead-window set, hold 2 s, extend; 8 reps.
  body_focus: Wrist anterior to ear line.
  authority: B

drill_footprint_landing_v2
  duration: 90 s
  phases: follow
  faults: fault_landing_forward_lunge, fault_stiff_landing, fault_asymmetric_landing
  setup: Tape footprints; mat optional.
  steps: Form jumper; land in marks with soft matched knees; 6 reps.
  body_focus: Two-foot soft stick.
  youth: Soft knees over footprint perfection.
  authority: A/B+ landing education summarized

drill_thumb_up_guide
  duration: 60 s
  phases: set, release
  faults: fault_offhand_thumb_over, fault_guide_hand_push
  setup: Frontal or 45° view.
  steps: At set freeze guide thumb vertical; open on extend; 10 slow reps.
  body_focus: Off-hand shelf without thumb-over.
  authority: B
