# Basketball Shooting — Phase Model (v1)
# Scope: jump shot (variant=jump) and free throw (variant=ft)
# Aligns: body-only OpenSim / pose keypoints; no ball required
# Stable phase IDs — do not rename without migration note in pack_readme

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Seven phases cover one complete shooting motion from stance through finish.
Jump shot and free throw share phases setup → set → release → follow; jump adds
dip, drive, and landing; free throw replaces dip/drive with optional quiet knee
flex (ft_load) and skips airborne landing.

Handedness convention (facing camera, square to basket):
- R-hand shooter: trail = shooting side (right wrist, right elbow, right ankle lead on step).
- L-hand shooter: mirror all joint names.

================================================================================
PHASE TABLE
================================================================================

| phase_id   | label_en        | variant   | start event              | end event                |
|------------|-----------------|-----------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| setup      | Setup / routine | jump, ft  | clip_start               | E_DIP_START or E_FT_BEND |
| dip        | Load / dip      | jump      | E_DIP_START              | E_DIP_MIN                |
| drive      | Leg drive / jump| jump      | E_DIP_MIN                | E_COM_PEAK               |
| transfer   | Upward transfer | jump, ft  | E_DIP_MIN (ft) or E_COM_RISE (jump) | E_SET_START |
| set        | Set point       | jump, ft  | E_SET_START              | E_RELEASE                |
| release    | Release         | jump, ft  | E_RELEASE - 3 frames     | E_RELEASE + 5 frames     |
| follow     | Follow / land   | jump, ft  | end(release)             | E_LAND+stable or E_FT_HOLD_END |

Free throw mapping:
- Skip `dip` and `drive` as active phases; optional micro-phase `ft_load` collapses into setup→transfer when
  knee flex < 5° (straight-leg FT style).
- `follow` ends at E_FT_HOLD_END (wrist speed below threshold for ≥200 ms), not landing.

================================================================================
EVENT DEFINITIONS (body-only)
================================================================================

E_SETUP_STABLE
  Definition: rolling std of pelvis position < threshold for ≥8 frames at clip start.
  Fallback: first frame of clip.
  Detector use: trim pre-motion dead time.

E_DIP_START (jump only)
  Definition: shooting-side or average knee flexion begins decreasing after setup (eccentric load).
  Proxy: d(knee_angle)/dt < -threshold for 3 consecutive frames.
  Fallback: setup_end + 5% of window span.

E_DIP_MIN (jump only)
  Definition: local minimum of knee flexion (max flexion angle) before E_COM_RISE.
  Proxy: argmin knee_angle in [E_DIP_START, E_COM_PEAK].
  Notes: small dips (<10° change) may indicate catch-and-shoot with minimal load — flag low confidence.

E_COM_RISE (jump only)
  Definition: vertical velocity of pelvis (or mid-hip) exceeds positive threshold.
  Proxy: v_y(pelvis) > 0.05 m/s (scale-normalized in product code).

E_COM_PEAK (jump only)
  Definition: local maximum pelvis height before release.
  Proxy: argmax hip_y in [E_DIP_MIN, E_RELEASE].

E_FT_BEND (free throw only)
  Definition: optional knee flexion during routine before upward motion; may coincide with E_SETUP_STABLE end.
  Proxy: same as E_DIP_MIN but without subsequent COM rise above jump threshold.

E_SET_START
  Definition: shooting wrist height enters upper quartile of its range in [E_DIP_MIN or E_FT_BEND, E_RELEASE].
  Proxy: wrist_y crosses 75th percentile of wrist_y in pre-release window.
  Coaching term: “set point” — ball would sit near forehead/chin for many styles; we only see wrist path.

E_RELEASE
  Definition: argmax ||v(shooting_wrist)|| in analysis window.
  Primary timing anchor for all shot types.
  Note: NOT official ball release without ball track; wrist speed peak is standard proxy in pose-only pipelines.

E_LAND (jump only)
  Definition: first frame after E_RELEASE where shooting-side ankle vertical velocity ≈ 0 and foot height near
  minimum in landing window.
  Proxy: zero-crossing of v_y(ankle_shooting) after E_COM_PEAK.
  Fallback: E_RELEASE + 40% of remaining clip.

E_LAND_STABLE (jump only)
  Definition: E_LAND + frames until knee flexion change rate < threshold (settled stance).
  Used for landing balance findings.

E_FT_HOLD_END (free throw only)
  Definition: time when ||v(shooting_wrist)|| stays below 15% of E_RELEASE peak for ≥200 ms after release.
  Marks end of follow-through hold.

================================================================================
TIMING RELATIONSHIPS (qualitative, B+ literature)
================================================================================

Typical skilled jump shot (not prescriptive):
- Leg extension and COM rise begin before or concurrent with rapid shooting-elbow extension.
- Wrist speed peak (E_RELEASE) occurs near or after COM peak for many catch-and-shoot styles.
- Free throw: minimal COM displacement; E_RELEASE often after quiet knee re-extension.

Youth: longer setup, smaller dip amplitude, lower COM rise — use age-tagged checkpoints in Quality/002.

================================================================================
CLIP TRIMMING GUIDANCE
================================================================================

Ideal clip: includes full setup routine through landing (jump) or 1 s hold (FT).
Minimum: 0.5 s before E_DIP_START through E_LAND_STABLE or E_FT_HOLD_END.
If clip starts late, setup phase may be empty — reduce confidence on balance/stance findings.
