# Basketball Shooting — Quality Definition (body-first)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel

For this pack, **shooting quality** is not make/miss. It is the degree to which visible body mechanics align with
patterns associated in open literature and reputable coaching with:

1. **Stable base** before and through release (setup, landing for jump shots).
2. **Coordinated sequence** — lower-body drive (when used) → trunk alignment → shooting-arm extension without
   obvious compensations visible in pose data.
3. **Repeatable timing** — similar event ordering (dip → rise → set → release) across reps when comparing clips.
4. **Controlled finish** — follow-through hold (FT) or symmetric soft landing (jump), without extreme joint
   collapse visible from the capture angle.

Quality scores in product code (future) should combine checkpoint pass rate, phase confidence, and capture quality
(occlusion flags) — not a single “perfect form” template.

## Dimensions

| dimension_id      | plain label              | primary phases      | shot_variant |
|-------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|--------------|
| base_stability    | Stance and balance       | setup, follow       | jump, ft     |
| load_rhythm       | Load-to-shoot rhythm     | dip, drive            | jump         |
| vertical_transfer | Leg drive to shot        | drive, transfer       | jump         |
| upper_alignment   | Shooting arm path        | set, release          | jump, ft     |
| off_hand_quiet    | Guide hand interference  | set, release          | jump, ft     |
| release_extension | Full extension timing  | release, follow       | jump, ft     |
| landing_control   | Landing symmetry         | follow                | jump         |
| routine_consistency | FT routine repeatability | setup, follow       | ft           |

Each dimension maps to checkpoints in `002_checkpoints.txt`. A rep can be “high upper_alignment, moderate
landing_control” — product copy should avoid single-number grades without dimension breakdown.

## Evidence basis

- Biomechanics reviews and kinematic studies of jump shots and free throws (B+): sequential extension, role of
  lower body in jump shot vs reduced leg work in FT.
- Coaching consensus (B): eyes on target, balance hand, follow-through — translated to body signals where visible.
- Clinical load guidance (A): landing mechanics and cumulative jump volume — referenced for landing_control severity,
  not for predicting injury in an individual.

## Youth vs adult quality framing

**Youth (approx. ≤14U):** prioritize balance, repeatable foot placement, and finish hold over maximizing dip depth or
jump height. Lower absolute wrist release height is normal with smaller stature and ball size.

**Adult / post-puberty:** same dimensions; allow greater dip and COM rise without labeling “excessive” unless
combined with landing fault or loss of upper alignment.

Never tell youth to copy elite NBA release height or leg load without coach supervision.

## Capture quality gates

Downgrade or suppress findings when:

- Shooting arm nearly parallel to image plane (foreshortening).
- Frame rate < 30 fps for jump shot (misses E_RELEASE timing).
- Feet or landing occluded.
- Clip labeled `ft` but COM rise indicates jump (variant mismatch).

## Relationship to findings

Checkpoints **pass/fail/unclear** at phase windows. Findings fire when checkpoints fail with sufficient confidence
or when fault-specific rules in Findings/001 trigger. Quality definition does not duplicate fault headlines — it
states the positive model.
