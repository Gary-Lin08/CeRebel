# Basketball Shooting — Common Faults (finding cards)
# Canonical v2. Body-first; see Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt. Authority B/B+ unless noted.
# Scope: jump shot + free throw + catch-and-shoot footwork; landing included.

---

finding_id: fault_arm_only_shot
headline_en: Shot rises mostly from the arm before the legs drive
phases: drive, transfer
body_signal: E_RELEASE occurs before E_COM_RISE or COM vertical displacement < threshold while shooting-wrist speed peak is high; knee extension lags wrist acceleration.
why_it_matters: Jump-shot literature links leg–trunk contribution to stable release height and timing under fatigue; arm-dominant patterns may shorten range and increase shoulder/wrist reliance (performance and load — not individual injury prediction).
feel_cue: "Ground first, then hands — feel the floor push your chest up before you extend the arm."
drill: 60s wall-leg-drive: stand 30 cm from wall, hands at set point without ball, dip and drive so hips touch wall lightly before arm extends (body-only).
severity_default: medium
sources: Knudson & Elliott basketball shooting kinematics summaries (B+); USA Basketball skill concepts (B)
youth_note: Landing education and free-throw routine over dip depth vs elite jumpers.

finding_id: fault_no_load
headline_en: Minimal knee bend before the jump
phases: dip
body_signal: Knee flexion change from setup to E_DIP_MIN < ~10° (scale-normalized; youth smaller band).
why_it_matters: Without eccentric load, vertical rise may be insufficient for contested release height; timing can feel rushed.
feel_cue: "Sit into your shoes, then spring — small hop, not a straight-leg lift."
drill: 45s chair-height dip: hips drop one hand-width, pause 1 count, then rise without ball.
severity_default: medium
sources: Okazaki et al. jump shot biomechanics (B+); NGB form-shooting (B)
youth_note: Shallower dip acceptable; prioritize balance.

finding_id: fault_excessive_dip
headline_en: Deep squat-like dip slows the shot
phases: dip, drive
body_signal: Knee flexion at E_DIP_MIN approaches squat depth with delayed E_COM_RISE (>300 ms at 60 fps from dip min to rise).
why_it_matters: Excessive load can delay release against closing defenders and disrupt upper-body rhythm.
feel_cue: "Load the ankles and knees, not the hips to the floor — quick sit, quick go."
drill: 90s metronome dip-rise at 80 BPM without shooting, then add arm at set on beat 2.
severity_default: medium
sources: Catch-and-shoot tempo coaching (B); skilled vs novice kinematics (B+)
youth_note: Landing education and free-throw routine over dip depth vs elite jumpers.

finding_id: fault_asymmetric_dip
headline_en: One leg loads more than the other in the dip
phases: dip
body_signal: Knee flexion difference L vs R at E_DIP_MIN > threshold; COM shifts laterally toward one ankle during dip.
why_it_matters: Asymmetric loading can carry into landing and reduce balance on off-target misses.
feel_cue: "Both shoelaces press the floor equally in the dip."
drill: 60s line drill: tape between feet; dip without tipping COM off center (front camera).
severity_default: medium
sources: Bilateral prep biomechanics (B); coaching balance drills (B)
youth_note: Landing education and free-throw routine over dip depth vs elite jumpers.

finding_id: fault_flyaway_elbow
headline_en: Shooting elbow drifts outward at the set point
phases: set, release
body_signal: Shooting elbow lateral to wrist in frontal view at E_SET_START; upper arm abduction elevated vs trunk.
why_it_matters: Outward elbow can reduce consistent release direction in pose-visible plane; common coaching correction.
feel_cue: "Elbow points at the rim, wrist follows — tuck the elbow under the ball line."
drill: 75s one-hand form: shooting hand only at set, elbow above hip, extend to finish (no ball or light sock).
severity_default: medium
sources: Knudson free throw / shooting form reviews (B+); coaching elbow-in cue (B)
youth_note: Landing education and free-throw routine over dip depth vs elite jumpers.

finding_id: fault_stiff_landing
headline_en: Legs stay straight at landing
phases: follow
body_signal: Knee flexion at E_LAND increases < ~15° from flight posture.
why_it_matters: Stiff landings increase ground reaction forces (biomechanics consensus); coaching aims for soft absorption — not a personal diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Land quiet — bend like a spring, not a stick."
drill: 90s drop-stick landing: small hop, land with knees flexing soft on mat.
severity_default: high
sources: Landing mechanics reviews (A/B+); basketball S&C summaries (B)
youth_note: Prioritize soft knees over hop height.

finding_id: fault_ft_rhythm_break
headline_en: Free-throw rhythm breaks between routine and release
phases: setup, transfer, release
body_signal: Setup low-velocity window duration varies >30% across FT reps, or E_RELEASE occurs without consistent pause after last bounce/breath proxy.
why_it_matters: Routine timing consistency associates with FT performance in observational studies; broken rhythm often precedes rushed or arm-only pushes (performance).
feel_cue: "Same clock every shot — bounce, breathe, set, shoot on the same beat."
drill: 90s metronome FT shadow: personal routine on 4 even beats, hold finish 2 s; 5 reps.
severity_default: medium
sources: FT routine timing research (B+); NGB FT progressions (B)
youth_note: Shorten routine but keep fixed order.

finding_id: fault_landing_forward_lunge
headline_en: Landing steps forward into a lunge after the shot
phases: follow
body_signal: After E_LAND, one foot travels forward >~0.5× shoulder width before E_LAND_STABLE; asymmetric knee flex with forward COM.
why_it_matters: Forward lunge landings often follow lean or fade and reduce stick-balance quality taught in form shooting.
feel_cue: "Land in your footprints — two feet, soft knees, no step toward the rim."
drill: 90s footprint landing: chalk takeoff feet; land back in marks after form jumper; 6 reps.
severity_default: medium
sources: Landing balance progressions (B); soft-landing education (A/B+ summarized)
youth_note: Prioritize soft knees over footprint perfection.

# Severity: high — stiff/asymmetric landing (education not fear); medium — arm-only, elbow, FT rhythm, catch-shoot plant; low — head jerk, set-behind, thumb-over.
# Youth: down-weight dip-depth; up-weight FT rhythm and landing softness.
