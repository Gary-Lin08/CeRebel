# Yoga pack — research notes (facts with URLs for build agents)
# Date: 2026-10-21

## Yoga for musculoskeletal health

- Multiple systematic reviews report yoga can improve pain and function in **chronic low back pain** for some
  participants vs waitlist; effect sizes vary; supervision and modification matter.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=yoga+chronic+low+back+pain+meta-analysis

## Injury and load

- Self-report surveys suggest **shoulder, wrist, and hamstring** regions appear often in yoga-related injury
  reports in recreational practitioners; mechanism often linked to repetitive vinyasa and overenthusiastic depth.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=yoga+related+injuries

## Plank and chaturanga

- Push-up variations show scapular stabilizer and trunk muscle engagement; form affects shoulder abduction angle.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=plank+scapular+muscle+activity

## Balance

- Yoga and tai chi class interventions appear in balance/fall-prevention literature for older adults (mixed quality).
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=yoga+balance+older+adults+systematic+review

## Hypermobility

- Clinical guidance favors **strength at mid-range** over passive end-range stretching for hypermobile individuals.
  Search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=hypermobility+physical+therapy+exercise

## Teaching safety conventions (non-primary literature)

- Foot not on standing knee in Tree — ubiquitous in teacher training safety lists (Yoga Alliance RYS materials, B).
- Heels down in Down Dog not required for benefit — common Iyengar-influenced pedagogy summary (B).
