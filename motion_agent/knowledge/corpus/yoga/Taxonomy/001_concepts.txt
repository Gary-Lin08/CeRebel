# Yoga — Concepts (v1 taxonomy for RAG)
# Stable concept_id slugs; English definitions with Sanskrit aliases where useful

concept_yoga: Physical practice integrating posture (asana), often breath and attention; CeRebel scope is asana motion only.
concept_asana: Posture; unit of analysis is pose_id hold or flow node.
concept_pranayama: Breath regulation — out of scope for pose-only detection (glossary only).
concept_vinyasa: Breath-linked movement sequence; maps to mode=flow.
concept_hatha: Slower held poses; maps to mode=hold emphasis.
concept_alignment: Body stacking for sustainable load — not aesthetic depth.
concept_drishiti: Gaze focal point; relates to fault_hold_wandering coaching.
concept_bandha: Muscular locks — not visible in 2D pose v1.
concept_prop: Block, strap, blanket — coaching text only.
concept_sun_salutation: Named flow template; see Training/001 Sun A table.
concept_counterpose: Pose that balances prior shape (fold after backbend).
concept_bilateral: Left-right symmetric intent (Mountain, Chair).
concept_unilateral: Single-leg or asymmetric (Tree, Warriors).
concept_closed_chain_upper: Hands on floor (Dog, Plank, Chaturanga).
concept_inversion_prep: Hips above heart approximate (Down Dog) — not full inversion findings.
concept_hip_hinge: Flexion primarily at hips with spine neutral priority.
concept_lumbar_flexion: Forward bending segment contribution — forward fold faults.
concept_lumbar_extension: Backbend segment contribution — dog sag, plank sag.
concept_knee_tracking: Frontal-plane knee over foot alignment.
concept_scapular_upward_rotation: Shoulder blade motion in overhead and dog — elevation fault inverse.
concept_hypermobility: Excess passive ROM — stability over depth policy.
concept_hold_stillness: Low joint velocity window after entry.
concept_transition_control: Bounded acceleration in flow mode.
