# Yoga — Common Poses Set (v1 CeRebel scope)
# Canonical pose_id values for RAG tags, findings, and coaching cross-links
# Sanskrit + English aliases for retrieval

================================================================================
STANDING — FOUNDATION
================================================================================

pose_mountain
  sanskrit: Tadasana
  english: Mountain Pose
  family: standing
  mode: hold
  notes: Reference upright stacking; feet hip-width; neutral spine baseline for other standing poses.

pose_upward_salute
  sanskrit: Urdhva Hastasana
  english: Upward Salute / Raised Arms
  family: standing
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Shoulder flexion overhead; rib flare checkpoint; common in Sun Salutation.

pose_forward_fold_standing
  sanskrit: Uttanasana
  english: Standing Forward Fold
  family: standing
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Hip hinge vs spine round; knee micro-bend allowed; osteoporosis caution in disclaimer.

pose_chair
  sanskrit: Utkatasana
  english: Chair Pose
  family: standing
  mode: hold
  notes: Knee flexion load; torso angle; arms forward or overhead variants.

pose_warrior_1
  sanskrit: Virabhadrasana I
  english: Warrior I
  family: standing
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Hip square toward front foot vs rear foot; back heel down; lunge depth.

pose_warrior_2
  sanskrit: Virabhadrasana II
  english: Warrior II
  family: standing
  mode: hold
  notes: Open hips; front knee over ankle line; arms abducted 90°.

pose_warrior_3_prep
  sanskrit: Virabhadrasana III (preparation)
  english: Warrior III prep / balancing lunge
  family: standing_balance
  mode: hold
  notes: v1 scores hip hinge and standing-leg alignment; full parallel-to-floor Warrior III optional future.

pose_triangle
  sanskrit: Utthita Trikonasana
  english: Extended Triangle Pose
  family: standing
  mode: hold
  notes: Front leg straight or micro-bend; side bend at hips; avoid collapsing into knee.

pose_tree
  sanskrit: Vrksasana
  english: Tree Pose
  family: standing_balance
  mode: hold
  notes: Foot above or below knee (not on knee joint); pelvis level; sway normal within checkpoint.

================================================================================
KNEELING / QUADRUPED / CORE
================================================================================

pose_tabletop
  sanskrit: Bharmanasana
  english: Tabletop / All Fours
  family: kneeling
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Neutral spine baseline for Cat-Cow.

pose_cat_cow
  sanskrit: Marjaryasana-Bitilasana
  english: Cat-Cow
  family: kneeling
  mode: flow
  notes: Repeated flexion-extension; not scored as static hold; phase model uses flow per cycle.

pose_child
  sanskrit: Balasana
  english: Child's Pose
  family: kneeling
  mode: hold
  notes: Hips to heels; arms extended or by sides; knee pain modifications via props (coaching only).

pose_low_lunge
  sanskrit: Anjaneyasana
  english: Low Lunge / Crescent lunge (knee down)
  family: kneeling
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Front knee over ankle; rear hip extension; common knee-over-toe coaching zone.

pose_plank
  sanskrit: Phalakasana
  english: Plank
  family: prone
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Sagittal line; scapular protraction; core engagement.

pose_chaturanga_low
  sanskrit: Chaturanga Dandasana
  english: Four-Limbed Staff (low)
  family: prone
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Elbow flex ~90°; shoulder not below elbow height coaching rule; high load transition.

================================================================================
PRONE / BACKBEND
================================================================================

pose_cobra
  sanskrit: Bhujangasana
  english: Cobra
  family: prone
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Pelvis on floor; thoracic extension; minimal arm straightening for beginners.

pose_up_dog
  sanskrit: Urdhva Mukha Svanasana
  english: Upward-Facing Dog
  family: prone
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Thighs lifted; shoulders over wrists; lumbar contribution monitored.

pose_down_dog
  sanskrit: Adho Mukha Svanasana
  english: Downward-Facing Dog
  family: inversion_prep
  mode: hold, flow_node
  notes: Hip hinge; shoulder flexion; heel reach secondary to spine length.

pose_locust_prep
  sanskrit: Salabhasana (preparation)
  english: Locust preparation
  family: prone
  mode: hold
  notes: Gentle spinal extension; arms by sides; gazing down.

================================================================================
SUPINE / SEATED
================================================================================

pose_bridge
  sanskrit: Setu Bandhasana
  english: Bridge Pose
  family: supine
  mode: hold
  notes: Knee–hip–ankle alignment; avoid knee splay.

pose_supine_twist
  sanskrit: Supta Matsyendrasana
  english: Supine Spinal Twist
  family: supine
  mode: hold
  notes: Both shoulders toward floor coaching cue; knee stack.

pose_seated_forward_fold
  sanskrit: Paschimottanasana
  english: Seated Forward Fold
  family: seated
  mode: hold
  notes: Hip hinge priority; strap modification in coaching.

pose_staff
  sanskrit: Dandasana
  english: Staff Pose
  family: seated
  mode: hold
  notes: Seated neutral spine reference.

pose_easy_seated
  sanskrit: Sukhasana
  english: Easy Pose
  family: seated
  mode: hold
  notes: Cross-legged; hip elevation prop cue.

pose_corpses
  sanskrit: Savasana
  english: Corpse Pose
  family: supine
  mode: hold
  notes: Stillness; minimal alignment findings except gross asymmetry or fidgeting (low priority).

RELATIONS (summary — see 002_relations.txt when present)
================================================================================

Sun Salutation A nodes (typical order):
  pose_upward_salute → pose_forward_fold_standing → pose_low_lunge (or high lunge variant) →
  pose_plank → pose_chaturanga_low → pose_up_dog → pose_down_dog → (repeat)

Peak load poses for flow mode: pose_chaturanga_low, pose_chair, pose_warrior_1, pose_bridge

Balance priority poses: pose_tree, pose_warrior_3_prep

Down-regulation / rest: pose_child, pose_corpses, pose_easy_seated
