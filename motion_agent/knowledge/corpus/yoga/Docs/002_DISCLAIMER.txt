# Yoga Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel yoga content summarizes **open exercise science, yoga therapy research reviews, and widely published
teaching cues**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy, diagnosis, or treatment of
spine, joint, nerve, cardiovascular, or mental-health conditions.

- Pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, headache, or sharp joint symptoms during or after practice require evaluation
  by a qualified clinician — do not rely on an app finding to “push through” discomfort.
- Yoga is exercise; intensity varies by style (restorative vs power). Match style to your health status with a
  qualified teacher or clinician when you have chronic conditions.

## High-risk populations (refer out — no automated override)

Automated alignment feedback is **coaching support**, not clearance to practice. Seek professional guidance before
mat work if you have or are recovering from:

- Pregnancy or postpartum (especially new to inversion or deep twist progressions).
- Osteoporosis or low bone density (flexion-loaded spine postures, aggressive forward fold).
- Glaucoma or uncontrolled hypertension (inversion prep, breath retention — not visible in pose-only analysis).
- Recent surgery, joint replacement, or acute disc/radicular symptoms.
- Hypermobility spectrum or Ehlers–Danlos — “deeper” is not “better”; stability over range.

The product may surface **general** caution text from this pack; it cannot know your full history.

## Not a certification or lineage authority

This pack does not replace RYT training, Iyengar / Ashtanga / Viniyoga certification, or studio liability policies.
Sanskrit names and common alignments are **pedagogical summaries**, not definitive scripture or guru instruction.

## Body tracking limitations

Single-camera 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Lumbar curvature and pelvic tilt when the trunk is flexed or the camera is oblique.
- Knee–toe relationship in lunges when the front leg is foreshortened.
- Shoulder flexion and scapular upward rotation in Down Dog when arms align with the camera plane.
- Neck extension in Cobra/Up Dog when the head is turned.
- Hip “squareness” in Warrior I when capture is purely frontal without 45° view.

Findings describe **visible tendencies** with confidence downgrades for occlusion, baggy clothing, long hair over
shoulders, and low frame rate on fast vinyasa transitions.

## Flexibility and “correct” shape

Open literature and teaching traditions emphasize **individual variation** in bone structure (hip socket orientation,
femoral neck angle) limiting squat depth, split angle, or “hips square” appearance. Automated feedback must not
shame natural anthropometry or equate aesthetic Instagram shapes with safety.

## Breath and nervous system

Pranayama, breath retention (kumbhaka), and psychological triggers are **out of scope** for pose-only analysis.
Do not infer breath quality from stillness of the rib cage in 2D pose.

## Injury prevention limits

Population studies link certain movement patterns with load; **individual injury risk cannot be predicted** from a
single clip. Knee valgus, end-range passive stretch, and cervical hyperextension findings are **coaching hints**,
not diagnoses of meniscus, labrum, or disc pathology.

## Youth

Growing athletes and children need age-appropriate duration, load, and supervision. Competitive “deepest pose”
culture is discouraged in youth coaching content in this pack.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support practitioners who already study with a qualified teacher. Beginners learning inversions
or deep backbends need in-person spotting and progressions.

## Emergency

For acute injury, syncope, or chest pain during practice, stop immediately and follow venue emergency procedures.

## Product behavior

- Yoga pack features may be gated as “coming_soon” until hold-window validation — absence of a finding is not proof
  of optimal alignment.
- Mislabeled `pose_id` (user selects Warrior II but performs Triangle) reduces accuracy; product should downgrade confidence.
