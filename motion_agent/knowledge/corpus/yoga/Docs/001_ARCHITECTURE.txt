# Yoga / Mobility Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first yoga and mat-mobility coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the practitioner’s body
is doing across recognizable **entry, hold, transition, and exit** windows for common asanas and short flow segments.

No props tracking, block placement, or instructor voice alignment is required. Mat boundary and wall distance are
optional context only.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Static holds** (15–90 s clips or trimmed hold windows): alignment, joint stacking, breath-linked stillness.
- **Slow transitions** between two named poses (e.g., Down Dog → Plank → Low Cobra).
- **Standing balance** (Tree, Warrior III prep) from single-camera frontal or 45° capture.
- **Floor poses** (Child’s, Bridge, Seated Forward Fold) when hips and spine remain visible.

Out of scope for automated findings (glossary or future packs only):
- Pranayama-only sessions without visible skeletal motion.
- Acro yoga, partner assists, aerial silks, wall rope systems.
- Advanced arm balances (Crow, Handstand) until dedicated detector validation — taxonomy lists them; findings v1
  focus on common beginner–intermediate poses in `Taxonomy/003_common_poses_set.txt`.
- Spiritual, medical, or diagnostic claims about organs, chakras, or disease treatment.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: joint angles, segment alignment, center-of-mass drift,
   shoulder–hip–ankle stacking proxies, knee–ankle–hip line in sagittal/frontal views, hold stillness (velocity norms).
2. **Evidence layering**: peer-reviewed yoga and clinical exercise summaries (B+), ACSM / NHS / Yoga Alliance
   scope-of-practice framing (B), established Iyengar / Krishnamacharya-lineage **coaching cues** as coaching advice
   (B), always paired with disclaimer docs — not as medical prescription.
3. **No fabricated degree targets**: hypermobility and limb-length differences change appearance; ranges are qualitative
   or attributed to published summaries with **youth vs adult** and **pregnancy / acute injury** separation in disclaimers.
4. **Pose-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `pose_id` and `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs.
5. **Hold vs flow fork**: `mode=hold` uses stable-window phases; `mode=flow` uses transition chain events.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- User or classifier selects `pose_id` (or top-3 hypothesis from coarse pose classifier).
- Required joints for hold-likeness: shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, pelvis; neck optional for
  cervical extension faults.

### Hold-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings:
- **Velocity quiet**: median ||v(key joints)|| below threshold for ≥2 s.
- **Pose hypothesis confidence**: template or classifier score for named pose.
- **Capture stability**: camera not panning rapidly.

Gate does not authorize medical advice; it prevents sport-mode false positives on walking or general stretching.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_CLIP_START | first frame | |
| E_ENTRY_END | joint speed drops below hold threshold for ≥12 frames | Start of hold analysis |
| E_HOLD_ANCHOR | argmin COM drift in hold window | Best alignment snapshot |
| E_HOLD_MID | temporal center between E_ENTRY_END and E_EXIT_START | |
| E_DRIFT_ONSET | shoulder or hip angle drift > threshold vs anchor | Optional finding trigger |
| E_EXIT_START | joint speed rises sustained | End hold scoring |
| E_ARRIVAL (flow) | speed min after transition peak | New pose registered |

Future detectors: lumbar flexion proxy from hip–shoulder line, knee valgus in Warrior II, scapular elevation in Down Dog.

### Coach brief generation (planned)

`build_coach_briefs(..., sport="yoga")` will consume:
- Pose ID and phase windows (hold anchor preferred for alignment findings).
- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × visibility.
- One feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding (from Coaching/).

## Youth vs adult

Children and teens differ in balance, femoral anteversion appearance, and hypermobility prevalence. **Hold duration**
and **depth targets** should be age-appropriate; never merge youth and adult flexibility tables without an age tag.

## Pregnancy, osteoporosis, glaucoma, hypertension

High-risk condition modifiers are **disclaimer and coach-referral** only — automated findings must not contradict
clinician restrictions. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.

## Internationalization

v1 corpus is English for RAG. Sanskrit pose names appear as aliases in Taxonomy.
