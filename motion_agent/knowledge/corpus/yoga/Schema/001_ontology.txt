# Yoga Sport Pack — Ontology (Schema)

Aligns with SPORT_PACK_KB_SPEC and yoga/_meta/brain_plan.txt v0.1.
Non-medical product KB: CoachingCaution is coaching education, not clinical screening.

## Entity: Practitioner

Attributes:
- practitioner_id (optional external)
- audience_band: youth | adult | unspecified
- experience_level: beginner | intermediate | advanced (optional UX only)

Relations:
- performs → AsanaInstance
- subject_of → FindingInstance (session-level, optional)

## Entity: PoseFamily

Attributes:
- family_id (e.g., F_INVERTED_V, F_STANDING_LUNGE)
- label_en, label_zh optional
- description_plain (body-first)

Relations:
- includes → Pose

## Entity: Pose

Attributes:
- pose_id (stable slug: downward_dog, warrior_2, tree, …)
- label_en, sanskrit_alias optional (taxonomy synonyms)
- primary_family_id
- bilateral: true | false | asymmetric_left_right
- typical_support: standing | hands_knees | prone | supine | seated

Relations:
- memberOf → PoseFamily
- has_caution → CoachingCaution (0..n)
- has_modification → ModificationOption (0..n)

## Entity: AsanaInstance

Attributes:
- instance_id (session clip)
- detected_pose_id (optional until classifier ships)
- clip_duration_sec
- confidence_band: high | medium | low

Relations:
- instanceOf → Pose
- has_phase_span → AsanaPhase (with start/end frame narrative)

## Entity: AsanaPhase

Attributes:
- phase_id: Y_ENTRY | Y_SETUP | Y_ALIGN | Y_HOLD | Y_EXIT | Y_INTEGRATION
- ordinal (integer)
- description_plain

Relations:
- follows → AsanaPhase
- contains_events → AsanaEvent

## Entity: AsanaEvent

Attributes:
- event_id (e.g., hold_start, align_window_start)
- start_rule (narrative, OpenSim-friendly)
- end_rule (narrative)
- detector_proxy (optional engineering string)

## Entity: Finding

Attributes:
- finding_id (slug, prefix fault_)
- headline (plain language)
- phase_id (primary)
- pose_id or family_id anchor
- severity_hint: info | form | load
- body_signals (list, measurable on body model)
- why_it_matters (comfort, efficiency, load awareness — non-diagnostic)
- authority_level: B+ | B typical
- evidence_type: BiomechRelationship | CoachingAdvice

Relations:
- has_cue → Cue
- has_drill → Drill
- suggested_modification → ModificationOption (0..n)
- related_caution → CoachingCaution (0..n)

## Entity: CoachingCaution

Attributes:
- caution_id (prefix caution_)
- headline (coach-facing, non-diagnostic)
- athlete_message (short UI copy)
- redirect_action: skip | modify | slow_exit | consult_professional
- authority_level: B+ | B (general movement education)

Relations:
- caution_applies_to → Pose | PoseFamily

Note: Do NOT encode ICD conditions or “ contraindicated if you have X diagnosis ” as
automated rules. Wording: “some people prefer to avoid or modify when they notice …”.

## Entity: ModificationOption

Attributes:
- modification_id (prefix mod_)
- label_en (e.g., “Bent knees in down dog”)
- instructions_short (ordered steps)
- props_optional: wall | chair | block | strap | none
- audience_band: youth | adult | all

Relations:
- modification_for → Finding | Pose

## Entity: Cue

Attributes:
- cue_id (cue_<finding_id>)
- finding_id
- feel_text (internal/external focus, body-first)
- coach_text optional

## Entity: Drill

Attributes:
- drill_id (drill_<finding_id>)
- finding_id
- duration_sec (30–90)
- setup (minimal; props allowed as optional)
- instructions (ordered steps)
- youth_variant optional

## Entity: Checkpoint

Attributes:
- checkpoint_id
- phase_id
- quality_dimension (from Quality/001)
- pass_description (body observable)
- fail_links → Finding

## Entity: MeasurementDefinition / AlignmentProxy

Attributes:
- series_id (see brain_plan section 5)
- signal_description
- unit
- primary_phases

Relations:
- measuredOn → AsanaPhase

## Entity: EvidenceChunk (RAG metadata)

Attributes:
- source_id (SOURCE_MANIFEST)
- authority_level
- evidence_type
- retrieved_at
- audience_band optional

## Naming conventions

- Phase IDs: prefix Y_
- Finding IDs: prefix fault_
- Caution IDs: prefix caution_
- Modification IDs: prefix mod_
- Cue IDs: cue_<finding_id>
- Drill IDs: drill_<finding_id>

## Bilateral / facing conventions

Default: camera-facing “front” leg in lunges = front_knee; mirror for left-leading clips.
Corpus uses “front leg / back leg” and “hand-side / opposite side” unless pose is symmetric.
Product logic may swap labels from detected stance; KB stays side-agnostic where possible.
