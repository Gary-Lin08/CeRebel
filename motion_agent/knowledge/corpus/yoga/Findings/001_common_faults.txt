finding_id: fault_dog_lumbar_sag
headline_en: Lower back dips in Downward-Facing Dog
phases: hold_anchor
body_signal: Lumbar extension proxy increases; hips below shoulder–ankle line in sagittal view; rib cage may flare posteriorly. (pose context: pose_down_dog).
why_it_matters: Excess lumbar extension can shift load away from shoulder girdle and hamstring lengthening pattern; population coaching — not individual diagnosis.
feel_cue: "Long spine first; heels toward the floor only if they go without losing length."
drill: 60s: Practice with breath awareness (see Coaching/002_drills.txt).
severity_default: medium
sources: Cramer et al. yoga LBP reviews (B+); Iyengar-style alignment (B)
youth_note: Long spine and balance foot placement; never force heel-to-floor.
