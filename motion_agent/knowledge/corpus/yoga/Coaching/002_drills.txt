# Yoga — Short Drills (30–90 s, body-first, linked to fault_* / pose_id)
# Each drill: drill_id | fault_id | duration | setup | steps | safety

---
drill_id: drill_wall_dog_hinge
fault_id: fault_dog_rounded_spine, fault_dog_lumbar_sag
pose_id: pose_down_dog
duration: 60s
setup: Stand arm’s length from wall, feet hip-width.
steps: Place palms on wall at shoulder height; walk feet back until hips hinge and spine lengthens. Soft knees. Hold 5 breaths. Repeat with 1 cm lower hand placement if comfortable.
safety: Stop if wrist or shoulder pain. Osteoporosis: avoid aggressive flexion elsewhere in session — not this drill.
authority: B
---

---
drill_id: drill_dog_pedal
fault_id: fault_dog_locked_knees, fault_dog_rounded_spine
pose_id: pose_down_dog
duration: 75s
setup: Down Dog or knee-down table variant.
steps: Alternate heel reach 4 cycles, pause 2 breaths with knees bent and spine long. Film one cycle for comparison.
safety: High blood pressure / glaucoma: keep head above heart if physician restricted inversions — use wall dog instead.
authority: B
---

---
drill_id: drill_knee_plank_line
fault_id: fault_plank_hip_sag, fault_plank_hip_pike
pose_id: pose_plank
duration: 90s
setup: Mat, optional dowel or broomstick for partner check.
steps: 30s knee plank with sagittal line; 20s rest; 20s full plank only if line holds; 20s rest. Partner or stick touches hip if line breaks.
safety: Wrist pain: fists or forearms on blocks.
authority: B
---

---
drill_id: drill_eccentric_chaturanga
fault_id: fault_flow_uncontrolled_drop, fault_chaturanga_shoulder_drop
pose_id: pose_chaturanga_low
duration: 90s
setup: Knees down, hands shoulder-width.
steps: 4-second lower from plank knees-down, 1-second pause at lowest safe point (upper arms parallel), optional press up. Max 3 reps in 90s window.
safety: Shoulder injury history: reduce depth; skip press-up.
authority: B+
---

---
drill_id: drill_wall_warrior_knee
fault_id: fault_warrior_knee_collapse
pose_id: pose_warrior_2
duration: 75s
setup: Side to wall, outer back hip near wall.
steps: Open into Warrior II facing away from wall; press front outer foot into floor; hold 5 breaths each side. Knee tracks over ankle.
safety: Knee pain: shorten stance and reduce bend.
authority: B

---
drill_id: drill_tree_wall_touch
fault_id: fault_tree_hip_jut, fault_balance_hip_drop
pose_id: pose_tree
duration: 60s
setup: Stand beside wall, inside hand light on wall.
steps: Enter Tree with foot on calf; touch wall only for balance; remove finger when stable 3 breaths. Switch sides.
safety: fault_tree_foot_on_knee rule — rehearse foot placement before lift.
authority: B

fault_id: fault_chair_knee_collapse
pose_id: pose_chair
duration: 45s
setup: Block between thighs, feet hip-width.
steps: Lower 3 inches on exhale, squeeze block gently, rise halfway on inhale. 4 cycles.
safety: Low back: keep ribs over pelvis.
authority: B

pose_id: pose_bridge

pose_id: pose_cobra

fault_id: fault_forward_fold_spine_round, fault_seated_slump
pose_id: pose_seated_forward_fold
duration: 90s
setup: Seated on blanket, strap around feet.
steps: Inhale sit tall; exhale hinge with strap taut but spine long — 5 breaths. No pulling nose to knee.
safety: Osteoporosis / acute disc symptoms: sit upright only, no end-range flexion.
authority: B+

pose_id: pose_down_dog, pose_plank

setup: Down Dog.

safety: Wrist load: shorten trips or use fists.

# Drill selection logic (product, internal)

- One drill per prioritized finding in coach brief; prefer ≤90s for mobile homework.
- If capture angle poor for pose, substitute generic drill from same fault family (e.g., wall variants).
- Youth: favor balance games and wall support over deep chaturanga eccentrics.
