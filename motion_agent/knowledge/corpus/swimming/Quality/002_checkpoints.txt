# Freestyle — Quality Checkpoints (body-measurable catalog)

Each checkpoint maps to quality axes in Quality/001_quality_definition.txt and phase windows in
Phases/001_phase_model.txt. Severity: info | low | medium | high (coaching priority, not injury severity).

---

checkpoint_id: cp_entry_shoulder_width
phase: swim_phase_entry
axis: body_line
headline_en: Hand enters near shoulder line without crossing midline
signal: Pulling wrist lateral position at E_ENTRY within shoulder-width band relative to sternum proxy
pass_criteria: Wrist entry lateral offset below crossover threshold for camera calibration
fail_finding: fault_crossover_entry
authority: B
confidence_modifiers: reduce if camera_front_only

checkpoint_id: cp_entry_forward_extension
phase: swim_phase_entry
axis: catch_pull
headline_en: Reach extends before catch begins
signal: Time E_ENTRY → E_CATCH within 0.05–0.25 s band at moderate pace; forward wrist peak visible
pass_criteria: Monotonic forward reach then downward path
fail_finding: fault_overreach_entry, fault_pause_at_catch
authority: B+

checkpoint_id: cp_catch_elbow_above_wrist
phase: swim_phase_catch
axis: catch_pull
headline_en: Elbow stays above wrist during catch (EVF proxy)
signal: Side view: elbow Y ≤ wrist Y (image coords) for ≥40% of catch window
pass_criteria: Sustained ordering through E_CATCH to E_PULL_MID
fail_finding: fault_dropped_elbow
authority: B+
confidence_modifiers: low if underwater occluded

checkpoint_id: cp_catch_no_downward_press
phase: swim_phase_catch
axis: catch_pull
headline_en: Hand does not press straight down before moving back
signal: Wrist vertical velocity before backward component; downward-only spike flags fail
pass_finding: fault_sculling_outside (if lateral instead)
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_pull_peak_timing
phase: swim_phase_pull
axis: catch_pull
headline_en: Pull accelerates to peak before push
signal: E_PULL_PEAK occurs after E_CATCH + minimum delay; single peak per cycle
pass_criteria: Unimodal wrist speed curve underwater
fail_finding: fault_double_peaked_pull
authority: B+

checkpoint_id: cp_pull_under_body
phase: swim_phase_pull
axis: body_line
headline_en: Pull path travels under body centerline proxy
signal: Wrist lateral path passes within trunk shadow band in side view
fail_finding: fault_wide_pull, fault_pull_under_body (if too deep and crossed)
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_push_past_hip
phase: swim_phase_push
axis: catch_pull
headline_en: Hand exits after passing hip region
signal: Wrist horizontal position relative to hip keypoint at E_EXIT
pass_criteria: Exit when wrist posterior to hip proxy
fail_finding: fault_short_push
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_recovery_elbow_height
phase: swim_phase_recovery
axis: body_line
headline_en: Recovery elbow leads wrist (high-elbow recovery model)
signal: Elbow Y above wrist Y for ≥50% of recovery phase
fail_finding: fault_low_elbow_recovery
authority: B
notes: Sprint straight-arm recovery may fail by design — tag stroke_style=sprint to relax

checkpoint_id: cp_breathe_rotation_not_lift
phase: swim_phase_breathe
axis: breathing
headline_en: Breath uses head rotation more than vertical lift
signal: Ratio head_yaw / head_vertical_displacement at E_BREATHE
pass_criteria: Yaw dominant in pool swimming
fail_finding: fault_excessive_head_lift
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_breathe_timing
phase: swim_phase_breathe
axis: breathing
headline_en: Breath window avoids peak pull on breathing side
signal: E_BREATHE offset from E_PULL_PEAK on same-side arm
fail_finding: fault_late_breath_timing
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_roll_amplitude
phase: swim_phase_body_roll
axis: rotation
headline_en: Shoulder roll sufficient for reach without snaking
signal: Peak-to-peak shoulder tilt per cycle within athlete baseline or generic 25–50° band
fail_finding: fault_flat_swim, fault_over_rotation
authority: B+

checkpoint_id: cp_kick_hip_driven
phase: swim_phase_kick
axis: kick
headline_en: Kick knee flexion stays moderate
signal: Knee flexion amplitude per kick cycle below bicycle threshold
fail_finding: fault_bicycle_kick
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_kick_narrow
phase: swim_phase_kick
axis: kick
headline_en: Feet stay inside body line projection
signal: Ankle lateral separation relative to hip width
fail_finding: fault_wide_kick
authority: B

checkpoint_id: cp_left_right_period
phase: full_cycle
axis: repeatability
headline_en: Left and right stroke periods match
signal: |T_L - T_R| / mean(T) < asymmetry_threshold (default 0.08)
fail_finding: fault_asymmetric_cycle_timing
authority: ProductLogic

checkpoint_id: cp_left_right_pull_peak
phase: swim_phase_pull
axis: repeatability
headline_en: Pull speed peaks balanced left vs right
signal: |peak_L - peak_R| / mean(peak) < 0.12 with ≥3 cycles each side
fail_finding: fault_asymmetric_pull_power
authority: ProductLogic

checkpoint_id: cp_youth_stroke_rate_sanity
phase: full_cycle
axis: workload_youth
headline_en: Stroke rate appropriate for skill stage (coaching context)
signal: Derived SPM compared to NGB band tables in Training/002_youth_development.txt
fail_finding: none automatic — info flag only
authority: B
audience: youth
