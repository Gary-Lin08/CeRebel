# Freestyle — Coaching Cues (body-first)

Pair with Findings/001_common_faults.txt by finding_id. Language is English primary; avoid guaranteed outcomes.
Authority: B coaching consensus unless noted.

---

## Catch and pull cues

cue_id: cue_elbow_leads
linked_findings: fault_dropped_elbow, fault_pause_at_catch
phase: swim_phase_catch
text_en: "Set your elbow high before you pull — feel water on your forearm, not just your palm."
body_focus: pulling elbow, forearm orientation proxy
duration_hint: use for 4–8 stroke cycles then video check

cue_id: cue_paddle_forearm
linked_findings: fault_dropped_elbow, fault_sculling_outside
phase: swim_phase_catch, swim_phase_pull
text_en: "Imagine your forearm is a paddle attached at the elbow — don't break the wrist down first."
body_focus: wrist-elbow ordering

cue_id: cue_pull_pocket
linked_findings: fault_wide_pull, fault_sculling_outside
phase: swim_phase_pull
text_en: "Pull in your pocket — elbow brushes your ribs on the way back."
body_focus: lateral wrist path

cue_id: cue_push_to_thigh
linked_findings: fault_short_push, fault_upward_exit
phase: swim_phase_push
text_en: "Push all the way to your thigh before you exit — finish the stroke."
body_focus: E_EXIT timing vs hip

cue_id: cue_continuous_catch
linked_findings: fault_pause_at_catch
phase: swim_phase_catch
text_en: "No dead spot — connect entry straight into the catch."
body_focus: wrist speed continuity

cue_id: cue_kick_steady

## Tempo and rhythm cues

cue_id: cue_metronome_stroke
linked_findings: fault_asymmetric_cycle_timing, fault_double_peaked_pull
phase: full_cycle
text_en: "Same beat every stroke — enter, catch, pull, finish."
body_focus: cycle period stability

cue_id: cue_slower_to_learn
linked_findings: fault_pause_at_catch, fault_dropped_elbow
phase: full_cycle
text_en: "Slow the hands to learn the catch — speed comes after shape."
body_focus: reduce SPM temporarily
audience: youth, beginners

## Shoulder-friendly language (non-medical)

cue_id: cue_stop_if_pinch
linked_findings: any shoulder-load pattern flags
phase: any
text_en: "If you feel pinching or sharp pain in the shoulder, stop and tell your coach — don't push through."
body_focus: safety
authority: A disclaimer tier — not a mechanics cue alone

## Cue delivery notes for CeRebel briefs

- Prefer **one cue per finding** in automated briefs to avoid overload.
- Match cue to detected phase window when timing data exists.
- For open_water_sighting metadata, deprioritize head-lift cues during tagged sighting strokes.
