# Freestyle — Drills (30–90 s and set patterns)

Equipment mentions are optional; coaches decide safety and pool rules. Link to finding_id from Findings/.
Authority: B NGB progressions and open coaching literature summaries.

---

drill_id: drill_fist_drill
linked_findings: fault_dropped_elbow, fault_pause_at_catch
duration: 30–60 s or 2 × 25 m
description: Swim freestyle with closed fists to emphasize forearm surface; forces earlier vertical forearm engagement after entry.
body_focus: catch, pull
coaching_points: Maintain roll; do not increase stroke rate to compensate; reopen hands for next 25 m comparison.
caution: Shoulder discomfort — stop if pain.

drill_id: drill_sculling_front
linked_findings: fault_dropped_elbow, fault_sculling_outside
duration: 45–90 s
description: Scull in front quadrant with elbows high, small figure-eight hand motion; then swim 25 m applying same elbow height into catch.
body_focus: catch
coaching_points: Elbows near surface; feel pressure on palms/forearms.

drill_id: drill_single_arm_freestyle
linked_findings: fault_crossover_entry, fault_wide_pull, fault_asymmetric_pull_power
duration: 6 × 25 m alternating arms
description: One arm strokes while other rests extended forward or at side; breathe away from stroking arm or use snorkel if coach approves.
body_focus: entry line, pull path
coaching_points: Enter at shoulder width; complete push past hip.

drill_id: drill_catch_up
linked_findings: fault_overreach_entry, fault_asymmetric_cycle_timing
duration: 4 × 50 m
description: Hands touch or nearly touch in front before next pull begins; slows cycle to emphasize alignment and roll.
body_focus: timing, body line
coaching_points: Do not pause excessively at front — touch and go.

drill_id: drill_6_kick_switch
linked_findings: fault_flat_swim, fault_excessive_head_lift
duration: 8 × 25 m
description: Six flutter kicks on side with bottom arm extended, then take one full stroke to switch sides.
body_focus: body roll, kick
coaching_points: Head down except when rotating to breathe; hips and shoulders stack.

drill_id: drill_vertical_kick
linked_findings: fault_bicycle_kick, fault_wide_kick
duration: 4 × 30 s at wall
description: Vertical flutter kick in deep water, arms crossed or above water; maintain narrow kick.
body_focus: kick
coaching_points: Hips drive; knees soft; continuous small kicks.

drill_id: drill_side_kick
linked_findings: fault_wide_kick, fault_snaking_hips
duration: 6 × 25 m per side
description: Kick on side with lower arm extended, head aligned with spine; optional snorkel.
body_focus: alignment, kick width
coaching_points: Top shoulder stacked over bottom hip.

drill_id: drill_finger_tip_drag
linked_findings: fault_low_elbow_recovery
duration: 4 × 25 m
description: Drag fingertips along water surface during recovery to promote high elbow path.
body_focus: recovery
coaching_points: Relaxed wrist; elbow leads.

drill_id: drill_bilateral_breathing
linked_findings: fault_excessive_head_lift, fault_asymmetric_cycle_timing
duration: 3 × 100 m breathe every 3
description: Bilateral breathing pattern to balance roll and timing.
body_focus: breathing, rhythm
coaching_points: One goggle in; exhale underwater.

drill_id: drill_snorkel_technique
linked_findings: fault_excessive_head_lift, fault_late_breath_timing
duration: 4 × 50 m
description: Front snorkel allows continuous head-down focus on catch and pull; remove snorkel for breath timing sets after.
body_focus: catch, pull
equipment: snorkel
caution: Clear snorkel properly; beginner supervision.

drill_id: drill_pull_buoy_pull
linked_findings: fault_wide_pull, fault_double_peaked_pull
duration: 4 × 100 m pull
description: Pull buoy between legs isolates arm stroke; emphasize continuous pull and push finish.
body_focus: pull, push
equipment: pull buoy
caution: Can mask kick issues — alternate with full stroke.

drill_id: drill_tempo_increase
linked_findings: fault_pause_at_catch
duration: 4 × 50 m (+2 SPM steps)
description: Gradually increase stroke rate while maintaining catch connection — use tempo beeper if available.
body_focus: rhythm, catch continuity
coaching_points: Do not sacrifice EVF proxy for rate in learning stages.

drill_id: drill_finish_touch_thigh
linked_findings: fault_short_push, fault_upward_exit
duration: 8 × 25 m
description: Emphasize hand brushing thigh on exit before recovery; may exaggerate briefly for learning.
body_focus: push phase
caution: Over-exaggeration can irritate shoulder — moderate range.

drill_id: drill_streamline_push_off
linked_findings: n/a (skill context)
duration: 4 × streamline to flags
description: Not a fault drill — teaches body line before stroke cycles; exclude push-off from fault detection in metadata.
body_focus: body line
notes: Tag trim_start_event=push_off in analysis.

drill_id: drill_open_water_sighting
linked_findings: fault_excessive_head_lift (contextual)
duration: 5 × 50 m sight every 6 strokes
description: Practice quick forward sight without full vertical lift; for open-water athletes.
body_focus: head motion
notes: Lower automated head-lift fault weight when tagged.

## Youth modifications

- Shorten distances to 15 m when attention or fitness limits apply.
- Prefer drill_6_kick_switch and drill_side_kick before band or heavy pull buoy volume.
- Emphasize exhale cues (drill_bilateral_breathing pattern) before advanced catch drills.

drill_id: drill_smooth_breath_return
linked_findings: fault_early_breath_drop, fault_excessive_head_lift
duration: 6 × 25 m
description: Breathe every 3 with slow head return; compare to snorkel set.
body_focus: breathe
coaching_points: No slam of head after inhale.

drill_id: drill_finish_quiet_exit
linked_findings: fault_thumb_first_exit_extreme, fault_short_push
duration: 8 × 25 m
description: Exaggerate finish to thigh then quiet recovery; coach watches exit only.
body_focus: push, exit

drill_id: drill_hip_wake_kick
linked_findings: fault_dead_hip_kick, fault_surface_kick_splash
duration: 4 × 25 m + 30 s vertical
description: Kickboard then swim matching hip rhythm; optional vertical kick.
body_focus: kick
