# Freestyle Swimming — Concept Taxonomy (v1)

## Primary concepts

concept_id: freestyle_front_crawl
label_en: Freestyle (front crawl)
definition: Continuous prone stroke with alternating arm pull and flutter kick; fastest competitive stroke; body-first analysis uses arm cycle timing without pool instrumentation.
synonyms_en: front crawl, freestyle, crawl stroke
synonyms_zh: 自由泳, 爬泳
parent: swimming
authority: A+ (competition naming via World Aquatics); B+ mechanics

concept_id: stroke_cycle
label_en: Stroke cycle
definition: One complete sequence from hand entry through pull, push, exit, and recovery on one arm; paired with contralateral arm half-cycle offset.
synonyms_en: stroke count cycle, arm cycle
related_phases: all
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: early_vertical_forearm
label_en: Early vertical forearm (EVF)
definition: Coaching construct: forearm oriented to press backward soon after entry with elbow flexed above wrist in side view; associated with effective catch in many biomechanics summaries.
synonyms_en: high elbow catch, vertical forearm, catch position
related_phases: swim_phase_catch, swim_phase_pull
authority: B+

concept_id: body_roll
label_en: Body roll (hip-shoulder rotation)
definition: Trunk rotation around longitudinal axis to facilitate reach and pull; amplitude varies by event distance and individual mobility.
synonyms_en: hip rotation, shoulder roll, rotation timing
related_phases: swim_phase_body_roll
authority: B+

concept_id: two_beat_six_beat_kick
label_en: Kick timing patterns
definition: Flutter kick frequency expressed relative to arm cycle — e.g., six-beat (six kicks per full arm cycle) common in middle-distance; two-beat in some distance models.
synonyms_en: flutter kick rhythm, beat cycle
related_phases: swim_phase_kick
authority: B

## Phase concepts (align to swim_phase_*)

concept_id: phase_recovery
label_en: Arm recovery
start_event: E_EXIT
end_event: E_ENTRY
body_focus: relaxed shoulder, elbow high, no crossover

concept_id: phase_entry
label_en: Hand entry
start_event: E_ENTRY
end_event: E_CATCH
body_focus: finger-first entry, extend along line, head neutral

concept_id: phase_catch
label_en: Catch
start_event: E_CATCH
end_event: E_PULL_MID
body_focus: elbow above wrist, forearm vertical proxy, no pause

concept_id: phase_pull
label_en: Pull
start_event: E_PULL_MID
end_event: E_PUSH_START
body_focus: accelerate backward under body, roll with pull

concept_id: phase_push
label_en: Push / finish
start_event: E_PUSH_START
end_event: E_EXIT
body_focus: complete push past hip, clean exit

concept_id: phase_breathe
label_en: Breathing
start_event: E_BREATHE_START
end_event: E_BREATHE_END
body_focus: rotate head, one goggle in, quick inhale

concept_id: phase_kick
label_en: Flutter kick
continuous: true
body_focus: hip-initiated, ankles relaxed, narrow kick width

## Event concepts

concept_id: event_entry
label_en: Hand entry event
definition: Frame where pulling hand transitions from recovery to water contact and forward extension completes.
detector: E_ENTRY

concept_id: event_catch
label_en: Catch initiation
definition: Elbow flexion accelerates and wrist depth increases after entry.
detector: E_CATCH

concept_id: event_pull_peak
label_en: Pull speed peak
definition: Maximum pulling-wrist speed during underwater phase.
detector: E_PULL_PEAK

concept_id: event_exit
label_en: Hand exit
definition: Hand leaves water near hip to begin recovery.
detector: E_EXIT

## Stroke variants (glossary, limited v1 findings)

concept_id: sprint_freestyle
label_en: Sprint freestyle
definition: Higher stroke rate, straighter recovery, stronger kick; shorter catch window.
authority: B

concept_id: distance_freestyle
label_en: Distance freestyle
definition: Longer stroke length, higher elbow recovery common, kick reduced for efficiency.
authority: B

concept_id: open_water_freestyle
label_en: Open-water freestyle
definition: Sighting strokes, higher head lift episodic, wider stroke for stability in chop.
authority: B

## Equipment and environment (non-finding)

concept_id: pool_swimming
label_en: Pool swimming
definition: Controlled lane swimming; side camera feasible.
authority: B

concept_id: open_water_swimming
label_en: Open water swimming
definition: Variable visibility and sighting; motion confidence flags apply.
authority: B

## Measurement concepts

concept_id: stroke_rate
label_en: Stroke rate
definition: Cycles per minute derived from E_ENTRY period; body-measurable from video.
synonyms_en: cadence, SPM
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: stroke_length_proxy
label_en: Stroke length (proxy)
definition: Distance per cycle estimated from body speed × cycle period when pool length unknown.
authority: ProductLogic

concept_id: shoulder_impingement_risk_language
label_en: Shoulder load language (non-diagnostic)
definition: Careful wording for dropped elbow and overreach patterns discussed in clinical reviews — not a diagnosis.
authority: A (clinical context only)
