# Freestyle — Common Faults (finding cards)

Authority: B / B+ summarized open literature and NGB coaching; not diagnostic. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.

---

---

finding_id: fault_dropped_elbow
headline_en: Dropped elbow during catch or pull
phases: swim_phase_catch, swim_phase_pull
body_signal: Pulling elbow drops below or level with wrist in side view during underwater phase; elbow flexion may appear but forearm not vertical relative to backward pull direction.
why_it_matters: Often associated with reduced propulsive surface area and higher shoulder stress in biomechanics summaries; swimmers may slip water and add shoulder workload over long sessions.
feel_cue: "Lead with your elbow; point fingers down and set the forearm like a paddle before you pull."
drill: 60 s sculling at catch depth, then 25 m fist drill focusing on forearm angle (see Coaching/002_drills.txt drill_sculling_front, drill_fist_drill).
severity_default: medium
sources: PMC freestyle kinematic reviews; USA Swimming skill progression summaries (B+ / B)
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_low_elbow_recovery
headline_en: Low elbow during arm recovery
phases: swim_phase_recovery
body_signal: Wrist at or above elbow height during recovery; arm swings wide or drags near water surface with elbow leading downward.
why_it_matters: Can increase frontal drag in some models and setup crossover entry; may reflect shoulder mobility limits or fatigue.
feel_cue: "Brush your elbow past your ribs; hand relaxed, elbow points mostly to the side."
drill: 4 × 25 m finger-tip drag recovery drill with pause at entry.
severity_default: low
sources: Coaching consensus B; distance freestyle models
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_crossover_entry
headline_en: Hand crosses centerline at entry
phases: swim_phase_entry
body_signal: Pulling wrist crosses midline of trunk proxy at or immediately after E_ENTRY.
why_it_matters: Associated with snaking hips and shoulder impingement risk language in clinical reviews; reduces stable catch platform.
feel_cue: "Enter at your shoulder width — hand in front of shoulder, not in front of nose."
drill: Single-arm freestyle with opposite hand on board; 6 × 25 m focusing entry target.
severity_default: medium
sources: B+ shoulder load discussions; NGB learn-to-swim B
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_overreach_entry
headline_en: Overreaching at entry
phases: swim_phase_entry
body_signal: Excessive forward reach with elbow hyperextension proxy before catch; entry far ahead of stable roll position.
why_it_matters: May increase shoulder extension load and weaken immediate catch connection in coaching models.
feel_cue: "Slide the hand in and catch underneath — don't reach for the wall."
drill: Catch-up drill with pause at entry, 8 × 50 m moderate.
severity_default: low
sources: B biomechanics summaries
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_flat_hand_entry
headline_en: Flat or palm-first entry (visual)
phases: swim_phase_entry
body_signal: Hand orientation appears parallel to surface at entry; splash or slapping sound if audio available (optional).
why_it_matters: Coaching texts link poor entry angle to bubbles on hand and missed catch timing; body-only confidence limited.
feel_cue: "Knife in fingers first, then wrist, then elbow."
drill: 10 × 25 m entry-only scull: enter and pause at extension.
severity_default: low
confidence_note: Requires clear hand visibility
sources: B coaching
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_pause_at_catch
headline_en: Pause or glide at catch
phases: swim_phase_catch
body_signal: Wrist speed local minimum lasting >0.12 s between E_ENTRY and E_PULL_PEAK without corresponding roll adjustment.
why_it_matters: Breaks continuous propulsion; common when swimmers over-glide distance sets.
feel_cue: "Don't hang — bend the elbow and start moving water backward immediately."
drill: Tempo trainer sets (+2 SPM) for 4 × 100 m focusing continuous catch.
severity_default: medium
sources: B+ propulsion timing reviews
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_sculling_outside
headline_en: Outward scull instead of backward pull
phases: swim_phase_catch
body_signal: Lateral wrist path exceeds backward component in catch window; elbows wide from trunk.
why_it_matters: Reduces forward propulsion efficiency in steady-state freestyle models.
feel_cue: "Pull past your suit logo — hand tracks under your body."
drill: Pull buoy optional: 6 × 25 m pull with band (if coach approves equipment).
severity_default: medium
sources: B
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_excessive_head_lift
headline_en: Head lifts straight up to breathe
phases: swim_phase_breathe
body_signal: Head vertical displacement dominates over yaw at E_BREATHE; hips may drop in side view.
why_it_matters: Increases drag and breaks body line in pool models; neck extension load increases.
feel_cue: "One goggle in the water — rotate to the wall, don't lift to the ceiling."
drill: 3 × 100 m bilateral breathing every 3; 25 m no-breath focus on rotation.
severity_default: medium
sources: B NGB; B+ drag/posture summaries
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

why_it_matters: Can compromise pull force and body line at critical propulsive instant.
feel_cue: "Breathe early in the stroke — begin rotation as recovery arm passes."
drill: 8 × 25 m breathe-at-recovery emphasis with snorkel comparison sets.
severity_default: low
sources: B
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_early_breath_drop
headline_en: Head drops abruptly after breath interrupting body line
phases: swim_phase_breathe, swim_phase_entry
body_signal: After E_BREATHE, head pitch returns with high angular velocity while recovering wrist still mid-recovery; hips may spike then drop in side view.
why_it_matters: Abrupt head drop can break streamline and delay next catch in coaching models (performance / drag framing).
feel_cue: Return the head smooth — cheek to water, then eyes down, no slam.
drill: 6 × 25 m breathe every 3 with slow head return emphasis; compare to snorkel set.
severity_default: low
sources: NGB freestyle breathing progressions (B); posture/drag summaries (B+)
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_thumb_first_exit_extreme
headline_en: Extreme thumb-first twist at exit collapses elbow
phases: swim_phase_push, swim_phase_recovery
body_signal: At E_EXIT, forearm pronates sharply with elbow flexion spike; wrist path rises medial before recovery elbow leads.
why_it_matters: Mild thumb-first exit is coached; extreme twist can shorten push and stress recovery path (coaching — not diagnosis).
feel_cue: "Finish long, then pinky-to-thumb soft — don't corkscrew the elbow out."
drill: 8 × 25 m exaggerate finish to thigh then quiet recovery; coach watches exit only.
severity_default: low
sources: Stroke-exit coaching consensus (B)
confidence_note: Needs clear side or 45° view of exit hand
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.

finding_id: fault_dead_hip_kick
headline_en: Hips stay rigid with minimal kick contribution
phases: swim_phase_kick, swim_phase_body_roll
body_signal: Ankle vertical oscillation amplitude near zero across ≥3 cycles while arm cycle continues; pelvis pitch variance flat.
why_it_matters: Kick supports body line and timing in freestyle models; dead hips often pair with sinking legs and rushed arms (performance).
feel_cue: "Wake up the hips — small constant flutter from the hip hinges."
drill: 4 × 25 m kickboard then 25 m swim matching same hip rhythm; optional vertical kick 30 s.
severity_default: medium
sources: Kick contribution coaching (B); freestyle coordination reviews (B+)
youth_note: Continuous catch and breath timing; avoid race-pace kick volume cues for beginners.
