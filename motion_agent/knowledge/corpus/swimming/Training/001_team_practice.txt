# Freestyle — Team Practice Structure (coaching context)

Authority: B NGB and club coaching conventions; summarized for RAG — verify live programs locally.
Not a substitute for certified coach planning or medical training advice.

---

## Session architecture (typical 60–90 min pool practice)

Most age-group and masters programs organize work into:

1. **Warm-up (10–15 min)** — easy swim, drill progression, activation. Purpose: tissue prep and skill priming, not max effort.
2. **Technique block (15–25 min)** — low to moderate intensity drills linked to weekly theme (catch, breathing, kick).
3. **Main set (20–40 min)** — aerobic, threshold, or sprint work with technique hold points.
4. **Cool-down (5–10 min)** — easy swim, optional stretch on deck per team policy.

CeRebel video clips often come from **technique block** or **main set** sub-intervals — note fatigue effects on findings.

---

## Weekly theme rotation (example mesocycle)

| Week focus | Technique emphasis | Sample drill linkage |
|------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Catch / EVF | elbow path, continuous catch | drill_fist_drill, drill_sculling_front |
| Body line | crossover, snaking | drill_catch_up, drill_single_arm_freestyle |
| Breathing | rotation, bilateral timing | drill_bilateral_breathing, drill_6_kick_switch |
| Kick | hip-driven, narrow | drill_vertical_kick, drill_side_kick |
| Race skills | turns, finishes (glossary) | drill_finish_touch_thigh |

Rotate themes rather than stacking all faults in one session.

## Set examples (moderate volume)

### Technique-heavy (distance group)

- 400 swim easy
- 8 × 50 drill/swim by 25 (drill in Coaching/002_drills.txt)
- 5 × 200 freestyle @ moderate hold stroke count
- 200 easy

### Sprint-oriented

- 300 swim
- 6 × 25 build drill → swim
- 8 × 50 @ threshold with focus on push finish
- 4 × 25 sprint kick
- 200 easy

### Masters short practice (45 min)

- 200 warm-up
- 4 × 50 technique focus (one finding from video review)
- 10 × 100 aerobic intervals
- 100 cool-down

Adjust distances for pool length (25 yd vs 25 m vs 50 m).

## Integrating video review

1. Capture **side view** during technique block before fatigue (cycles cleaner).
2. Optional second clip from **main set** mid-set to compare fault emergence under load.
3. Coach selects **one finding + one cue + one drill** for homework (align with Coaching/ files).
4. Re-shoot after 2–3 sessions — track repeatability axis in Quality/001_quality_definition.txt.

## Dry-land relationship (high level)

Dry-land strength and mobility programs support swimming but are **not** analyzed by this pack.
Open literature links scapular control and thoracic mobility to shoulder comfort — refer athletes to qualified
S&C coaches; no prescriptive exercises in this corpus beyond generic stop-if-pain language.

---

## Equipment policies

Pull buoys, paddles, fins, bands, and snorkels change stroke mechanics. When reviewing video:
- Tag `equipment=none` for plain stroke analysis when possible.
- If paddles/fins used, downgrade automated catch/pull finding confidence in product metadata.

---

## Open water training add-ons

- Sighting sets (drill_open_water_sighting)
- Drafting practice (no body findings)
- Wetsuit sessions alter body line — note in metadata

---

## Communication with athletes

Use plain language from Coaching/001_cues.txt; avoid jargon ("EVF") with beginners unless explaining briefly.
Pair automated briefs with coach observation of breathing comfort and effort level.

---

## Load monitoring (non-medical)

Coaches track:
- Weekly distance and intensity distribution
- RPE and mood
- Shoulder complaints — any pain stops technique progression until evaluated

This pack does not compute training load from video alone.
