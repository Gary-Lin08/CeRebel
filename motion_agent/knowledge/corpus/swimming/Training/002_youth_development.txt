# Freestyle — Youth Development and Volume Context

---

## Developmental stages (conceptual)

Most NGB models progress swimmers through:

1. **Water comfort and safety** — floating, kicking, breath control (may precede full freestyle cycle).
2. **Linear body line** — kick with board, side kicking, long spine.
3. **Arms integration** — single-arm, catch-up, first full freestyle with breathing.
4. **Endurance and legal stroke** — continuous 25 m → 50 m → longer repeats with legal touch turns (rules in glossary).
5. **Performance training** — periodized volume and race skills.

CeRebel fault severity should be **lower** for stages 2–3 on advanced checkpoints (EVF strictness) until catch exists consistently.

---

## Skill priorities by stage (freestyle-focused)

### Stage 2 — kick and line

- fault_bicycle_kick, fault_wide_kick, fault_excessive_head_lift (when breath introduced)
- Drills: drill_side_kick, drill_vertical_kick, drill_6_kick_switch (short distances)

### Stage 3 — arms and breath

- fault_crossover_entry, fault_hold_breath_tension, fault_flat_swim
- Drills: drill_catch_up, drill_single_arm_freestyle, drill_bilateral_breathing (every 4–5 for beginners)

### Stage 4 — catch and efficiency

- fault_dropped_elbow, fault_pause_at_catch, fault_short_push
- Drills: drill_fist_drill, drill_sculling_front (supervised)

### Stage 5 — performance

- axis_repeatability, asymmetric patterns under fatigue
- Integrate race pacing; avoid adult elite distance templates on pre-adolescent swimmers without coach plan.

## Stroke rate guidance (coaching bands, not medical)

Youth stroke rates vary widely with height and skill. Coaches use **stroke count** per length plus subjective ease.

Illustrative **info-only** bands for 25 m pool freestyle (verify locally):

| Level (approx.) | SPM range (info) | Notes |
|-----------------|------------------|-------|
| Learning continuous swim | 40–70 | Higher rates common; length short |
| Age-group trained | 50–80 | Technique themes rotate weekly |
| Advanced junior | 60–90 | Event-specific coaching |

Product checkpoint cp_youth_stroke_rate_sanity flags **info** when derived SPM falls far outside athlete's recent baseline — not automatic fault.

## Volume and rest (principles)

- Increase volume **gradually**; technique degradation at end of long sets is expected — interpret findings with fatigue tag.
- Multi-sport youth should coordinate with other coaches to avoid cumulative shoulder load.
- No single universal yardage cap in this pack — cite team and NGB policies.

Pitch Smart analog for swimming does not exist as one global standard; use **club age-group guidelines**.

---

## Safety and supervision

- Constant supervision per venue rules.
- Teach exhale underwater early to reduce breath-holding anxiety (fault_hold_breath_tension context).
- Stop for pain, dizziness, or breathing distress — disclaimer mandatory.

---

## Parent-facing language (RAG)

When users ask about child’s stroke:
- Emphasize **fun, safety, and long-term skill** over split times.
- Recommend certified learn-to-swim or team coaches for progression.
- Avoid comparing child to elite video templates.
