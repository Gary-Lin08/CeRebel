================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for freestyle (front crawl) stroke mechanics with
arm–body–leg timing inferred from monocular or multi-view deck video (preferred:
side profile + optional overhead). Other competitive strokes (backstroke,
breaststroke, butterfly) are glossary + phase stubs only in v1 — no automated
finding catalog until dedicated packs or stroke classifier ships.

Out of scope v1:
- Lap time, split times, SWOLF, pace per 100, turn analysis, underwater-only footage
- Touchpad / starting-block instrumentation, wearable IMU stroke-rate as required input
- Medical diagnosis, shoulder impingement claims from 2D pose alone
- Water-line segmentation required for "in water vs recovery" (use body proxies)

Build agent writes full Findings/Coaching bodies from this map; brain agent does not.

================================================================================
2. PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

- All measurable claims tie to reconstructed body: joint angles, segment speeds,
  trunk roll, head/neck orientation relative to spine — NOT wall-clock stroke rate
  unless derived from repeating cycle events in the same video clip.
- Capture guidance: side view for arm path, kick depth, head position; above view
  for body roll symmetry, arm crossing, kick width. Oblique reduces roll accuracy.
- Each user-facing finding MUST map to: phase window, series/signal id, feel cue,
  30–90s drill (deck or dryland where noted).
- Youth vs adult numeric norms: separate bands in Biomechanics/001; never present
  youth numbers as adult targets.
- Authority: A/A+ for federation safety & volume; B+ for coaching consensus;
  A for peer-reviewed swim biomech summaries; no C in checkpoints/findings.
- Do NOT require pool timing systems or official meet data for any product feature.

4. PHASE MODEL — FREESTYLE (UI + FUTURE DETECTOR ALIGNMENT)
================================================================================

Phase IDs (exact — stable contract for future sport_analysis swimming window):

| id            | label_en        | label_zh     | role |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|------|
| fs_glide      | Glide           | 滑行         | Brief extension between cycles; minimal wrist speed |
| fs_entry      | Entry           | 入水         | Hand enters forward; arm extends toward line |
| fs_catch      | Catch           | 抱水/抓水    | Elbow lifts; wrist drops; early vertical force proxy |
| fs_pull       | Pull            | 划水         | Propulsive phase; hand accelerates backward vs trunk |
| fs_finish     | Finish          | 推水         | Hand passes hip; elbow extends late |
| fs_recovery   | Recovery        | 移臂         | Elbow leads; arm clears above body proxy |
| fs_breath     | Breath (opt.)   | 换气         | Head rotates; only when visible in clip |
| fs_kick_beat  | Kick downbeat   | 打腿下压     | Ankle vertical velocity peak (side view) |

Event definitions (body-only, OpenSim-friendly, one stroke cycle on analysis arm):

- cycle_anchor: repeating argmax pull-side wrist speed backward component in window
  (or min-to-max elbow flexion cycle — future code picks one primary).
- E_ENTRY: pull-side wrist forward displacement local max before catch flexion rise.
- E_CATCH: pull-side elbow flexion local min→rise inflection + wrist below shoulder proxy.
- E_PULL_PEAK: argmax ||v(pull_wrist)|| in backward direction vs trunk mid-cycle.
- E_FINISH: pull-side shoulder extension local max / wrist passes hip landmark proxy.
- E_RECOVERY_START: pull-side elbow flexion drops after finish; wrist rises relative to trunk.
- E_BODY_ROLL_MAX: |trunk_roll| peak toward pull side during catch–pull (side or above).
- E_KICK_DOWN: argmax ankle vertical velocity (same-side or bilateral average) near catch.

Phase spans (conceptual — Phases/001 expands fallbacks):

- fs_glide: prior E_FINISH → E_ENTRY (short if continuous sprint).
- fs_entry: E_ENTRY → E_CATCH.
- fs_catch: E_CATCH → 30% of E_CATCH→E_PULL_PEAK interval.
- fs_pull: end(fs_catch) → E_PULL_PEAK.
- fs_finish: E_PULL_PEAK → E_FINISH.
- fs_recovery: E_FINISH → E_ENTRY (next cycle).
- fs_breath: overlap fs_recovery/entry when head yaw exceeds threshold (optional phase).
- fs_kick_beat: E_KICK_DOWN nearest E_CATCH (±25% cycle — heuristic).

Other strokes (no v1 phase IDs in UI): see Taxonomy/001 stroke_family_* stubs.

5. MEASUREMENT SURFACE (CODE ↔ KB)
================================================================================

Analysis board series (define norms & checkpoints per series):

| series_id           | signal                                      | unit | primary phases        | view hint |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------|------|------------------------|-----------|
| body_roll           | trunk roll angle (pull-side down)           | °    | fs_catch, fs_pull      | side/above |
| head_alignment      | head vs trunk long axis (side bend)          | °    | fs_breath, fs_glide    | side |
| pull_elbow_flex     | pull-side elbow flexion                     | °    | fs_catch, fs_pull      | side |
| pull_wrist_path     | pull-side wrist depth vs shoulder           | cm*  | fs_catch, fs_pull      | side |
| recovery_elbow_lead | recovery-side elbow height vs wrist         | cm*  | fs_recovery            | side |
| kick_amplitude      | ankle vertical excursion (cycle avg)      | cm*  | fs_kick_beat           | side |
| kick_timing         | phase offset E_KICK_DOWN − E_CATCH          | ms   | fs_kick_beat, fs_catch | side |
| hip_drop            | pelvis lateral tilt / COM sink proxy        | cm*  | fs_catch, fs_pull      | side |
| arm_crossover       | pull-side wrist medial to spine (above)     | cm*  | fs_entry, fs_recovery | above |
| stroke_cycle_time   | E_ENTRY → next E_ENTRY from same video      | ms   | all                    | derived |

* Scale from stature estimate or shoulder–hip segment; not absolute pool meters.

NOT in v1 series: hand force, propulsive impulse, true S pull path without 3D.

================================================================================
6. CLINICAL / COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED)
================================================================================

Use as RAG reference bands, NOT medical advice. Build adds URLs in SOURCE_MANIFEST.

ADULT (fit/competitive swimmers; mostly B+ coaching + A kinematic reviews):

- Body roll: often ~30–45° toward pull side at catch in coached distance freestyle (B+);
  product flags excessive flat (< ~15°) or over-roll (> ~55°) as qualitative checkpoints.
- Catch: early vertical forearm cue; elbow flex often increases notably after entry (B+);
  avoid prescribing exact ° as universal — youth differs.
- Kick: 6-beat vs 2-beat stylistic; product measures amplitude + timing vs catch, not
  "correct beat count" without full cycle visibility.
- Head: neutral alignment in non-breath phases; excessive lift during breath (B+ fault).
- Hip position: hips near surface — proxy via pelvis vs shoulder line in side view (B+).

YOUTH (learn-to-swim through age-group; B+ / federation safety A+ for volume):

- Higher head lift common; shorter lever arms; lower body roll — down-weight severity.
- Emphasis on horizontal balance and pain-free shoulder path over elite catch angles.
- No high-volume paddle prescriptions in product KB without coach context.

Exclude pool timing norms (e.g. "X seconds per 100") from automated findings.
