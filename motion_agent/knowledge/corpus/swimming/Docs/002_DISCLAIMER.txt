# Swimming Sport Pack — Disclaimer and Safe Use

## Not medical advice

CeRebel swimming content summarizes **open coaching literature, biomechanics reviews, and federation learn-to-swim
guidance**. It is **not** a substitute for licensed medical care, physical therapy diagnosis, or return-to-swim
protocols after injury.

- Shoulder, neck, or lower-back pain during or after swimming requires evaluation by a qualified clinician — do
  not rely on an app finding to increase volume or ignore symptoms.
- Youth swimmers differ in maturity, stroke learning stage, and tolerance for volume; follow club and NGB
  progression guidelines rather than copying elite training distances.

## Not a lifeguard or safety system

This product does **not** detect drowning, hypoxia, cardiac events, or pool rule violations. Always swim with
appropriate supervision, lifeguard coverage, and venue safety rules.

## Body tracking limitations

Pool video and 2D pose estimation can mis-estimate:

- Hand and forearm orientation underwater (refraction, bubbles, turbidity).
- Elbow angle when the arm passes beneath the trunk or is merged with the torso silhouette.
- Kick depth and knee flexion when the body roll hides the legs behind the torso.
- Head position during breathing when water splash occludes the face.

Findings describe **visible tendencies** from the captured angle, not instrumented tether tests or force plates.
Confidence should be reduced for front-only cameras, low frame rate, or open-water sighting patterns.

## No guaranteed performance outcomes

Stroke cues may support efficient patterns discussed in research, but **speed, endurance, and injury outcomes**
depend on training history, sleep, nutrition, cumulative volume, pool conditions, and individual anatomy.
Do not promise time drops from a single drill or cue.

## Biomechanical “norms”

Open literature reports **ranges and group averages** across skill levels. Limb length, flexibility, and breathing
side preferences change appearance. This pack avoids universal degree targets; it describes relationships
(e.g., early vertical forearm after entry) with source attribution.

## Coaching relationship

Automated briefs support coaches and athletes who already have a training plan. They do not replace in-person
coaching for breath control, flip turns, racing starts, or beginner water safety skills.

## Emergency

For acute injury, drowning risk, or medical emergency in aquatic venues, follow venue emergency procedures and
seek immediate professional help.

## Product behavior

- The system may **refuse** to analyze clips that appear to be dry-land sports mislabeled as swimming.
- Absence of a finding is not proof of safe mechanics or appropriate training load.
- Beta sport status may limit auto-select; knowledge retrieval can still answer stroke questions with disclaimer.

## Authority and updates

Sources are listed in `_meta/SOURCE_MANIFEST.txt` with authority levels. Federation coaching frameworks change;
retrieval dates matter. When in doubt, cite the live official source.

## User-facing short disclaimer (for UI copy)

“Coaching tips from motion analysis — not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain. Swim only in supervised, safe conditions.”
