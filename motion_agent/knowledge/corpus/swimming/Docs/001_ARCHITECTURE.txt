# Swimming Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first freestyle (front crawl) coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the swimmer’s body
is doing across recognizable stroke-cycle phases. Lane lines, touchpads, and split times are optional enrichments,
not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Freestyle (front crawl)** in pool or open-water–style continuous swimming, side or front oblique camera.
- Bilateral breathing and single-side breathing; default stroke side labeled in session metadata when known.
- One arm cycle per analysis window, or rolling average over multiple cycles when cycle detection is stable.

Secondary glossary scope (not full automated findings in v1):
- Backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, starts, turns, and underwater dolphin kick — concepts in taxonomy only.

Out of scope for automatic findings:
- Exact hand pitch/yaw underwater when wrist is occluded by torso or surface glare.
- Propulsive force, drag coefficient, SWOLF, or heart-rate zones without external sensors.
- Disqualification rulings (FINA/World Aquatics stroke judges) — summarized for coaches only, not automated.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: shoulder/elbow/wrist paths, head position during
   breathing, trunk roll amplitude, kick rhythm proxies, timing between entry and catch.
2. **Evidence layering**: open biomechanics reviews (B+) inform propulsion and shoulder load relationships;
   national governing body learn-to-swim progressions (B) inform drills; clinical consensus (A) informs pain
   and overuse context — always paired with disclaimer docs.
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: avoid prescribing single “ideal” elbow angles; describe patterns and
   attribute ranges to published summaries; separate youth developmental stages from elite performance models.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs so
   the motion pipeline can attach findings to time windows within a stroke cycle.

## Capture and camera guidance

### Preferred views

- **Side view (profile)**: Best for entry, catch depth proxy, elbow flexion during pull, exit timing, body roll,
  and kick amplitude. Camera height near water line or slightly above; minimize lane rope occlusion.
- **Front oblique (45°)**: Useful for crossover at entry, head position, and asymmetry between left/right strokes.
- **Overhead**: Rare in consumer capture; good for symmetry when available.

### Clip length and framing

- Include **at least 3–6 full stroke cycles** at steady pace when possible (roughly 4–12 s at moderate fitness).
- Keep full arm length and head in frame; cropping at wrists reduces pull-phase confidence.
- Underwater cameras improve catch visibility but are not required for v1 heuristics.

### Environment flags

- Open water: higher body roll and sighting may trigger false `fault_excessive_head_lift` — lower confidence.
- Push-off from wall: exclude first 5–8 m or mark `trim_start_event=push_off` so glide phases do not dominate.

## Motion pipeline integration

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Recommended joints: both wrists, elbows, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, nose/head keypoint.
- Stroke side: `stroke_side=right` means right arm is the **pulling** arm in the analyzed cycle (or dominant
  side for asymmetry summaries).

### Stroke-likeness gate (conservative)

Before enabling full product findings, compute a **soft** freestyle-likeness score from:

- **Alternating arm peaks**: left and right wrist speed peaks alternate with period in plausible swim band (~0.8–2.5 s).
- **Elevated shoulder roll**: thorax/shoulder line tilt oscillates with stroke rhythm.
- **Sustained horizontal body**: pelvis height variance below sprint-running profile; not vertical jumping.

Score gates sport-mode selection; it does not authorize medical advice.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

Until dedicated underwater event detectors ship, align narrative phases to computable proxies on the **pulling**
arm unless metadata specifies analysis arm.

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_ENTRY | pulling wrist forward speed local max at hand-forward position; often near water surface Y | Side view |
| E_CATCH | elbow flexion rate peak after entry; wrist depth proxy increases | Noisy if 2D only |
| E_PULL_PEAK | max pulling-wrist speed while elbow flexed | Propulsion window |
| E_EXIT | pulling wrist rises toward surface; forward speed drops before recovery | Recovery start |
| E_BREATHE | head yaw or lateral tilt peak if breathing on analyzed side | Optional |
| E_KICK_PEAK | ankle vertical velocity peak on same side or contralateral per 2-beat/6-beat pattern | Low confidence |

Future detectors may add finger-tip depth, pitch angle from multi-view, and roll phase from IMU fusion.

### Coach brief generation

`build_coach_briefs(..., sport="swimming")` consumes:

- Phase windows when cycle segmentation succeeds.
- Finding cards from Findings/001_common_faults.txt filtered by phase and confidence.
- Cues/drills from Coaching/ linked by finding id.
- Disclaimer snippets from Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt for any shoulder or breathing load topic.

### RAG indexing

- Index all paths except `_meta/` (enforced globally).
- Chunk boundaries prefer `---` section breaks and finding card headers (`finding_id:`).
- English primary; Chinese synonyms appear in taxonomy for query expansion only.
