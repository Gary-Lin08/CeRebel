# Weightlifting — Phase Model (v1)
# Scope: back squat, conventional deadlift, strict overhead press (OHP)
# Aligns: body-only OpenSim / pose keypoints; bar optional
# Stable phase IDs — do not rename without migration note in pack_readme

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Each lift variant shares a high-level arc: setup → eccentric/load → transition → concentric → lockout → recovery.
Squat and deadlift use vertical COM and hip/knee angles; OHP uses wrist/elbow vertical path with strict knee
quietness (no dip-drive in v1).

Tag every chunk and finding with:
  lift_variant = squat | deadlift | ohp

Handedness: bilateral lifts use left/right joint suffixes; asymmetry findings compare L vs R when visible.

================================================================================
EVENT DEFINITIONS (body-only)
================================================================================

E_SETUP_STABLE
  Definition: rolling std of pelvis position below threshold for ≥10 frames at clip start.
  Fallback: first frame.
  Use: trim walk-up noise.

--- Squat ---

E_ECC_START
  Definition: average knee flexion increasing (eccentric) for 3 consecutive frames after setup.
  Proxy: d(knee_angle_avg)/dt > +threshold.
  Fallback: setup_end + 5% window span.

E_BOTTOM
  Definition: local minimum pelvis vertical position (or max knee flexion) before ascent.
  Proxy: argmin hip_y in [E_ECC_START, E_LOCKOUT]; tie-break with max knee_angle_avg.
  Coaching: “bottom” — not IPF depth without bar/box reference.

E_ASCENT_START
  Definition: positive vertical velocity of pelvis after E_BOTTOM.
  Proxy: v_y(pelvis) > threshold for 2 frames.

E_LOCKOUT (squat)
  Definition: knee_angle_avg and hip extension within ~10° of standing reference at clip start; |v_y(pelvis)| low.
  Fallback: argmax hip_y in rep window.

E_RECOVERY
  Definition: E_LOCKOUT + frames until knee flexion begins next rep OR 300 ms idle.

--- Deadlift ---

E_PULL_START
  Definition: vertical velocity of hands/wrists or shoulder center becomes positive; hip extension velocity increases.
  Proxy: first frame where v_y(mid_wrist) > threshold AND hip_angle decreasing (hinge closing) ended.

E_KNEE_PASS
  Definition: knee flexion stops decreasing and begins extending while hips still extend (classic “knee pass”).
  Proxy: inflection in knee_angle_avg between pull start and lockout.
  Fallback: pull_start + 35% of rep span (low confidence flag).

E_LOCKOUT (deadlift)
  Definition: hip and knee near full extension; shoulders behind or over ankle proxy in sagittal view.
  Same family as squat lockout with hip-dominant extension check.

E_LOWER_START
  Definition: hip flexion velocity sustained negative after lockout (controlled down).

--- OHP ---

E_PRESS_START
  Definition: vertical velocity of mid-wrist exceeds threshold; elbows begin extension phase.
  Strict filter: knee_angle_avg change < 5° in prior 200 ms (reject push press for v1).

E_OHP_LOCKOUT
  Definition: local maximum wrist height; elbow flexion near minimum in window.
  Proxy: argmax wrist_y with elbow_angle > 160° (scale-normalized in product).

E_HOLD_END
  Definition: wrist speed below 15% of press peak for ≥150 ms after lockout.

================================================================================
TIMING RELATIONSHIPS (qualitative, B+ literature)
================================================================================

Squat:
- Skilled lifters often show controlled descent then faster concentric; exact tempo varies by intent (strength vs warm-up).
- Forward trunk lean increases with load and stance; not automatically a fault without knee/hip coupling.

Deadlift:
- Hips and shoulders rise together in first pull when technique is balanced; early hip-only rise (“stripper pull”) visible in sagittal pose.
- Lockout completes with hip thrust, not excessive lumbar hyperextension alone.

OHP:
- Strict press: minimal knee/hip flexion; bar path proxy tracks wrist vertical line near mid-foot in side view.
- Lockout with elbows extended and head “through the window” is style-dependent; measure shoulder flexion cautiously in 2D.

Youth: smaller ROM, slower bar speeds, wider stance experimentation — use audience-tagged checkpoints.

================================================================================
CLIP TRIMMING GUIDANCE
================================================================================

- Include full setup brace if possible; findings on descent need ≥0.5 s before E_BOTTOM.
- Deadlift: start before pull slack removal visible as hand movement; end at lockout hold ≥0.3 s.
- OHP: include unrack step-back if analyzing setup stability; strict press only for v1 phase fork.
- Multi-rep clips: segment per rep using E_RECOVERY / next E_ECC_START.

================================================================================
CROSS-LIFT CONFUSION GUARDS (planned)
================================================================================

- Deep squat vs deadlift setup: deadlift shows higher hip hinge, hands below knees at E_PULL_START.
- OHP vs squat: OHP has rising wrists without simultaneous large knee flexion.
- Document mismatch in coach copy when lift_variant detector confidence < threshold.
