# Weightlifting Sport Pack — Architecture (CeRebel)

## Purpose

This pack supplies retrieval-grounded knowledge for **body-first barbell strength coaching** in CeRebel.
The product reconstructs human motion from video (2D/3D body keypoints) and explains what the lifter’s body
is doing across recognizable phases of the **back squat**, **conventional deadlift**, and **strict overhead press (OHP)**.
Bar path, plate load, and rack height are optional enrichments — not prerequisites for coaching briefs or chat answers.

Primary motion scope (v1):
- **Back squat** (high-bar or low-bar appearance; body-only cannot distinguish bar position on back).
- **Conventional deadlift** from floor (sumo labeled in taxonomy but v1 checkpoints tuned for conventional stance).
- **Strict standing overhead press** (no leg drive / push press in v1 phase fork).

Out of scope for automatic findings (glossary or future packs only):
- Snatch, clean & jerk, bench press, front squat as separate phase models (may share hinge/squat concepts).
- Velocity-based training metrics, RPE logging, program periodization math.
- Medical diagnosis of disc, meniscus, or rotator cuff pathology from a single clip.

## Design principles

1. **Body-measurable claims only** for automated findings: hip/knee/ankle flexion trends, trunk angle vs femur,
   segment speeds, timing between descent and ascent events, bilateral symmetry where both limbs are visible.
2. **Evidence layering**: NSCA / ACSM consensus and peer-reviewed resistance-training reviews (A/B+) inform load
   and safety framing; coaching federation progressions (B) inform cues; product heuristics are internal (B).
3. **No fabricated medical norms**: depth and “neutral spine” are described qualitatively or as coaching ranges
   from literature summaries — not universal degree prescriptions for every anthropometry.
4. **Phase-first retrieval**: faults, checkpoints, and cues index to `Phases/001_phase_model.txt` event IDs and
   `lift_variant` tags (`squat`, `deadlift`, `ohp`).
5. **Lift fork**: each clip should declare or auto-detect one primary lift; mixed warm-up clips may gate findings.

## Motion pipeline integration (planned)

### Input contract

- Session adapter produces a `MotionSequence`: joint positions over time, FPS/dt, duration.
- Required joints for lift-likeness scoring (future `lift_score.py`): pelvis/hip, both knees, both ankles,
  both wrists or bar-proxy (mid-hand), shoulders, thorax/spine proxy.
- Camera: sagittal or 45° oblique preferred; frontal-only limits trunk and knee tracking.

### Lift-likeness gate (conservative, planned)

Before enabling full product findings, a **soft** lift-likeness score may use:
- **Squat**: bilateral knee flexion increase then decrease with COM vertical oscillation.
- **Deadlift**: hip hinge with minimal knee travel early, then knee/hip extension together in pull.
- **OHP**: vertical wrist/hand rise with limited knee flexion (strict) and peak wrist speed at lockout.

Score alone does **not** authorize max-effort loading advice; it only gates sport-mode selection.

### Phase detection (v1 heuristic alignment)

| Event ID | Proxy signal | Lifts |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| E_SETUP | low joint speeds, stable COM | all |
| E_ECC_START | start of knee/hip flexion (squat) or hip flexion (deadlift) or wrist rise (ohp) | per variant |
| E_BOTTOM | min COM height (squat/deadlift) or max knee flex (squat) | squat, deadlift |
| E_ASCENT_START | d(COM_y)/dt positive after E_BOTTOM | squat, deadlift |
| E_LOCKOUT | knee/hip near extension + low vertical velocity | squat, deadlift |
| E_PRESS_START | wrist vertical velocity sustained positive | ohp |
| E_OHP_LOCKOUT | argmax wrist height + elbow extension | ohp |

Future detectors may add barbell tilt proxy (wrist height asymmetry), forward knee travel, and lumbar flexion rate.

### Coach brief generation (planned)

`build_coach_briefs(..., sport="weightlifting")` will consume:
- Phase windows and `lift_variant`.
- Top finding cards ranked by severity × confidence × phase alignment.
- One feel cue + one short drill per prioritized finding (from Coaching/).

## RAG chunking guidance

- English primary text; short definitional sentences for embedding.
- Repeat stable finding IDs (`fault_*`) in Findings and cross-link in Coaching.
- Tag chunks with `lift_variant=squat|deadlift|ohp|all`.
- Include `authority_level` in chunk metadata at ingest time.
- Do not index `_meta/build_report.txt` as coaching truth — ops only.

## Youth vs adult

Youth lifters: emphasize technique, submaximal loads, and qualified supervision (B/A consensus).
Do not apply adult powerlifting depth rules to children without coach context. Tag `audience=youth|adult` at ingest.

## Internationalization

v1 corpus is English for RAG. Phase IDs and finding slugs stay stable for future localization.
