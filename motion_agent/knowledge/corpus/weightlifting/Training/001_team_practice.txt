# Weightlifting — Training & Practice Structure
# Scope: squat, deadlift, OHP in gym / team S&C settings
# authority: B (coaching progressions) + A (supervision/youth consensus)

================================================================================
SESSION ARCHITECTURE (typical 60–75 min)
================================================================================

1. General warm-up (5–8 min): brisk walk, row, or bike; dynamic hip/ankle/shoulder mobility.
2. Movement prep (10 min): empty-bar or PVC complex linking hinge, squat, and press patterns.
3. Primary lift (20–30 min): one main squat OR deadlift OR OHP day — technique focus at submaximal RPE.
4. Accessory (10–15 min): unilateral work, core bracing, upper-back for DL, shoulder stability for OHP.
5. Cool-down (5 min): easy breathing, optional light stretch — not forced static stretch before lifting heavy.

CeRebel use: capture 1–3 technique reps during primary lift at working warm-up weight, not every max attempt.

================================================================================
LIFT DAY TEMPLATES
================================================================================

squat_day
  primary: back squat 3–5 sets × 3–8 reps @ technique-focused load (coach prescribes)
  prep_drills: drill_band_knee_spread, drill_tempo_321_squat (from Coaching/002)
  accessories: split squat, goblet squat, core brace holds
  video_capture_window: warm-up sets 2–3, same camera angle each week for comparison

deadlift_day
  primary: conventional deadlift 3–5 × 3–5
  prep_drills: drill_wall_hip_hinge, drill_lat_activation_setup
  accessories: RDL, row variation, hip thrust or glute bridge
  notes: avoid max singles without spotter/familiar platform; film side view

ohp_day
  primary: strict standing press 3–5 × 5–8
  prep_drills: drill_seated_strict_press, drill_z_press
  accessories: lateral raise light, face pull, unilateral dumbbell press
  notes: strict variant only for v1 phase model — tag push press separately if filmed

================================================================================
PROGRESSION PRINCIPLES (non-prescriptive)
================================================================================

- Technique stability before load increase (B).
- Add load in small steps when checkpoints pass consistently across 2+ sessions (coach judgment).
- Deload weeks for teams in-season — reduce volume, keep movement quality filming.
- Never test 1RM without qualified supervision and platform safety (A/B consensus).

Youth teams:
- Emphasize bodyweight and light implement mastery; limit competition max testing.
- Session length shorter (45–50 min); more coaching feedback, fewer heavy sets.
- Parent/coach present per organizational policy — not encoded here.

================================================================================
WARM-UP COMPLEX (empty bar, 90s)
================================================================================

From Coaching/002 drill_barbell_complex_empty:
  5 RDL (hinge pattern)
  5 muscle-clean grip squats (front rack mobility — not Olympic catch)
  5 strict presses
  5 back squats

Cues: cue_brace_360, cue_midfoot_balance, cue_ohp_quiet_legs

================================================================================
FILMING FOR MOTION ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Camera:
- Side or 45° for squat/deadlift trunk and depth proxies.
- Oblique front for knee tracking (squat) — second angle optional.
- Full body in frame including feet at setup and lockout.

Clip length:
- Single rep or 3-rep set trimmed per Phases/001 guidance.
- Label lift_variant in session metadata when product supports it.

Avoid:
- Filming only from behind for OHP (elbow flare faults need oblique/front).
- Cropping feet during deadlift pull.

================================================================================
TEAM PERIODIZATION (high level, no numbers)
================================================================================

Off-season: higher volume technique blocks, frequent video review.
In-season: maintain strength with reduced volume; prioritize recovery and fault monitoring on primary lifts.
Pre-competition (powerlifting team): opener-focused doubles — film openers not third attempts unless safe.

================================================================================
INTEGRATION WITH FINDINGS
================================================================================

Weekly coach workflow:
1. Review top 1–2 faults from latest capture (fault priority in Findings/001).
2. Assign one linked drill from Coaching/002 for next practice.
3. Re-film same angle after 2 sessions to compare phase timing (E_BOTTOM, E_LOCKOUT stability).
