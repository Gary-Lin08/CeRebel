# Weightlifting — Quality Definition (body-first)

## What “quality” means in CeRebel

For this pack, **lifting quality** is not the weight on the bar. It is the degree to which visible body mechanics
align with patterns associated in open literature and reputable coaching with:

1. **Stable setup** — balanced base, intentional brace before moving (setup phase).
2. **Coordinated sequence** — hips, knees, and trunk move in roles appropriate to the lift (squat vs hinge vs press).
3. **Controlled bottom / pull** — no abrupt collapse or uncontrolled segment ordering visible in pose data.
4. **Symmetric finish** — lockout without obvious lateral shift or one-sided collapse when both sides are visible.
5. **Repeatability** — similar event ordering across reps in a set (when comparing clips).

Quality scores in product code (future) should combine checkpoint pass rate, phase confidence, capture angle, and
`lift_variant` — not a single template copied from elite powerlifters.

## Dimensions by lift

### Squat (lift_variant=squat)

| dimension_id       | plain label              | primary phases        |
|--------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| base_balance       | Foot balance & stance    | setup, bottom, lockout |
| descent_control    | Eccentric control        | descent, bottom        |
| depth_appropriate  | ROM for intent           | bottom                 |
| knee_tracking      | Knee vs foot alignment   | descent, ascent        |
| trunk_coupling     | Chest–hip timing         | descent, ascent        |
| lockout_completion | Full hip/knee extension  | lockout                |

### Deadlift (lift_variant=deadlift)

| dimension_id       | plain label              | primary phases        |
|--------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| hinge_pattern      | Hip-dominant load        | setup, pull            |
| bar_close_proxy    | Hands over mid-foot      | setup, pull            |
| lumbar_stability   | Trunk rigidity in pull   | pull, transition       |
| knee_hip_sync      | Shoulders & hips rise    | pull                   |
| lockout_hip        | Glute lockout not lean   | lockout                |

### Overhead press (lift_variant=ohp)

| dimension_id       | plain label              | primary phases        |
|--------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| strict_lower       | Quiet legs               | setup, press           |
| bar_path_vertical  | Wrist path near vertical | press, lockout         |
| rib_stack          | Trunk not over-lean      | press                  |
| lockout_support    | Shoulder/elbow finish    | lockout                |
| head_room          | Clearance without crunch | lockout                |

Each dimension maps to checkpoints in `002_checkpoints.txt`. A rep can be “strong hinge_pattern, weak lumbar_stability”
— product copy should show dimension breakdown, not one grade.

## Evidence basis

- NSCA and ACSM position stands on resistance training (A): health, supervision, progression.
- Systematic reviews of squat and deadlift kinematics/kinetics (B+): joint moments, trunk angle, stance effects.
- Coaching consensus (B): bracing, foot pressure, “spread the floor,” “ribs down” — translated to body signals.

## Youth vs adult quality framing

**Youth:** prioritize learning positions with light load, full supervision, and conservative depth. Smaller femur/torso
ratios change apparent trunk angle — do not label “good morning squat” without context.

**Adult:** same dimensions; allow competition-depth squats and heavier apparent trunk lean when checkpoints for knee
tracking and lockout still pass.

Never instruct max testing from a single app session.

## Capture quality gates

Downgrade or suppress findings when:

- Camera purely frontal for knee valgus or sagittal trunk (need 45° or side view).
- Feet or hands occluded by plates/rack.
- Frame rate < 30 fps on fast deadlift pull.
- `lift_variant` mismatch (push press labeled strict OHP).

## Relationship to findings

Checkpoints pass/fail/unclear at phase windows. Findings fire when rules in Findings/001 trigger with confidence.
