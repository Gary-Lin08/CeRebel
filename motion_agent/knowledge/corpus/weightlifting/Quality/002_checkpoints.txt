# Weightlifting — Checkpoints (body-measurable)
# Format: checkpoint_id | lift_variant | phases | signal | pass (qualitative) | fail hint | authority

================================================================================
SQUAT CHECKPOINTS
================================================================================

cp_squat_setup_feet_stable
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: setup
  signal: lateral velocity of ankle midpoint; distance between ankles vs clip start
  pass: feet remain planted; ankle drift below threshold for 0.3 s before E_ECC_START
  fail: visible step or stagger during brace
  authority: B (coaching consensus)

cp_squat_descent_control
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: descent
  signal: d(knee_angle_avg)/dt magnitude vs d(hip_y)/dt
  pass: smooth monotonic knee flex increase without sudden pelvis drop spikes
  fail: abrupt “collapse” frame cluster at start of descent
  authority: B+

cp_squat_knee_tracking
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: descent, bottom, ascent
  signal: knee center vs foot center in frontal/oblique view (2D projection)
  pass: knees track over mid-foot without sustained inward drift relative to toes
  fail: sustained medial knee displacement vs foot (valgus tendency) — flag capture angle
  authority: B+ (knee loading literature); confidence reduced frontal-only

cp_squat_trunk_hip_coupling
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: descent, bottom
  signal: thorax angle vs femur angle timing; chest drop before hip flexion increase
  pass: chest and hips lower together without extreme isolated chest pitch early
  fail: chest pitches forward while hips remain high (good-morning pattern proxy)
  maps_to_finding: fault_squat_good_morning
  authority: B+

cp_squat_depth_intent
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: bottom
  signal: hip_y relative to knee_y; knee flexion magnitude
  pass: hip crease at or below knee line proxy for “training depth” intent (user/coach setting)
  fail: shallow stop with high hip_y vs knee_y when user tagged “depth focus”
  notes: NOT competition depth without bar/box; qualitative only
  authority: B

cp_squat_ascent_knee_rebound
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: ascent
  signal: knee flexion re-increases after E_ASCENT_START
  pass: knees extend through ascent without large mid-rep flexion bounce
  fail: “dive bomb” rebound visible as knee re-flex >10° mid-ascent
  maps_to_finding: fault_squat_bounce_bottom
  authority: B

cp_squat_lockout_hip_knee
  lift_variant: squat
  phases: lockout
  signal: knee_angle_avg, hip extension vs setup standing
  pass: near-full extension both knees; pelvis not laterally tilted > threshold vs setup
  fail: incomplete knee extension or obvious lateral hip hike
  authority: B

================================================================================
DEADLIFT CHECKPOINTS
================================================================================

cp_dl_setup_hinge
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: setup
  signal: hip angle flexed; shoulders over or slightly ahead of mid-foot proxy; shins near vertical
  pass: recognizable hinge with hands below shoulder height at start
  fail: squat-style setup with knees far forward and hips low without hip hinge
  authority: B

cp_dl_bar_close
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: setup, pull
  signal: horizontal distance wrist midpoint to ankle midpoint (sagittal)
  pass: wrists stay within moderate forward distance of mid-foot through first pull
  fail: bar “drifts” forward as large wrist–ankle separation increase
  maps_to_finding: fault_dl_bar_forward
  authority: B+

cp_dl_hip_shoulder_sync
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: pull
  signal: vertical velocity correlation hip center vs shoulder center
  pass: shoulders and hips rise together in first third of pull
  fail: hips shoot up while shoulders lag (stripper pull proxy)
  maps_to_finding: fault_dl_hips_shoot
  authority: B+

cp_dl_lumbar_flexion_rate
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: pull, transition
  signal: thorax–pelvis angle change rate (lumbar proxy)
  pass: gradual trunk angle change; no sudden lumbar rounding spike under load appearance
  fail: rapid thorax pitch increase early in pull
  maps_to_finding: fault_dl_rounding_pull
  authority: B+ (not pathology — technique load framing)

cp_dl_knee_extension_timing
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: transition
  signal: knee extension vs hip extension timing after E_KNEE_PASS
  pass: legs drive through as hips complete lockout
  fail: soft knees at lockout with hips extended only
  authority: B

cp_dl_lockout_glute
  lift_variant: deadlift
  phases: lockout
  signal: hip extension; excessive lumbar hyperextension proxy (thorax beyond vertical)
  pass: upright finish driven by hips; ribs not flared extreme
  fail: lockout achieved mainly by leaning back with rib flare
  maps_to_finding: fault_dl_hyperextend_lockout
  authority: B+

================================================================================
OHP CHECKPOINTS
================================================================================

cp_ohp_strict_knees
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: setup, press
  signal: knee_angle_avg range during press
  pass: knee flex change < 5–8° (scale-normalized) through press
  fail: visible dip-and-drive (push press) when strict variant selected
  maps_to_finding: fault_ohp_leg_drive
  authority: B

cp_ohp_bar_path
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: press
  signal: wrist midpoint path vs vertical line through mid-foot
  pass: wrists travel near vertical with minimal forward loop
  fail: large forward wrist displacement then back overhead (looping path)
  maps_to_finding: fault_ohp_forward_loop
  authority: B+

cp_ohp_rib_lean
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: press
  signal: thorax angle backward; lumbar extension proxy
  pass: trunk lean modest; press driven by shoulder flexion
  fail: exaggerated layback before bar clears head
  maps_to_finding: fault_ohp_excessive_leanback
  authority: B

cp_ohp_elbow_position
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: press
  signal: elbow height relative to wrist at press start (frontal/oblique)
  pass: elbows under or slightly in front of bar proxy at start
  fail: elbows flare wide with wrists inside (poor stack) when visible
  maps_to_finding: fault_ohp_elbow_flare
  authority: B

cp_ohp_lockout_stack
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: lockout
  signal: shoulder flexion; elbow extension; wrist over shoulder–hip line proxy
  pass: full elbow extension; wrists balanced over base
  fail: lockout with arms not vertical relative to torso lean
  authority: B

cp_ohp_head_clearance
  lift_variant: ohp
  phases: press, lockout
  signal: head translation forward (“chin tuck”) vs bar path
  pass: head moves slightly back then through without cervical crunch proxy
  fail: excessive forward head before lockout (capture noise possible)
  authority: C → do not auto-fault; coach note only

CROSS-CUTTING
================================================================================

cp_capture_sagittal_ok
  lift_variant: all
  phases: all
  signal: metadata camera_angle
  pass: side or 45° oblique documented
  fail: pure frontal for trunk-dominant faults — suppress lumbar and good-morning findings

cp_bilateral_symmetry
  lift_variant: squat, deadlift, ohp
  phases: lockout
  signal: hip height L vs R; shoulder height L vs R
  pass: side-to-side difference below threshold
  fail: persistent unilateral hike — may indicate mobility or load shift (non-diagnostic)
  authority: B

cp_youth_load_context
  lift_variant: all
  phases: all
  signal: audience=youth tag
  pass: checkpoints evaluated with wider tolerances; no max-effort language
  fail: N/A — policy gate for copy
