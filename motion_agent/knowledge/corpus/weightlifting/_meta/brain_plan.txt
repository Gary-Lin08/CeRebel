================================================================================
1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

Deliver RAG-ready knowledge for barbell strength patterns coaches use for
squat, deadlift (hinge), and press families. Analysis uses reconstructed body
only (joint angles, segment positions, speeds). Bar path is inferred from hand
/wrist height proxies when visible — never required for a finding.

In scope v1:
- Back squat (high-bar default narrative; low-bar noted as style variant in Biomechanics)
- Conventional deadlift from floor (sumo = stance variant, same phase IDs)
- Standing overhead press (OHP); bench press shares press phase IDs with floor-start note

Out of scope v1:
- Olympic lifts (snatch, clean & jerk) — separate pack / phase model later
- Load on bar (kg), 1RM, RPE, program design, peaking blocks
- Medical diagnosis, pain treatment, return-to-lift protocols (redirect to licensed providers)
- Belt/wrap/chalk product advice; monolift / specialty equipment rules

Build agent writes full Findings/Coaching bodies from this map; brain agent does not.

================================================================================
2. SAFETY-FIRST (NON-NEGOTIABLE — BEFORE ANY FORM FINDING)
================================================================================

Product behavior (Docs/002 + Coaching preface — build agent):

1. PAIN / INJURY LANGUAGE: If user or session metadata mentions sharp pain, numbness,
   joint locking, or recent surgery — do NOT prescribe drills. Surface safety-pain-stop
   and "stop lifting and consult a qualified professional."

2. NO MEDICAL NORMS AS PRESCRIPTION: Angle bands are coaching heuristics for typical
   cohorts in open literature — NOT individualized medical clearance.

3. YOUTH: No max-effort singles, no 1RM estimation, no "grind through" cues.
   Findings severity down-weight; emphasize technique, supervision, and load caps
   (NSCA / ACSM youth summaries — A/B+). No weighted max testing in product KB.

4. SPOTTER / BAIL: For bench (future video) and heavy squat — KB states body-only
   analysis cannot verify spotters or safety pins; flag safety-environment-unknown
   when user context implies near-max attempt without stating safety setup.

5. LUMBAR SPINE: Findings about "rounding" or "hyperextension" describe visible
   posture under load and possible tissue/load implications in careful wording —
   never diagnose disc injury.

6. ASYMMETRY: Bilateral capture preferred; single-camera monocular OpenSim increases
   uncertainty — checkpoints use conservative thresholds (see section 5).

7. REFUSE WRONG SPORT: When golf/baseball detectors exist elsewhere, weightlifting pack
   must document sport-mismatch in Architecture (build); KB does not mix swing mechanics.

Every user-facing finding card AFTER safety gate includes: id, phases, body signal,
why (performance/load — cautious), feel cue, 30–90s drill, sources[], authority level.

3. LIFT FAMILIES & PHASE MODELS (UI + FUTURE DETECTOR)
================================================================================

Three parallel phase chains. Phase IDs are stable contracts (prefix by family).

--- SQUAT (sq_*) — 7 phases ---

| id          | label_en    | label_zh   | role |
|-------------|-------------|------------|------|
| sq_setup    | Setup       | 深蹲预备   | Bar on back, stance, grip width proxy |
| sq_brace    | Brace       | 深蹲憋气   | Trunk stiffening before descent |
| sq_descent  | Descent     | 深蹲下放   | Controlled eccentric, hips/knees flex |
| sq_bottom   | Bottom      | 深蹲底部   | Turnaround; max knee/hip flex window |
| sq_ascent   | Ascent      | 深蹲上升   | Drive out of hole; knee/hip extend |
| sq_lockout  | Lockout     | 深蹲锁定   | Hips/knees near full extension at top |
| sq_rerack   | Rerack      | 深蹲回架   | Walk-back / bar to rack (if in clip) |

Events (body-only, OpenSim-friendly):
- sq_bottom_event: frame of maximum bilateral knee flexion (mean knee_angle_l/r) in rep window
- sq_lockout_event: first local max pelvis vertical position after sq_bottom_event
- sq_brace_end: last frame before knee flexion velocity exceeds negative threshold
- sq_descent: sq_brace_end → sq_bottom_event
- sq_ascent: sq_bottom_event → sq_lockout_event

--- HINGE / DEADLIFT (dl_*) — 7 phases ---

| id          | label_en      | label_zh     | role |
|-------------|---------------|--------------|------|
| dl_setup    | Setup         | 硬拉预备     | Stance, shins near bar proxy, neutral spine intent |
| dl_brace    | Brace         | 硬拉憋气     | Lat engagement proxy; trunk stiffening |
| dl_break    | Break floor   | 硬拉离地     | Initial separation from floor |
| dl_knee_pass| Knee pass     | 硬拉过膝     | Bar/hands pass knee height; leg drive |
| dl_lockout  | Lockout       | 硬拉锁定     | Hip extension complete; upright finish |
| dl_lower    | Lowering      | 硬拉下放     | Controlled eccentric (if full rep in clip) |
| dl_reset    | Reset         | 硬拉复位     | Return to setup between reps |

Events:
- dl_break_event: max upward vertical velocity of wrist midpoint (or hand bodies) after stillness
- dl_knee_pass_event: knee_angle peaks decreasing while hip still extending (heuristic)
- dl_lockout_event: max hip_flexion closure (min hip flexion angle) / max pelvis height post-pull
- dl_lower: optional phase if eccentric visible after lockout

--- PRESS (pr_*) — 6 phases (OHP default; bench = pr_setup on bench, skip long walkout) ---

| id          | label_en    | label_zh   | role |
|-------------|-------------|------------|------|
| pr_setup    | Setup       | 推举预备   | Rack/unrack position, grip |
| pr_unrack   | Unrack      | 出架       | Clear bar from rack; initial overhead/bench hold |
| pr_eccentric| Eccentric   | 推举下放   | Bar lowered to chin/chest band |
| pr_drive    | Drive       | 推举发力   | Concentric press |
| pr_lockout  | Lockout     | 推举锁定   | Elbows near extension; bar over base |
| pr_rerack   | Rerack      | 回架       | Return to rack |

Events:
- pr_drive_event: max positive vertical velocity of wrist midpoint during concentric
- pr_bottom_event: max elbow_flex during eccentric (OHP: before drive; bench: touch proxy)
- pr_lockout_event: max elbow extension (min elbow_flex) after drive

Handedness: bilateral bar — use mean/symmetry of L/R angles; flag asymmetry finding when |L−R| exceeds band.

5. CLINICAL / COACHING NORMS (EVIDENCE-BANDED — NOT MEDICAL ADVICE)
================================================================================

Wording: "typical in published cohorts / coaching consensus." Build adds URLs in
SOURCE_MANIFEST + research_notes.

ADULT (recreational to intermediate barbell; authority A/B+):

Squat depth (body proxy): knee flex at bottom often ~100–130° in coached full-depth
squats (B+); product flags "likely shallow" if peak knee flex < ~90° without mobility
context (conservative for noisy IK). Partial squat is valid training — do not shame;
label as depth checkpoint only.

Torso angle (squat): forward lean varies by build and bar position; flag extreme
combinations of forward lean + lumbar flexion increase (dl/sq shared concern) rather
than single absolute torso degree (A systematic reviews on squat biomechanics).

Deadlift start: hip height between squat and stiff-leg; "hips shoot up first" =
hip flexion decreases before knee extension begins (B+ coaching heuristic).

Press: lumbar extension increase under load (excessive arch) — compare lumbar_extension
delta from pr_setup to pr_lockout; conservative flag > ~15–20° change (B+, not diagnostic).

YOUTH (≤16 emphasis on supervision; NSCA/ACSM — A/B+ for policy, B+ for angles):

- Shallower absolute depth targets OK; no max load language.
- Separate checkpoint rows: cp_*_youth vs cp_*_adult in Quality/002.
- Growth plates: technique and submaximal sets only in Coaching/002 drill copy.

Exclude authority C from checkpoint/finding thresholds.

6. QUALITY MODEL
================================================================================

Quality/001_quality_definition.txt (build):

Dimensions:
(1) Safety & bracing readiness (setup/brace across families)
(2) Spinal neutrality under load (squat + hinge priority)
(3) Lower-body symmetry and knee/hip tracking (squat, hinge)
(4) Bar-body vertical coordination (proxy: hands/wrist vs pelvis timing)
(5) Press path and shoulder/elbow stacking (press)
(6) Controlled tempo / no collapse at turnaround (bottom, eccentric)

Score v1: checkpoint pass count — not a single composite load score.
