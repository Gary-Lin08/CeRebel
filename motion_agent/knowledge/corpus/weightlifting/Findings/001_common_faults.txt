# Weightlifting — Common Faults (finding cards)
# Canonical v2. Scope: squat, deadlift, overhead press FORM ONLY — no 1RM programming.
# Not medical diagnosis. See Docs/002_DISCLAIMER.txt.

---

finding_id: fault_squat_good_morning
headline_en: Chest drops ahead of hips on the way down
phases: descent, bottom
body_signal: Thorax pitch increases faster than hip flexion; hip angle lags knee flexion early in descent.
why_it_matters: Often shifts load toward hip extensors and lumbar tissues; common coaching concern when lifters lose brace under load (performance and comfort — not a diagnosis).
feel_cue: "Chest and hips drop together — no dive with the shoulders first."
drill: 60s: Practice pattern (see Coaching/002_drills.txt).
severity_default: medium
sources: Escamilla et al. squat kinematics reviews; NSCA squat teaching (B+)
youth_note: Brace and bar path under light load; do not cue max depth under fatigue for youth.
