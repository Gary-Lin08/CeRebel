What comes back

39°

Shoulders turn past the hips

At the top: shoulders 71°, hips 32°.

Why it matters. The gap between shoulders and hips stretches the trunk at the top, and the downswing gets its speed from letting that stretch unwind, hips first.

What to keep. Let the hips stop turning before the shoulders do. Keep the trail knee flexed so the hips stay quiet.

Today's drill. Pause at the top for a count of two, then start down with the lead hip, not the arms. Eight reps.

Questions about your swing, answered from your swing

Why does the gap between my shoulders and hips matter?

At the top your shoulders are at 71° and your hips at 32°. That 39° gap stretches your trunk, and the downswing gets its speed from letting it unwind, hips first.

What should I practise today?

Pause at the top for a count of two, then start down with the lead hip, not the arms. Eight reps.
