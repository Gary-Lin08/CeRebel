    Questions about your swing, answered from your swing

    Why does the gap between my shoulders and hips matter?

    At the top your shoulders are at 71° and your hips at 32°. That 39° gap stretches your trunk, and the downswing gets its speed from letting it unwind, hips first.

    What should I practise today?

    Pause at the top for a count of two, then start down with the lead hip, not the arms. Eight reps.
