This policy explains how {{COMPANY}} ("CeRebel", "we", "us") handles personal information in the CeRebel web app. It is also our public retention schedule and destruction policy for body data that some laws treat as biometric.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), an optional coach code, login sessions, when you accepted the Terms and this policy, when and to which version of the video consent you agreed, and when you last used CeRebel.
  • Videos you upload. To process a video we also keep, for a short time, the frames extracted from it, a playback copy for your report, and one still image.
  • Body data derived from your videos (see section 2): a 3D body model, joint positions, joint angles, estimated joint loads, swing phases and the coaching results.
  • Coach questions. Your questions and the coach's answers are processed to answer you. We do not keep a transcript on our servers; we record only that a question was asked (time, account and swing).
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status, plan and a Stripe customer ID, never your card number.
  • Usage events such as "upload started" or "report viewed", with a time, your account ID, the swing ID and a random browser ID, to improve the product.
  • Technical data: our web server and Cloudflare process your IP address, browser type and the pages you request to deliver and secure the service, and our server writes them to its technical logs.

2. The 3D body model and joint angles

From your video, our software estimates where your joints are in every frame and fits a 3D model of a human body to them: a skeleton with your pose over time, plus numbers that describe your overall body shape and proportions. From the skeleton we calculate joint angles (for example hip turn or wrist hinge), speeds, swing phases, and model-based estimates of the loads on your joints.

  • We use this only to measure and coach your movement. We do not use it, or your video, to recognise or verify who you are, and we do not compare it against other people.
  • We do not extract face landmarks, measure facial features or create a face template. Because the model covers the whole body it includes an approximate head shape, but the skeleton we analyse has a single point for the head, and the 3D figure shown in the app is a generic body posed like you.
  • Because this data describes your body, some laws treat it as biometric or health-related information, and we handle it under the stricter rules described here.

3. Not medical advice

CeRebel gives coaching feedback, not a medical diagnosis or treatment. Joint loads are model-based estimates from a single phone video. Talk to a doctor or physical therapist about pain or injury.

4. How we use your data

Only to provide CeRebel to you: to rebuild and analyse your movement, show your history, answer your questions, run billing, send account and "your analysis is ready" emails, keep the service secure, and improve the product from usage events. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use your videos or body data to train third-party AI models.
