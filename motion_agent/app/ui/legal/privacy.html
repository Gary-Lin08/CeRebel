Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Back to CeRebel · Terms of Service

In short: we process your videos only to analyse your movement for you, we never sell them, and you can delete them, or your whole account, at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account: email address, a salted hash of your password, optional coach code, and when you accepted these terms.
  • Videos you upload and what we compute from them: a 3D body model, body measurements such as segment lengths, joint angles and estimated forces. Because this describes the geometry of your body, some laws treat it as biometric information.
  • Questions you ask the coach and its answers.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and a Stripe customer ID, never your full card number.
  • Usage events such as "upload started" or "report viewed", tied to your account, to improve the product.

2. How we use it

Only to provide and improve CeRebel for you: to rebuild and analyse your movement, show your history, answer your questions, run billing, keep the service secure, and contact you about your account. We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for advertising profiles, or use your videos to identify you.

3. Your consent to video processing

When you create an account you agree that we may process the videos you upload, and the body data derived from them, for these purposes. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your videos or your account.

4. Who else processes your data

  • Our cloud hosting and GPU provider, which stores and processes videos on our behalf.
  • Stripe, for payments.
  • An AI language-model provider for the coach chat. By default it receives your question and the computed numbers, not your video frames.

These providers may only use the data to provide their services to us.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your videos and the body data derived from them until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting removes them from our servers right away.

6. Your rights

You can access, download, correct or delete your data. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California, Illinois, Texas, Washington or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we will honour them.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, sessions expire, each account can only see its own videos, and connections are encrypted in transit.

8. Children

CeRebel is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has uploaded a video, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will notify you before material changes take effect.
